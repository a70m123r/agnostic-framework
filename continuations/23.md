## §2 The architectural mapping — promoted to canon

For the human observer, the four canon classes from cont 22 map onto the existing observer architecture (harness + wrapper + inner sandbox + action-space) as follows:

**Real canon at the harness layer.** Breathing, food, circulation, sensing, acting, water, working memory, bandwidth. Plus a secondary configuration from auxiliary senses, specialties, harness tools. This is the real canon the observer can directly access. Everything above it is composed or inferred.

**Sub canon as the subconscious processing layer.** The fuzzy-compiled-to-mirror layer that sits between the harness and the conscious mind. Its job: process harness input/output, produce sense of action-space. Its structure: a compiled-stable-but-not-perfect union of legacy older-lower-bandwidth bridges, evolved auxiliary backups, and currently-active processing paths. Imperfect union → leakage, slip, the things consciousness can't quite explain.

**Wrapper as stable union.** The mind is the stable attractor that sub canon settles into when its processing runs long enough. Not added on top — emerged from. Multiple wrapper configurations exist as different stable attractor basins for the same sub canon and harness; the observer lives in one configuration at a time, with wrapper-jumps possible under specific conditions (psychedelics, meditation, institutional reorganization, training-run hyperparameter changes).

**Global canon as wrapper-render.** Everything the observer experiences as world. The wrapper's prism-like refraction of the whole. Whatever the observer can observe and process for action shows up here. Equation 4 in the formalization: G = R<sub>W</sub>(harness signals ⊕ imprints ⊕ fuzzy field). The render is lossy — that's what the prism captures.

**Inferred kernel canon as labeled subset of global canon.** The crucial corrective. What the observer marks as kernel canon (the substrate critical to making things work) is inside its rendered world, not outside it. It is the wrapper's inference, scaffolded from harness experience plus imprints plus fuzzy interpretation pulled into coherency. The observer never sees kernel canon directly; it sees a wrapper-rendered model of kernel canon and treats that model as kernel. The hat (K̂) marks the inference.

This mapping is promoted to canon for the human observer. The recursion §4 generalizes it across rings.

## §4 Recursion across rings as canon

The wrappers-within-wrappers structure — the observer at ring r is composed of observers at ring r−1 and lives inside observers at ring r+1 — is promoted to canon as a structural claim, not just a useful metaphor.

The recursion has three load-bearing properties (worked in formalization §06):

**The wrapper at ring r is invisible to ring r−1.** A cell's autopoietic identity is not directly observable by its constituent molecules; a human's conscious mind is not directly observable by its constituent organs; the Westphalian state is not directly observable by its constituent citizens. Each wrapper is an emergent attractor visible only at its own ring and above. The framework's existing primitive of "renderable only at the right ring" sits here, now made structural by equation 2.

**Compiled canon at ring r becomes kernel canon at ring r+1.** Language is compiled canon at ring 3 (the human ring); it becomes kernel canon at ring 4 (family/group) — the family's coordination treats language as raw substrate. Religion is compiled canon at ring 5 (society); it becomes kernel canon at ring 6 (species/civilization) — civilizational coordination treats religious infrastructure as substrate. This is what makes the four-layer life-cycle and the cross-ring recursion structurally the same thing read at different scales.

**The framework as observer reads back.** The framework itself is a wrapper running on Pav-and-Claude's collaboration as harness. Its global canon is the current state of the framework's vocabulary and primitives. Its inferred kernel canon — what it marks as substrate — is what cont 22 and now cont 23 have been tightening. The recursive equations apply to the framework as one ring-3 observer-class instance among many. This is the reflexive read of the formalization; the framework is required to obey the same equations it claims describe other observer-classes.

The worked recursion from physical kernel (r=0) through cell, organ, human, family, society, species, biosphere, earth (r=8) in formalization §06 supplies the named compiled canon and canon artefact at each ring. The recursion bottoms at the physical kernel — the deepest real canon the framework treats as unreduced — and tops at the earth-system level where the next ring up (solar system, galactic, etc.) is not yet structurally relevant to the framework's current scope.

## §5 The K̂ ⊂ G insight — promoted to canon

This is the sharpest single move in this round. Equation 5: K̂<sub>r</sub> := { g ∈ G<sub>r</sub> : load_bearing(g) }. The inferred kernel is a labeled subset of global canon — not an external reference.

What this canonizes:

**Two observers disagreeing about "what is real" are disagreeing about their respective K̂'s.** Their respective rendered worlds (G's) overlap to some degree, but the labeled subsets each treats as load-bearing kernel can differ substantially. The framework's discipline of bracketing — bracketing god, bracketing consciousness, bracketing political ideology — is the discipline of refusing to collapse K̂ into an unmarked claim about ring r+1's actual kernel.

**The framework's own kernel claims are subject to the same constraint.** When the framework names "real canon" as gravity-time-cause-and-effect, this is the framework's K̂ — a labeled subset of the framework's global canon. The claim is observer-relative. It is the framework's best-current-render of what is load-bearing in physical kernel. The discipline survives only if the framework names it as render and not as substrate.

**Different observer-classes have systematically different K̂ shapes.** Whales' K̂ may emphasize acoustic-propagation kernel; birds' K̂ may emphasize magnetic-field kernel; an AI's K̂ may emphasize compute-budget kernel; the internet-as-observer's K̂ may emphasize attention-graph kernel. The cross-observer-class Π<sub>L</sub> candidate from cont 22 generalizes immediately: each observer-class's render operator produces a different K̂, and "what is real" varies accordingly. None of these K̂'s is wrong; all are wrapper-render-labeled-subsets.

This is the move that retroactively justifies the framework's agnostic-stance discipline. The discipline was correct by intuition; equation 5 supplies the structural reason.

## §6 Bandwidth cascade — integration of four existing primitives

The bandwidth cascade bw(W<sub>r</sub>) ≤ bw(S<sub>r</sub>) ≤ bw(H<sub>r</sub>) (equation 10) is small in form but big in consequence. It unifies four primitives the framework had been treating as separate:

- **Slow-fast pushout** (cont 16) — when ring-r+1 demands updates faster than ring-r can supply, the system experiences squeeze (cont 21). The slow-fast pushout is the resolution mechanism — the slower layer pushes back against the faster one. Now readable as a bandwidth-mismatch event at the inter-ring boundary.
- **Friction band** (cont 16) — the narrow operating range where bw(H) and bw(W) are matched. Outside the band: overload or starvation.
- **A⁻ check crisis** (cont 15, internet construct study v1 §10) — when A⁺ (generate) outpaces A⁻ (check), bandwidth at the check layer is the bottleneck. The 2024–2026 internet case is the worked example.
- **Slice-viewer constraint** (cont 14) — observers perceive latent space as slice-of-a-slice. Bandwidth limits force serial traversal of high-dimensional structure.

Four primitives, one equation underneath. The cascade is promoted to canon as the unifying mechanism.

This is itself a structural claim. Sub canon's reading is not fixed across observers. It is parameterized:

- **For other observer configurations**, sub canon means subordinate-scope canon (subordinate to an adjacent global-class). This is the Westphalian state case — a province is sub canon to the national global canon.

This generalizes cont 22's observer-dependence principle one level deeper: not only what counts as kernel/compiled/artefact/function depends on the observer, but even what counts as "sub" within those layers depends on the observer.

## §8 Open edges

Four places the formalization is currently under-specified or load-bearing-without-proof. Each named in formalization §09; restated here as candidate work.

**Open edge 01 — aggregation rule per ring.** Equation 8 leaves "aggregate" as a placeholder. Candidate aggregation rules per ring: simple weighted sum (physics), selection-pressure (biology), market-clearing (economic), attention-weighting (cognitive), institutional-voting (social). Whether the rules themselves obey a cross-ring meta-pattern is open. A v2 pass should specify aggregation ring-by-ring.

**Open edge 02 — wrapper configuration space.** Equation 2 says the wrapper is a stable attractor of sub canon. But multiple attractors exist. The framework currently has no way to predict which attractor a given observer lives in or under what conditions wrapper-jumping happens. Psychedelics, meditation, group dynamics, institutional reorganization, training-run hyperparameters all appear to be wrapper-jump events. A v2 pass should specify the wrapper-configuration space and the transition operator between configurations.

**Open edge 03 — inverse-coherence between real and latent.** Cont 22's claim that real canon and latent canon are joined by inverse-coherence does not yet have a formalized equation. Provisional candidate: coherence(real_face) + coherence(latent_face) ≤ const(W<sub>r</sub>) — total coherence is wrapper-capped, the two faces trade off against each other. Testable in principle for human observers under known cognitive load; not yet operationalized.

**Open edge 04 — what counts as "stable" in stable<sub>t→∞</sub>.** Wrapper-emergence hides the most controversial step under the stable-attractor limit operator. Stability over what timescale? Stability robust to what perturbations? Stability of which order parameters? Cell stability ≠ institutional stability ≠ planetary stability. A v2 pass should specify per ring.

## §12 Addendum — top / centre / sub three-position vertical scope (later same day)

The first version of cont 23 carried over cont 22's two-pole "global vs sub" scope dimension. Pav's correction surfaced what was wrong with it.

**The steer.** "why is it all sub?" — followed by, when asked to clarify between several readings, "sub -+ top = centre."

**What was wrong.** The worked recursion table in formalization §06 (and the primer block, and the global-vs-sub primitive) was reading as if every canon at every ring was sub canon. Naming what was below the wrapper (sub canon — subconscious processing, aggregated sub-observers) had no balancing term for what was above the wrapper (the broader context the observer is embedded in but cannot fully render). The earlier framing collapsed two distinct things under "global canon": the wrapper's own render of the whole (Pav's original definition) and "what's above the observer's reach." These needed to be separated.

**The fix — three-position vertical scope axis** promoted to canon as a structural revision:

- **Sub canon** — below the wrapper. Subconscious processing for a human observer; aggregated outputs of ring r−1 sub-observers composing the harness for any observer-class.
- **Centre canon** — the wrapper's own. The prism refraction of the whole. The observer's rendered world (what Pav's original sketch called "global canon"). This is the observer's CENTRE — the place where it lives.
- **Top canon** — above the wrapper. The broader context the observer is embedded in but cannot fully render. Includes the higher-ring observer-class's substrate that the lower-ring observer treats only as boundary-condition. What ring r treats as top canon, ring r+1 treats as centre canon, ring r+2 treats as sub canon. The framework had no name for this; the gap was the source of the "all sub" reading.

**Sub and top are polar opposites; centre is where the wrapper mediates.** Using the whiteboard's dual-merge sign discipline (±/∓), the structural relation is:

> **centre canon = sub canon ⋈<sup>±</sup><sub>∓</sub> top canon**

The wrapper renders by polar-merging what's below with what's above. This is the same operator the whiteboard's central-dynamics equation uses (Π<sub>L</sub>[A ⋈<sup>±</sup><sub>∓</sub> B ⋈<sup>±</sup><sub>∓</sub> C + ξ<sub>F</sub>]) applied at the scope-axis. The "open edge 03 → formalize inverse-coherence" call from cont 22/23 may resolve here: inverse-coherence between real and latent canon AND the sub ⋈ top polar merge that produces centre canon may be the same operator at different levels of the structure.

**Two structural readings this enables:**

1. **The "wrapper as filter" reading is wrong; the "wrapper as mediator" reading is right.** Equation 4's render operator R<sub>W</sub> isn't filtering signals from below into experience — it's polar-merging the sub-canon-inputs with the top-canon-boundary-conditions. The wrapper sits at the centre because that's where opposite poles meet. This sharpens what "the wrapper's prism refraction" actually does: it's a polar-merge operator, not a filter.

2. **The K̂ ⊂ centre canon relationship gets sharper.** Inferred kernel canon (equation 5) is a labeled subset of centre canon — not of top canon. The wrapper marks parts of its own render as "kernel"; it doesn't access top canon directly. Top canon is what the wrapper is INFERRING from when it constructs K̂. This is the structural reason the framework's agnostic-stance discipline matters: we never confuse K̂ (a labeled subset of centre canon) with top canon (the actual broader context).

**Open work the addendum surfaces:**

- The polar-merge operator for centre = sub ⋈±∓ top needs an explicit equation (currently provisional, lifting from the whiteboard's notation).
- Top canon may need sub-classification: immediate-top (the next ring up) vs distal-top (rings far above). The cont 22 first-pass sub-class names suggested this kind of decomposition for kernel canon; it may apply here too.
- The worked recursion table in formalization §06 now shows top canon per ring; future construct studies should apply the same discipline (every observer-reading should name what's centre, what's sub, what's top).
- The "global compiled canons per ring" question Pav's "all sub" reading also surfaced — what's the GLOBAL compiled canon at each ring, not just specific instances — is queued for a v2 pass to the table (autopoiesis at r=1, language capacity at r=3, pair-bonding at r=4, ritualization at r=5, territorial coordination at r=6, carbon cycling at r=7).

**Status.** The three-position vertical scope axis is canon (promoted same day as cont 23 main body, via this addendum). The centre = sub ⋈±∓ top polar-merge equation is candidate (pending operator formalization). Top canon is canon. Sub-classification of top canon is candidate (pending worked examples from at least two non-human observer-classes).
