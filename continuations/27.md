## §1 Energy-floor failsafe promoted to canon

**Canonical statement.** For any wrapper W that participates in an overlap dynamic with other wrappers and exhibits stable distinction (parents persist as procedural-root stubs through and after the overlap, per cont 24 hairy sphere + cont 18 procedural lineage), there exists a substrate-specific coherence quantity E_W with a positive lower bound E_min > 0, such that the wrapper-overlap dynamic preserves E_W(t) ≥ E_min. The lower bound is the wrapper's internal failsafe (cont 26 §3 internal pressure class); its violation triggers a substrate-specific phase transition — dormancy (cont 20), supersession (cont 25 §1), or break (cont 25 §1).

**Four substrate worked examples that meet the criteria** (full detail in `candidates/energy_floor_failsafe.md`):

- **ACMP / Graph Neural Networks** — Dirichlet energy, theorem-proven positive lower bound (Wang et al. 2022), oversmoothing as the failure mode when violated. Mathematically rigorous; the cleanest worked example. Reading 03.
- **Cell metabolism** — ATP density / adenylate energy charge as the coherence quantity, evolved-homeostasis maintenance with apoptosis triggered as a controlled failsafe when bound is approached. Strong fit; biology provides the failure-mode-as-evolved-program reading that matches the framework's cont 26 §3 "evolved failsafe environment."
- **Creole genesis** — mutual intelligibility within the speech community as the coherence quantity, pragmatic + child-acquisition mechanisms maintaining bound, dialect fragmentation as failure mode (with procedural-root stubs persisting in descendant wrappers per cont 18). Strong fit; sociolinguistics has measurable intelligibility-testing protocols.
- **Institutions** — coordination capacity as the coherence quantity, cognitive limits + selection pressure maintaining bound, institutional fragmentation as failure mode. Moderate fit; the coherence quantity is real but harder to measure than ACMP or biological substrates.

**What this promotion changes in the framework:**

- **PRIMITIVES gains a new canon entry** — "Energy-floor failsafe" alongside the existing cont 26 §3 "L0 internal-pressure failsafes" framing. The energy-floor primitive is the mathematical operationalization of cont 26 §3's qualitative claim.
- **Reading 03's candidate primitive moves from speculative to canon status.** Reading 03 §5 explicitly anticipated this promotion contingent on three additional substrate examples; cont 27 §1 executes it now that the four substrates are in place.
- **Construct studies (marriage, religion, language, nation-state, internet) gain a refactor target.** Each should now be annotated with: (a) its coherence quantity, (b) its empirical or theoretical lower bound, (c) its known failure mode when the bound is violated. This becomes part of the standard construct-study template.
- **Formalization §09b (the candidate reaction-diffusion PDE)** — its energy-floor corollary is now canon, not candidate. The PDE itself remains candidate (gradient flow on three-term energy is one possible formalization; the bound on the energy is the canon part).

**What this promotion does NOT change:**

- The bound is substrate-specific in form (E_W depends on what the wrapper is) and in numerical value (E_min varies by wrapper); the framework does not claim a universal lower bound or universal coherence quantity exists.

## §2 Three-tier epistemic framework formalized

The framework has been operating with three distinct epistemic registers without naming them. Cont 27 §2 names them and makes the procedure explicit for future readings and candidates.

### The three tiers

**Tier 1 — Epistemological.** Claims about how observers know things, perceive things, render things. Empirically testable in cognitive science, psychology, sociology, anthropology, or other substrate-appropriate fields. Does not require committing to contested ontology. Example: cont 26 §4 perceptual rate as sense — different observers sampling the same wrapper at different rates get different fidelity reads. Well-evidenced cog-sci (temporal binding window, intentional binding, attention modulation, sensory substitution).

**Tier 2 — Ontological-candidate.** Claims about what the world is, conditional on contested external evidence (a specific physics interpretation, a contested empirical finding, an emerging research program). The framework can hold the claim conditionally — committing structurally but flagging the contested status of the supporting evidence. Example: Reading 04 compilation-rate-as-time — conditional on Diósi-Penrose / CSL quantum collapse models being right (a minority position in physics), the observer's experience of temporal flow IS the rate at which their wrapper compiles. The framework can structure the claim cleanly while holding the conditionality honestly.

**Tier 3 — Speculative.** Claims with structural coherence to existing framework primitives but no current empirical purchase. Held to preserve framework coherence without endorsement. Promotion to Tier 2 or higher requires concrete falsifiable predictions, worked examples, or measurement protocols. Examples: cont 17 entry "latent space as fundamental substrate (Wheeler-style)"; `candidates/aux_channel_l0_occlusion.md` (L0 occlusion as auxiliary channel for time-cone extension). The framework holds these to be honest about what it cannot dismiss without overclaiming, and to maintain the option of promoting if evidence arrives.

### Why three tiers, not two

A simpler "evidenced vs unevidenced" binary would collapse two distinct moves: (a) committing structurally to a claim while the underlying ontology remains contested (Tier 2) and (b) holding a structurally-coherent claim without committing structurally because no measurement framework exists yet (Tier 3). The Bortolotti et al. mapping (Reading 04) is a clean Tier 2 case — the physics is contested but the framework's claim is sharp; if the physics is right, the framework's claim is right. The aux-channel hypothesis (candidates/aux_channel_l0_occlusion.md) is a Tier 3 case — even if the framework's claim is structurally coherent, there's currently no measurement protocol that would make it testable, so "if the X is right" cannot be specified yet.

The distinction matters because confusing the two registers leads to one of two failure modes: (i) treating Tier 2 claims as already canon (premature endorsement of contested ontology); (ii) treating Tier 3 claims as Tier 2 (smuggling speculation into conditional canon territory). Both failures damage the framework's epistemic credibility differently — (i) by attaching the framework to specific physics interpretations that may not survive; (ii) by drifting toward woo.

### Tier conventions for future readings and candidates

Every new reading or candidate doc should explicitly assign its claims to one of the three tiers in a header field. Multi-claim docs (like `candidates/aux_channel_l0_occlusion.md` with its three-layer decomposition) assign different claims to different tiers within the same doc, with the decomposition made visible.

Tier assignment rules:

- **Tier 1 (Epistemological)** assignment requires: a well-evidenced empirical base in at least one substrate field; testable predictions in that field; no requirement to commit to contested ontology.
- **Tier 2 (Ontological-candidate)** assignment requires: a specific external source (paper, finding, research program) the framework's claim is conditional on; honest accounting of the contested status of that source; framework claim that is sharp enough to be wrong if the source's claim is wrong.
- **Tier 3 (Speculative)** assignment requires: structural coherence with existing framework primitives; named promotion bars (what would need to be true to advance to Tier 2 or Tier 1); long-horizon predictions (typically 3+ years) framed to make the claim concrete enough to be wrong rather than to claim it's right.

### Promotion procedure

A Tier 3 entry advances to Tier 2 when at least one named promotion bar is met. A Tier 2 entry advances to Tier 1 when the contested ontology it depends on gains substantial empirical support (e.g., a successful experimental verification, broad community adoption, multiple independent replications). A Tier 1 entry can become canon proper when it's been integrated into framework primitives (the existing canon vs candidate distinction in PRIMITIVES already handles this stage).

The procedure is: an explicit promotion proposal in a continuation, with the bar that was met named, with what changes in the framework specified, with what doesn't change specified. The cont 27 §1 energy-floor promotion is the worked example of this procedure executed cleanly.

## §3 Speculation pruning procedure

Speculations have stated prediction horizons. Without a pruning procedure, the framework accumulates speculations indefinitely; each one stays at Tier 3 forever even if its predictions go unmet and no new evidence arrives. This produces an unhealthy accumulation that drifts the framework toward speculation-by-volume rather than speculation-by-merit.

Cont 17 attempted some primitive pruning and was partially wrong (cont 18 corrected by promoting one pruned primitive — procedural lineage — back to canon). The lesson from that earlier round: pruning is real work and benefits from explicit procedure, not ad-hoc judgment. Cont 27 §3 specifies the procedure for speculations specifically.

### Pruning criteria

At a speculation's stated prediction horizon, the framework evaluates:

1. **Were the predictions met?** If yes, the speculation likely qualifies for promotion to Tier 2; promotion follows §2's procedure.
2. **Has new evidence arrived that wasn't anticipated?** If yes, the speculation may need refactoring (new predictions, possibly tier change) before re-evaluation.
3. **Are the predictions still unmet but the speculation still structurally relevant?** If yes, the speculation may have new prediction horizons assigned (with explicit reasoning), but cannot persist indefinitely without periodic re-evaluation.
4. **Are the predictions unmet AND no new evidence has arrived AND no concrete promotion path exists?** This is the prune trigger.

### Two pruning outcomes

When the prune trigger fires, the framework chooses between:

- **Demote to "stale"** — entry remains in framework material with explicit "stale" marking. Visible to anyone working with the framework but no longer treated as live speculation. Reserved for speculations that still have structural coherence with framework primitives but have failed their evidence-acquisition timeline.
- **Archive** — entry removed from active framework material (continuations, candidates, speculations sections) but preserved in repo history. Reserved for speculations whose structural coherence has decayed (e.g., a framework primitive they depended on was itself refactored or pruned).

The choice between demote and archive is documented in the continuation that executes the prune.

### Pruning prevention via tier-honest framing

The best protection against the prune-or-promote crisis is honest tier assignment at intake. Reading 04's compilation-rate-as-time is Tier 2 because it is conditional on contested physics with named contestation — if the physics fails, the claim is wrong, the framework knows what to do. The aux-channel hypothesis is Tier 3 because no measurement protocol exists — the framework knows what bars would need to be met before promotion is even possible.

Speculations that ARE Tier 2 (conditional on contested evidence) but get assigned to Tier 3 (no evidence framework available) drift into permanent speculation because no one knows what would resolve them. Speculations that ARE Tier 3 (no evidence framework) but get assigned to Tier 2 (claiming conditionality on evidence that doesn't exist) become unfalsifiable claims-with-citations. Honest tier assignment at intake prevents both failure modes.

## §4 Worked example — the three-layer rate-of-time decomposition

The clearest demonstration of the three-tier framework in operation is the rate-of-time question, which surfaced across cont 26 §4 + Reading 04 + candidates/aux_channel_l0_occlusion.md:

- **L1 / Tier 1 (epistemological canon):** perceptual rate is a sense the observer brings to the world. Cont 26 §4. Well-evidenced (temporal binding window, intentional binding, attention modulation). Empirical purchase via cog-sci; no ontological commitment required.
- **L2 / Tier 2 (ontological-candidate):** compilation-rate-as-time — the observer's experience of temporal flow IS the rate at which their wrapper compiles. Reading 04. Conditional on Bortolotti et al. quantum collapse models being right (contested physics). Framework claim is sharp enough to be wrong if the physics is wrong.
- **L3 / Tier 3 (speculative):** L0 itself is occludable; gaps would be real shared gaps across observers; gaps could serve as auxiliary channel. `candidates/aux_channel_l0_occlusion.md`. No measurement protocol currently exists; three explicit promotion bars (physical signature / shared-observer case / experimental protocol) named so the speculation knows what would advance it.

This decomposition is the framework's canonical worked example of three-tier epistemic discipline. Future readings or candidates that touch the rate-of-time question can point to this decomposition as the standard for how to handle multi-tier hypothesis structures.

The framework should be able to gesture at this when asked "but doesn't this commit you to consciousness-causes-collapse?" or "isn't this just Penrose-Hameroff?" — no, the framework holds L1 as canon, L2 as candidate conditional on contested physics, and L3 as speculative-tier with no commitment. The decomposition keeps the framework's commitments visible.

## §8 Related work and adjacent framings

The three-tier epistemic framework is not novel as philosophical territory — it has substantial company:

- **Lakatos's research programmes** (progressive vs degenerate; hard core vs protective belt; predictive successes earning research-programme credit) — the foundational influence on the framework's self-conception. The three-tier procedure operationalizes Lakatosian discipline at the level of individual claims.
- **Popperian falsifiability** with degrees — Popper insisted on falsifiability as the bright line; the three-tier framework is gentler, allowing Tier 3 entries that aren't currently falsifiable but have named promotion bars. The Popperian bright line still applies for canon promotion.
- **Bayesian credences over claims** — the three tiers can be read as discrete credence ranges (Tier 1 ~ high credence given evidence; Tier 2 ~ conditional credence on contested evidence; Tier 3 ~ low credence held for structural reasons). The framework prefers discrete tiers to continuous credences because the tier distinctions correspond to distinct operating procedures, which credences alone don't capture.
- **Quine-Duhem holism** — claims are tested in clusters, not in isolation. The three-tier framework respects this: a Tier 2 ontological-candidate isn't tested in isolation but within the contested research programme it depends on. When that programme moves, the candidate moves with it.
- **Imre Lakatos vs Paul Feyerabend** debates about methodological discipline — the framework is closer to Lakatos than to Feyerabend's "anything goes." The pruning procedure exists precisely because the framework rejects unlimited speculation accumulation.

The cont 27 procedure is best read as Lakatosian methodology made operationally explicit at the granularity of individual claims rather than at the granularity of entire research programmes.
