## §1. The primitive named

**Canon dormancy with imparted learning on return.**

Stated as canon:

*Canon at the social-substrate ring (and at every ring above biology) does not dissolve. It either persists (load-bearing maintained), goes dormant (load-bearing migrated away but the canon itself preserved as procedural memory in the population's default behavior), or waits in an open action-space (dormant before successor compiled, multiple candidate carriers being scouted). Dormancy is reversible: if the squeeze that produced the canon returns — or any sufficiently similar squeeze emerges — the canon can wake. When it wakes, it does not return identical to its earlier form. It returns refactored, carrying whatever the dormancy period imparted as the new substrate the wake-compile runs on.*

The empirical examples are the entire force of the claim:

**Religious canon waking from dormancy.** Late Soviet-era religious revival across the former USSR after the 1990s. Orthodox Christianity returned, but not as it had been in 1917. It returned as a national-identity carrier intertwined with post-communist state-building, carrying the imparted learning of a 70-year dormancy under official atheism. Same canon-name; different compile. Iran's 1979 Shia revival had a similar shape: religious canon returned after secularizing modernization, but as theocratic state structure rather than dispersed clerical authority. The institutional form was novel; the canon-name was the original.

**Traditional craft revival.** Bookbinding, letterpress, hand weaving, blacksmithing, sourdough baking, fermentation — each went dormant under industrial mass production in the 20th century, then woke from the 1990s onward. The wake-form is not the original. Modern letterpress uses laser-cut plates and digital design files; modern hand weaving uses synthetic dyes that didn't exist before WWII; modern sourdough is documented in real-time online and uses precision-fermented commercial yeast as a cross-reference standard. The canon (the craft) is the same; the compile is post-industrial.

**Vinyl records.** Went dormant after CD adoption in the late 1980s. Woke from the late 2000s onward, now outselling CDs in many markets. The wake-form: heavier vinyl pressings than the 1970s norm, audiophile mastering practices that didn't exist before digital recording, distribution via streaming-platform tie-ins, demographic centered on collectors and the post-millennial generations rather than the original audiences. Different compile, same canon.

**Bicycle infrastructure.** Heavily built out in many European cities in the early 20th century, dismantled or dormant under automobile dominance from the 1950s through the 1980s, woken since the late 2000s. The wake-form is not the original bicycle infrastructure — it includes protected bike lanes that didn't exist before, integrated with electric-bicycle and public-transit networks, designed with traffic-calming principles that emerged during the dormancy period. The Dutch and Copenhagen models are widely cited as having "preserved" bicycle infrastructure through the dormancy, which is partly correct: they preserved a substrate that other cities are now using as the template for the wake-compile.

**Marriage canon — the live case.** The marriage construct study (artifacts/marriage_v1.html v1.2) reads three regional configurations through the dormancy frame. Nordic regions: institution dormant, imparted learning preserved as the cohabitation-protocol default. MENA / South Asia: institution still load-bearing, no dormancy. East Asia: institution dormant before a successor compiled (open action-space). The v1.2 refactor of the marriage essay was the immediate trigger for promoting this primitive to canon. The primitive applies recursively to itself: if welfare-state-backed cohabitation collapsed in the Nordics, marriage canon could wake — but in refactored form, carrying the gender-equal partnership norms and child-outcome equivalence the dormancy period imparted. Cont-20 is committing to the prediction that any such wake would not be a return to mid-20th-century marriage; it would be a new compile.

## §2. What this refines in existing canon

**Pruned, kept honestly (cont 17) was already the dormancy mechanism, retrospectively.**

Cont 17's pruning entries for spiral substrate cycle, compile-capacity enrichment, and procedural canon used the verb "fold" rather than "delete." The pruned primitives weren't discarded; they were folded into other primitives where they survived as procedural inheritance. Cont 18 then woke "procedural canon" by promoting procedural lineage — the wake-form was a substantially different compile, carrying the imparted learning of cont-17's pruning decision. That is canon dormancy with imparted learning on return, applied to the framework's own canon graph. Cont 17 didn't have the vocabulary; cont 20 supplies it retrospectively.

This re-anchors the pruning discipline. Pruned ≠ dead. Pruned = dormant. Cont 20's correct read of cont 17 is: *"the pruning decision moves three primitives from active canon into dormant canon, where they remain available for wake-with-imparted-learning if a later round's evidence warrants reactivation."* That is exactly what cont 18 did to procedural canon. The mechanism was already running; cont 20 names it.

**Moving target (cont 19) gains bidirectionality.**

Cont 19's moving target said canon, primitives, hidden protocols are in flux. Cont 20 adds: the flux is not just forward (compile, refactor, prune) but also backward (wake from dormancy). This sharpens the moving-target framing: the framework's epistemics commit to canon being able to travel from active to dormant and back, in either direction, on its own timeline. The canon graph is not a tree (compiled, then pruned-and-forgotten); it is a graph with reactivation edges.

**Procedural lineage (cont 18) gains dormant-as-substrate.**

Lineage describes physics → chemistry → biology → cognition → society → person as concentric rings, each compiling on top of the previous. Cont 20 adds: dormant canon at any ring is still part of the substrate the next ring compiles on. A secular European who has never attended church is still standing on substrate that includes a thousand years of Christian canon compiled into the moral vocabulary, the calendar, the legal system, the architecture, the music. The canon is dormant in that person's active practice; it is not dormant in the substrate they walk on. This is a substantive sharpening — it means the procedural lineage carries dormant-canon residue at every layer, and a careful reading of any social-ring artifact (a law, a building, a moral intuition) should trace which dormant canon is still in the substrate.

**The catalyst principle (cont 19) gains an internal-fuel mechanism.**

Cont 19's catalyst principle said external substrate is the framework's growth metabolism. Cont 20 adds an internal complement: dormant canon is the framework's *reserve fuel*. When external catalyst is sparse and the framework needs to refactor, the dormant canon graph contains pruned-but-not-deleted primitives that can be re-examined. The cont 17 → cont 18 sequence is the worked example: cont 17 pruned three primitives, cont 18 woke "procedural canon" from the dormant pool in refactored form (procedural lineage). The framework's growth metabolism is *both* external catalyst absorption *and* internal dormant-canon reactivation. The two are complementary, not competing.

## §3. The imparted-learning mechanism

The novel claim of cont 20 — beyond just "canon is reversible" — is the *imparted learning* clause. The mechanism deserves its own paragraph.

When canon goes dormant, what is happening is that the *carrier* (the institution, the practice, the protocol) lapses out of active maintenance, but the *substrate it ran on* continues to evolve under whatever new carriers have absorbed the function. The substrate's evolution during dormancy is the imparted learning. When the original canon wakes, it has to compile against the *current* substrate, not the substrate that existed when the canon first went dormant.

Three layers of imparted learning operate during dormancy:

**Substrate evolution.** The biological, technological, infrastructural, and cognitive substrates the canon originally compiled against have themselves changed during the dormancy. Religious revival post-Soviet had to compile against a population that had been universally literate, urbanized, exposed to scientific worldview, with mass-media-mediated identity — none of which were the substrate the original Orthodox canon compiled against in 1917. The wake-form is shaped by that substrate update.

**Successor-carrier residue.** During dormancy, alternative carriers absorbed pieces of the original function. Those carriers leave residue. Even if the canon wakes and reclaims function, the alternative carriers' patterns are now part of what people expect from the function. Bicycle infrastructure waking in cities that have lived through automobile dominance for 60 years cannot ignore the safety expectations, traffic patterns, and urban planning practices that developed during the dormancy. The wake-compile incorporates them.

**Memory of the squeeze.** The population, the institution, the substrate carry memory of why the canon went dormant in the first place. That memory is itself a constraint on the wake-compile. Marriage canon, if it ever woke after a welfare-state collapse, would do so in a population that remembers what gender-unequal partnership looked like; the wake-form would be constrained by that memory in ways the original canon wasn't.

Together, these three are the imparted learning. They are why "canon doesn't return in the same way." The substrate it has to compile against has changed; the alternative carriers it has to coexist with have changed; the memory it has to compile around has changed. A wake-compile is always a refactor, not a restoration.

## §4. Implications for other primitives

**Squeeze-to-pull / friction band.** The friction-band primitive (cont 16) describes the band of conditions inside which canon-formation can occur. Cont 20 implies: dormant canon sits *outside* the friction band — neither actively forming nor actively dissolving — but can re-enter the band if conditions shift. The friction band is not a one-way gate. This sharpens the primitive without contradicting it.

**Open action-space.** The marriage essay v1.1 named open action-space as a configuration where canon dissolves before a successor compiles. Cont 20's correction: canon doesn't dissolve; it goes dormant. Open action-space is therefore *canon-dormant-before-successor-compiled*. The framework's prediction for what happens at the end of the open-action-space scouting phase has three possible exits, not just one: (a) a new carrier compiles and absorbs the function (the successor that was being scouted); (b) the dormant canon wakes in refactored form because none of the scouted carriers is adequate to the function; (c) the function itself stops being load-bearing because the underlying squeeze relaxed. Bubble 02 of the marriage essay implicitly assumed only (a); cont 20 widens to (a)+(b)+(c).

**Provenance carrier methodology.** Continuation 16 named "carrier before canon" as methodology. Cont 20's worked example — cont 17 → cont 18 — is also "carrier before canon," but at the framework's own canon graph: cont 17 produced the prune-into-dormancy carrier; cont 18 produced the wake-with-imparted-learning carrier; cont 20 names the compiled canon that both were operating in. This is consistent and reinforcing.

**Multi-architecture universal approximation (cont 14 candidate).** The candidate says multiple architectures can compile the same canon if the substrate constraints are right. Cont 20 implies: the same canon can be approximated by different compiles at different times, with the wake-compile being a different architecture from the original-compile. This is a small extension of the candidate; worth tracking but not promoting yet.

**Computational irreducibility (cont 13 canon).** Irreducibility says substrates must be run, not solved. Cont 20 adds: dormant canon is not solvable in advance for what its wake-form will be — the wake-compile must be run on the actual substrate at the actual moment, not pre-computed. This sharpens irreducibility's domain to include the wake-from-dormancy case explicitly.

## §6. Open edges

The new canon entry is not without open edges.

**One.** The framework can't yet predict ahead of time which dormant canon will wake. The cont-17 prunes (spiral substrate cycle, compile-capacity enrichment, procedural canon) included one that woke (procedural lineage) and two that haven't (yet). Why those two didn't wake within the same conversational round — was it that they were less load-bearing, that their substrate hadn't shifted enough, that no external catalyst arrived to wake them — is currently unclear. Cont 20 doesn't supply that prediction mechanism; it just names the dynamic.

**Two.** The reversibility claim is stated for the social-substrate ring but the framework has been applying it implicitly across rings (procedural lineage substrate carries dormant residue). Whether dormancy-with-wake operates the same way at the biology ring (vestigial structures, dormant genes), at the cognition ring (dormant memories, dormant skills), at the physics ring (we have no biological-or-cultural-style precedent at the physics ring) is open. The cross-ring claim is implicit in the primitive's positioning under procedural lineage; making it explicit is future work.

**Three.** Distinguishing dormant canon from genuinely-dead canon is hard in practice. Most theological scholars in 1955 would have said Russian Orthodox Christianity was dead under Soviet rule. The dormancy-vs-death distinction is observable in retrospect (because dormant canon wakes, dead canon doesn't) but not predictively. The framework's discipline here is: assume dormancy is the default unless there is a structural reason to think the substrate the canon ran on has itself been destroyed. Death-of-canon is the limit case where the substrate goes with the canon; dormancy is the general case where the substrate persists and the carrier lapses.

**Four.** The "imparted learning" clause needs a sharper mechanism. Cont 20 §3 lists three layers — substrate evolution, successor-carrier residue, memory of the squeeze — but these are descriptive rather than predictive. A future continuation should specify how to measure imparted learning, ideally with a worked metric on one of the empirical anchor cases. This is the cleanest follow-up work.

## §10. Pre-publication self-checks

The framework's discipline says to name worries before they get raised externally.

**One. Is canon dormancy too generous?** The framework now has two ways to handle a primitive that doesn't currently have evidence: (a) keep as candidate, (b) prune into dormancy. Risk: the framework never has to admit it was wrong, because everything pruned can later be claimed as dormant and woken. The discipline that prevents this: cont 17's prune-with-counter-evidence-named practice, which forces the pruning round to articulate why the primitive is failing rather than just demoting it. A dormant primitive should have an explicit counter-evidence note that any future wake-attempt has to reckon with.

**Two. Is the imparted-learning clause too speculative?** §6 #4 names this. The cleanest move is to identify a measurable case (the vinyl example is the most tractable) and run it as a worked example before treating the imparted-learning clause as canon-ready. Continuation 20 is committing to the *direction* of the claim; the imparted-learning mechanism's specific operational form remains candidate until the worked metric is produced. This is a partial promotion: dormancy is canon; imparted-learning-on-return is canon-as-direction-with-candidate-mechanism.

**Three. Does the primitive over-couple to the marriage construct study?** The primitive was triggered by the marriage essay's v1.2 refactor; the marriage essay is the worked example most-cited in this continuation. Risk: the primitive is a marriage-essay artifact masquerading as a framework primitive. The check: cont 20 §1's empirical anchors (religious revival, traditional craft revival, vinyl, bicycle infrastructure) are all independent of the marriage case, and each is independently observable in the standard literature. The marriage essay was the trigger; it is not the only evidence. If the marriage case were retracted, the primitive would still have four independent empirical anchors.

**Four. Where could the primitive be wrong?** The most plausible failure mode is that "dormancy" and "imparted learning" become container terms that absorb any case where canon-language has been retired or refactored, regardless of whether the structural claim (substrate-evolution + successor-residue + squeeze-memory) actually applies. The discipline: each new application of canon-dormancy should explicitly identify which of the three imparted-learning layers is doing the work. If none of the three is identifiable, the application is loose and should be flagged as candidate-grade rather than canon-grade.
