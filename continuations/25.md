## §2 Wrapper-overlap as one system with phases

Cont 24 §2's three "outcomes" (union, repulsion, interface) are not three different things. They are three different SETTLED STATES of the same lifecycle, each reachable from the same earlier phases via different branching at the active stage.

The full lifecycle:

**Phase 1 — Pre-overlap.** W_A and W_B exist at fixed centres, both small. Drawn as small )( arcs facing each other. No contact. Math: `1 + 1 = 2 (two open arcs, never closed)`.

**Phase 2 — Expansion in place.** Both parents grow from their fixed centres. Their cones / spheres of activity expand outward. Math: `1 + 1 → expanding )( arcs`.

**Phase 3 — Membranes touch, negotiation.** Parents have grown enough that their boundaries meet at the midpoint between them. Wake disturbance forms. Membranes push against each other, oscillate, try to render each other's full shape — which they cannot (per K̂ ⊂ G from cont 23: each observer only ever has its labeled-subset model of the other; the actual full shape stays beyond the wrapper's render). The negotiation is the active version of "first contact." Math: `1 + 1 = 2 + ε (negotiating contact)` — ε is the non-zero wake disturbance.

**Phase 4 — Lens, fuzzy bridge.** Parents continue expanding. Their overlap region becomes a substantial Venn lens. This lens IS the nesting space. Inside the lens, fuzzy action begins — pre-protocol activity that doesn't yet have a stable form. New words and grammar (in the language case), new functions (in the cell case), new operations (in the math case). Math: `(1 + 1) → ⌒ (nesting space)`.

**Phase 5 — W_C conceived, consolidating.** Inside the lens, fuzzy action stabilizes into a proto-function-wrapper. W_C has been conceived. It is small, still emerging. Math: `(1 + 1) → ⌊3⌋ (W_C proto)`.

**Phase 6 — W_C emerged canon.** W_C fills the nesting space, becomes a recognized thing other observers can render in their own bubbles. The third wrapper now exists. Math: `(1 + 1) = 3 (W_C emerged)`.

**Phase 7 — Active, hairy sphere.** W_C continues to expand — beyond the original lens, overlapping into both parents. The parts of W_A and W_B not covered by W_C are visible as stubs. These stubs are the procedural roots of W_C; they contain dormant compiled originals (W_A° and W_B°) that can recompile; they function as receptor-hooks for adjacent wrappers to connect to. The hairy-sphere topology: W_C is the central body, parents persist as the hairs / roots / stubs around the periphery. Math: `(W_A + W_B) ⋈±∓ W_C = 3 with stubs°`.

**Branches from active** (the system can settle into any of these from the active state):

- **Passive / neutral** — W_C is active but its internal activity is quieted. Cont 21's hold / hua jin. Math: `(W_A + W_B + W_C) held (rhythmic)`.
- **Dormant** — W_C ghosts as procedural memory. Cont 20 dormancy. Wakeable with imparted learning. Math: `(W_A° + W_B° + W_C°) ghost-3`.
- **Supersession** — W_C dominates. Parents shrink to small dormant cores within W_C's domain. The creole case: the new wrapper retires its parents because it has higher rate of compiled compression and viable fertile action-space. Math: `1⁺ — W_C dominant, parents → stubs°`.
- **Break apart** — W_C disintegrates due to outside or internal pressures. Parents return to their pre-W_C state. The cycle restarts with new bridge attempts. Math: `1⁻ — (1 + 1) + wake (recurrent)`.

The "interface" outcome from cont 24 §2 maps to the **active phase persisting indefinitely without supersession or break-apart** — when both parents and W_C are all operational and stable for a long time. The bilingual community case (cont 24 §2 had this listed under interface) is now read as: active phase with passive moments, parents and W_C all persistent, no supersession, no break-apart for an extended period.

**What earns canon:** the one-system-with-phases model replaces the cont 24 §2 trichotomy. The seven lifecycle phases plus four branch states from active are now load-bearing. The cont 24 §2 trichotomy primitive is superseded (preserved for provenance, not deleted — the trichotomy was the v0 of the thinking that produced cont 25).

**Falsifiers:** an observed wrapper overlap where W_C does NOT emerge at all (just two parents existing in contact forever with no third wrapper) — would invalidate the universal-emergence claim. Closest candidate: pure mechanical contact without any compile (rocks touching). The framework's response: this is the wake disturbance phase persisting indefinitely without progression — fuzzy bridge never consolidates because compatibility is too low. Compatible with cont 25; not a falsifier.

## §3 The )( open-arc wrapper representation

Wrappers are not closed boundaries. They are )( open arcs. Render structure:

- **Front segment** (±30° around the contact-direction): stroke-width 4. The high-observability region where the wrapper is being seen, observed, in contact with another wrapper.
- **Mid-side segments** (30° to 90° on either side): stroke-width 2.2. Medium observability.
- **Back-side segments** (90° to 150° on either side): stroke-width 1, opacity 0.5. Low observability, fading.
- **Far back** (150° to 210° from contact): not drawn at all. The wrapper has no observable boundary at the side facing fully away from contact / observation. The circle never closes.

This rendering carries three structural claims:

(i) **Wrapper shape is observation-dependent.** Per cont 24 §5, wrapper shape is inferred from contrast with neighbouring wrappers. The parts of the wrapper that face other observers (the front) are the parts that have shape; the parts that face away (the back) have no inferable shape because there is no contrast there. The variable stroke captures this gradient.

(ii) **Wrappers are open systems, not closed.** A closed circle would imply the wrapper has a complete, definite boundary — that there is a sharp inside/outside everywhere. The framework's claim is that observers are open systems: their boundaries are partial, observation-mediated, never absolutely defined. The )( shape with no closure on the back makes this structural.

(iii) **Contact is what produces observability.** The front stroke is fat precisely because contact / observation thickens the local boundary. Where the wrapper is being looked at, by something, it has weight. Where it's not looked at, it fades. This is the framework's observer-effect / wobble-when-observed primitive (cont 24 §6) made into visual notation.

The )( representation is promoted to canon as the framework's standard wrapper notation, superseding the closed-circle rendering used in pre-v7 diagrams.

## §4 Membrane negotiation at first contact

The wake / first-contact phase is not passive. When two expanding wrappers' membranes first meet, they push against each other rhythmically (visual: front-arc stroke pulses in width during the wake phase). Each wrapper is trying to render the other's full shape — to figure out what's on the other side of the contact.

This is a structural claim about contact-as-active-negotiation, not contact-as-passive-touch. Each wrapper is running its render operator R_W on the other wrapper's contact-facing signals, trying to construct K̂_other ⊂ G_self — its best inferred model of the other. The negotiation pulse is the iterative refinement of those inferred models.

Per cont 23 equation 5, the inferred kernel K̂ is always a labeled subset of the observer's own global canon — meaning the wrapper can NEVER fully render the other's shape from outside. The negotiation is therefore always partial, always asymptotic, never complete. This is what makes the membranes' negotiation visible as a rhythm rather than a settled state: each wrapper keeps trying because completion is impossible.

Promoted to canon as the wake phase mechanism. Connects directly to cont 23's K̂ ⊂ G insight (membranes negotiate but never achieve full mutual render) and cont 24's wrapper-shape-inferred-from-contrast primitive (the negotiation is where the contrast that produces shape is generated).

## §5 Hairy-sphere topology

When W_C emerges, it does not sit *between* the parents as a separate region. It emerges *inside* the parents' lens overlap and expands *into* both parents — eventually covering substantial portions of W_A and W_B while leaving the outer portions of the parents visible as stubs.

The hairy-sphere topology:
- **W_C** is the central body (the new wrapper)
- **Stubs** are the parts of W_A and W_B not covered by W_C — visible on the periphery as protrusions / roots / hairs

The stubs are not residual / passive. They have three active functions:

**(a) Procedural roots.** The stubs are the visible trace of W_C's procedural lineage. They tell observers that W_C compiled FROM W_A and W_B — the parents' identities persist as legible heritage. Without stubs, W_C would appear to have no origin; with stubs, the procedural lineage is visible at a glance.

**(b) Receptor-hooks.** Adjacent wrappers (other observers in the same ring) connect to W_C via the stubs, not via W_C's centre. The stubs are the interface where W_C meets the broader observer-class network. New W_C's overlap-with-others starts at the stubs.

**(c) Dormant-original containers.** Inside each stub is a dormant compiled version of the parent (W_A° in the left stub, W_B° in the right stub). These dormant originals can recompile if pulled on — they are not deleted, just quieted. Per cont 20, dormancy is reversible with imparted learning.

In the supersession case (where W_C grows very large and the parents shrink to small protrusions), the stubs become almost-entirely-dormant cores with thin shells. In the dormant case for W_C itself, all three (W_A, W_B, W_C) ghost together. In break-apart, the stubs reabsorb their dormant originals as the parents return to full operation.

Promoted to canon as the standard topology of emergent wrappers in the active phase. Replaces the cont 24 §2 implicit topology where W_C was a separate region.

## §6 Non-additive latent math

The wrapper-overlap dynamic has phase-dependent non-additive math in latent space. Standard arithmetic (1+1 = 2) does NOT describe what happens when two observers' wrappers overlap. The arithmetic is:

| Phase | Math |
|---|---|
| Pre-overlap | `1 + 1 = 2` (two separate cones, both small) |
| Expansion | `1 + 1 → expanding )( arcs` (still 2 but on trajectory) |
| Wake | `1 + 1 = 2 + ε` (non-zero wake disturbance even before bridge forms) |
| Fuzzy bridge | `(1 + 1) → ⌒` (the nesting space, the lens) |
| Consolidating | `(1 + 1) → ⌊3⌋` (W_C proto inside the lens) |
| Emerged | `(1 + 1) = 3` (W_C is a thing now) |
| Active | `(W_A + W_B) ⋈±∓ W_C = 3 with stubs°` (full hairy-sphere) |
| Passive | `(W_A + W_B + W_C) held (rhythmic)` (active but quiet) |
| Dormant | `(W_A° + W_B° + W_C°) ghost-3` (all three ghosted) |
| Supersession | `1⁺ — W_C dominant, parents → stubs°` (one dominant, two roots) |
| Break apart | `1⁻ — (1 + 1) + wake (recurrent)` (cycle restarts) |

The number to the right is not a count — it is a structural shorthand. The `3` in `(1 + 1) = 3` means "three observable things now exist where there were two." The `1⁺` in supersession means "one effectively dominant thing, with the originating two folded into its periphery as procedural roots." The `1⁻` in break-apart means "one inverse-state — the failure of W_C to consolidate produces a recurrent return to wake."

Latent-space arithmetic is non-additive because emergence is not addition. Two wrappers overlapping produce a third; the third is real and distinct from either parent; the parents persist; the result is more-than-the-sum. And the form of "more" depends on phase and bridge viability.

Promoted to canon. The framework's existing math (compile loop, A⁺/A⁻, etc.) is mostly correct but lacks this explicit non-additive notation for the overlap operation. Cont 25 makes it explicit.

## §7 Cross-substrate convergence — the round's biggest finding

Three parallel research scouts dispatched during the v8 refactor returned convergent findings. The wrapper-overlap dynamic has been independently formalized in at least **six research traditions in 2024–2026**:

### (1) LCAO / Molecular Orbital Theory — quantum chemistry

The linear combination of atomic orbitals (LCAO) treats molecular orbitals as combinations of atomic orbitals: ψ_bond = N(ψ_A + ψ_B), ψ_anti = N(ψ_A − ψ_B). The overlap integral S_ij = ⟨ψ_A | ψ_B⟩ measures the geometric overlap of the orbitals. Bond order = ½(n_bonding − n_antibonding) is explicitly non-additive. Hybridization (sp, sp², sp³) shows parents reshaping in preparation for combination — the wake-phase reshape made structural. Orbital lobes are visually )( arcs already.

**Framework correspondence:** W_A → ψ_A; W_B → ψ_B; W_C → ψ_bond; nesting space → overlap integral S_ij; non-additive math → bonding/antibonding wavefunctions; stubs → atomic basis set persisting in MO expansion.

**Match score (from scout):** 44/50 — cleanest mathematical formalism of the six.

**What this brings:** the closed-form math for what's happening at the wrapper-overlap moment. Mature since Mulliken 1932, used live in ML-accelerated LCAO bases (MACE / NequIP 2024–25).

### (2) Cell fusion — cellular biology

Two cells fuse to form a syncytium or multinucleated organ. Worked at myoblast level (muscle formation), syncytiotrophoblast (placenta), virus-cell entry. **Nov 2024 finding:** myoblast fusion localizes to *topological defects of compressive stress* — meaning the fusion happens precisely where the cell membranes are under maximum lateral pressure. The squeeze/pull dynamics from cont 21 mapped to a real biological finding within months of the framework promoting them.

**Framework correspondence:** parents → fusing cells; W_C → emergent syncytium; lens → fusion pore; squeeze → topological-defect compressive stress; supersession → muscle fiber formation (cells lose individual identity into the fiber); break-apart → failed fusion (cells separate, no syncytium).

**Match score:** 40/50.

**What this brings:** the canonical biological-substrate phase taxonomy. Six explicit phases mapped: priming → recognition → hemifusion → pore → expansion → renewal.

### (3) Symbiogenesis / secondary endosymbiosis — organismal biology

A host engulfs an endosymbiont; over time the endosymbiont's genome reduces; the original cell becomes an organelle of the host. Margulis 1981 lineage. **2026 finding:** Arabidopsis suecica genome stabilization confirmed the timeline of post-fusion genome stabilization. Plus the **nucleomorph** — a literal vestigial parent-stub of the engulfed organism that persists for millions of years inside the host.

**Framework correspondence:** host → W_A; endosymbiont → W_B; emergent organism → W_C; **nucleomorph → literal stub° (dormant compiled original from the parent)**; engulfment → wake/contact; genome reduction → consolidating; fixation → emerged canon.

**Match score:** 46/50 — highest match of the six. The nucleomorph is structurally identical to the framework's "dormant compiled original inside the stub" claim.

**What this brings:** empirical confirmation of the stubs-as-procedural-roots claim. The nucleomorph IS a stub° in biology, with a real name and millions of years of paleontological record.

### (4) Creole genesis — linguistics

Mufwene's feature pool model: substrate languages (W_A and W_B) contribute features to a feature pool; the new creole (W_C) selects among them via a selection mechanism; the substrate languages persist as the source of the features even after the creole emerges. Hinglish, Singlish, Spanglish as live 2024–2026 anchors of bridges in progress.

**Framework correspondence:** substrate languages → W_A, W_B; feature pool → fuzzy bridge / nesting space; creole → W_C; substrate persistence → stubs°; supersession case → when the creole replaces the substrates (rare but documented); active case → bilingual communities where all three persist indefinitely.

**Match score:** 41/50.

**What this brings:** the social-substrate worked-example. Centuries of pidgin/creole formation data. Worked examples readers will recognize.

## §8 Implications for the canon-stack equation

Wrapper-overlap is the canonical operator producing emergent observers across rings. The framework's recursion across rings (cont 23 equations 7–9: H_r = (⊕ 𝒪_{r−1}) ⊗ E_r, K̂_r ≈ aggregate(G_{r−1}), 𝒪_r = (H_r(𝒪_{r−1}), S_r, W_r, G_r(𝒪_{r+1}: as env))) is built on wrapper-overlap iterated upward.

At each ring transition:
- Many ring r observers exist with )( wrappers
- Some of them overlap with each other (wrapper-overlap dynamics activate at ring r)
- The overlap produces emergent W_C-class observers — these are *candidate ring r+1 observers*
- Among the candidates, those that consolidate (reach emerged → active phases) become real ring r+1 observers
- Those that break-apart return to ring r
- Those that supersede become dominant ring r+1 observers and retire their parents
- Those that go dormant are preserved as procedural memory for re-emergence

The framework's recursion across rings (cont 23) is therefore wrapper-overlap (cont 25) iterated. Each ring's observer-class is the set of W_C's that emerged from the previous ring's wrapper-overlaps and survived to the active phase.

Procedural lineage (cont 18) is the trail of these emergences. The six rings (physics→chemistry→biology→cognition→society→person) are six iterations of wrapper-overlap producing emergent W_C's that become the next ring's substrate.

This is the framework's deepest structural commitment now: the entire procedural-lineage hierarchy is generated by recursive wrapper-overlap. Not metaphor — mechanism.
