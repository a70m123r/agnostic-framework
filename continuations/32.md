## Part D — Spectrum primitive (NEW) — Tier 1 epistemological for the structure; Tier 3 speculative for the cross-spectrum bridging claim

**Status:** New explicit canon entry. Consolidates scattered prior material into a new graded axis.

**Claim — the spectrum:**

**Reality → theory → hypothesis / conjecture / speculation → imagination / probability projection → dreams.**

This is a graded axis along **mental-content concreteness**, orthogonal to the framework's two existing graded axes:
- Cont 22 dual-kernel = polar-binary axis (physical vs latent)
- Cont 27 three-tier procedure = epistemic-confidence axis (Tier 1 / 2 / 3)
- **Cont 32 spectrum (this entry)** = mental-content axis (most-concrete to most-abstract)

**Per-layer definitions:**

- **Reality.** The actualized substrate-anchored content. Feeds the harness (Part B.3). Most-concrete; load-bearing-now.
- **Theory.** Structured framing of reality with internal coherence and external testability, not yet actualized in current observer's action-space but structurally well-formed. (Lakatosian research programmes; physics theory in the abstract.)
- **Hypothesis / conjecture / speculation.** Three nearby sub-layers held at increasing distance from current empirical purchase. Hypothesis is sharpest (testable claim with explicit conditions). Conjecture is sharper than speculation but less specified than hypothesis (structural claim with proposed testability). Speculation is structural-coherence without empirical purchase (Tier 3 of cont 27).
- **Imagination / probability projection.** Two nearby sub-layers. Imagination is generative (the Imagine Engine producing phantom fuzzy per cont 12 §2.1). Probability projection is structured-counterfactual (Bayesian-weighted forward inference, simulation across hypothetical futures).
- **Dreams.** Least structured by reality. Internal-process-only. Maps to cont 22 §2 latent-Kanon's "substrate of dreams and subconscious."

**Each layer is canon-shaped within the observer's L0** — each is content the observer can compile coherently for operational purposes (Part B.4). Different layers serve different operational functions:
- Reality enables acting in current action-space
- Theory enables systematic understanding across action-spaces
- Hypothesis enables testing what would advance understanding
- Conjecture enables holding structural claims pending sharper formulation
- Speculation enables holding structurally-coherent insights without empirical purchase
- Imagination enables generating phantom action-spaces beyond current L0
- Probability projection enables anticipating-but-not-committing to future outcomes
- Dreams enable processing without operational constraint (the framework brackets the specific function of dreams per cont 17 phenomenology bracket — but the layer exists as latent content distinct from imagination)

**A+/- A-/+ asymmetric scaffolding between layers** (Part E): each layer scaffolds adjacent layers via coupled discipline. The forward direction (A+/A-) generates candidates at one layer and the next-deeper-layer checks them. The reverse direction (A-/A+) compiled-canon at one layer scaffolds candidate-generation at the adjacent abstract-layer.

**Tier-tagging:**
- The spectrum as a graded axis = **Tier 1 epistemological** (most layers are empirically tractable in their respective fields: psychology of reasoning, cognitive science, philosophy of science, dream research)
- The specific between-layer bridging-via-A+/- A-/+ claim (Part E) = **Tier 3 speculative** (structural-coherence with cont 13 A+/A- and Reading 07/08 bidirectional patterns, but no empirical purchase yet for the specific cross-spectrum claim)
- Bar A for Tier 2 promotion of the cross-spectrum bridging: at least one worked example surfaces where the A+/- A-/+ scaffolding generates predictions or insights about a specific spectrum-layer-pair that simpler per-layer treatments couldn't.
- 12-month pruning checkpoint for the Tier 3 cross-spectrum claim: 2027-06-04.

**This is NOT a new cross-substrate convergence.** It's a new structural axis within the framework itself. Per audit v06 §10.4 closure-via-convergence-multiplication discipline: convergence list stays at 9 (LCAO through BES). The spectrum is structural depth on the framework's existing observer-architecture, not a new substrate-level pattern.
