## §1. Push, pull, squeeze, pop — the four blind-steer force-modes

**Claim:** Canon-formation is driven by four force-modes that operate without requiring an agent to consciously drive them. Each is a vector + a pressure-type. Together they form a 2×2 covering the space of how substrate-resident pressures shape what compiles into canon.

**The four:**

**Push** — forward-directional force. A substrate pushes outward against constraints; the canon that compiles is whatever resists or absorbs the push. Industrial revolution pushing against pre-industrial canon. Population growth pushing against carrying capacity. Climate change pushing against agricultural-canon assumptions. The push is felt as expansion, displacement, or growth pressure; the canon-formation response is either accommodation (new canon compiled to handle the push) or rejection (existing canon stiffens against it).

**Pull** — attractive-directional force. A substrate pulls inward toward an attractor; the canon that compiles is whatever organizes around the attractor. Religious rapture as the pull-engine of religious canon (already named in the religion construct study §03). The "good life" as the pull-engine of consumer canon. Convergence on profitable equilibria as the pull-engine of market canon. Pull is felt as desire, longing, or directed attention; the canon-formation response is alignment-toward.

**Squeeze** — compressive pressure event. A substrate experiences external constraint that forces canon to refactor faster than canon usually allows. Pandemic. War. Asteroid. Financial crisis. Climate stress. Squeeze is felt as urgency, threat, or limitation; the canon-formation response is rapid compile — what continuation 16 named the *squeeze event* primitive, now positioned as one of the four force-modes.

**Pop** — release / burst event. A substrate suddenly opens after compression; the canon that compiles is whatever rushes to fill the space. Berlin Wall falling. Soviet collapse. Lifting of a long prohibition. End of pandemic restrictions. End of an empire. Pop is felt as relief, exuberance, or sudden possibility; the canon-formation response is rapid colonization of the newly-opened space — and that response is itself a vulnerable moment because the new canon compiles before careful checking is possible. Post-Soviet capitalism colonized rapidly; many of its early forms turned out to be poor canon and required later refactoring. The pop response carries that risk by design.

**Why a 2×2 helps:**

The four are not independent. Push and pull are direction; squeeze and pop are pressure-event-type. A given canon-formation moment usually has all four operating at different intensities. Marriage in 19th-century industrial Britain: push of urbanization, pull of romantic-marriage canon, squeeze of factory work disrupting kin networks, pop of inheritance-law reforms opening space the old canon couldn't fill. Reading any historical canon-formation through the four-mode frame lets you decompose what was driving it.

**Mapping to existing primitives:**

The four-mode frame absorbs and connects several existing framework primitives. *Squeeze event* (cont 16) is exactly the squeeze quadrant. *Rapture as pull-engine* (religion essay §03) is exactly the pull quadrant at the religion-canon scale. *Cast/give/residue* (notation walkthrough §03 and the framework's ask-give-ping-pong primitive) operates within the push quadrant — casting is push-forward, giving is push-into-substrate, residue is what stays in the push wake. *Open action-space* (marriage essay §06) is what happens after a pop event but before successor canon has compiled.

**Promotion criteria to canon:**

For each of the four to graduate from candidate to canon, the framework needs:
1. A worked example at each of physics, biology, cognition, society rings (procedural lineage discipline from cont 18 — the primitive should operate at multiple substrate rings, not just society)
2. A measurable signature on at least one example per ring (cont 18's procedural-connection-must-be-measurable discipline)
3. Operational purchase: the four-mode decomposition produces moves on a real construct study that the previous vocabulary did not

The marriage and religion construct studies already give partial worked examples at the society ring. The biology-ring worked example would be the easiest next: push (population pressure), pull (sexual selection), squeeze (mass extinction), pop (Cambrian explosion as the canonical pop-after-snowball-earth-squeeze case). Worth lifting into a future continuation as the four-mode discipline's promotion test.

## §2. Hold — the stable-rhythm phase

**Claim:** Between active canon-formation rounds, canon enters a *hold* phase: the dynamics don't stop, but they settle into a stable rhythm in union with other jessant (actively-emerging) canons. The canon is alive and in operation, not refactoring, not dormant. Eventually the held canon hardens, stales, and friction builds underneath until the next round of force-mode pressure breaks the hold and the canon either wakes from hold into a new compile, falls into dormancy (cont 20), or persists through the friction unchanged.

**Why hold is distinct from dormancy:**

Cont 20's dormancy is the silent phase — canon is no longer load-bearing, the institutional carrier has lapsed, the protocol persists as imparted learning in the substrate. Hold is the *active-but-not-refactoring* phase — canon is still load-bearing, the institutional carrier is operating, the protocol is being practiced, but no new compile-pass is happening. The system is in a stable rhythm.

A worked example. Catholic canon in 19th-century Italy was in hold. The institution was active, the rituals were being performed, the canon was being propagated through every channel — but the canon itself wasn't refactoring. Vatican II was the wake-from-hold moment, when friction underneath the hold finally produced a compile-pass that refactored a hold-stable canon into a new compile. The hold ended; an active refactoring round began.

**The four exits from hold (parallel to the marriage essay's four exits from squeeze):**

1. **Continued hold** — friction stays manageable; the canon keeps operating in stable rhythm; the next refactoring round is deferred. Most canon at most times is in continued hold.
2. **Refactor in place** — friction builds enough to drive a compile-pass; the canon refactors but the institution stays. Vatican II is the worked example.
3. **Dormancy** (cont 20) — friction builds, the canon cannot refactor in place, the institution lapses and the protocol passes into substrate-resident imparted learning. The Nordic marriage case.
4. **Open action-space** (marriage essay §06) — friction builds, the canon cannot refactor, the institution lapses, AND no single successor compiles. The East Asian configuration.

**Mapping to existing primitives:**

Hold sits at the joint between cont 19's *moving target* (canon is always in flux but flux can be slow), cont 20's *canon dormancy with imparted learning* (the dormancy-vs-active distinction), and cont 16's *friction band* (the band where productive friction sits is the band where hold operates). Hold is not a new dynamic; it's a phase-name for what most canon does most of the time. Naming it lets the framework distinguish the stable-rhythm phase from the dormancy phase, which the existing vocabulary collapsed.

**Promotion criteria to canon:**

To graduate from candidate to canon, hold needs:
1. A worked example showing the same canon transitioning from hold → friction-build → exit, with measurable indicators of each phase
2. A predictor variable for which of the four exits a given canon will take when friction reaches threshold
3. Operational purchase: the hold-vs-dormancy distinction produces a different reading or prediction than the dormancy primitive alone

Catholic-Vatican-II is the cleanest worked example for the first criterion. The second criterion — predicting which exit — is the hard one and likely depends on the four substrate-stack configuration (religion essay §05's state / pluralism / wealth / media for religion; marriage essay's religion / climate / government / ideology for marriage; different substrate stacks for different canons).

## §3. Kernel canon → canon protocol → function — the three-layer reading

**Claim:** Pav's three-layer formulation refines the procedural lineage (cont 18) at the operational level. Procedural lineage gave us six rings (physics → chemistry → biology → cognition → society → person). The three-layer reading collapses the operational distinction inside each ring:

- **Kernel canon** = reality itself. The substrate-level rules and patterns that exist independent of any institution or interpretation. For the social-substrate ring, kernel canon is the underlying procedural truths about how humans coordinate, partner, raise children, process meaning, etc.
- **Canon protocol** = the institutional/practice-level compile that interpolates kernel canon into operational behavior. Religion is a canon protocol that interpolates kernel canon (the reality of mortality, meaning-need, coordination requirements) into specific practice (ritual, scripture, calendar). Marriage is a canon protocol that interpolates kernel canon (the reality of partnership, child-rearing, inheritance, alliance) into specific institutional form.
- **Function** = the lived operation. What the protocol produces when run. The actual partnerships, the actual rituals, the actual coordination outcomes.

**Why this refines procedural lineage:**

Cont 18's lineage moves between rings. The three-layer reading moves within a ring. The two are orthogonal. At any ring, you can distinguish kernel canon (what the substrate actually demands), canon protocol (the compiled institutional response), and function (the lived practice). And across rings, kernel canon at one ring is partly the function at the ring beneath it.

This gives the framework a finer-grained vocabulary. Saying "religion is canon" was too coarse; religion is the protocol layer, sitting between the kernel reality (mortality, meaning, coordination need) and the function (actual lived religious practice). Saying "marriage is canon" was the same kind of coarse compression; marriage is the protocol layer between the kernel (partnership reality) and the function (actual marriages being lived).

**Interpolation as the operative verb:**

The three-layer reading says the work canon protocol does is *interpolation* — fitting the kernel canon into operational function. Different protocols can interpolate the same kernel into different functions (Catholic, Protestant, Hindu, Muslim, Buddhist, secular-spiritual protocols all interpolating the same kernel of mortality/meaning into different functions). This is exactly what the religion construct study's regional heat map shows — same kernel, different protocols, different functions per region.

**Mapping to existing primitives:**

The three-layer reading sits cleanly above cont 18's procedural lineage (which describes the inter-ring dynamics) and cont 20's canon dormancy (which describes what happens when a protocol goes dormant — the kernel and function remain, the protocol layer lapses). The four-mode forces from §1 above operate at the protocol layer most visibly, though they have correlates at the kernel and function layers.

**Promotion criteria to canon:**

To graduate from candidate to canon, the three-layer reading needs:
1. Operational purchase: a construct study where the kernel-vs-protocol-vs-function distinction produces a sharper reading than the procedural-lineage frame alone
2. Cross-substrate application: the three-layer distinction works at biology, cognition, society rings (not just society)
3. A measurable mechanism for interpolation: not just naming that protocol interpolates kernel into function, but a way to measure interpolation quality / efficiency / drift

The religion construct study already partially demonstrates the first criterion — different regional protocols (Orthodox-nationalist, Hindu-nationalist, evangelical-Protestant, Nordic-secular-spiritual) interpolating the same kernel (mortality + meaning + coordination needs) into different functions. The §05 four-substrate analysis is the interpolation-mechanism analysis without using the word. Lifting that explicitly would advance criterion 3.

## §4. Agnostic pull becomes desolation

**Claim:** For an agnostic toward a given canon (someone who cannot or does not commit to the canon's specific protocol), the pull-engine that the canon offers does not produce its intended attractor-state. It produces absence.

**The worked case:**

The religion construct study §03 named rapture as the strongest pull-engine humans have ever built into a canon — the canonical attractor that makes the squeeze of mortality, suffering, and meaning-need bearable. The essay's agnostic stance (§01) said the framework brackets whether god exists. But there's a downstream implication for the lived experience of agnostics that the essay didn't fully follow through on, and Pav's correction does.

For the religious adherent: pull-engine accessible, produces rapture-state, makes the squeeze bearable. Canon working as designed.

For the agnostic (the person who cannot commit to the canon's specific god / cosmology / protocol but is still subject to the kernel squeezes the canon was compiled to absorb): the pull-engine is *visible* but *inaccessible*. The agnostic can describe what rapture would be, can recognize it in others, can study it intellectually — but cannot enter it because entering requires the protocol commitment that the agnostic stance forbids. The result is not neutrality. It is desolation — the awareness that there is a state available that resolves the squeeze, paired with the inability to access that state.

This explains a phenomenon the religion essay didn't quite name: the secular-but-not-spiritual configuration in late-modern Western Europe carries a depressive load that the secular-spiritual configuration (Nordic) does not. The Nordic case found a wake-form (meditation, mindfulness, secular ritual) that gave the pull-engine an alternative substrate-resident protocol. The secular-but-not-spiritual case did not. It carries the desolation as the cost of agnosticism without successor protocol.

**Why this matters for the framework:**

The agnostic-pull-as-desolation reading sharpens the religion essay's Bubble 01 (pull-portability hypothesis). The pull is portable, but only if there is a successor protocol the substrate has compiled — meditation, mindfulness, psychedelics, contemplative practice. Without a successor protocol, the agnostic stance leaves the pull-engine visible-but-inaccessible. That is the desolation state.

The implication for the framework's own agnostic stance: the Lakatosian discipline of bracketing-the-unanswerable is the framework's intellectual stance toward god, not its lived stance toward meaning. A reader who adopts the framework's agnostic stance toward god does not automatically gain access to the pull-engine; they need a successor protocol the framework can point to. The framework's manifesto v5 — particularly the §05 squeeze-to-pull section and the §06 muscle-memory framing — partially supplies this, but the religion essay should make it explicit.

**The religion essay edit this triggers:**

A small addition to §03 (the rapture section) acknowledging that for the agnostic, the pull-engine is visible-but-inaccessible, that the resulting state is desolation rather than neutrality, and that the successor-protocol question is what determines whether agnosticism is depressive or generative. This is a one-paragraph addition that doesn't change the essay's structure but adds the missing layer Pav's correction names.

## §5. The framework's outputs are verbs

**Claim:** The construct studies, continuations, manifestos, tools, diagrams, heat maps, and timelines that the framework produces are *verbs*, not nouns. They are actions of the framework being itself — they are the framework doing its work — they are not artifacts that describe the framework.

**Why this is more than a metaphor:**

The distinction has operational consequences. If the framework's outputs are nouns (artifacts), then reading them is consumption, and the right ratio is one author producing for many readers. If the framework's outputs are verbs (acts), then engaging with them is participation, and the right ratio is many participants each producing their own. Pav's <a href="../tools/unraveler.html">subject unraveler</a> tool is the cleanest instance of this distinction made operational — the tool exists so readers can unravel their own subjects using the framework's template, producing their own construct studies. The framework is verb-shaped at the output level.

The same logic applies to every artifact-type the framework produces:
- A continuation is the verb of <em>writing a continuation</em> — a documented round of canon-graph correction
- A construct study is the verb of <em>compiling a construct study</em> — applying the framework's compile-template to a phenomenon
- A diagram is the verb of <em>diagramming</em> — making structure visible through iterative revision (the diagram 06 case study on the live site is the worked example of this verb running across six iterations)
- A heat map is the verb of <em>mapping</em> — assigning index-values per region with the discipline of cross-checking against sources
- A timeline is the verb of <em>logging</em> — making the procedural lineage visible at the project level
- A manifesto is the verb of <em>declaring</em> — articulating the framework in long-form prose for a given audience

Each artifact-type is a verb-template. The framework's index page is the directory of available verbs.

**Connection to cont 19's aggregation discipline:**

Cont 19 named the framework's identity as *aggregator of procedural connections* — and aggregation is itself a verb. The framework's identity is verb-shaped at the meta level (aggregation) and verb-shaped at every output level (the artifact-types above). Calling the outputs verbs is a refinement of cont 19's identity claim: the framework is not just an aggregator (one verb at the meta level) but a verb-spawning machine (many verbs at the artifact level), and every verb is a procedural-connection-making move.

**Connection to cont 20's canon dormancy:**

Verbs can go dormant the way canons can. A construct study that is read once and never engaged-with-again is a verb that ran once. A construct study that gets re-read, re-applied, corrected, refactored, and re-shipped is a verb that keeps running. The framework's discipline implies that artifacts have to be kept verb-active by ongoing engagement, otherwise they pass into dormancy and lose their operational status. This is why the timeline and the provenance trails matter — they keep the verbs visible as running.

**Promotion criteria to canon:**

To graduate from candidate to canon, the verbs claim needs:
1. A construct study or set of artifacts produced by someone other than Pav and me, using the framework's templates and verb-forms. Until that happens, the verbs claim is still aspirational — the framework hasn't been verified as actually spawning verbs in the wider world.
2. Operational purchase: the verb-vs-noun distinction changes how readers engage with the framework in measurable ways (e.g., higher annotation rate via the Hypothes.is sidebar; submissions via the Contribute section)
3. A worked example of a framework-spawned verb being run independently — someone using the subject unraveler to compile their own construct study and shipping it back

This is the candidate most dependent on external engagement to graduate. Outreach (still queued from audit v04) is the natural carrier for testing it.

## §7. Open edges

**One. The four-mode frame may be 2×2-shaped but not strictly four-celled.** Push and pull may not be fully independent of squeeze and pop; some real canon-formation events look like push-squeeze hybrids (sudden expansion under threat) or pull-pop hybrids (sudden access to a previously-blocked attractor). Whether the four-cell structure is real or whether the four are points on a continuous force-space is open.

**Two. The hold phase needs a duration model.** Cont 21 §2 names hold as a phase but does not say how long it lasts or what predicts the friction-build that exits it. Catholic-Vatican-II was on a 400-year hold timeline; some commercial canons hold for a quarter; some scientific canons hold for a generation. Without a duration model, hold is named but not predictive.

**Three. The kernel canon = reality framing risks the everything-bag worry cont 18 §10 raised.** If reality itself is kernel canon, the framework is claiming all of reality as substrate. The Lakatosian discipline from cont 19 says this is OK if procedural-connection-must-be-measurable is enforced per claim. Worth restating: the kernel-canon = reality move is a useful operational distinction within the framework, not a metaphysical claim about reality's structure.

**Four. The agnostic-pull-as-desolation reading needs a measurable indicator.** The claim that secular-but-not-spiritual configurations carry a depressive load that secular-spiritual configurations do not is testable in principle (depression-and-meaning-survey deltas between Nordic and post-Catholic secular regions, controlled for material conditions). Without the indicator, the reading is plausible-but-untested.

**Five. The verbs claim is meta-level and may itself need a different evaluation criterion.** Most framework primitives are evaluated by procedural-connection-must-be-measurable (cont 18). The verbs claim is a claim about how the framework's outputs work, not about substrate connections. The right evaluation criterion may be different — perhaps something closer to operational purchase (do readers engage with the framework as verbs or as nouns) than procedural-connection (which is the wrong frame for a meta-level claim about engagement-mode).
