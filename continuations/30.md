## §2 The cycling reframe — how it maps to existing canon

Per cont 27 §2 discipline: the reframe is NOT a new primitive. It's an *integration* of existing canon, made visible by the confounded-null forcing the framework to look at what its claim actually depends on.

**§2.1 Squeeze ↔ pull cycling = cont 13 A⁻/A⁺ coupled pattern at social substrate.**

[Cont 13](13.md) promoted A⁻ to primary discipline: not just A⁺ generation but coupled A⁻ verification that gates which forward candidates survive. Reading 07 (BES) + Reading 08 (γ-World) documented this pattern at search-methodology and world-model substrates respectively. Pav's "squeeze and pull" is the same coupled-forward-backward pattern at social-substrate scale:

- **Squeeze** = system-wide A⁻ tightening (admission protocols narrow; canon-artifact selection becomes stricter; the substrate enforces concordance). Maps to dense-feedback regime of BES Theorem 4.4.
- **Pull** = system-wide A⁺ generation (more candidate variety enters the substrate; admission protocols loosen; new wrappers form). Maps to forward-evolution regime of BES.
- **Healthy cycling** = both phases active, alternating; the substrate retains the *capacity* to oscillate.
- **Locked squeeze** = A⁻ becomes destructive (over-correlated, brittle, no recovery). Cont 25 §1 supersede dynamic.
- **Locked pull** = A⁺ runs without dense feedback (white-noise-random, no stable wrappers form). Cont 25 §1 break-apart dynamic.

This is genuine integration: the framework now has three substrates instantiating the same coupled-forward-backward pattern (search via BES, world-models via γ-World, social-systems via cycling). The pattern was already in canon at cont 13; what's new is the explicit recognition that **the social-substrate L0 failsafe IS this oscillation**, not a fixed β value.

**§2.2 Loss of cycling = cont 20 dormancy OR cont 25 break, depending on which way it locks.**

[Cont 20](20.md) canon dormancy is "preserved substrate, reduced expression." In the cycling frame: locked-squeeze IS dormancy at substrate scale — A⁻ becomes total, expression collapses, but the substrate is preserved (the system can in principle recompile when the squeeze releases). Reading 06 §10.3 expected this to show as β >> 1 (over-correlated). Empirically inaccessible in #150 due to volume confound; should show as τ(t) variance collapse in #150b.

[Cont 25 §1](25.md) break-apart is the other direction: locked-pull. A⁺ runs free without dense feedback, no stable W_C forms, the substrate fragments. Reading 06 §10.3 expected β ≈ 0 (white-noise-controlled). Same empirical accessibility issue; same #150b fix.

**§2.3 The cycling reframe makes Reading 06 §10.3 falsifiable in a way it wasn't before.**

The original §10.3 hypothesis ("authoritarian = lower β") was confounded by the operationalization choice. The cycling version ("loss of τ(t) cycling = failsafe failure, in either direction") is falsifiable via:

- Within-country τ(t) variance over the decade (does it cycle? does the variance shrink during sustained capture?)
- Within-country τ(t) trajectory vs independently-measured openness (V-Dem / RSF) trajectory (do they co-move in either direction?)
- Cross-country comparison only AFTER within-country baselines control for volume

The framework gains a sharper Reading 06 §10.3 claim by abandoning the static binary. The volume confound that killed #150 doesn't recur (or is handled in the time domain per `PILOT_150b_cycling_seed.md` §3 gate). This is what cont 27 §3 narrowing is *supposed* to look like: the claim refines and gets stronger; only the failed operationalization gets pruned.

**§2.4 The reframe is structurally consistent with the cont 28 supersede dynamic at discovery substrate.**

[Cont 28 §2](28.md) named the supersede dynamic: old substrate (gatekeeper algorithms) persists as procedural-root stubs while new substrate (personal AI agents) increasingly uses the old as one tool among many. The same dynamic plays at the political substrate: gatekeeper media institutions (legacy press, state media) persist as procedural-root stubs while the substrate increasingly diversifies via social media, personal-attention managers, agent-mediated discovery. Squeeze ↔ pull cycling is the rhythm of this supersede process operating in real-time. The framework's failsafe-health claim is consistent with cont 28's supersede claim — both describe substrate-level oscillations between dense-feedback-anchored and broadband-generative regimes.

## §7 Discipline lessons (what to remember for next pre-registration)

Per Claude Code's discussion.md §9 + this round's full visibility:

1. **Pre-registered z-scoring removes amplitude scale but NOT spectral-floor mechanism.** Future pre-registrations on sparse / heterogeneous-rate signals must EXPLICITLY name and control for the white-noise floor in the spectral domain. Either Poisson-thin to common rate, or volume-match pairs, or use a volume-robust estimator (DFA-α) as primary.

2. **Paired permutation test ceiling at N=3 (p ≥ 1/8) was caught by running the protocol against synthetic data BEFORE GDELT examined.** This validation step should be a standard pre-registration discipline going forward. Every locked pre-registration should be tested against synthetic data with known properties before any real data examined.

3. **IAAFT was expected to be a clean β-null and turned out not to be.** Surrogate-method assumptions must be tested on synthetic data; if the assumption fails, the surrogate becomes diagnostic only (not inferential). Locked permutation test was correctly designated as the inference; IAAFT moved to diagnostic. Same discipline applies to future pilots.

4. **Method substitutions in supportive components (BigQuery, block-bootstrap CI) are FINE as long as fully disclosed in confounds.md with rationale, and neither touches the locked H1 test.** Cont 30 endorses this as standard practice.

5. **Post-hoc cherry-picking is a foreseeable failure mode and must be EXPLICITLY rejected in the result-commit.** discussion.md §4 named this rejection. Future result-commits should include the equivalent.

6. **A reframe written after seeing a null is NOT a clean pre-registration.** `PILOT_150b_cycling_seed.md` is a hypothesis-generating sketch, not canon. The next pre-registration must be independently re-derived from the framework's claims, NOT from the seed. Discipline preserved.

7. **The narrow-before-demote procedure (cont 27 §3) executed cleanly this round.** Future Tier 2 conditional candidates that hit confounded nulls should follow the same pattern: narrow the operationalization, hold the underlying claim, queue a sharper version.

8. **Candidate-layer narrow-before-extend** (added 2026-06-04 per audit v07 T1 fix; lesson surfaced by cont 31 over-extending the adversarial-substrate-dynamics candidate before Bar A was worked). When a candidate's promotion bar is unworked and a structural-depth addition is proposed, the default discipline is to work the bar first. The framework can override with explicit reasoning, but the override must be explicit. This is the candidate-layer analog of substrate-layer narrow-before-demote (lesson 7), and the candidate-layer parallel to the cross-substrate convergence-list closure-via-multiplication discipline (audit v06 §10.4). All three are versions of the same meta-discipline: resist adding structure when the existing structure has open work.
