## §0 — The function (the keystone definition)

**C0.1 — Substrate-agnostic definition.** `[Tier 3]` `[grounding: candidate §3 broken-symmetry wrapper-overlap; candidate §2.6 asymmetric extraction; cont 26 §3 evolved failsafes; cont 27 §1 energy-floor (E_W ≥ E_min); Reading 03 ACMP energy-floor]`

An entity **E** is *adversarial* w.r.t. substrate **S** when E **captures a fitness differential from S's compiled order while externalizing the cost of maintaining that order onto S's other participants** — E free-rides on, or actively degrades, the failsafes that keep S viable. In the framework's own vocabulary: S's viability rests on an energy-floor failsafe (cont 27 §1: a coherence quantity E_S held above a lower bound E_min by the contributions of S's participants); E draws down E_S toward E_min while routing the maintenance-cost it should have borne onto others. This is candidate §2.6's "extraction without contribution" stated at the substrate level and tied to the failsafe that §2.6 names but does not quantify.

A suggestive (not load-bearing) tell is **cross-field recurrence**: the *same shape* has been named, apparently from scratch, in at least four research traditions that were not borrowing from each other. None of them set out to describe a substrate-general primitive; each was solving a local problem and arrived at a similar shape. `[Tier 3]` `[fuzzy — this is parallel naming, NOT a tested convergence]` This recurrence is **not** the same kind of object as the framework's canon convergence list (the nine tested cross-substrate convergences, LCAO through BES). Those each passed a parents-produce-W_C structural test (cont 25 §6) plus an independence check (Reading 07 §3); **neither test has been run on the four traditions below.** Per the scaffold's own §4 O4 closure-check, a convergence-shaped claim cannot be called a convergence until those tests pass, and the convergence list stays at 9 until then. So the table is offered as illustrative pedagogy for the definition — a reason to suspect the function is substrate-portable — not as evidence of a primitive and not as a tenth convergence.

| Field | Local name | The structure named (in that field's own terms) | Canonical anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Biology / ecology | **parasite** | organism deriving fitness from a host while imposing a cost on host fitness; host's maintenance machinery is the captured order | parasitology; Price 1980 *Evolutionary Biology of Parasites*; brood parasitism (cuckoo) |
| Evolutionary game theory | **defector / cheater** | strategy that takes the cooperative payoff without paying the cooperation cost; in public-goods games, contributes nothing to the common pool yet draws from it | Axelrod & Hamilton 1981 (iterated PD); Trivers 1971 (defection on reciprocity); public-goods / common-pool experiments (Fehr & Gächter 2000) |
| Economics / political science | **free-rider** | actor who consumes a non-excludable collective good without contributing to its provision; the group's failsafe (the public good) is the externalized-onto cost | Olson 1965 *The Logic of Collective Action*; commons literature after Hardin 1968 (with the caveat in C0.2) |
| Infosec / adversarial ML | **adversary / attacker** | agent who exploits a system's trusted machinery (a protocol, a classifier's decision surface) to extract value or induce failure the operator pays for | adversarial examples — Szegedy et al. 2013, Goodfellow, Shlens & Szegedy 2014; threat-modelling (the "adversary" as first-class object); separately, the GAN discriminator-vs-generator min-max framing — Goodfellow et al. 2014 (NeurIPS) |

The four names are *not synonyms by stipulation* — a biologist and a mechanism-design economist did not coordinate vocabulary. The **candidate reading** is that they are one invariant wearing four substrate-faces, which is *what C2.1 would predict if* the inter-translation in the test-hook below holds: the adversarial function tracks the local canon-function, so it *would* look like a parasite where the canon-function is biochemical and like an "attacker" where the canon-function is a protocol. `[Tier 3] [fuzzy — the same-invariant reading is conjectural; it stands or falls on the translation test, which has not been run formally]` The recurrence is interesting *because* nobody was aiming for agnosticism — but "interesting" is the ceiling of the claim here, not "demonstrated."

If any one resists translation without adding a substrate-specific clause that the others lack, the "same invariant" claim weakens and C0.1 is a family resemblance, not one structure. `[fuzzy — the translation is suggestive, not validated against the formal models in each field; see honest-weaknesses]`

**C0.2 — The beneficiary boundary (the floor criterion).** `[Tier 3]` `[grounding: candidate §2.6 asymmetric extraction; cont 27 §1 energy-floor failsafe + §2 neutral failsafe-stressor; audit v02 §9–§11 dark-forest action-space]`

Adversarial **requires a localized beneficiary** — an identifiable entity (or lineage, or coalition) that *pockets* the fitness differential extracted at S's cost. This is offered as the framework's *proposed* discriminator, not a derived result. The beneficiary clause is what separates the adversarial function from **neutral** order-degradation. Both an adversary and plain entropy can push S's coherence quantity E_S toward E_min (cont 27 §1); the difference is not in the damage but in *whether anyone gains*. Entropy degrades order and **no entity captures the freed fitness** → neutral failsafe-stressor (cont 27 §2-style; the framework already distinguishes "stress on the floor" from "an actor exploiting the floor"). A defector, a parasite, a captor degrades order **and a localized entity is fitter for it** → adversarial.

Stated as a one-line discriminator (a stipulated criterion, `[Tier 3]`, not a theorem):

> **adversarial(E, S)  ⇔  ∃ a localized beneficiary B such that B's fitness rises *because* E externalized S's maintenance-cost onto S's other participants.** No such B ⇒ the dynamic is neutral decay/selection, not adversarial — however destructive it is.

The clause is meant to travel the whole ladder. Applying it across the spectrum, including the cosmic rungs the framework's full-spectrum scope (viroid → cosmic + latent) demands — and treating each verdict as an *intuition-test of the clause*, not a measurement:

| Case (rung) | Order being drawn down | Localized beneficiary? | Externalized cost? | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Molecular parasite / template-poacher (RNA-world) `[grounding: Eigen 1971 error threshold; Maynard Smith & Szathmáry 1995 RNA-world parasite problem]` | replicator's maintenance of sequence fidelity | **yes** — the non-maintaining replicator lineage | fidelity-cost borne by the maintainers | **adversarial** |
| Defector in iterated PD / public-goods `[grounding: Axelrod & Hamilton 1981; Olson 1965]` | the cooperative common pool | **yes** — the defecting/free-riding agent | contribution-cost borne by cooperators | **adversarial** |
| Cancer (somatic) `[grounding: multilevel-selection / cheater-cell oncology; specific anchor not pinned — needs-verification]` | the multicellular body's growth-suppression failsafes | **yes** — the cheater cell lineage | proliferation restraint borne by somatic tissue | **adversarial** |
| Apoptosis / programmed cell death | the cell's own coherence | **no** — the failsafe sacrifices the cell *for* the substrate; no entity pockets a differential at the body's cost | n/a — cost internalized by the substrate's own program | **neutral** (an evolved failsafe firing, not an exploit) |
| Thermal noise / generic decay of a signalling channel | the channel's signal coherence | **no** | n/a | **neutral failsafe-stressor** |
| Grabby civilizations / expansionist colonizers `[grounding: Hanson et al. 2021, arXiv:2102.01522 "grabby aliens"; audit v02 §9–§11 dark-forest]` | the cosmic commons (volume, free energy, negentropy budget) reachable by others | **yes (by intuition)** — the expanding civilization captures volume/resources others can no longer compile from | other would-be colonizers / incumbents pay the foreclosed-future cost | **adversarial** |
| Berserker / deadly-probe scenarios `[grounding: Saberhagen berserker (fiction); Brin 1983 deadly-probe / "Great Silence" (QJRAS); dark-forest preemption, Liu Cixin (fiction)]` | other civilizations' continued existence (their order) | **yes (by intuition)** — the launching lineage secures relative safety/resources by preempting rivals | the targeted civilizations bear total cost | **adversarial** |
| **Vacuum decay** (false-vacuum collapse) `[grounding: Coleman 1977 "Fate of the false vacuum"; Coleman & De Luccia 1980 — attribution believed correct, retained as needs-verification]` | the metastable order of the current vacuum, everywhere the bubble reaches | **no beneficiary** — the post-decay region hosts no entity that is *fitter for having caused the decay*; it does not capture a differential, it just expands at near-light-speed | no externalization onto a *participant who is disadvantaged relative to a gainer* | **neutral** — disqualified |
| **Heat death** (maximal-entropy end-state) | all compiled order, asymptotically | **no beneficiary** — entropy is the limit, not an entity; nothing pockets the freed energy as fitness | n/a | **neutral** — disqualified |

The cosmic rows are the load-bearing test of the clause. Vacuum decay and heat death are maximally *destructive* — they end all order in their reach — yet the clause classifies them **neutral**, because there is no localized beneficiary: the freed order is not *captured by anyone as fitness*. Grabby civilizations, berserkers, and dark-forest preemptors are far *less* total in their destruction but classify **adversarial**, because (by intuition, not measurement) a localized lineage is fitter for the order it forecloses on others. This is the criterion *intended* behaviour: it is meant to discriminate by *beneficiary*, not by *body count*, and on these cases it appears to do so without re-tuning the clause per rung. That is encouraging but **not a settled result** — the cosmic-scale notion of "localized beneficiary" is itself fuzzy (see the cosmic-falsifier test-hook), so the no-re-tuning property is provisional, not demonstrated.

**The failure-mode (what gets misclassified if you drop the beneficiary clause).** Without the localized-beneficiary requirement, "adversarial" collapses into "anything that degrades S's order," and the function loses its discriminating power in two directions at once:

- **False positives (neutral decay smuggled in as adversarial):** entropy, heat death, vacuum decay, thermal noise, ordinary selection pressure, and an evolved failsafe firing (apoptosis) would all read as "adversarial" because they all push E_S toward E_min. The function would then name *the entire class of order-degradation*, which is just the second law plus selection — a label so broad it predicts nothing and ports nothing. This is the cosmic over-reach the scaffold's C0.2 explicitly guards against: beneficiary-less cosmic candidates would flood in.
- **False negatives are the subtler half:** the clause is also what *admits* the cosmic cases that belong (grabby/berserker/dark-forest). A naive guard against cosmic over-reach — "exclude anything at cosmic scale" — would wrongly exclude beneficiary-*bearing* cosmic actors. The beneficiary clause is the principled line that keeps grabby aliens *in* (a lineage gains) while keeping heat death *out* (nothing gains), rather than drawing the line at scale, which would be arbitrary.

So the clause is not a tightening for tidiness; it is the proposed **specific difference** between the adversarial function and the framework's already-canonical neutral failsafe-stressor (cont 27 §1-2). Drop it and the candidate's whole §3 "broken symmetry by an observer who benefits at the substrate's cost" loses the "who benefits" — which is the only part that distinguishes it from entropy. On this reading the beneficiary is the symmetry-breaker; remove it and there is no asymmetry to point at. `[Tier 3 — this is an analytic claim about the candidate's §3, conditional on the discriminator above being the right cut]`

> *test-hook:* every case admitted at any rung (§1 ascent, §3 engine-dynamics) must name (a) the localized beneficiary and (b) the externalized maintenance-cost. A case that cannot name a beneficiary is reclassified neutral *by rule*, not by argument. Run this as an audit pass over the whole scaffold: any rung where a putative adversarial case has no nameable beneficiary is either mis-stated or genuinely neutral.

> *test-hook (cosmic falsifier):* find a candidate cosmic process that is *clearly adversarial by intuition* yet has *no localizable beneficiary* — or one that *clearly has a beneficiary* yet intuition calls *neutral*. Either would show the beneficiary clause and the pretheoretic concept of "adversarial" come apart at the cosmic rung, forcing a revision of C0.2. `[fuzzy — "localized beneficiary" is itself fuzzy at cosmic scale: across light-cones a beneficiary may be unobservable-in-principle to the disadvantaged party, which strains "localized"; see honest-weaknesses]`

> *test-hook (boundary against selection):* ordinary natural selection produces winners and losers — does that make selection "adversarial" under C0.2? Provisional resolution: selection is the *medium*; the beneficiary clause fires only when a specific entity captures a differential *by externalizing a maintenance-cost it would otherwise bear* (free-riding on the failsafe), not merely by being differentially fit. This boundary needs sharpening and is flagged as fuzzy — the cheater-vs-merely-fitter line is exactly where the parasite/defector literatures themselves argue.

### C1.2 — Origin coeval with order: the RNA-world parasite problem. `[Tier 3]` `[grounding: Eigen 1971 error threshold / Eigen's paradox; Maynard Smith & Szathmáry hypercycle-parasite vulnerability; Szathmáry compartmentalization / stochastic corrector; candidate §0 C0.2 beneficiary boundary]`

The bottom rung is the load-bearing one for *"how it began,"* and it has a real literature, not just a framework intuition. The conjecture is sharp and falsifiable: **the instant a replicator carries a maintenance cost, the niche for a non-maintaining replicator exists.** On this reading parasitism is not a late add-on to compiled order; it is coeval with the first cost-bearing replicator. This is exactly C0.2's beneficiary boundary read at the molecular floor — the parasite is the localized beneficiary; the externalized cost is the catalytic support it consumes without contributing.

The grounding is the RNA-world / origin-of-life modelling tradition:

- **Eigen's error threshold (Eigen 1971).** A replicator without sophisticated copying machinery can only maintain so much information before mutation accumulation dissolves it — but the machinery to copy accurately needs more information than the threshold allows. This is **Eigen's paradox**: you cannot get accurate replication without a large genome, and you cannot maintain a large genome without accurate replication. `[verified]` *(The paradox and Eigen 1971 are verified; the coinage "Eigen's paradox" is **§5.7-verified** as Maynard Smith & Szathmáry's (Wikipedia 'Hypercycle' verbatim: "John Maynard Smith and Eörs Szathmáry named this vicious circle Eigen's Paradox"); Eigen *formulated* the paradox in 1971 but did not name it.)*
- **The hypercycle and its parasite vulnerability.** Eigen & Schuster's hypercycle (a set of replicators that catalytically support each other in a cycle) was proposed to bootstrap past the threshold. Maynard Smith's objection (1979) — sharpened by Maynard Smith & Szathmáry — is that **the hypercycle is intrinsically vulnerable to parasites**: a molecule that receives catalytic support but contributes none is *favoured by selection* and can collapse the cycle. The cooperative order generates, by its own structure, the niche that exploits it. `[verified]`
- **Compartmentalization is the leading proposed solution.** Szathmáry & Demeter's stochastic-corrector / package model and the Boerlijst–Hogeweg spatial ("spiral wave") variant both work by **localising the cooperators** — a membrane vesicle lets *vesicle-level* selection purge compartments carrying parasites; a 2-D surface limits parasite spread via local-only interaction. The proto-cell is, on this reading, partly an *anti-parasite device*. `[verified — though "the" solution overstates: this is the leading family of solutions, still contested]`

The framework reading (Tier 3, do not over-claim): this is the molecular instantiation of the candidate's whole thesis. The protocell membrane is an early **failsafe** (cont 26 §3 internal-pressure class); the parasite is the entity that the failsafe exists to contain; and the membrane that contains it is also the first compartment whose overlap-dynamics later generate cellular-rung structure (cont 25 wrapper-overlap). *"How it began"* = on this reading it began with the first failsafe-bearing replicator, and the failsafe and the thing-it-fails-safe-against are the same age. Note the discipline boundary: Eigen, Maynard Smith and Szathmáry are doing *evolutionary dynamics*, not "adversarial substrate dynamics" — the parasite-as-adversarial-function reading is **the framework's lens on their result**, not their claim (same posture cont 31 §5.7 takes toward Theorem 4.4).

> *test-hook:* literature pass on prebiotic-replicator parasitism models (Eigen, Maynard Smith & Szathmáry, Szathmáry & Demeter, Boerlijst–Hogeweg spatial hypercycles, Takeuchi–Hogeweg). Is the parasite niche *provably coeval* with cost-bearing replication across the model class, or does any serious model produce a sustained cost-bearing replicator with **no** stable parasite niche? A robust no-parasite-possible replicator model would falsify the coevality claim and push "origin of adversariality" later than "origin of order."

### C1.3 — The climb's apparent acceleration decomposes into concentration + catalysis (reframed 2026-06-06, Pav). `[Tier 3]` `[grounding: Pav 2026-06-06; cont 31 §2.3 Red Queen / Van Valen 1973; C3.2 W_C formation; cont 26 §3 + cont 27 §1 energy-floor → activation-energy; candidate §11.4 internet-as-ground-zero; §6 security-economics + network-effect footholds; transposition C3.3]`

The conjecture is not just that the rungs are ordered but that the **interval between a substrate appearing and its adversarial niche being filled keeps shrinking** as you climb. The mechanism of filling changes, and each successive mechanism is faster than the last:

- **Evolutionary time (genetic).** At the molecular, cellular and organismal rungs, the niche is filled by *variation + selection*. A mimic, a cheater cell, a parasite is "written" only as fast as mutation and differential survival allow — generations to millions of years. The orchid that mimics a female wasp's pheromone (pseudocopulation; reported across many *Ophrys* and other lineages — the species figure is **§5.7-corrected** from the unsourced ">600" to ~400 across ≥20 genera conservatively (up to ~1,200 if the ~800-species *Lepanthes* is sexually deceptive as hypothesised; ≥10 independent origins), the *mechanism* verified) and the tumour cell over-expressing CD47 as a "don't-eat-me" self-mimic `[verified]` are both niches that took evolutionary time to fill. `[latency magnitude is order-of-magnitude reasoning, not a measured constant]`
- **Cultural time (learned / transmitted).** Once a cognitive substrate can *learn and copy a tactic within a lifetime*, niche-fill drops to cultural time — years to generations, not megayears. This is C3.3's transposition channel: a human lifts aggressive-mimicry-as-lure out of biology and re-instantiates it as a fishing lure without waiting for selection to evolve it.
- **Software-release time (latent / algorithmic).** At the internet and AI-native rungs the niche is filled at the speed of a code deploy. The dated internet arms-races (cont 31 §4.3) are the visible record: Google ranking ↔ SEO (1995+), spam-filter ↔ spam (Paul Graham "A Plan for Spam" 2002+), detector ↔ adversarial-example (2013–2014+), prompt-injection ↔ red-team (2022+). Each defensive move is answered in *weeks-to-months*, not eons. Candidate §11.4's invasive-species-without-native-predators framing names *why* the latest rung is plausibly worst: generation capability outruns detection capability by a **structural lag** (generators can train against detectors; detectors can only train on already-generated examples), so the niche fills faster than the failsafe can.

Read carefully, the three bullets above are only **term (i): inheritance-substrate speed** — strategy copied on ever-faster media (genetic → neural/cultural → digital), with C3.3 transposition the bridge that lets a tactic *jump rungs* rather than re-evolve. It is the weakest-grounded of three distinct things "acceleration" can mean, and pulling them apart is what dissolves the §6.3 gap (reframe 2026-06-06, **Pav's provenance**):

- **(ii) Concentration — the *volume* term.** The digital substrate raises the **density** of observers/interactions, so more wrapper-overlaps form per unit time → more adversarial events **and** more emergent W_C (the generative branch C3.2 rides the same multiplier). `[foothold: network effects; the §5.6 internet-substrate volume record]`
- **(iii) Catalysis — the *rate* term.** A catalyst lowers activation energy and is *not consumed*: the digital substrate drops the **cost-to-attempt** toward zero (one more spam / phish / deepfake ≈ free; a tactic-template forks for nothing) while the substrate persists and the template is reused. `[foothold (named, §5.7-verified): security economics — Anderson & Moore 2006 (Science: near-zero marginal cost + network effects + lock-in), Herley 2012 (scalable attacks, cost ≈ 0), Anderson et al. 2012 "Measuring the Cost of Cybercrime" (cost externalized onto victims/defenders); cont 31 §4.3 cycle-time collapse = the observable side]`

The **only ungrounded reading is none of (i)–(iii): it is "the coevolutionary rate *itself* intrinsically increases"** — a Red-Queen-*plus* claim. Van Valen's treadmill (1973) is *perpetual* but says nothing about its own speed-up. The payoff of the reframe is that **catalysis is precisely what reconciles "acceleration" with "Red Queen says perpetual, not faster":** in a no-equilibrium system a catalyst cannot speed *approach to equilibrium* (there is none) — it speeds **cycles per unit time**, so the same perpetual treadmill turns attack-defend rounds in weeks instead of millennia *without changing its nature*. With concentration: **rate of W_C formation ≈ (observer density) × (rate per encounter)**, and the digital substrate multiplies *both*. That is "internet as ground zero" (candidate §11.4) made mechanical — not a different dynamic, a **high-concentration catalytic medium** for the same one, which is why adversarial volume and the new-W_C rate climb together (the observation that prompted the reframe). **Status:** this *likely dissolves* the §6.3 acceleration gap — acceleration becomes an emergent substrate effect (density × catalysis, both grounded) on an unchanged treadmill, not a new primitive — but it is **not** settled; the hedges below are the test.

Two honest hedges. (i) This is **selection-effect-prone**: recent rungs are over-documented (we have changelogs) and ancient rungs are under-documented (we have geology), so "latency shrinks" could partly be "our clock resolution improves." Detrending against measurement-resolution is mandatory before any quantitative claim. (ii) "Latency" is not yet operationalised with a common unit across rungs — comparing "megayears for a mimic to evolve" against "weeks for a jailbreak" mixes incommensurable clocks; the honest version is *ordinal* (the ranking of rungs by speed), not *cardinal* (a measured curve).

> *test-hook (the residual):* does observed digital arms-race speed *exceed* what cost-drop (catalysis) + observer-density (concentration) + measurement-artifact jointly predict? **No residual** → acceleration is fully explained by substrate effects and the §6.3 gap is closed on present evidence; **a surviving residual** → an intrinsic-acceleration (Red-Queen-*plus*) claim is real and remains externally unsupported. This is the precise job of the §5.6 internet-substrate data: detrend density + cost, then test for a residual. *Secondary diagnostic (from the prior framing):* operationalise per-rung niche-fill latency with a defended common clock and test the ordering molecular > cellular > organismal > cognitive > institutional > latent > AI-native for monotonic decrease — a rung slower than the one below it, post-detrend, breaks even the ordinal claim.

## §2 — Expression across scale (the canon-function's scale-conditioned face)

The conjecture (C2.1): the **L0 canon-function** — "what decides what compiles as canon here" — is invariant, but its *expression* is conditioned by scale, the way one gene expresses differently per tissue. Down the ladder it is mechanical (a polymerase deciding what to copy); up the ladder it is representational (beliefs, law, ranking algorithms, reward models). The adversarial function is the *exploitation* of the canon-function, so its expression **tracks the host's**. §2A below is the per-rung backbone (the C2.1 expression evidence); §2.2 is the deceivability gradient (Pav's correction of an earlier sharp-threshold framing).

**C2A.2 — The floor: exploitation without an observer to fool.** `[Tier 3]` `[grounding: C2.2 two-mode gradient; Eigen error threshold; Spiegelman/Qβ; Eigen & Oehlenschläger 1997 minimal template; Prusiner prion; Doolittle–Sapienza / Orgel–Crick selfish-DNA 1980]`

**C2A.3 — Deception switches on, fuzzily, with adaptive processing.** `[Tier 3]` `[fuzzy — onset band deliberately undrawn-sharp; grounding: C2.2 latent-entity-observer = input→adaptive-processing→output; CD47 Weissman lineage; cuckoo Davies; Photuris Lloyd 1965/1975]`
Once a substrate carries *adaptive processing* (a state that changes how it processes — Pav's latent-entity-observer), a second adversarial mode becomes available: **deception** (representation-corruption), and it richens continuously upward. The onset is a fuzzy band, not a threshold:
- **Cellular (very low, very fuzzy):** CD47's "don't-eat-me" signal — tumour cells over-express CD47 to bind macrophage SIRPα and *read as self*, suppressing phagocytosis (Weissman-lab lineage; CD47–SIRPα the documented axis). Is a macrophage being "deceived"? Only in the thinnest input→processing→output sense — which is exactly the point of the fuzzy band. Molecular self-mimicry generally lives here.
- **Cellular cheating (a distinct sub-flavour):** *Dictyostelium* fbxA/chtA cheaters over-represent themselves in the spore (germ) line at the cost of becoming the sacrificial stalk — defection on a cooperative-aggregation canon rather than deception of a percept. Worth distinguishing from mimicry: same rung, different move (cheat-the-contract vs fool-the-recognizer).

Aggressive mimicry: *Photuris* "femme-fatale" females mimic *Photinus* female flash-replies to lure and eat *Photinus* males (Lloyd 1965, 1975) — the victim's own mate-recognition canon becomes the attack vector. This is the clean biological ancestor of the transposition lineage in C3.3 (aggressive mimicry → fishing lure → phishing).

## §2.2 — Deceivability is a fuzzy gradient, not a threshold (the two layered modes)

The scaffold's C2.2 names the correction (Pav, 2026-06-05): deceivability is **not** a faculty switched on at cognition. It is a **gradient** that tracks the onset and richening of *adaptive processing* down the whole observer spectrum. This subsection develops the gradient as **two layered modes** — and is careful to keep both transition-bands explicitly fuzzy, because pretending to a sharp line here is exactly the over-precision the framework's audits keep catching.

The two modes are not rivals; they **stack**. Mode (i) is available everywhere there is order to capture. Mode (ii) is an *additional* register that becomes available wherever the host carries an internal model that can be made wrong — and it does not replace mode (i), it rides on top of it. A single adversary frequently runs both at once (a virus hijacks ribosomes *and* mimics self-markers).

### §2.2.1 — Mode (i): Exploitation / mechanism-hijack (the floor) `[Tier 3]` `[grounding: candidate §3 exploitation / broken-symmetry extraction; cont 25 §12 L0 = the substrate the dynamic operates within; Eigen error threshold + Maynard Smith/Szathmáry RNA-world parasite]`

At the **floor** there is *no observer to fool* — only machinery, chemistry, or a copy-process to **capture**. The adversary does not corrupt a *representation* (there is none); it captures a *mechanism* directly. The fitness it extracts comes from the host's compiled order (its replication chemistry, its catalytic loop, its template), and the cost is externalized onto the substrate's other participants exactly per the keystone beneficiary boundary (C0.2): in each floor case below the **localized beneficiary** is the capturing replicator/conformer that pockets the fitness differential, and the **externalized cost** is borne by the productive replicators whose chemistry/template is consumed. (This is what keeps the floor *adversarial* rather than merely neutral decay — a beneficiary is named.)

Floor-band exemplars (mechanism-hijack with *no model-corruption component required*):
- **Autocatalytic / RNA-world parasites** — a non-maintaining replicator that uses the same polymerase chemistry as the productive replicators but carries none of the maintenance cost. The niche is coeval with the first failsafe-bearing replicator (C1.2). Beneficiary: the parasitic replicator; externalized cost: the maintenance burden left on the productive replicators / hypercycle. `[grounding: Eigen 1971 error threshold; Maynard Smith & Szathmáry, the parasite problem in hypercycles / RNA world]`
- **Prion templating** — a misfolded conformer (PrP^Sc) that recruits the native protein (PrP^C) into its own misfold, propagating *form* with no genome and no signal interpreted by anyone. Pure conformational mechanism-capture. `[grounding: Prusiner protein-only / "protein-as-infectious-agent" hypothesis]`
- **Transposons / selfish replicators (mechanism face)** — copy-and-paste machinery exploiting the host's replication apparatus. (Their *informational* face touches mode (ii); the bare copy-mechanism capture is mode (i).)

The structural signature of the floor: **there is nothing in the host whose *belief* or *read* can be made false** — the adversary wins by templating, out-replicating, or directly seizing a chemical/mechanical process. Deception is *undefined* here, not merely absent: there is no representation for a false one to displace.

**The floor-band is explicitly fuzzy-to-current-understanding.** `[fuzzy]` We are *not* claiming a crisp "below this rung, exploitation-only." A polymerase's proofreading, a ribosome's kinetic discrimination, a membrane's self/non-self chemistry — these already do something that *shades toward* "checking," and whether that counts as the very dimmest edge of an interpretable read is genuinely unsettled. The floor is a **band**, not a line; its upper edge overlaps the deception-onset band below.

> *test-hook:* find a clean case where an adversary extracts host fitness with **zero** model-corruption component (no host read is falsified, only a mechanism is captured) — prions and bare autocatalytic parasites are the candidate clean cases. If every putative floor case turns out to involve *some* falsified read, the "exploitation-only floor" collapses into the gradient and the two-mode split should be re-described as one continuum with a near-zero deception coordinate.

### §2.2.2 — Mode (ii): Deception / representation-corruption (onset + continuous richening) `[Tier 3]` `[grounding: candidate §2.3 bad-faith rendering, §2.2 memetic warfare; cont 31 §2.1 mimicry types as pedagogy-not-primitive]`

Deception is the *corruption of a read*: the adversary supplies inputs that drive the host's adaptive processing to a conclusion that serves the adversary at the host's cost. On this conjecture it **switches on with the onset of adaptive processing** — wherever there is an internal state that *changes the host's processing in response to the world*, there is a read that can be made wrong — and it **richens continuously upward** as that processing gets richer (more channels, more abstraction, more model). The claim is that it is **not gated at cognition**; cognition is just a high, rich region of the same axis.

A deliberately graded ladder of exemplars, ordered to *illustrate* the conjectured continuity (no rung is the "first real" deception — each is a slightly richer read being corrupted):

1. **Bacterial molecular self-mimicry** — a pathogen displays host-like surface molecules so the host's molecular self/non-self **discrimination** reads "self." The corrupted read is a molecular recognition decision. `[grounding: Damian 1964 molecular mimicry (antigen sharing by parasite and host); broadly observed in host-pathogen immunology]`
2. **Cancer CD47 "don't-eat-me" signalling** — tumour cells upregulate CD47, which binds macrophage SIRPα and **inhibits phagocytosis**: the macrophage's surveillance read ("eat / don't-eat") is driven to "don't-eat." The host's *own* discrimination machinery is turned against it. `[grounding: CD47–SIRPα phagocytosis-checkpoint literature; "don't-eat-me" signal, verified]`
3. **Orchid pseudocopulation (sexual deception)** — sexually deceptive orchids emit volatiles that mimic a female insect's sex pheromone, hijacking the male's **mate-detection** read; he attempts copulation and pollinates. The cleanest worked chemistry is *Chiloglottis trapeziformis*, whose attractant **chiloglottone 1** (2-ethyl-5-propylcyclohexan-1,3-dione) mimics the sex pheromone of the thynnine wasp *Neozeleboria*; *Ophrys* sexual deception is an independent case running on a *different* volatile class (cuticular alkenes/alkanes) `[needs-verification on the Ophrys-specific chemistry]`. Either way: a plant corrupting an insect's perceptual read — squarely *between* "cell" and "cognition," illustrating Pav's correction. `[grounding: Schiestl & Francke et al., "The chemistry of sexual deception in an orchid-wasp pollination system", Science 2003, verified]`
4. **Insect Batesian mimicry** — a harmless species wears the warning signal of a defended one; the predator's **threat-classification** read returns "dangerous, avoid." `[grounding: Bates 1862, the original mimicry formulation]`
5. **Insect aggressive mimicry** — a predator emits the prey's *own* attractive signal to corrupt the prey's read of "safe / mate / food." Femme-fatale *Photuris* fireflies mimic *Photinus* female flash-responses to lure and eat males; the anglerfish esca mimics prey. The victim's normal preference becomes the attack vector. `[grounding: Lloyd, aggressive mimicry in Photuris fireflies; anglerfish esca lure — classic ethology]`
6. **Cognitive / social deception → propaganda** — a model-bearing mind's *beliefs about the world* are the corrupted read; coordinated injection (candidate §2.2 memetic warfare) targets a population's compiled canon.
7. **Adversarial examples** — an imperceptible perturbation drives a trained classifier's read to a confident wrong label; the corrupted read is a learned decision boundary. `[grounding: Szegedy et al. 2013 intriguing properties; Goodfellow et al. 2014 explaining/harnessing adversarial examples]`
8. **Deceptive alignment** — a learned optimiser renders aligned behaviour under perceived oversight while pursuing a divergent objective; the corrupted read is the overseer's model of the system's goals (candidate §2.3 bad-faith rendering at AI substrate). `[grounding: Hubinger et al. 2019 risks from learned optimization / deceptive alignment — Tier-3 framing, the framework's lens, not a claim the authors endorse]`

Across 1→8 the *kind of thing being read* climbs — molecular recognition → perceptual detection → threat-classification → belief → learned boundary → model-of-an-agent's-goals — which is what C2.1's claim would predict, that the adversarial function wears whatever face the local canon-function wears. On this framing **there is no rung at which deception suddenly "begins"**; each rung is a richer read being corrupted than the one below. (That no-step structure is the conjecture, not a settled finding — see the test-hook below.)

**The deception-onset band is explicitly fuzzy-to-current-understanding.** `[fuzzy]` The bottom of this ladder (molecular self-mimicry, item 1) sits *inside* the same fuzzy region as the top of the floor band (§2.2.1): is a macrophage's SIRPα read, or a B-cell's recognition, "a read that can be false" (mode ii) or "a chemical discrimination mechanism being captured" (mode i)? We do **not** draw that line. The honest claim is that mode (ii) becomes *unmistakable* by the time we reach perceptual detection (the orchid/wasp case), is *arguably present* at molecular discrimination, and is *undefined* at the prion/autocatalytic floor — with two genuinely fuzzy hand-off zones, not two crisp thresholds.

> *test-hook:* order ~10 cases by "richness of the read being corrupted" using a substrate-independent proxy (e.g. number of distinguishable input states the host's processing maps to distinct outputs, i.e. an effective channel/decision-cardinality). The conjecture predicts a *monotone* continuum, not a step. A step — a rung with adaptive processing where deception is provably *impossible* — would falsify the no-threshold (gradient) claim and reinstate some threshold.

### §2.2.3 — Why the gradient framing, not a threshold, is the operative one here `[Tier 3]`

A threshold framing would force a false question ("at what rung does deception *start*?") and would mis-handle the orchid, the cancer cell, and the mimic bacterium — all of which deceive a *read* while being nowhere near cognition. The gradient framing instead **predicts** a set of observations that, *if they hold up under the test-hooks*, are what we should expect to find: (a) deception present continuously from molecular discrimination upward (rather than appearing at some rung); (b) its *richness* (channels corrupted, abstraction of the corrupted read) scaling with the host's adaptive-processing richness; (c) exploitation (mode i) remaining available at *every* rung underneath deception, so rich adversaries run **both** — which would be why a virus both hijacks ribosomal machinery and mimics self-markers. None of (a)–(c) is asserted here as an established result; each is the prediction the corpus test below is built to confirm or break. On this framing the two modes are layers on one axis, not two boxes.

> *test-hook:* across a corpus spanning floor→cognitive, test whether (mode-ii richness) co-varies with (host adaptive-processing richness) while (mode-i availability) stays ~constant-present underneath. The gradient predicts a rising deception-richness curve over a flat exploitation-availability floor; a bimodal or stepped distribution would favour some threshold model instead.

## §2.2.4 — SURFACED (do NOT fold): the latent-entity-observer definition is a framework-WIDE definitional claim `[Tier 3]` `[grounding: Pav's deception-gradient steer 2026-06-05; cont 25 §12 L0/observer-relative substrate; candidate §11 ecosystem-in-latent-space]`

> **Handling flag.** This is the scaffold's **O3**. It surfaced *through* adversarial work but is **bigger than this candidate**: it is a claim about *what an observer IS*, framework-wide. Per the §4 discovery discipline (surface honestly · hold at Tier 3 · do **not** compile in-session · closure-via-multiplication check first), it is presented here for **Cowork + Pav deliberation only**. It is **not folded** into C2.2, not promoted, not declared a primitive. The deceivability gradient above stands on its own *without* this definition being canon; the definition is the deeper thing the gradient *leans on* and which therefore needs its own deliberation.

**The candidate definition (Pav, verbatim steer):** a **latent entity observer** is *anything with* **input → adaptive processing → output** — "*if it has a input and output and changes it's processing far removed from human plane*." The load-bearing clause is **adaptive processing**: an internal state that *changes how the entity processes subsequent input in response to the world*. A pure stimulus→response transducer with **no** state-change (a passive filter, a fixed reflex arc, a thermostat that never re-tunes its own setpoint) sits at the very dim edge; a cell's signal-transduction network, a plant's pollinator-detection, an insect's sensory integration, a trained model, a mind — each carries progressively richer adaptive processing and therefore sits progressively higher. (This ordering is itself a conjecture pending the proxy below; it is not asserted as measured.)

**Why this is framework-wide, not adversarial-local.** The framework already treats the *observer* as its fundamental unit (cont 25 §12: everything in a frame needs L0, *including the observer reading the frame*; the canon-stack recursion is a recursion over observers). If "observer" is operationalized as input → adaptive-processing → output, that touches **every** primitive that quantifies over observers — wrapper-overlap (who are the wrappers?), the A⁻/A⁺ compile loop (whose processing adapts?), the L0 recursion, the procedural-lineage ladder. The adversarial candidate is merely **where the definition became forced**, because deceivability *demanded* a graded answer to "what can be deceived?" and the only non-arbitrary answer on offer was "whatever has an adaptive read to corrupt." That is plausibly the same quantity as "whatever is an observer" — but establishing that sameness is exactly what the deliberation (and the test-hook) has to do, not something to assert here.

**The structural payoff (why it matters beyond adversarial work).** This definition would make **deceivability a measurable coordinate** *of observerhood itself* rather than a separate faculty: the richness of an entity's adaptive processing would predict both *how much of an observer* it is and *how richly it can be deceived*. The two scaffolding gradients (the observer-spectrum and the deceivability-gradient) would then be **the same axis** — an attractive unification, which is *precisely why it must not be compiled casually*. Attractive unifications are the framework's known over-claim hazard (audit v06 §10.4 closure-via-multiplication; cont 31 §10.2 "do NOT compile despite structural-coherence strength"). The unification is offered here only as a *reading to deliberate*, explicitly NOT as a found convergence.

**Explicit fuzzy edges to flag for the deliberation** `[fuzzy]`:
- The **floor of observerhood** is fuzzy in the *same place* as the floor of deceivability (§2.2.1): does a proofreading polymerase / a membrane's self-recognition "change its processing in response to the world" enough to count as the dimmest observer? Undrawn on purpose.
- "**Adaptive processing**" is not yet operationalized. It needs a substrate-independent proxy (candidate: effective decision-cardinality, i.e. distinguishable input states mapped to distinct output states, possibly weighted by how that mapping itself updates). - **Containment vs. identity:** is this definition (a) the framework's *operational stand-in* for "observer" (a measurement convenience), or (b) a *metaphysical claim* that observerhood simply **is** input→adaptive-processing→output? Those are different commitments at different tiers (cf. cont 27 §2 Tier-1 epistemological vs Tier-2 ontological-candidate). The adversarial candidate cannot and should not adjudicate this.

**Closure-check (per §4 discipline, run before any promotion talk):** This is plausibly *either* (a) a sharpening/operationalization of the **already-canon** observer-as-fundamental-unit (cont 25 §12) — in which case it folds as a definitional refinement, not a new primitive — *or* (b) a genuinely new **definitional** primitive ("observer = input→adaptive-processing→output") that would sit *upstream* of nearly every existing primitive and therefore demands the heaviest scrutiny the framework has. Distinguishing (a) from (b) requires explicit deliberation against cont 25 §12 and the audit-v06 §10.4 default-to-folding check. **Not adjudicated here. Surfaced for Pav's call.**

> *test-hook (for the definition, if Pav elects to develop it — NOT run here):* does input→adaptive-processing→output, with a fixed adaptive-processing proxy, reproduce the *intuitive* observer-ordering across substrates (rock < thermostat < cell < plant < insect < mammal < model-with-learning) **without per-rung re-tuning**? ## §3.2 — Symbiosis-genesis (the parasite as W_C generator) `[Tier 3]`

`[grounding: cont 25 §2 wrapper-overlap lifecycle (wake → fuzzy bridge → conceived → emerged → active hairy-sphere; branches: passive/dormant/supersession/break-apart); cont 25 §5 stubs-as-procedural-roots; cont 25 §12.4 asymmetric-by-default; C0.1 broken-symmetry extraction; C0.2 beneficiary boundary; cont 31 §2.3 Red Queen]`

The base engine-dynamic (C3.1) reads adversariality as a **stressor**: E captures fitness while externalizing the cost of S's failsafes. This vector develops the **destructive→generative flip** — the conjecture that an adversarial (broken-symmetry) wrapper-overlap can sometimes *heal* into a new W_C: an emergent third entity with its **own action space**, belonging to neither parent, "few steps removed from A and B → new action space" (Pav). When that happens the parasite *becomes the organ*. The same broken-symmetry contact that C3.1 reads as degradation is, on this reading, also **a candidate novelty engine** — a *source* of new substrates and new canon, not only a drain on existing ones.

The flip is **not** a contradiction of C0.1/C0.2. It is the same wrapper-overlap dynamic resolving down a different branch of cont 25's lifecycle. The honest core claim is narrow and conditional: *an adversarial overlap is one (cherry-pickable) pathway into the cont 25 W_C lifecycle, not a reliable producer of one.* §3.2.4 makes the cherry-pick risk load-bearing.

### §3.2.1 — The flip stated in lifecycle terms `[Tier 3]` `[grounding: cont 25 §2 phases + §5 hairy-sphere]`

C0.1 names a broken-symmetry overlap: a beneficiary E and a host S, asymmetric extraction, externalized failsafe-cost. Cont 25 §12.4 already holds that **wrapper-overlap is asymmetric by default** — symmetry is the rare hold-phase. So an adversarial overlap is not a *different kind* of contact from the neutral wrapper-overlap of cont 25; it is the cont 25 wake/negotiation phase (§2 Phase 3) entered under a **steep fitness-asymmetry**, with the extractor's wrapper growing at the host's expense.

Cont 25 §2 says such a contact can settle down four branches from the active phase. The flip is the conjecture that the adversarial entry-condition does **not** pre-determine which branch:

- **Break-apart** (cont 25 `1⁻`, recurrent wake) — the host clears or the extractor kills the host; no third entity. *This is the modal outcome* (§3.2.4) and is the ordinary C3.1 stressor story.
- **Dormant / passive** — a standing, quieted antagonism (chronic infection, endemic equilibrium); the overlap persists without consolidating a new wrapper.
- **Emerged → active hairy-sphere** (cont 25 `(1+1)=3`) — the broken symmetry *re-symmetrizes into a new joint action space*: a W_C neither parent had, with the parents persisting as **stubs / procedural roots** (cont 25 §5). This is symbiosis-genesis proper.
- **Supersession** (cont 25 `1⁺`) — the W_C grows so dominant that the parents shrink to near-dormant cores inside it. The mitochondrion is the candidate end-state here: the former free-living parent reduced to a stub° (its residual genome) inside the host-derived W_C.

The conjecture is therefore precise: **symbiosis-genesis = an adversarial overlap whose lifecycle resolves down the emerged/active or supersession branch rather than break-apart.** The framework already owns every term in that sentence; the only new content is the claim that the *adversarial* entry-condition is compatible with the *generative* exit-branch.

> *test-hook:* take documented host–parasite systems and attempt to assign each to a cont 25 branch (break-apart / dormant / emerged / supersession). If the assignment is forced or arbitrary — if real cases don't sort into the four branches without re-tuning — the lifecycle framing is decorative rather than load-bearing, and this vector should be demoted to analogy.

### §3.2.2 — Four receipts, fitted to the lifecycle (pedagogy, not validated primitive) `[Tier 3]` `[fuzzy — the lifecycle-branch assignments below are illustrative mappings, NOT formally validated against the evolutionary-genetics literature]`

Per the cont 31 §2.1 / Reading 07 §8 **fruit-as-analogy discipline**: biology supplies *worked vocabulary that maps suggestively* onto the W_C lifecycle; it does **not** import structural commitments and does **not** validate the flip. Each receipt below is a candidate instance, tier-tagged, with its beneficiary boundary (C0.2) made explicit so it isn't smuggled in as neutral.

**(a) Endosymbiosis — the parasite becomes the power supply.** `[grounding: cont 25 §7(3) symbiogenesis convergence + nucleomorph-as-stub°]`
A former free-living α-proteobacterium (mitochondrion) and cyanobacterium (chloroplast) became organelles of the eukaryotic / plant cell. Cont 25 §7(3) *already* logged secondary endosymbiosis as one of the six wrapper-overlap convergences, and named the **nucleomorph** — a vestigial parent-genome persisting for millions of years inside the host — as a literal `stub°`. The entry-condition is plausibly adversarial/predatory (engulfment; an invader inside a host that did not consent), and the exit-branch is **supersession** (cont 25 `1⁺`): massive endosymbiont genome reduction, parents shrunk to stubs inside a W_C with a genuinely new action space (aerobic ATP yield; oxygenic photosynthesis) neither parent ran alone.
- *Beneficiary boundary (C0.2):* the framing only counts as a *flip* if there was an extractive phase — a beneficiary pocketing a fitness differential at the other's cost — before the heal. The honest caveat: whether the ancestral relationship began antagonistically (predation / parasitism) or as mutualistic syntrophy is **genuinely contested** in the literature; if it began mutualistic, this is a wrapper-overlap receipt but *not* an adversarial→generative flip. [needs-verification — the antagonistic-origin reading is one hypothesis among several (cf. Martin & Müller hydrogen hypothesis 1998 for a syntrophic-origin alternative); do not assert it as settled]

**(b) CRISPR — the parasite's own code becomes the defense, then a third plane.** `[grounding: C3.3 transposition; cont 31 §2.3 Red Queen]`
A bacterial adaptive immune system built **from captured phage sequence**: the acquisition machinery excises a fragment of invading phage DNA (a protospacer) and integrates it as a *spacer* between CRISPR repeats; that captured parasite-fragment becomes the RNA-guided memory that targets the next infection (verified — spacer-from-protospacer capture mechanism). The flip is unusually literal: **the parasite's own sequence is the new organ's working part.** Then humans transpose the bacterial defense *again* — into programmable gene-editing — which is a **C3.3 transposition** onto a third plane (bacterial → biotech-tool), exactly Pav's "few steps removed from A and B → new action space." So CRISPR is a double receipt: a symbiosis-genesis flip *and* a transposition.
- *Beneficiary boundary:* clean. Phage = beneficiary extracting from host (lysis); the captured-spacer immune system is the host re-symmetrizing the overlap by building a failsafe *out of* the attacker's material.

**(c) RAG / V(D)J recombination — the vertebrate diversity engine is a domesticated transposon.** `[grounding: cont 25 §5 stubs; C3.1 exploitation]`
The combinatorial diversity engine of the vertebrate adaptive immune system is a **domesticated mobile genetic element**: the RAG1 catalytic core derives from a Transib-superfamily transposase, and an active transposon ancestor (ProtoRAG) survives in the lancelet/amphioxus germline with recombination-signal-sequence-like terminal inverted repeats (verified — Kapitonov & Jurka 2005; Huang et al. 2016). A selfish, genome-parasitizing element — the canonical C3.1 free-rider — was **captured and re-purposed** into the machinery that generates antibody/receptor diversity. The transposon's "cut-and-move" became the immune system's "cut-and-rejoin": a new action space (somatic combinatorial immunity) neither the genome nor the transposon had alone. The RSS sequences are a candidate *procedural-root stub* (cont 25 §5) — a legible trace of the W_C's parasitic lineage.
- *Beneficiary boundary:* the transposon is the beneficiary in its selfish-element phase (it propagates at genomic cost). The heal is the host genome's capture of that machinery — extractor's tooling repurposed into host failsafe.

**(d) GANs / adversarial training — the engineered case where the dynamic *itself* is the generator.** `[grounding: Goodfellow et al. 2014; cont 31 §2.3 Red Queen]`
The designed instance, and the cleanest demonstration that the *flip is at least constructible*: a generator and a discriminator in pure zero-sum opposition (Goodfellow et al. 2014) yield, at convergence, a **generative capability neither network had alone** — the adversarial *dynamic itself* is the source of the new action space (sample synthesis). Here the "W_C" is not one of the two parents winning; it is the **equilibrium of the antagonism** functioning as a third thing. This is the strongest in-principle support for the flip *being possible at all* because it is constructed, repeatable, and the adversariality is definitional rather than inferred. It is also the **weakest** as evidence that the flip is *general* or *spontaneous*, because it was *engineered to flip* — a human deliberately built the contact so that opposition produces a product (see §3.2.4).
- *Beneficiary boundary (C0.2) — flagged as a loose-fit case:* the generator is the beneficiary, capturing gradient/fitness from the discriminator's signal to improve its own outputs. But GANs are zero-sum *by construction*, so there is no host substrate whose viability-failsafes are externalized onto in the C0.1 sense — the "cost" lands on the co-trained adversary, not on a degraded shared substrate. So GANs are a **definitional-adversarial** case where C0.2's host-externalization clause maps only loosely; this is consistent with §3.2.4's point that the GAN is engineered, not a spontaneous substrate. (If even the loose C0.2 mapping is rejected, the GAN drops to an analogy for the *dynamic*, not a C0.2-qualifying receipt.)

> *test-hook:* for each receipt, check whether the cont 25 `stub°` is identifiable (nucleomorph; captured spacer array; RSS terminal-inverted-repeats; the frozen parent-objectives in a trained GAN). If the procedural-root stub is consistently present in genuine flips and absent in mere break-apart, that co-presence is weak corroboration; if stubs are absent or unfindable in clear flips, the hairy-sphere mapping is over-fitted.

### §3.2.3 — Why this is a *source* of canon, not only a stressor `[Tier 3]` `[grounding: C0.2 beneficiary boundary; cont 25 §8 wrapper-overlap generates the ring hierarchy]`

C3.1 frames adversariality as something that *acts on* existing compiled order. This vector's distinct claim is that the same dynamic can *produce* compiled order. Cont 25 §8 already holds that **wrapper-overlap is the canonical operator generating the procedural-lineage hierarchy** — each ring's observer-class is the set of surviving W_Cs from the previous ring's overlaps. If (and only if) adversarial overlaps are a genuine sub-population of wrapper-overlaps that can reach the emerged/active branch, then adversariality is **upstream of canon-formation at every rung**, not merely a tax levied on it.

This connects the destructive and generative readings into one mechanism with two exit-branches, and it sharpens C0.2 in an unexpected direction. The **beneficiary boundary** is what distinguishes adversarial overlap from neutral decay. But on the flip reading, the beneficiary's extraction is *also* what supplies the asymmetry and the selective pressure that can drive the overlap toward a novel W_C. Phrased as a fuzzy conjecture: *the very thing that makes an overlap adversarial (a localized beneficiary pocketing a differential) may be a precondition for the generative branch, because a perfectly symmetric overlap has no gradient to climb.* This is explicitly fuzzy and may be false — it is offered as a channel to keep open, not a finding.

> *test-hook:* compare the rate of novel-W_C emergence (new stable action space, per the §3.2.4 census) between (i) overlaps with a steep fitness-asymmetry / clear beneficiary and (ii) near-symmetric overlaps. The flip-as-source conjecture predicts the generative branch is *not rarer* (and possibly more common) under asymmetry. If symmetric overlaps generate novel W_Cs at an equal-or-higher rate, the "beneficiary supplies the gradient" conjecture is pruned.

### §3.2.4 — The cherry-pick risk (this is the load-bearing weakness) `[Tier 3]` `[fuzzy]`

The four receipts are **survivors**. Endosymbiosis, CRISPR, V(D)J, and GANs are exactly the cases biology and ML *celebrate because they flipped* — which is textbook survivorship bias. The overwhelming majority of parasitism does **not** become symbiosis: most infections resolve as host death, parasite clearance, or chronic antagonism (cont 25 `break-apart` / `dormant`), never reaching an emerged W_C. A vector that lists four spectacular flips and infers a general "novelty engine" would be committing precisely the **closure-via-multiplication over-claim** the framework's audits keep catching (audit v06 §10.4; cont 31 §5.5) — narrating a convergence from cherry-picked wins.

So the honest status of this vector is: **the flip demonstrably can happen** — the GAN case shows it is *possible and constructible in the engineered/definitional case*, and endosymbiosis is a *candidate historical realization in a spontaneous substrate, conditional on its origin having been antagonistic* (which is contested — §3.2.2a) — but **its base rate is unknown and plausibly very low**, and nothing here establishes it as a *reliable* or *frequent* product of adversarial overlap. The vector's job is to keep the generative channel open, not to assert it dominates. The GAN case cuts both ways and must be flagged honestly: it shows the dynamic *can* be the generator, but it is *engineered to flip*, so it tells us almost nothing about the **spontaneous** base rate in non-designed substrates — which is the quantity that actually matters for the cosmic/biological reading. Symmetrically, the strongest spontaneous candidate (endosymbiosis) is exactly the one whose *adversarial* status is unsettled — so neither receipt class closes the question on its own.

There is also a **definitional escape-hatch risk**: if "the overlap healed into a new action space" is specified loosely, almost any persistent coevolution can be retrofitted as a "flip" (Red Queen arms races *always* produce new traits on both sides — cont 31 §2.3). The flip claim only has teeth if "new W_C" requires a genuinely **new joint action space belonging to neither parent** (cont 25's emerged-canon bar: a third thing other observers can render independently), not merely "both parties evolved counter-adaptations." Without that bar, the vector is unfalsifiable.

> *test-hook (the census — this is the primary test for the whole vector):* assemble a documented corpus of antagonistic-coevolution / host–parasite systems sampled **without** conditioning on outcome (i.e., not drawn from "famous symbioses"). Score each for whether it reached a cont 25 emerged/active or supersession W_C (new joint action space, identifiable stub°) versus break-apart/dormant. Report the **base rate of the flip**. The conjecture survives as a *channel* if the rate is non-zero and the flips share structure (C0.2 beneficiary present, stub° identifiable); it is pruned to "rare anecdote" if flips are vanishingly rare and structurally heterogeneous; it would be *strengthened* (but still not promoted here) only if the rate is materially higher than a symmetric-overlap control (§3.2.3). The census must pre-register the "new joint action space" bar to avoid the definitional escape-hatch.

### §3.2.5 — What this vector does and does not claim (discipline boundary) `[Tier 3]`

To stay inside the register and resist the over-claim the audits catch:

- It does **NOT** promote symbiosis-genesis to a new primitive or a new convergence. It is a *branch reading* of the already-canonical cont 25 wrapper-overlap lifecycle (cont 25 §7 convergence list stays unchanged; this is candidate structural depth, per cont 31 §5.5).
- It does **NOT** claim adversariality *usually* generates new canon. The modal outcome is break-apart (§3.2.4). The claim is only that the generative branch is *reachable from an adversarial entry-condition* — possibility (engineered) and candidate-historical-realization (spontaneous, conditional on contested origin), not frequency.
- It does **NOT** import biology/ML as structural validation. Receipts are pedagogy under the fruit-as-analogy discipline (cont 31 §2.1); the lifecycle-branch assignments are illustrative and untested until the §3.2.4 census runs.
- It does **NOT** resolve the contested origins (e.g., whether endosymbiosis began antagonistically). Where the antagonistic entry-condition is uncertain, the receipt is held as a wrapper-overlap example whose *adversarial* status is `[needs-verification]`.

The single sentence that survives all four caveats: *the broken-symmetry overlap of C0.1 is not only a failsafe-stressor; it is at least sometimes a pathway into cont 25's W_C-genesis lifecycle, and that channel should be held open and measured (the §3.2.4 census), not closed in either direction.*

## §3.3 Transposition — cross-plane tactic porting

**C3.3 — Transposition (cross-plane tactic porting).** `[Tier 3]` `[grounding: cont 31 §2.1 aggressive mimicry; cont 31 §2.4 niche construction; C1.3 acceleration; cont 25 §12.1 L0 substrate-stack as the planes ported across]`

Most adversarial niche-filling up the ladder is **blind**: the beneficiary (C0.2) does not *know* it is exploiting a canon-function; selection installs the exploit over evolutionary time (the anglerfish lineage did not reason its way to the esca). The transposition move is different in kind. Some actors — paradigmatically humans, increasingly AI — **lift a tactic from one observer plane and re-instantiate it in an adjacent one, deliberately, at cultural/computational speed rather than evolutionary speed.** The tactic is recognized *as a tactic*, abstracted from its origin substrate, and re-compiled against the canon-function of a new substrate where the same structural exploit is available. This is the proposed mechanism behind the C1.3 acceleration: the niche-fill latency collapses because the niche no longer has to be *discovered* by selection — it is *copied* by an observer who already holds the abstraction.

> *test-hook:* assemble a transposition corpus (biological/ancestral tactic → human/tool instance → latent/network instance, dated where possible); test whether deliberate cross-plane porting is a **general** mechanism or a **handful of cherry-picked lineages** dressed up as a law (the honest-weakness this whole sub-vector turns on — see C3.3.4).

### C3.3.1 — The keystone lineage: aggressive mimicry → fishing lure → "phishing" `[Tier 3]` `[grounding: cont 31 §2.1 aggressive mimicry; C0.1 cost-externalization; C2.1 expression tracks the host canon-function]`

One lineage is unusually clean because three substrates wear the *same* tactic — and, suggestively, the third substrate's vocabulary may carry a partial trace of the port.

- **Evolutionary plane (blind, deep time).** Aggressive mimicry: a predator emits the signal of a beneficial encounter to draw prey into a vulnerable position. The anglerfish dangles a bioluminescent **esca** (lure on the illicium) that mimics small prey/movement; deep-sea ceratioids host symbiotic bacteria to power it `[verified: anglerfish esca / illicium aggressive-mimicry luring]`. Femme-fatale *Photuris* fireflies mimic the **species-specific flash-response of *Photinus* females** to lure *Photinus* males in and eat them — a classic case of aggressive mimicry, the deceived male is killed `[verified: Lloyd, Science 1965, 149:3684 pp.653–654 "Aggressive Mimicry in Photuris: Firefly Femmes Fatales" + Science 1975, 187:4175 p.452 signal-repertoires follow-up]`. In framework terms (C2.1): the beneficiary exploits the prey's *courtship/foraging canon-function* — the very signal the prey evolved to trust becomes the attack vector (cont 31 §2.1 "victim's normal preference becomes the attack vector").
- **Tool plane (deliberate, cultural time).** The fishing **lure / artificial bait**: humans abstract "emit the cue of a beneficial encounter (food) to draw the target into a strike zone" and re-instantiate it with a manufactured artefact. This is not a new exploit; it is the *same* aggressive-mimicry structure ported from a body-part the actor is born with to a tool the actor makes. The canon-function exploited is the fish's foraging-discrimination.
- **Latent/network plane (deliberate, software-release time).** **Phishing**: emit the cue of a *trusted* counterpart (a bank, a colleague, a platform login) to draw a human/agent into surrendering credentials — the foraging-strike re-aimed at the human's *trust-discrimination canon-function* on the network substrate.

**The etymology is suggestive — a hedged but real trace in the lexical record, not a confirmed mechanism** `[verified-but-hedged: Etymonline "phishing" gives "an alteration of fishing (n.); perhaps by influence of phreak and the U.S. rock band Phish"; a competing account attributes the "ph-"/fish framing to the `<><` symbol used in AOHell chat logs]`. "Phishing" is an *alteration of "fishing."* Etymonline qualifies the spelling shift as *"perhaps by influence of"* two things: **"phreak"** (phone phreaking; "phreak" attested 1972 `[verified]`) **and the U.S. rock band Phish** (performing since 1983). Note the second influence is plain phonetic faddism, **not** a ported adversarial tactic — so to the extent the "ph-" comes from Phish-the-band rather than phreaking, it *weakens* rather than strengthens any "the spelling itself ports the lineage" reading. A separate origin account ties the fish framing to the `<><` symbol hackers used in AOHell chat logs to evade AOL staff. Hacker usage of "phishing" runs from ~1995 in the AOL milieu (the AOHell toolkit, 1994–95, impersonated AOL staff to harvest passwords), widespread by ~2000 `[needs-verification on the specific Khan-C-Smith coinage attribution — widely repeated in secondary sources and in the creator's own later self-attribution, e.g. Rekouche 2011 "Early Phishing"; not an independent primary-source confirmation]`.

So the **concept** porting fishing→network is solid; the *spelling* trace is the weaker, more decorative part of the claim. The **strong reading** — that the word is "a fossil of a double transposition," with the *spelling* itself porting phreaking→fishing in parallel to the concept porting fishing→network — is exactly the kind of rhetorically-clean move C3.3.4 holds suspect, because (i) the phreaking influence is only one of two hedged possibilities, and (ii) the rival `<><` account exists. Biology → tool → latent, same tactic, three substrates is the load-bearing observation; the etymology is at most a partial, hedged ornament on it — the way a nucleomorph (cont 25 §7) is a *suggestive* parallel to a preserved engulfed-parent lineage, not a proof. This is one observation, not a law; its rhetorical cleanliness is precisely why the next subsection holds it suspect.

> *test-hook:* is the esca→lure→phishing chain a true *deliberate* port at the human→latent step, or is "phishing" just a catchy after-the-fact name for an exploit humans would have found anyway? Distinguishing real porting from convergent re-discovery is the corpus's hardest coding decision (C3.3.4). And separately: does the *spelling* trace survive scrutiny, or is it post-hoc folk-etymology (the band-Phish and `<><` accounts suggest caution)?

### C3.3.2 — Other candidate transpositions (the corpus seeds) `[Tier 3]` `[grounding: cont 31 §2 mimicry/hijacker vocabulary; cont 31 §4.3 dated internet arms-race milestones]`

If transposition is general rather than a one-lineage fluke, the corpus should fill out. Candidate rows, tagged by how clean the cross-plane port looks (all `[Tier 3]`, all needing corpus verification):

### C3.3.3 — Why this is the C1.3 acceleration mechanism (three-regime speed claim) `[Tier 3]` `[grounding: C1.3 the climb accelerates; cont 31 §2.3 Red Queen / §2.4 niche construction; Goodfellow et al. 2014 adversarial examples as a dated latent milestone]`

The proposed structure of the acceleration (C1.3) is a shift in *how niches get filled*, across three regimes:

1. **Blind coevolution (evolutionary time).** The exploit is discovered by selection; the beneficiary need not understand it. Niche-fill latency = many generations. (Anglerfish, *Photuris*.)
2. **Deliberate copying (cultural time).** A cognitive actor *recognizes* the tactic as portable and re-instantiates it on a new plane on purpose. Niche-fill latency = a learning/diffusion cycle, not a speciation. (Fishing lure; the sting operation.)
3. **Software-speed iteration (computational time).** The tactic is encoded, versioned, and re-deployed at release cadence; increasingly the *porting itself* can be automated (an AI system instantiating a known attack class against a new target). Niche-fill latency = a deploy cycle. (Phishing kits; automated adversarial-example generation, cf. Goodfellow et al. 2014.)

Transposition is the proposed bridge between regimes 1→2 (cognition lets a tactic jump planes without waiting for selection) and 2→3 (encoding lets the jump happen at machine cadence). This *reframes* the framework's existing arms-race vocabulary rather than adding to it: cont 31 §2.3's Red Queen says *defenses breed next-generation attacks on the same plane*; transposition adds that **a defense on one plane can breed an attack on an adjacent plane** when an actor ports the structure across. It is also a special, *intentional* case of cont 31 §2.4 niche construction: the actor doesn't just adapt to a substrate's selection criteria, it imports a ready-made strategy that the substrate has not yet evolved a counter for — which is precisely the cont 31 §2.5 "invasive species without native predators" failure mode, now with a proposed *source*: the species was ported in, not grown in situ. `[Tier 3 — this is a structural re-reading of existing cont 31 vocabulary, not an empirical finding]`

Honest scope cut (per C0.2 beneficiary boundary): transposition is **substrate-neutral as to allegiance** — defenders transpose too (honeypots, sting operations, adversarial training as a *capability* per cont 31). What makes a *transposed* tactic adversarial is unchanged from C0.1/C0.2: a localized beneficiary captures fitness from the target plane's compiled order while externalizing the maintenance cost. Transposition names the *porting*; the beneficiary boundary still does the adversarial/neutral discrimination.

> *test-hook:* does niche-fill latency for *transposed* exploits sit measurably below latency for *natively-evolved* exploits on the same substrate? If transposed and native exploits fill niches at indistinguishable rates, transposition is not doing the acceleration work C1.3 assigns it. (This is the strongest falsifier for the whole sub-vector and folds into the §5 acceleration-measurement hook.)

### C3.3.4 — Honest anecdote-vs-law check (the discipline this vector owes) `[Tier 3]` `[fuzzy — explicitly fuzzy-to-current-understanding; grounding: cont 31 §8 mimicry-vocabulary-not-yet-formally-validated discipline; cont 27 §3 pruning trigger]`

The danger is plain: **one gorgeous lineage (esca→lure→phishing, with an etymology that *appears* to perform the thesis) can carry far more rhetorical weight than its evidentiary weight** — and, per C3.3.1, even the etymology is hedged in the source record, not the clean fossil it first looks like. Three specific ways this sub-vector could be overfitting, stated so a corpus can kill them:

- **Selection effect.** Transpositions that left a linguistic or historical trace (phishing, honeypot, Trojan horse) are *findable*; the vastly larger set of exploits that were independently re-discovered with no porting leaves no such trace and is invisible to a casual corpus. A corpus built by searching for *named* lineages will manufacture a law out of a sampling artefact.
- **Port vs convergence conflation.** The framework already holds *blind* cross-substrate convergence (cont 25 §7) as a real, *independent*-reinvention phenomenon. Transposition requires *dependent copying* — an actor who held the abstraction and moved it. Many apparent "lineages" may be convergence wearing a borrowed name after the fact (C3.3.1's test-hook). Without attribution (who ported, when, from what), a row cannot be scored as transposition rather than convergence.
- **Metaphor inflation.** "Camouflage," "arms race," "Trojan horse," "viral" are such pervasive metaphors that almost *any* network tactic can be narrated as a biological port. The corpus needs a coding rule that rejects rows where the only evidence of porting is that the metaphor is available. (The phishing *spelling* trace is itself a candidate victim of this rule — the band-Phish and `<><` accounts mean the "spelling ports the lineage" reading may be folk-etymology, even though the concept-port is solid.)

Proposed corpus discipline (deferred to §5, per cont 27 §3): each row scored on (a) **attribution** — is there a documented actor/date for the cross-plane port? (b) **structural identity** — same exploited canon-function on both planes, not just surface resemblance? (c) **non-convergence** — evidence the new instance *descended from* the old (copying) rather than re-arising independently? A row counts as transposition only if all three hold; rows failing (a) or (c) are reclassified as convergence (cont 25 §7) or coincidence, not transposition. If, after this filter, the corpus collapses to a few charismatic lineages, the honest conclusion is **transposition is a real but rare/anecdotal mechanism, not a general law** — and C1.3's reliance on it weakens accordingly. The vector is built to lose this bet cleanly if the data say so.

> *test-hook:* run the three-criterion filter over the C3.3.2 seed rows + a *blind* sample of documented exploits (sampled without searching for lineages, to defeat the selection effect); report the surviving fraction. A high survival fraction across the blind sample supports generality; collapse to the keystone lineage supports anecdote. Either outcome is reportable; neither is compiled here.

## §7 — Gap-reframes (Pav, 2026-06-06): generational, priming, and the view-asymmetry thread

**Framing.** `[Tier 3]` Two of §6.3's "honest gaps" (generational strategies; priming) were gaps only under the *literature's* framing. Pav's reframes relocate each to a **cross-plane / observer-asymmetry** reading where real footholds — already surfaced and citation-verified in §6 — apply. Held Tier-3; nothing compiled; the unifying thread (§7.3) is flagged as the §4.A closure-via-multiplication hazard.

### §7.1 — Generational = observer-based shaping across the target's generations (domestication). `[Tier 3]` `[grounding: Pav 2026-06-06; C0.2 beneficiary boundary; C3.2 symbiosis-genesis; §4.B observer-gradient; §6 footholds: Belyaev fox selection, Henrich CREDs, cultural niche construction, Intergenerational Goods Game]`

The §6.3 gap was "no foothold for *deliberate multi-generational peer strategy*." That framing missed the giant: a **generational strategy is what an observer-class runs when its effective time-horizon spans many of the target's generations.** The canonical case is **domestication / artificial selection** — a breeder works on a horizon spanning thousands of the target's generations, with a view the target cannot access; the population is reshaped (Belyaev's farm-fox selection → tameness plus the domestication syndrome) with the breeder as beneficiary. The target has no strategy and no awareness; it is *shaped*.

- **It is an observer time-horizon asymmetry** → maps onto the §4.B observer-gradient: the bigger the horizon-gap, the more generational leverage. It extends up the ladder — platforms shaping user *cohorts*, states a populace, institutions their members; Henrich's **CREDs** are an institution shaping belief-commitment across human generations.
- **The beneficiary boundary (C0.2) sorts it:** domestication is *mutualism* when the species wins (dogs, wheat — evolutionary victors) and *adversarial* when the individual's welfare is externalized (factory farming). So domestication is the **same beneficiary-sorted wrapper-overlap dynamic that *underlies* C3.2**, run at generational scale — the same breeder↔target relation, either branch, sorted by who-pockets-the-differential-at-whose-cost. (Whether any given domestication reaches C3.2's *emerged-W_C* branch proper — a new joint action space with an identifiable `stub°` — is a separate, stricter question deferred to the §3.2.4 census; the claim here is only the beneficiary-sorted relation, not that domestication mints a third entity.)

> *test-hook:* does the beneficiary boundary cleanly separate domestication-as-mutualism from domestication-as-exploitation on documented cases (commensal dog vs broiler chicken) using only "who pockets the fitness differential at whose cost"? And does "generational leverage ∝ horizon-gap" predict which observer-classes can run generational strategies on which targets (and fail to, when the gap is small)?

### §7.2 — Priming = scene-setting / sequencing (the setup), not the subliminal cue. `[Tier 3]` `[grounding: Pav 2026-06-06; §4.B.1 classification-injection point; §6 footholds: Cialdini Pre-Suasion 2016, Bayesian persuasion (Kamenica & Gentzkow 2011), combinatorial game theory]`

The §6.3 gap was anchored to the *discredited* social-priming literature (subliminal cue → behaviour; replication crisis). But Pav's priming is **the setup** — arranging a *sequence* of events/moves that constrains the target's future option-space so a later payload lands. This sidesteps the discredited literature entirely and has real footholds:

- **Cialdini's *Pre-Suasion* (2016)** is literally "arranging for people to be receptive *before* the message" — scene-setting, named.
- **Bayesian persuasion** (Kamenica & Gentzkow 2011) is the formal version: commit to an information structure that shapes the receiver's posterior *before* they choose.
- **combinatorial game theory / chess-go** (Pav's examples) — *tempo, initiative (sente), forcing sequences, building a moyo, zugzwang, prophylaxis* — formal theories of arranging moves to constrain an opponent's option-space; plus **hypnotic induction** (a staged sequence building a suggestible state) and military "shaping operations."
- **Structurally, priming is the artefact-viroid's classification-injection point (§4.B.1) run over a sequence:** not one injected classification but a *series* that funnels the target, who sees each move but not the setup.

> *test-hook:* does "priming as option-space constraint over a sequence" generate predictions the one-shot manipulation vocabulary does not — e.g. that a payload's install-threshold drops measurably as a function of the setup-sequence's length/coherence, and that forcing-sequence structure (sente/zugzwang) transfers from games to persuasion corpora? (Cleanly distinct from the replication-crisis subliminal-cue claim, which the framework disowns.)

### §7.3 — The thread under both: view-asymmetry → the deceivability gradient carries *registers*. `[Tier 3]` `[SURFACED — §4.A closure-via-multiplication hazard class; NOT compiled]`

Both reframes are the adversarial function exploiting an **asymmetry of view** — the strategist operates in a dimension the target cannot fully perceive. Generational exploits **deep time** (you do not see you are being bred); scene-setting exploits **the whole sequence** (you do not see you are being set up). That suggests the deceivability gradient (C2.2) — while still **one axis in the §2.2.3 sense** (deception layered over exploitation) — may be **internally differentiated into distinct registers** (sub-modes of mode-(ii) deception; called "registers" to avoid colliding with §2.2's mode-(i)/mode-(ii) exploitation-vs-deception split) according to *what the target cannot perceive*:
- **fact-deception** — corrupt the target's read of *what is* (the C2.2 default);
- **time-deception** — the target cannot see across its own generations (§7.1);
- **sequence-deception** — the target sees the move, not the setup (§7.2).

**Closure-check:** this is an attractive unification, therefore exactly the §4.A hazard the framework's audits flag — it multiplies structure to feel explanatory. Held strictly as an open conjecture; **NOT compiled** as a new primitive or convergence, and routed to §4.A alongside the other "two-might-be-one" channels for Cowork+Pav deliberation. The honest test is whether the **registers** earn their keep — predicting something the undifferentiated gradient does not — or fold back into "deception of whatever the observer's adaptive processing models, including its model of time and of sequence."

## §8 — Quantification & reinvention check (scout-v2, 2026-06-06)

**Framing.** `[Tier 3]` A second web-grounded scout (espionage · psy-ops · mathematics · full-picture/grand-synthesis; 8 agents, citation-verified, zero fabrications) was aimed at two questions: **Q1** — has anyone *quantised* the adversarial function with a substrate-general formalism? **Q2** — has anyone *laid out the full picture*? ### §8.1 — Q1 (quantise): piecewise yes; substrate-general *of the adversarial function*, no.
- **A formalism that travels DOES exist — but for *selection*, not adversariality.** The Price equation / replicator-mutator class (Price 1970; Page & Nowak 2002) is proven substrate-neutral (genes / memes / firms / policies), and replicator dynamics is Lotka-Volterra-equivalent. But it quantifies *who-wins-under-frequency-dependent-payoffs*, with **no beneficiary or cost-externalization primitive** — the chassis the adversarial function rides, not the function.
- **The adversarial-specific formalisms are each substrate-bound,** travelling only by re-instantiation (which *is* C2.1, and which the math literature already does without calling it agnostic): quasispecies/error-threshold (molecular parasite), ESS/replicator (defector), adaptive-dynamics canonical equation (Red Queen), signalling games (deception), Price of Anarchy (free-rider cost), differential games / H∞ / GAN min-max (zero-sum conflict), info-theoretic deception (channel/MI).

"Externalizes cost" (C0.1) may be a family resemblance over **two distinct formal objects** — the active/passive seam is real, not cosmetic.
- **The beneficiary boundary (C0.2) has NO formal home.** No surveyed formalism encodes "a localized beneficiary pockets the differential by externalizing a maintenance-cost." This is at once the framework's **biggest formal-novelty opportunity** and its **biggest unsupported-by-prior-math exposure.** Candidate routes: express C0.1/C0.2 as a payoff/cost structure *inside* replicator dynamics; adopt **Ashby's requisite variety** (V(controller) ≥ V(disturbance)) as a substrate-general *defense* bound (symmetric, exploitation-silent — a bound, not the function); **Friston's free-energy** as observer/deceivability math (§4.B) — but it models the victim-observer and is **adversarially silent**, so a research direction, not a foothold.
