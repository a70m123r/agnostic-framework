## Headline

The "synergy gate" asks whether two parents A,B make an emergent THIRD M that is **more than a separable** `f(A)+g(B)`. Done right that is the **functional-ANOVA / GAM 2-way interaction** — a **standard, PID-adjacent measure, not a novel discovery**. Across four candidates the soundest gate is **held-out predictive gain with a jointly-fit (backfit) separable baseline — "gain_v2"**: `R2_oos(joint) − R2_oos(additive-fit-by-joint-LS)`. It is the only candidate that simultaneously (a) floors the confirmed `A²+B²` false-positive, (b) does **not** count irreducible noise as interaction, and (c) does **not** false-flag pure-additive merges of **correlated** parents — the failure mode that would otherwise flag essentially every real model-merge. Two candidates are honest negatives reported straight (co-information; the kernel/dCor layer). Separately, the framework's **observer-kernel / contextual-scaling frame-relativity is REAL and principled** — the SAME `M=A*B` genuinely flips interactive→additive when you change **which range of the relevant parent is in frame** (locality of nonlinearity), cleanly distinct from the killed grid/quantization artifact — but it validates the framework as a **precise reading of known statistics (effect modification / conditional-vs-marginal effects), not a newly discovered phenomenon**.

## (a) MENU of candidate emergence-functions — RANKED

Calibration target throughout: **HIGH** on INT (`A*B`) and XOR (`sign(A)*sign(B)`); **~0** on ADD (`0.5A+0.5B`) and the critical floor SEP (`A²+B²`, which the buggy affine-residual gate FALSE-POSITIVED at 1,040,352 bits — above INT).

### Rank 1 — Held-out predictive gain, gain_v2 (joint-LS / backfit separable baseline) — **ADOPT**
`gain = R2_oos(joint 2D-cell mean) − R2_oos(additive)`, 5-fold CV over the 65536 iid triples; the additive baseline fit by **joint ridge-stabilised least squares** on `[1 | one-hot(A-bins) | one-hot(B-bins)]` (the piecewise-constant GAM = backfit to convergence). Modules: `cand_predictive_gain.py` (v1 protocol) + `review2_probe6_fix.py` (`predictive_gain_v2`, the baseline-fitter repair).

- **Calibration (v1, independent parents, bins=8):** INT **+0.89224**, XOR **+0.98220** (binary log-loss gain +0.6713), ADD **+0.00005**, SEP **−0.00034**. Wider: SYN +0.29631, ALLOY **+0.01751**, max(A,B) +0.13960; all separable falsifiers floored (A³+B³ −0.00036, |A|+|B| −0.00007, sin(A)+cos(B) +0.00002). HIGH vs ~0 separate by **>300×**.
- **Misspecification stress (gain vs bins 4/8/12/16/24):** SEP `A²+B²` = −0.00015/−0.00034/−0.00032/−0.00068/**−0.00112** — pinned ~0, drifting slightly **negative** (the held-out-penalty signature, NOT growing); INT climbs 0.737→0.974; XOR flat ~0.983. Held-out gain is not fooled by main-effect curvature — the opposite of the in-sample coarse-resolution failure in `ratio_frame_test.py` (where ADD→0.51, SEP→0.88 blew up).
- **Why it is rank 1 (two failure modes its siblings miss):**
  - **Noise (probe P3):** on `M=0.5A+0.5B+0.5*noise` (zero interaction) gain reads **−0.00039** — correct floor — while the GAM frac reads **0.338 FALSE-HIGH** (the GAM scalar `1−separable-R2` counts noise + anything-unexplained as "interaction", and its knot-sweep cannot rescue it: noise is flat-in-knots, same signature as genuine interaction).
  - **Correlated parents (probe P4 → fix P6):** v1's marginal-means additive baseline false-flags pure-additive correlated merges — `0.5A+0.5Bc` reads gain **+0.73335** at r=0.9 and **+0.90116** at r=0.99. **gain_v2 fixes it:** ADDc → **+0.00299** (r=0.9), **+0.00101** (r=0.99); SEPc → +0.02285/+0.00804; and v2 **reproduces every independent-parent number** (INT +0.89224, XOR +0.98220, ADD −0.00004, SEP −0.00040, ALLOY +0.01742, NOISY-ADD −0.00045). The hole was in the **baseline fitter, not the measure's design**.
- **PROS:** held-out CV penalizes a joint model that overfits separable curvature/noise → SEP floors and *stays* floored; interpretable units ("fraction of M's out-of-sample variance only the joint structure explains") + native log-loss variant for discrete targets; symmetric monotone-invariant binning; separates ALLOY's true small interaction (0.0175) from the floor by ~40×; generalizes to a real merge substrate by swapping the numpy plug-in for GBT-vs-GAM under the same CV protocol.
- **CONS / honest limits:** (1) **NOT novel** — held-out nested-model (joint vs additive-GAM) CV comparison, functional-ANOVA/PID-adjacent; it formalizes "earns its keep on unseen data", it does not transcend the standard toolkit. (2) Joint 2D-cell model is curse-of-dimensionality-bound at fine bins (cells thin → backoff to additive), which is why pure `A*B` climbs gradually (0.74→0.97) rather than reading ~1 immediately. (3) **No calibrated significance threshold** — the 0.05 flag-cut is ad hoc; it would floor ALLOY (0.0175), and at n~1000 the held-out noise floor is ~±0.03 (P2: SEP at h=0.15 reads −0.030). Verdicts near the cut need a **permutation null or fold-spread n-aware error bar**. (4) It is a **conditional-MEAN** gate — variance-only/distributional interaction is a blind spot (see Rank 2 con + Scope). (5) Controlled ground-truth only; the real model-merge run remains owed (and must wait for gain_v2 — v1 would false-flag everything at high parent correlation).

### Rank 2 — GAM / functional-ANOVA interaction fraction (held-out, flexible-spline separable) — **keep as the misspecification DIAGNOSTIC, not the standalone scalar**
`frac = Var_test(M − [ghat_A(A)+ghat_B(B)]) / Var_test(M)`; each parent gets an independent cubic B-spline (8 interior quantile knots), ridge-stabilised LS on a 50/50 TRAIN split, scored on the disjoint HELD-OUT split. Module: `cand_gam_anova.py`.

- **Calibration:** INT **1.0066**, XOR **1.0008**, ADD **0.0016**, SEP **0.0153**, SYN 0.3351, ALLOY 0.0211, ROT 0.0018, COPY 0.0022. Clean gap: every separable/affine case ≤0.015; every joint case ≥1.00.
- **Misspecification sweep (frac vs knots 0/2/4/8/16/24):** SEP `A²+B²` = 0.071/0.025/0.020/0.015/0.011/**0.009** — monotonically **DECREASES** (more flexibility *resolves* the separable quadratic mains, does not manufacture interaction); ADD/ROT/COPY pinned ~0; INT/XOR HIGH and flat; SYN flat at 0.335. This **directional** sweep is the diagnostic value: a true-floor case decreases, an under-resolved main creeps UP.
- **PROS:** directly implements the cross-model-recommended fix (flexible + held-out separable model) and floors the affine-gate false-positive by construction (a per-parent spline absorbs ANY separable main); standard/well-understood; clean numeric gap.
- **CONS (why it is demoted below gain_v2):** (1) **Counts ANY unexplained variance as interaction** — noisy-additive reads **0.338 false-HIGH** (P3), and the knot-sweep cannot detect it (noise flat-in-knots). On real substrates with irreducible residual it systematically **over-reads emergence**. (2) **Cannot separate ALLOY's true small interaction (0.021) from SEP's finite-knot leak (0.015)** — overlapping floor band (predictive gain separates them 40×). (3) Confound BOUNDED not ERASED — read **with** its sweep, never as a standalone scalar. (4) Reads **>1.0** (not a true fraction — held-out noise on ~0-R2 separable models). (5) Says nothing about observer-kernel frame-relativity by itself.
- **Recommended role:** report `mainR2 = 1−frac` alongside gain_v2 so **"low gain + low mainR2" reads as "unexplained but not joint"** — the read that distinguishes noise from interaction.

### Rank 4 — Co-information / interaction-information (McGill 1954; Bell), in bits — **CORRECTLY DEAD (well-documented honest negative)**
`II_synergy = I(M;A,B) − I(M;A) − I(M;B)`, binned plug-in over the 65536 triples. Module: `cand_coinformation.py`.

- **Calibration (bins=16):** INT **+2.1576**, XOR **+0.9733** (correct, textbook ~1-bit synergy), ADD **+2.1475** (should be ~0 — reads higher than every interactive case bar INT), SEP **+1.0973** (should be ~0). **Separation margin (min HIGH − max FLOOR) = −1.1742 → DOES NOT SEPARATE.**
- **DECISIVE: the FLOOR failure is STRUCTURAL, not finite-sample bias.** Stability-in-N (bins=16): II(ADD) = +2.1475 (65k) / +2.1504 (1M) / **+2.1513 (16M)**; II(INT) = +2.1576/+2.1690/+2.1790. ADD and SEP do **not** shrink toward 0 as N→16M, and ADD ≈ INT. Co-information measures **joint determination** (each parent alone underdetermines M, both pin it), which is synergistic in Shannon terms for a **SUM as much as a PRODUCT**.
- **PRO (real, narrow):** genuinely **sidesteps the main-effect-misspecification confound** — fits/subtracts NO parametric model, so SEP's `A²` curvature cannot leak in as fake interaction (MI is invariant to single-variable reparam). **CONS:** fails the calibration target structurally; documented synergy/redundancy confound (=0 when they cancel — why PID was invented); binning-dependent absolute value; cannot floor ADD without subtracting an additive surrogate, which **collapses it back into the GAM-bits machinery**. NET: a correct measure of multivariate information *balance*, the **wrong tool** for the additive-vs-interactive (non-separability) gate. It is, however, the **complementary** tool for the variance-only/distributional blind spot of the mean-gates (see Scope) — not a rival there.

**Ranked summary:** `gain_v2 (1) > GAM-frac as diagnostic (2) > kernel 1−R2 route (3, duplicate) > co-information (4, structurally dead)`.

## (b) Contextual (observer-kernel) frame-relativity — VERDICT: **REAL-AND-PRINCIPLED** (with two honest deflations)

Module: `contextual_frame_test.py` · report `CONTEXTUAL_FRAME_TEST.md`. **Distinction that fixes the earlier artifact:** ZOOM = change RESOLUTION within a fixed frame (the grid-LOD sweep — **confirmed a quantization artifact**, settled). CONTEXTUAL SCALING = adjust the FRAME ITSELF (the relevance / context / depth cutoff — a DIAL, not a grid step). Phase-2 tests whether the emergence verdict GENUINELY changes when you change the **context/frame**, never the grid.

**Result (real numbers, same system `M=A*B`; gain = held-out R2[joint]−R2[additive]; flag≥0.05):**

- **OP(I) off-center band a0=2 — VERDICT FLIPS:** h=3.0 (A-range [−1.0,+4.4]) gain **0.7984** INTERACTIVE → h=0.6 **0.0254** → h=0.15 (A-range [+1.85,+2.15]) **0.0025** ADDITIVE. GAM frac flips identically (0.888→0.034→0.006). **Two orders of magnitude, both measures agree.**
- **OP(I) center band a0=0 — correctly does NOT flip:** gain stays **0.90–0.93** at every band incl. h=0.15 (no local linear term exists at a0=0; the measure is not fooled, exactly as the principle predicts).
- **Anti-artifact discriminators (these are where the evidence lives):**
  - **equal-n (n=1010 fixed across all bands):** still **0.757 → 0.0025** — same n, opposite verdict → NOT sample size.
  - **IRRELEVANT-AXIS Z (same n-reduction 65k→7.9k):** gain pinned **0.892→0.886** (no flip). Matched-n head-to-head at n=29620: narrow-Z **0.892** vs narrow-A **0.799** — only narrowing the RELEVANT axis's RANGE moves it.
  - **Taylor curvature law:** measured decline tracks `Var(d)/(a0²+Var(d))` — a power collapse cannot follow a curvature law.
- **Second system XOR:** seam A=0 in frame (a0=0) stays **0.98** INTERACTIVE; seam pushed out (a0=2) floors **0.87→0.0003** ADDITIVE (sign(A)→const → M=sign(B), additive). Same locality-of-nonlinearity law.
- **OP(II) context variable C** (`M=A*C`): OUT-of-context R2[A]=**−0.0002** (looks irreducible) → IN-context R2[A,C]=**+0.8920** (fully explained). Flip = +0.8921, driven by what variable is in scope.

**Why REAL-AND-PRINCIPLED, not artifact:** the SAME `M=A*B` flips interactive(0.80)→additive(0.0025) **purely by changing which RANGE of the relevant parent is in frame** — locality of nonlinearity (a product is interactive globally, near-linear locally near a0≠0 because `a0*B` dominates the cross term `d*B`). Mechanistically distinct from the grid artifact on three independent counts: survives equal-n; **absent when an irrelevant axis is narrowed by the identical n-reduction**; the decline follows a first-principles curvature law a power collapse cannot. It is also **selective and correct** (does NOT flip at a0=0; XOR flips in the predicted seam-in/out direction). The grid sweep, by contrast, is verdict-stable for a fixed frame and only degrades under coarse-bin underpower.

**Net verdict:** observer-kernel frame-relativity is **real, principled, theorem-backed, quantitatively predicted, ONE-DIRECTIONAL (range-frames can hide interaction, never manufacture it; variable-scope frames can reveal), and cleanly distinct from the quantization artifact** — but it validates the framework as a **precise, falsifiable reading of known statistics, not as a newly discovered phenomenon.** (If forced onto the 3-way scale this is "real-and-principled" with the explicit deflation that "principled reading of standard statistics" ≠ "new phenomenon"; a reviewer who weights novelty would read the same evidence as "partly".)
