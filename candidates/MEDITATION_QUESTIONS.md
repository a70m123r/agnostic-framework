# MEDITATION — what other questions should we be asking?

*A reflective pass across the whole program, merging the five meditation lenses (INSTRUMENT, FRAMEWORK, OLYMPICS, DATA, FALSIFICATION) with the gaps the Opus review named that no lens had asked. Ranked top 12, each with why-it-matters + status, then grouped by lens for navigation.*

> **Tier-3 footer up front:** none of this promotes anything. The convergence list **stays 9**. These are *questions*, not claims; the viewer is a disclosed-proxy **illustrator**, not yet an instrument; the substrate is a 2026-06-10 winners'-encyclopedia snapshot. Read every "status" as "what it would take to make this falsifiable," not "what we believe."

## The three we would bet on first

1. **Run the owed real-merge gain_v2 pilot — and name the KILL threshold before the data.** Everything load-bearing routes through one number that has been *measured exactly once* (the frame-lock pilot: 2 hits / 3 misses on known answers) and *never on a real two-parent model merge*. Until gain_v2 beats plain PID and Uzzi at separating a genuine weld from an additive blend — **and** a disconfirming result is pre-committed to kill the *claim* rather than refine the estimator a fourth time — the synergy gate is vocabulary and the convergence-9, the latent-cosmology, and "parents produce W_C" all rest on a borrowed, undelivered metric. Cheap, pre-registerable now.

2. **Test whether "32.7% corroborated" is triangulation or an echo.** The substrate is 76.6% English Wikipedia, ~84% Wikipedia+Wikidata+Britannica, and the "independent" verifier pool inherits *from* Wikipedia. Run the one test we never ran: of the 275 corroborations, how many survive requiring the second source to be **provenance-disjoint from Wikipedia's citation graph**, not merely a different domain? Until that number exists, every narration claim wears the costume of triangulated fact while possibly being single-source.

3. **Name one render that, if "wrong," changes a belief instead of fixing a data-entry bug — and give it a unit and an error bar.** This is the line between an instrument and an art piece. Right now size/opacity/glow are hand-weighted list-length proxies whose "ground truth" is the same field they're computed from, so no channel can be wrong. Pick the readout (a date? a proxy-mass? a ratio ± uncertainty?) that two observers scrubbing to 1905 would transcribe identically — or stop calling the outputs "findings." *(Foothold shipped this pass: a render-faithfulness linter + a version-controlled `PROXY_SPEC.md`; the missing piece is an external calibration target per channel.)*

These three are independent, buildable now, and none is currently answered. (1) is the deepest; (2) gates whether the data may speak at all; (3) decides what the whole apparatus is *for*.

## Ranked top 12

**1. The real-HF merge gain_v2 pilot + a pre-committed KILL threshold.** *(FALSIFICATION × FRAMEWORK — the two lenses' sharpest questions are the same leg.)*
Does gain_v2 beat PID and Uzzi on real `theta_A, theta_B, theta_M` at telling a weld from an additive blend, recovering all 5 called shots (SYN PASS, ADD/ROT FAIL, COPY NULL, ALLOY FAIL@r_top) across compressor siblings? And what numerical result makes us *abandon* parents-produce-W_C rather than refine the estimator again?
*Why it matters:* three operational refutations have each been absorbed as "the idea is sound, the operationalization was wrong" — the signature of an unfalsifiable core protected by an auxiliary belt. Binding a disconfirming result to the claim before the data is the whole ballgame.
*Status: needs-build (pilot) + needs-decision (kill threshold).*

**2. Provenance-DISJOINT corroboration test.** *(DATA)*
How many corroborations survive a second-source-disjoint-from-Wikipedia's-citation-graph requirement, vs the mere different-domain check we actually ran?
*Why it matters:* if "verified" mostly means "Wikipedia agreed with a Wikipedia mirror," the substrate's independence is an illusion that R6 narration and any future measurement inherit wholesale. This is the honest-proxy standard applied at the data layer.
*Status: needs-build.*

**3. Can the instrument return a reading its makers didn't put in?** *(INSTRUMENT)*
Name one render — a blob position, weld date, solidity opacity, narration segment — that, if it came out wrong, forces a belief change, not a data-entry fix. Define the output as a reproducible (value ± uncertainty) tuple.
*Why it matters:* a channel you can't calibrate against anything external is decoration. This meta-question forces proxies, certainty, and disputes to all become concrete.
*Status: needs-decision. (Partial: lint layer + versioned proxy weights now exist.)*

**4. Presenter-architecture fork.** *(OLYMPICS — sharpest)*
Precompiled-auditable segments, live LLM, or the honest middle (a retrieval-only constrained generator that can emit only substrate spans + fixed connective grammar) — and the fabrication-rate eval the middle requires?
*Why it matters:* this single fork decides whether the entire presenter/commentary/scouts program is a text-asset pipeline or an inference service, and it gates the trust contract, the scout definition, and narrator governance. The current build is precompiled — the safe choice; keep it until a fabrication eval exists.
*Status: needs-decision.*

**5. A falsification condition for each proxy channel.** *(INSTRUMENT × DATA)*
Size, solidity, surprise-glow, force-coupling are all list-length proxies with hand-tuned weights. What independent measurement, if it disagreed, would retune or retire each?
*Why it matters:* the proxy and its "ground truth" are currently the same field, so the channel can never be wrong — we will mistake the length of a Wikipedia list for the mass of an idea.
*Status: needs-decision. (Foothold: `PROXY_SPEC.md` now versions the weights so "retune or retire" has an artifact to act on.)*

**6. Should the viewer render the missing failed-weld base rate?** *(THE GAP NOBODY ASKED — Opus, ×DATA)*
The corpus is 7 hand-picked, success-selected welds. The bedrock "accumulation" and global-view swimlanes render them as a clean cumulative tree — so the *medium itself* manufactures the inevitability thesis regardless of substrate caveats. A documentary of 7 winners is a hagiography by construction. Should empty lanes / aborted smears / the failed-weld base rate be visually present?
*Why it matters:* no lens asked what the 7-specimen selection costs the *viewer specifically*. Survivorship rendered as structure is the substantive distortion, not a cosmetic one — and it is the one open item among the four gaps Opus named (the other three were closed this pass; see below).
*Status: needs-decision / needs-data (failed-weld coverage rate is near-zero today).*

**7. Convergence-list base-rate census.** *(FALSIFICATION)*
Every one of the 9 was surfaced *by searching for the pattern*. What is the hit-rate of three-force / parents-produce-W_C structure across a NEUTRAL sample not picked for it, and at what rate does the convergence stop being surprising (i.e. is generic to any two-parent process)?
*Why it matters:* a convergence claim assembled by hunting for named instances cannot distinguish a deep cross-substrate law from a pattern generic enough to find anywhere. The framework already demanded this census discipline of the adversarial-niche claim; it owes it to itself.
*Status: needs-build.*

**8. The trust contract: a (certainty, bucket) → register rule.** *(OLYMPICS × DATA)*
65.4% of best values are single-source "pending"; 22 carry disputed alternatives. What maps `(certainty, verification_status)` to `narrate-flat | hedge | omit | show-both`? Below what bucket may a fact speak in the presenter's voice rather than a "reportedly" register?
*Why it matters:* a fluent sentence built on a 0.3-certainty single source is the most dangerous output the system can produce, because fluency reads as authority. Without this rule the honest-proxy discipline does not extend to the presenter — its weakest-disclosure surface.
*Status: needs-decision.*

**9. A grounding linter for narration + a synthesis-level adversary.** *(FALSIFICATION × INSTRUMENT)*
Enforce "no new claims" *mechanically* (every asserted proposition resolves to a fact_id) and add an adversary that reads for smuggled causation — "A, then B" reads as "A caused B" even when every atom is sourced.
*Why it matters:* the propositional layer is citable; the editorial layer (which 3 of 1029 facts to voice, in what causal order) is not, and that is where fabrication-by-selection lives. "No new claims" is currently honor-system.
*Status: needs-build. (Foothold: the data-layer ref-resolution linter shipped this pass; the claim/causation adversary does not exist yet.)*

**10. Define "scout," the watchable unit, and the spectator.** *(OLYMPICS)*
Is a scout the existing harvest fleet (data-ops, already built) or a live scrub-time gap-filler with its own freshness/verification gate (a second async service that can introduce unverified data mid-experience)? Is the MVP the unblocked DOCUMENTARY (no submission surface, shippable now) or the governance-blocked public COMPETITION?
*Why it matters:* the v2 build is overwhelmingly the documentary, which has none of the §2.1 governance blockers because there is nothing to capture. Naming the watchable unit could un-stick the whole Olympics by shipping the unblocked product and shelving the blocked one.
*Status: needs-decision.*

**11. A forced-retirement rule.** *(FALSIFICATION)*
Has the program ever *killed* a load-bearing claim on the merits — not narrowed, not refined, not deferred-pending-a-2nd-instance, but retired? If not, adopt a rule: a Tier-2 candidate that fails its named promotion bar twice is demoted to illustrative, not held open indefinitely.
*Why it matters:* a program that only narrows, folds, and defers is asymptotically unfalsifiable regardless of how disciplined each local move looks. A pre-committed retirement rule is the cheapest falsifiability insurance and the strongest signal that Tier-3 honesty is real, not just well-narrated.
*Status: needs-decision.*

**12. Is observer-relative solidity more than credence — and are the two charges secretly one?** *(FRAMEWORK)*
What distinguishes observer-relative solidity from Bayesian credence / QBist probability (it is defined, not demonstrated, and the cross-model pass found epistemology analogs)? And was the cont-15 asymmetry ever reconciled with the cont-13 charge algebra, or is this a one-charge theory dressed as two?
*Why it matters:* solidity is the named new residue; if it evaporates into credence the contribution evaporates. The charge question is answerable now from existing notes and every charge reading rests on it.
*Status: needs-decision (solidity) / answerable-now (charge algebra).*
