## A. PER-DIMENSION VALIDATION — did D1–D5 hold up on real data?

For each dimension: the **verdict** (held / partly / needs-change), the **evidence from the three specimens**, and **what the cross-cut adds.** The single most important meta-finding, repeated below where it bites: the design law worked — every dimension that defaults empty on a sharp case (Maxwell) stayed empty there and only expanded where the record forced it.

### D1 — Weld lifecycle + type classification → **HELD, with a vocabulary + binary-coarseness change-request**

**Verdict: held as the structure (a graded phase-trajectory + a weighted weld-type index), needs-change in two specifics (the controlled vocabulary must extend; `revival.kind` is too coarse).**

**Evidence.**
- The **graded trajectory** (memberships, not an enum) rendered cleanly and richly: Manhattan = a **6-phase** ultra-compressed trajectory (complementarity 0.9 → conception 0.9 → a *short shallow* pre-consummation dormancy 0.6 → revival=same 0.85 → welding 1.0 → hardening 0.95 with `to:null` meaning *never-stops-mattering*); Keynesian = an **8-phase** trajectory (the richest), with *two* reinterpreted revivals; deep learning = an **8-phase** trajectory with a **double dormancy** (two winters) and **three** reinterpreted revivals. The phase-membership-as-the-soft-value (no separate confidence-on-a-weight) held — the hardening cut from the draft was correct.
- The **weighted weld_type** (a weld is several types at once) was genuinely multi-valued every time: Manhattan = 5 weighted (state/war-mobilized 0.95 + crisis-pulled 0.95 + mediator-welded 0.9 + instrument-enabled 0.85 + unifier 0.8); Keynesian = 7 weighted; deep learning = 6 weighted (led by *instrument-enabled* 0.85 and *state/funding-mobilized* 0.90). The 8-term **core closed vocabulary covered Manhattan with no new term needed** — the cleanest confirmation the closed set is well-sized for a consummated case.
- The **lifecycle-as-weighted-index discipline held** (the §6-item-1 hazard): all three used `lifecycle` as an index over `dormancy_intervals`/`revival`/`status_trajectory`/`weld_chain` and did **not** restate them. No specimen double-recorded the pre-weld antagonism strength (the §2.2.h fix held).

**What needs changing (returned by the data):**
1. **The controlled vocabulary needed extending three times** — and in all three the new term was **correctly proposed in a `discrepancies` row before use** (the §6-item-7 discipline *worked*): Keynesian proposed `foil-constructed-weld` (0.55) and `dormancy-revival-cycling-weld` (0.85); deep learning proposed `double-dormancy-revival-weld` (0.90). This is not a failure — it is the discipline behaving exactly as designed — but it means **the "core closed set" is closed only for consummated/sharp cases; multi-cycle and foil cases need ratified additions** (see v3 R17/R18).
2. **`revival.kind: same | reinterpreted` is too coarse.** Keynesian and deep learning both revive *multiple times*, each time waking a **method-incompatible** object under the inherited name (Keynesian: New-Keynesian DSGE, then MMT; deep learning: perceptron→backprop-net→deep-belief-net→deep-CNN, and the science-to-engineering reframing). **Cross-cut adds.** Reading the lifecycle across batch-1 + v2, the phase-trajectory now spans the full range from **QG's `never_born_frontier` (no consolidation phase at all)** through **Maxwell's collapsed single-consolidation phase**, **Darwin+Mendel's pluralist→hardened→contested**, to **deep learning's double-dormancy-triple-revival**. The trajectory shape is itself diagnostic — and the v2 cases prove it scales to multi-cycle without breaking, *provided the vocabulary and the revival-kind granularity grow*.

### D2 — Actors generalized beyond people → **HELD AND FORCED (earned first-class status emphatically; the strongest validation in the batch)**

**Verdict: held, and not merely held — *forced*. This is the dimension the data most clearly demanded. The bridge thesis (actors connect the physical and latent layers) is load-bearing, not decorative.**

**Evidence — actors-as-institutions earned their first-class status:**

The specimen needed **22 actors across 7 kinds** (individual, government, company, university, lab, institution, movement). The single fact that one *parent* decomposes into a dozen institution-actors of different kinds is the clinching argument for D2.
- **Deep learning is the strongest D2 case.** The latent lineage's *entire life* is driven by physical-layer institutions: **CIFAR (a foundation) literally carried the wrapper through a winter** (the clearest flame-keeper/dormancy-patron in the whole corpus); **DARPA (a government agency) was the chief squeeze** (Mansfield Amendment, the 1987 "deep and brutal" cut); **NVIDIA (a company) supplied the gating compute instrument** (CUDA 2007). 20 actors across individual/lab/government/institution/company.

- **The two role-relations both fired and both proved necessary.** `carrier_of` (champion-of, UP into latent) and `inhabitant_of` (operator-of, WITHIN) were exercised richly, including the cases that *only* a two-relation model can hold: **reflexive/consumed actors** — Oppenheimer *carries* bomb-integration UP and *inhabits* the MED that **later destroyed him in 1954**; Hicks *carries* (founds) IS-LM and later *inhabits-then-exits* the paradigm he founded (and disowns it); Hinton *carries* deep learning and *inhabits* CMU→Toronto→CIFAR→Google Brain. **Cross-cut adds.**

Batch-1's Maxwell, by contrast, was **almost pure individuals** with empty `carrier_of`/`inhabitant_of` — exactly the design-law default. ### D5 — Fuzzy edges by design → **HELD CLEANLY (did NOT get unwieldy; the design law did its job)**

**Verdict: held — graded membership was the pervasive default, a crisp boundary was the special case, and it did *not* become unwieldy. The design law (optional-default-empty) bounded the sharp-case cost to near zero, exactly as intended.**

**Evidence — fuzzy-by-default worked rather than bloating:**
- **The signature demo:** Manhattan's W_B "war-state" parent is **~0.6 as a single parent but ~0.95 decomposed** into institutions — the *same fact at two membership values*, which is precisely what graded-by-default is for. The weld extent then *fades* into a 0.5-confidence counterfactual root ("no bomb ever without the war"); the child *straddles* (physical 0.55 device / latent 0.85 nuclear order); a single forcing event carries two directional strengths (Nazi persecution 0.9 pull / 0.8 squeeze).
- **Keynesian** is graded along the *time axis*: the "classical" parent is a graded straddle between a real wrapper (0.88) and Keynes's caricature (0.6); "Keynesianism" is a graded family of four wrappers; `pre_weld_relationship` is itself a graded straddle (complementarity 0.6 / antagonism 0.4); every forcing event's influence is a graded strength. **A crisp boundary is nowhere the default.**
- **Deep learning** deliberately leaves the **dormancy-interval endpoints soft** (`~1995`/`~2006`) *because* Haigh disputes whether the winters had crisp edges at all — graded membership used as an honest representation of genuine historiographical fuzz, not as decoration.
- Mechanically verified: **zero graded values out of [0,1]** across all three JSONs (193 leaf scalars checked), and the two-bin `fuzzy_layer` is consistently presented as a *derived digest* of the per-node memberships (the §2.7 / §6-item-6 clarification held).

**Why it did NOT get unwieldy.** The fear (schema §6 self-flagged weakness) was that pervasive grading would bloat every case. It did not, because **the design law made grading free on the sharp case**: Maxwell (re-checked) still reads as a thin solid trunk with `certain_core`≈0.95 and a few frontier roots at ~0.7–0.9 — grading there is just "membership happens to be ~1.0." The grading only *costs* where the record is genuinely fuzzy (the war-state parent, the Keynesian time-drift, the deep-learning winter edges), which is exactly where you *want* to spend the bits. **D5 is the design law in its purest form** (the certain core is *defined* as membership≈1.0), and the v2 cases confirm it scales without becoming noise.

**Cross-cut adds — the `core_boundary_locus` thermometer now has three+ readings.** Batch-1 gave it two readings (Maxwell: core at the trunk; QG: core sinks *below* the child / hollow crown; Darwin+Mendel: fuzz climbs *into* the trunk at parent-identity). The v2 cases add: **Manhattan** = core spans the *whole* trunk, fuzz pushed *out* to a thin lateral sheath (the counterfactual root *beneath* + the moral valence *beside*) — the geometric *inverse* of QG; **Keynesian** = a genuinely new *third type*, **fuzz along the time axis** (solid at any instant, identity drifts *across* instants); **deep learning** = the fulcrum sits *high*, fuzz in two *lateral* pockets (the winter-narrative and the credit dispute) that never touch the trunk. So `core_boundary_locus` is confirmed as a real schema-level observable with at least **five** distinct geometries now attested.
