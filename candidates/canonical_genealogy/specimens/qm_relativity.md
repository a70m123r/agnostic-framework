### The surprise (synergy) — **conf 0.85**

The deepest surprise is **structural**: pushed together at S, the parents do not yield *one* third — they yield a **contradiction** that forces a choice about *which parent's deepest commitment to keep*, and each choice grows a *different* child the other camp rejects as the wrong question. Neither parent alone predicts this.

- **(i)** QM alone never predicts that quantizing the metric **destroys the background you quantize against** (the stage *is* the thing fluctuating) — the "problem of time" / loss of local observables. Reachable only from *both* parents jointly.
- **(ii)** GR alone never predicts black-hole entropy **S = A/4** — finite, and proportional to **area not volume** — a holographic, information-theoretic fact needing ħ ∧ G ∧ c together. The single hardest constraint and the richest seed (→ holography, AdS/CFT).
- **(iii)** *Covariant line, 1974 (Scherk-Schwarz & Yoneya):* a theory built for the **strong nuclear force** was found to **necessarily contain a graviton** in its closed-string spectrum — "string theory predicts gravity whether you want it or not." The response: shrink the string ~20 orders of magnitude and reinterpret the whole edifice as quantum gravity.
- **(iv)** *Canonical line (LQG), 1994-95 (Rovelli-Smolin):* **area and volume operators have discrete spectra** — space is granular at the Planck scale, with a smallest possible area — labelled by Penrose's spin networks (drawn ~1964 for an unrelated reason).

**Meta-surprise (the schema's lesson):** a genuine high-synergy seam can stay an **open conjecture for a human lifetime**, splitting into rival children instead of fusing into one. Synergy is real and enormous; the child is unborn. *(`surprise_confidence` 0.85 and `child confidence` 0.4 are deliberately decoupled — see gaps.)*

## Discrepancies carried into the record (all 7 — the real-record frontier)

1. **[framework-vs-record] RIVAL CHILDREN (the most valuable row).** Parents → *one* `W_C` in the schema, but the record has **two-plus mutually-rejecting candidate children** over the *same* parents and *same* S (string / LQG / causal sets / CDT / asymptotic safety / …). Not sub-welds of one pushout — they disagree about what the child *is*. **No schema slot exists** for "a weld with competing outputs and no winner." → caps child-confidence at ~0.4; raises the specimen's value as a schema stress-test.
2. **[contested-genealogy] Which parent dominates is the fault line.** Covariant/string = QFT-method on gravity (**W_B swallows W_A**, accused of not being background-independent); canonical/LQG = background-independence primary (**W_A swallows W_B**). SEP: "two camps almost certainly talking past each other." The *direction* of a symmetric two-parent weld is disputed — a variable the schema flattens.
3. **[priority-dispute] Origin of the field is multi-rooted.** Einstein 1916 / Klein 1927 / Rosenfeld early-1930s / the cube (Gamow-Ivanenko-Landau 1928) / Bronstein 1935-36 / "graviton" named ~1934. **No single founder** — exactly the fuzzy deep-root frontier the schema predicts.
4. **[dormancy-contested] Bronstein's neglect was EXTERNAL.** A root node went dormant because its author was **executed (NKVD, 1938)** — a *physical/political-frame* dormancy cause, not internal-to-the-idea. The schema's `why` should tag the frame.
5. **[date/attribution-conflict] The Wheeler-DeWitt name was unstable for ~20 years.** DeWitt called it the "Einstein-Schrödinger equation" (crediting Wheeler); Wheeler called it the "DeWitt equation"; settled only at the 1988 Osgood Hill conference. `who_called_it` is genuinely *time-dependent* and disputed among the originators themselves.
6. **[framework-vs-record] Is the weld even NEEDED?** A serious minority (Rosenfeld → Mattingly, vs Eppley-Hannah / Page-Geilker) holds gravity may couple to *classical* spacetime (**semiclassical gravity**). If so, **there is no child — `W_C` is a category error.** The falsifier-of-the-weld; the strongest form of the fuzzy frontier. → "is a third needed?" should be a first-class confidence.
7. **[whig-vs-revisionist] "Which child is winning" is era-relative.** String dominant by headcount since the mid-1980s (Witten 1995: "the mathematics of the next millennium will be dominated by string theory"); revisionist counter (Smolin / Woit 2006: unfalsifiable landscape, no SUSY at the LHC); Rovelli 2016: "the string planet is infinitely less arrogant than ten years ago." **Status on a frontier weld is observer- and era-relative** — why `frame_of_weld` here engages {meaning}.

## Schema gaps — where this specimen was hard to fill (the missing variables)

This is the deliverable's payload — the variables a settled case never reveals:

1. **STATUS ENUM GAP (#1).** None of {risen, active, dormant, resurrected, stale} fits a ~90-year-old high-synergy weld that *never produced a settled child*. The schema assumes welds **resolve**. Recommend a new value: **`open-conjecture` / `live-unconsummated-weld` / `perpetual-frontier`**.
2. **MISSING VARIABLE — RIVAL CHILDREN (biggest gap).** `weld` maps to *one* `W_C`; here one seam yields a **family** of mutually-rejecting candidates. Recommend **`{candidate_children[], unresolved:true, contest_axis}`** (contest axis = which-parent-dominates / background-dependent-vs-independent).
3. **`survived`/`dropped` are not single-valued.** Each rival child drops a *different* parent-feature. Needs a per-candidate **`{feature, kept_by[], dropped_by[]}`** matrix.
4. **Weld-necessity is itself contested.** No field for "some serious observers deny a third is needed." Recommend a first-class **`weld_necessity_confidence`**, not buried in discrepancies.
5. **Dormancy `why` needs a frame tag.** Three dormancies here span *knowledge-frame* (no-go theorems), *tool-orphaning*, and *physical/political-frame* (a purge). Recommend **`dormancy.why_frame`** — a lineage can be severed by physical-frame events (a death) as well as knowledge-frame ones.
6. **Surprise/synergy vs consolidation are decoupled.** The schema (and the Phase-1 DB's logic) ties high synergy to a *risen* child. QG shows synergy can be enormous while the child stays unborn — so **`surprise_confidence` (0.85) and `child confidence` (0.4) must diverge**. Make them explicit independent axes.
7. **`bits_note` is a principled null.** You cannot MDL-score a theory you don't have. Flag that **frontier specimens may have intrinsically-null bits** until/unless a child consolidates — a blank by necessity, not by laziness.
8. **Wikidata structured graph not cleanly harvested.** Only `Q2067074` (canonical QG) and `Q18344764` (problem of time) confirmed adjacent; the influence-graph backbone leans on SEP + Rovelli + Wikipedia. A real provenance gap to close.
9. **Extends-linkage gap.** QG is *not* an existing Phase-1 row, so `extends.phase1_class_name` is a **proposed** new row, not a pointer. The Phase-1 DB has no frontier/unconsummated-weld row type — this specimen argues for adding one.
