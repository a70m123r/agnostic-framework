The honest set adds **Wicksell / the Stockholm School** (an *independent near-identical content-parent* — Sweden arguably adopted demand-management first) and **Kahn's multiplier + Keynes's liquidity preference** as the **method/instrument welding-operator** (the active machinery, weight on `parent_kind: method`).

**Mediating welder (R1) — the population-genetics analogue:** the **IS-LM / neoclassical synthesis** (Hicks 1937 "Mr Keynes and the Classics"; Modigliani 1944; Samuelson 1948/55) sits a *layer up* from Keynes/Marshall/Malthus and is **what actually produced the durable taught child**. *Disowned-mediator wrinkle:* Hicks later **renounced** IS-LM, and post-Keynesians reject the synthesis as "bastard Keynesianism" — the mediator was repudiated by its own author and by a school.

**Shared sub-object `S`:** the **multiplier + liquidity-preference + IS-LM machinery** — the seam where "savings/investment/interest" (classical) and "deficient demand" (underconsumptionist) become **one income-determination system**. The precise seam: Keynes kept the classical identity `I(r)=S(Y)` but reinterpreted it so that **income, not the interest rate, adjusts** to equate saving and investment. **`S` is non-unitary and contested (R12, `agreed:false`):** the IS-LM formalization of `S` (Hicks) is rejected by post-Keynesians as a distortion of the *true* seam (fundamental uncertainty + liquidity preference) — the seam itself has a rift, exactly like Darwin+Mendel's Fisher-Wright-fractured `S`.

### The surprise (synergy) — **conf 0.90**

Neither parent alone predicts a **stable under-full-employment equilibrium.** Classical economics says the economy self-corrects (Say's law); underconsumptionism intuited shortfall but had no mechanism. Only the **weld** — *liquidity preference × marginal efficiency of capital × multiplier* — shows the economy can **settle and stay stuck** at involuntary unemployment.

- **Second-order:** the weld **invents macroeconomics** as a level distinct from summed markets, with **aggregate demand as a primary cause** and the **paradox of thrift** (individually-rational saving is collectively self-defeating — an emergent aggregate property absent in any agent, exactly a wrapper-level property not in the parts).
- **Deepest:** it makes the **state the residual demand-maker**, converting an economic theory into a permanent **reorganization of the 20th-century state** — a meaning-frame shift.

### Schema gaps (frontier of the MODEL, for ratification)

1. **NEW VARIABLE: strawman / reconstructed-parent.** Unlike all prior specimens, one parent (W_A "classical economics") was substantially *manufactured* by the welder (Keynes) as a foil rather than found pre-existing. The schema assumes parents pre-date the weld; it has no slot for "parent partly reconstructed/caricatured by W_C's author to be welded against." Rendered here via discrepancy #1 + the proposed `foil-constructed-weld` weld-type, but a first-class `parent.reconstructed_by_welder` flag is the cleaner home.
2. **`status_trajectory` needs DISCONTINUOUS re-identification.** This child does not just *harden* (Darwin+Mendel) — it *dies and reincarnates as a different object*, **twice**. The trajectory needs to mark identity *breaks*, not only state-relabeling.
3. **`revival.kind: same | reinterpreted` is too coarse.** Each of the two revivals wakes a *method-incompatible* object (New-Keynesian DSGE; MMT) under the inherited name. Needs a **degree-of-reinterpretation / method-continuity axis**, and ideally a per-revival `kind` (the field currently holds one).
4. **`candidate_children[]` assumes SIMULTANEITY.** Here the four mutually-critical "Keynesianisms" are real candidate-children *spread across time* (successive revivals), not held at once (QG). They are rendered as `harvest.descendants` + `feature_ledger` + `status_trajectory`, but a **diachronic-candidate-children** mode is not cleanly captured.
5. **EXOGENOUS-FORCING may deserve to be the PRIMARY timeline.** For a crisis-pulled wrapper, the external-event layer *is* the spine of the lifecycle (Depression→birth, WWII→consolidation, stagflation→dormancy, GFC/COVID→revivals), not a secondary "weather" overlay.
