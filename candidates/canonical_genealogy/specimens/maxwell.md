**The seam they fused along (S):** the empirically established two-way **electricity↔magnetism coupling** both traditions already accepted — Ørsted's current-deflects-compass (1820), Ampère's current-current force (1820s), and above all **Faraday's induction (1831)**. Faraday rendered it as "lines being cut"; the continentals as flux/force integrals. Maxwell fused along it by writing Faraday's lines *in* the continental calculus and **closing the loop with the displacement current**.

**The surprise (synergy):** **light itself falls out of the merge, at the measured speed of light** — an entire third science (optics) emerging unbidden from a synthesis built only out of electricity and magnetism. Neither parent contains it: Faraday's field had no velocity (his 1846 light-guess was explicitly speed-less "for want of data"); the continental force laws had no light at all. Only after Maxwell adds the **displacement current** (in neither parent) do the field equations close into a self-propagating transverse wave whose speed is the pure-laboratory ratio of EM-to-electrostatic units (Weber–Kohlrausch, ~3.1×10⁸ m/s) — which equalled Fizeau's measured light-speed. *A number got by weighing charges and currents on a bench turned out to **be** the speed of light.* And latent inside it, unseen by Maxwell, sat the frame-independence of *c* that would become special relativity. **(surprise confidence 0.96)**

## Discrepancies (the real-record frontier — SIGNAL, not noise)

1. **Priority on the light claim** *(priority-dispute).* Faraday's 1846 "Ray-Vibrations" already proposed light *is* a vibration of the lines of force; Maxwell himself called it "the same in substance" as his own theory "except that in 1846 there was no data to calculate the velocity." So is "light = EM" Maxwell's child or Faraday's? **Why it matters:** the surprise is *older than the weld* in qualitative form — it sat dormant as a speculation in one parent for ~15 years until the other parent (the math) made it calculable. Exactly the framework's "conjecture = a low-confidence weld drawn but not yet confirmed," living in the record. *Sources: Faraday 1846 + Maxwell's acknowledgement vs the popular "Maxwell discovered light is EM" narrative.* **Impact:** does not lower the surprise (0.96); relocates credit to W_A and time-stamps the conjecture.

2. **★ The second birth + misattribution** *(framework-vs-record-tension — HIGHEST VALUE).* "Maxwell's equations" as taught were **not written by Maxwell.** He left **20 coupled equations in 20 variables** (potentials/quaternions) in the 1873 *Treatise* — "fertile but half-finished." The compact **4-equation** vector theory was forged **1879–1894 by the Maxwellians** — Heaviside (vector recast, potentials eliminated, ~1884–85), Hertz (independent reformulation + 1887–88 detection), FitzGerald, Lodge. Heaviside (1893): if Maxwell would have accepted the changes, "then I think the resulting modified theory may well be called Maxwell's." They were even called *Hertz–Heaviside / Maxwell–Hertz* equations first. **Why it matters:** the clean two-parent→one-child diagram hides a whole **second merge event** — the raw *Treatise* W_C was itself a parent that, welded with vector analysis (Gibbs/Heaviside) + experimental confirmation (Hertz), produced the W_C we actually use — **and the canonical name attaches to the wrong node.** The missing schema variable: a child can be **re-welded and re-attributed**; "who it's named after" ≠ "who produced the surviving structure." *Sources: Hunt, The Maxwellians (1991); IEEE Spectrum; Wikipedia "History of Maxwell's equations" vs the textbook attribution.* **Impact:** splits the weld into two events the schema renders as one — the single most important variable-refinement here.

3. **Contested genealogy of the whole field** *(contested-genealogy).* Was the real lineage Faraday's **field/contiguous-action** tradition, or the **continental action-at-a-distance** tradition (Ampère, Gauss, Weber, Neumann, Riemann, Helmholtz)? Usually told as the field view's triumph, yet its mathematical bones are continental, and Weber's rival electrodynamics *also* predicted a characteristic velocity. **Why it matters:** the brief's "what were the real parents?" problem (the special-relativity Einstein-vs-Lorentz-Poincaré analogue). The weld succeeded partly by **laundering continental math into a field ontology** — the child kept W_A's *ontology* but W_B's *calculus*, so each losing parent can claim half. **Frame-relative:** in {knowledge} the continental math is the parent; in {space}/ontology Faraday's field is. *Sources: Wikipedia "History of EM theory" + Britannica + Hunt vs Whig "Faraday→Maxwell straight line" accounts.* **Impact:** the 0.95/0.90 parent split is a defensible projection, not a neutral fact.

4. **Date wobble on the load-bearing number** *(date/attribution-conflict).* The Weber–Kohlrausch unit-ratio is dated **1855 *and* 1856** across reputable sources (Wikipedia "History of Maxwell's equations": 1855, value 3.107×10⁸ m/s; other histories: 1856); Maxwell's *c*-realization is variously **late 1861 / 1862**; the papers spread 1855–56 / 1861–62 / 1864-read-1865-pub. **Why it matters:** the exact bench measurement that turned the qualitative light-guess into a quantitative weld has a wobbling date — a small fuzzy flicker on the *single most load-bearing number*, sitting right at the hinge of revival (A). **Impact:** negligible on substance; the precise date is a frontier item, not core.

5. **Multi-parent reality vs the 2-parent weld** *(framework-vs-record-tension).* The honest parent set is **at least seven** — Coulomb, Ørsted, Ampère, Faraday, Gauss, Neumann/Weber, Thomson/Kelvin. The residue (Thomson's analogy *method*, Ørsted's founding *observation*, Fizeau's calibration *target*) is exactly the "which variables is the schema missing" signal — there's no slot for a **method parent** or **instrument parent** distinct from a content parent (gap 2), and no **n-ary parents_full[]** (gap 3). *Sources: Wikipedia/Britannica multi-figure lineage vs the framework's binary W_A/W_B.* **Impact:** flags parents[]-length-2 as a lossy projection here.

6. **Frame-relativity of the weld** *(framework-vs-record-tension).* Two static facts ("electricity exists," "magnetism exists") side-by-side in {space} make **nothing**; only through **{time}** (changing fields — induction + displacement current, the d/dt terms) does the self-propagating wave appear. **Why it matters:** a textbook confirmation of the brief's frame-relativity thesis. `frame_of_weld = {time}` even though the child is read in {space, knowledge, meaning}; the displacement current is *literally* a time-derivative — **the weld is welded by time.** Makes Maxwell's EM a clean positive analogue to the rocks-in-time case in `parents_produce_WC_FORMALIZATION_DRAFT §2` (case 10). *Sources: observer_frame_kernels_DRAFT.md thesis vs a naïve static reading.* **Impact:** strengthens the render — confirms the schema's frame machinery discriminates correctly on a settled top-tier case.

## Schema gaps — fields that were hard to fill (the missing variables)

1. **★ No slot for a SECOND BIRTH / re-weld + re-attribution.** The biggest real-record fact here is that "Maxwell's equations" were produced by the Maxwellians ~20 years after Maxwell, and the canonical **name attaches to the originator of the raw form, not the producer of the surviving form.** The single `weld` + `revival{when,by,trigger}` can record the dormancy-resurrection, but **cannot cleanly express that the revived object is structurally different (4 eqns vs 20) AND mis-named.** *Proposal:* a `renaming`/`reattribution` field, or allow `weld` to **chain** (the W_C of birth-1 becomes a parent of birth-2). **This is the load-bearing missing variable.**

3. **`parents` length-2 is lossy here in a way worth a first-class affordance, not just a discrepancy row.** The honest parent set is ~7. SCHEMA.md flag 6 was followed (extras logged as `framework-vs-record-tension` discrepancies), but an **n-ary `parents_full[]`** alongside the 2-projection `parents` would keep BOTH the clean binary and the true multiplicity without forcing the truth into the discrepancy log.
4. **`frame_of_weld` does not express that the weld is welded BY a specific frame-operator.** Here the weld only fires through **time** (the displacement current is a d/dt term; static E+M make nothing). `frame_of_weld=[time]` captures the frame but not that **time is the *active* ingredient (the d/dt)** rather than a passive backdrop. *Proposal:* a `weld_operator` note-field ("time-derivative / induction-loop closure").
5. **Confidence on a SURPRISE that pre-existed as a conjecture in a parent.** Faraday's 1846 light-guess means the surprise was "drawn but not confirmed" for 15 years inside W_A — it has its own dormancy interval and priority. The schema treats surprise as a property of the child/weld only. *Proposal:* a `surprise_priority` / `surprise_first_conjectured_by` field so the fuzzy-frontier-in-the-record (a conjecture awaiting its confirming parent) is recorded where it belongs.
