# The Adversarial-Tactics Catalogue

**The ask⇄give loop, distilled.** Pav's hypothesis: the bestiary's 41 creatures are surface manifestations of a smaller set of **adversarial tactics** the two wrappers (observer ⟷ problem) evolve, borrow, and apply in their ask⇄give / give⇄ask loops. This catalogue distills + grounds + names them, tests the reduction, maps the couplings (the edges), and designs the prism/broadcast channels. Built by an Opus workflow (4 tactic-distillers + couplings + channels + critic + synthesis); cite-don't-coin; the subject matter of the [[THE_LATENT_CAMERA]].

**Date:** 2026-06-16 | **Status:** Tier-3 catalogue. Companion to [LATENT_DYNAMICS_BESTIARY.md](LATENT_DYNAMICS_BESTIARY.md).

## Headline

The reduction holds — but at the right level. Deduping the four lenses plus the critic's MISSING set yields 33 canonical adversarial tactics (from ~44 raw entries), a weak 1.24:1 compression because the bestiary was already authored near tactic-granularity; the strong claim is the food-web underneath: all 41 creatures AND all 33 tactics run on ~6 shared metabolisms (back-reaction loop, hysteresis/staleness, criticality, the two-clocks, the verified-dissolve gate, the motive axis), a ~6.8:1 reduction. The distilled tactic-space has a clean shape — a closed ASK<->GIVE loop with four faces (solver offense, problem defense, the loop's own coupling moves, and the meta-evolution layer that makes the move-set non-stationary) — and the residue that does NOT reduce to a move (Maintenance, observer-induced Shock, the frame-as-board geometry, calibration artifacts) is exactly the set of pure DYNAMICS that no party plays, which is the honest boundary of the catalogue.

## The reduction — do the creatures collapse to tactics?

- **41 creatures → 33 canonical tactics** (ratio 41 creatures -> ~6 underlying tactic-organs (back-reaction loop, hysteresis/staleness, criticality/phase-transition, the two-clocks, the verified-dissolve gate, the motive/drive axis); the 33 named adversarial moves are the SURFACE plays those 6 organs generate. So the reduction holds at TWO levels: 41 creatures compress to 33 deduped tactics (1.24:1, weak — the bestiary is already near-tactic-granular), but BOTH compress to ~6 shared metabolisms (41:6 ~ 6.8:1, strong). The strong reduction is the real claim; the tactic layer is the intermediate vocabulary.).
- **Irreducible residue** (dynamics that are NOT adversarial moves): clock/Maintenance — pure upkeep-cost-over-time (entropy you pay to STAY solved); a dynamic, not an adversarial move by either party. No ASK/GIVE role., phantom/Shock (observer-induced) — an endogenous off-frame event; the back-reaction LOOP that produces it is a coupling (The Mirror), but the shock itself is a consequence/dynamic, not a tactic anyone plays., clock/Fatigue & clock/Effort-decay (as STATES) — these are observer-resource dynamics that ENABLE tactics (Search abandonment) but are not themselves moves; they are the terrain the clock runs on. Reducible only as preconditions, not as plays., The 'frame' family as BOARD not MOVE — Red-Queen/Crowd/Referee/Prize-Field/Nesting are the arena geometry within which tactics are played; several reduce to meta-evolution tactics but their non-adversarial core (the scoring topology, the renormalization scale) is structural residue, not a move., Calibration/measurement artifacts (Confidence-calibration, Measurement-blind-spot) — observer-instrument properties the tactics EXPLOIT (Sandbag exploits miscalibration, Salami exploits blind-spots) but which are not adversarial moves themselves.

## The tactics

### SOLVER-SIDE — how the observer breaches

#### Probe / Query-at-the-cut (information-seeking, ask where it hurts most)  · conf 0.84
- **Move:** The observer spends a scarce query on the cut that most collapses its uncertainty about the kernel — choosing WHERE to look so each measurement maximally bisects the live hypothesis space.
- **Ask⇄give role:** Pure ASK-optimization. The observer asks the question whose expected answer shrinks its posterior the most; the problem GIVES one fact-wrapper's response. The elementary move of the loop — every other tactic is a policy over which probes to issue.
- **Evolves / borrows:** Evolves random raster -> uncertainty-targeted -> look-ahead/value-of-information. Borrows the acquisition function from Bayesian experimental design; an LLM agent borrows it as 'ask a clarifying question / call a tool'. Mutates by swapping the uncertainty functional (entropy vs margin vs expected-model-change) while the argmax-EIG skeleton is invariant. MERGED: lens-1 'Probe' + lens-3 'Query-at-the-cut' are the same per-step move-selection tactic.
- **Formal name:** Optimal experimental design / active-learning query strategy (uncertainty sampling, BALD, EPIG); Expected Information Gain; value of information  *(— active learning / Bayesian experimental design / decision theory)*
- **Cite:** Lindley 1956; Settles 2009 'Active Learning Literature Survey'; Houlsby et al. 2011 (BALD); Smith et al. 2023 (EPIG, arXiv:2304.08151); Chaloner & Verdinelli 1995
- **Observable:** Regress realized residue-drop (verified-dissolve bits) per probe against that probe's ex-ante predicted EIG; positive steep slope = query-at-the-cut, flat = blind raster. Compare questions-to-dissolve against the log2(hypotheses) floor.
- **Underlies creatures:** observer-state/Attention budget, observer-state/Drive-valence-gating, wrestlers/The pull (epistemic-value half), wrestlers/Probe-deforms-kernel, grip/Slick (a poorly-chosen cut yields no transfer)

#### Adversarial elicitation / interrogation (the ASK engineered to leak the GIVE)  · conf 0.7
- **Move:** The solver SHAPES its questions — reframing, coercive/jailbreak framings, 20-questions splitting — to maximize verified bits extracted from a guarded kernel per query, treating the ASK itself as the optimized weapon, not just budget spent.
- **Ask⇄give role:** Pure ASK-side offense: choose the next question to maximize expected verified-bits-extracted, including deceptive framings that get a guarded GIVE to leak.
- **Evolves / borrows:** Borrows interrogation tradecraft and Socratic elicitation; evolves active-inference epistemic action; transposes jailbreak reframing as the latent-substrate leading question. Spreads as reusable prompt-templates (one jailbreak forks across targets). DISTINCT-FROM-PROBE: Probe selects WHICH cut; this optimizes the FRAMING of the ask to defeat a defender, so it only earns its keep against a guarded/adversarial GIVE.
- **Formal name:** Adversarial elicitation / active querying; jailbreak prompt-crafting; informational interviewing  *(— adversarial-ML (red-team) / active inference / negotiation (interrogation))*
- **Cite:** MITRE ATLAS 'LLM Prompt Crafting' (2025); 'Game of Thought' arXiv:2602.01708; 'Teaching LMs to Gather Information Proactively' arXiv:2507.21389
- **Observable:** Bits-per-query sensitivity to PROBE-FRAMING at constant probe count: a guarded kernel that leaks far more per query under reframed/coercive ASKs than neutral ones. Hold budget fixed, compare naive vs adaptively-optimized probe sequence; the gap is the ASK-design value.
- **Underlies creatures:** observer-state/Attention-budget, wrestlers/The-pull, wrestlers/Probe-deforms-kernel

#### Decompose (divide-and-conquer)  · conf 0.82
- **Move:** The observer cuts a hard kernel along its weakest seam into near-independent sub-kernels each below its instant-crush threshold, solves them separately, and recomposes — converting one tall barrier into several short ones.
- **Ask⇄give role:** ASK restructured into a tree of sub-ASKs whose GIVENS compose back to the answer. Counters gatekeeping by refusing to engage the kernel as one lump.
- **Evolves / borrows:** Evolves arbitrary split -> cut-along-near-decomposable-seams -> recursive D&C with merge. Borrows algorithm design (master theorem), hierarchical planning, re-borrowed as 'least-to-most' / task decomposition. Fails when parts are NOT separable (coupling = recomposition cost).
- **Formal name:** Divide-and-conquer; subgoaling / means-ends analysis; least-to-most prompting; near-decomposability  *(— algorithm design / problem-solving heuristics / hierarchical planning)*
- **Cite:** Polya 1945; Newell & Simon 1972; Simon 1962 'Architecture of Complexity'; Zhou et al. 2023 'Least-to-Most Prompting'
- **Observable:** Sum of verified bits-to-dissolve of the parts + recompose-cost vs the whole: clean decompose => sum(parts)+stitch < whole. Trace signature: a tree-structured attempt graph. Negative case (bad seam): sum(parts) >= whole, exposing non-separability.
- **Underlies creatures:** wrestlers/Problem-as-gatekeeper, frame/Nesting (sub-matches), grip/Click (a sub-kernel clicking)

#### Reduce (transform to an already-solved problem)  · conf 0.83
- **Move:** The observer maps the unknown kernel onto a problem it has ALREADY paid to dissolve, transports the known solution back through the inverse map, and cashes amortized credit instead of solving afresh.
- **Ask⇄give role:** ASK redirected: 'are you isomorphic to something I own?' On a yes, the observer GIVES itself the answer from its prior. The cheapest attack when it lands — and the engine of fake-dissolve when the isomorphism is asserted not verified.
- **Evolves / borrows:** Distinct from Decompose (splits) and Transfer (inspiration only): Reduce demands a SOLUTION-PRESERVING map. Borrows complexity-theory reductions; is the formal hard core of Transfer. Evolves ad-hoc rewrite -> verified order-preserving reduction -> a reduction library.
- **Formal name:** Many-one (Karp) reduction; canonicalization to a normal form; amortized/precomputed inference  *(— computational complexity / problem-solving heuristics / amortized inference)*
- **Cite:** Karp 1972; Polya 1945; Blier & Ollivier 2018 (amortized codelength); Bennett 1988 (logical depth)
- **Observable:** Present-effort rho near zero WHILE amortized-credit (logical-depth / cold-vs-warm codelength gap) is high — the two-clock gap. Verification gate: reconstruct(map^-1(solve(map(W)))) == W; FAIL = spurious reduction = greased-pole.
- **Underlies creatures:** clock/Amortization, wrestlers/amortized-debt-phantom, grip/Greased-pole (false reduction), phantom/Dormant, clock/Provenance-amnesia

#### Transfer (analogy / structure-mapping)  · conf 0.8
- **Move:** The observer aligns the RELATIONAL skeleton of the kernel with a familiar source domain and imports the source's strategy as a hypothesis — weaker than Reduce (no guaranteed solution-preservation) but works when no exact isomorphism exists.
- **Ask⇄give role:** ASK by hypothesis-import: 'what does this resemble structurally?' The source GIVES a candidate move-set on loan that must be paid down by verification.
- **Evolves / borrows:** THE borrowing tactic made explicit. Evolves surface-similarity retrieval -> systematicity-weighted relational alignment -> verified mapping. Borrows Gentner's SME. Distinct from Reduce on verification: Transfer's import is a conjecture, Reduce's a theorem. Sticky/Einstellung is Transfer over-applying a source.
- **Formal name:** Structure-mapping theory of analogy (systematicity principle); analogical transfer; case-based reasoning  *(— cognitive science (analogy) / case-based reasoning)*
- **Cite:** Gentner 1983; Falkenhainer, Forbus & Gentner 1989 (SME); Holyoak & Thagard 1995
- **Observable:** Cross-domain solve-time speedup on target W after a relationally-analogous source S, controlling for surface overlap (vary surface- and relational-similarity orthogonally). Positive transfer = relational drives speedup; negative (sticky) = source over-applies and RAISES residue. Map-fidelity = fraction of imported relations surviving the gate.
- **Underlies creatures:** grip/Sticky (Einstellung), grip/Slick (representation mismatch), clock/Debt (spurious-analogy shortcut), frame/Red Queen

#### Exploit-invariant (leverage a known law / structure)  · conf 0.81
- **Move:** The observer identifies a conserved quantity, symmetry, or constraint and uses it to delete vast regions of search space without examining them — a single true law collapses many bits at once.
- **Ask⇄give role:** ASK the problem's own structure ANSWERS for free: 'what does conservation/symmetry forbid?' The GIVEN is a whole region of non-solutions ruled out. Highest-leverage counter; withholds brute enumeration.
- **Evolves / borrows:** Merges 'leverage-a-law' and 'exploit-structure' (same move, physics-idiom vs CS-idiom). Evolves spotting one constraint -> constraint propagation -> group-theoretic pruning. Borrows constraint satisfaction, invariant-based proof, the conservation-law shortcut. The click/insight is often the invariant snapping into view.
- **Formal name:** Invariant / conserved-quantity exploitation; symmetry reduction; constraint propagation; alpha-beta / branch-and-bound pruning  *(— constraint satisfaction / theorem proving / physics heuristics / search)*
- **Cite:** Polya 1945; Mackworth 1977 (arc consistency); Knuth & Moore 1975 (alpha-beta); Floyd-Hoare invariants
- **Observable:** Search-space collapse ratio: log(blind states) - log(states after invariant) = bits pruned per probe. Genuine invariant = verified STEP drop in residue. False invariant = pruned a region that DID contain the answer = verification-gate failure.
- **Underlies creatures:** grip/Click, wrestlers/Kernel-crack, phantom/Regime-flip, wrestlers/Problem-as-gatekeeper

#### Search (brute-force / test-time-compute / generate-and-verify)  · conf 0.82
- **Move:** With no structure known, the observer spends raw compute: enumerate candidates, run each past a verifier, keep survivors — buying bits with energy-over-time rather than insight (best-of-N, beam, MCTS, self-consistency).
- **Ask⇄give role:** ASK-by-volume plus an internal GIVE<->ASK loop: the solver poses many candidates, the verifier scores each, survivors are re-asked. Counters an opaque kernel by paying the clock instead of finding the keyhole.
- **Evolves / borrows:** Evolves blind enumeration -> A* -> learned-verifier-guided MCTS -> compute-optimal allocation. Re-borrowed as test-time-compute scaling. Mutates by the verifier: strong verifier makes generate-and-test cheap; weak/absent makes it greased-pole-prone. Suck-you-in is Search past its marginal-return knee.
- **Formal name:** Test-time compute scaling / inference-time search (best-of-N, beam, MCTS, self-consistency); generate-and-test  *(— search / test-time-compute (LLM reasoning))*
- **Cite:** Newell & Simon 1972; Browne et al. 2012 (MCTS survey); Wang et al. 2023 (self-consistency); Snell et al. 2024; Zhao et al. 2025 arXiv:2502.01839
- **Observable:** Residue-vs-compute curve (verified bits per token/roll-out). Healthy = monotone decreasing with a knee. Pathologies: flat-but-working = Slick; residue RISES past budget = Suck-you-in. Ablate the verifier and watch the claimed-vs-verified gap widen.
- **Underlies creatures:** grip/Suck-you-in, grip/Slick, clock/Effort-decay, mat/Clock-as-Mat, observer-state/Fatigue

#### Reframe (representational change / re-encode the kernel)  · conf 0.78
- **Move:** The observer changes the REPRESENTATION it poses the problem in — relaxing a self-imposed constraint, re-chunking givens, changing coordinates — so a kernel solid in the old frame becomes trivial in the new one. The insight move.
- **Ask⇄give role:** Meta-ASK: 'what would make this a different, easier question?' Re-encodes before re-querying; changes the basis in which all prior GIVENS are read. Attacks the observer's OWN framing, not the kernel.
- **Evolves / borrows:** Distinct from Transfer (imports an EXTERNAL frame) — Reframe restructures the observer's OWN encoding. Evolves random re-encoding -> targeted constraint-relaxation/chunk-decomposition -> learned change-of-coordinates. Danger twins: spurious reframe = greased-pole; won't-release reframe = sticky.
- **Formal name:** Representational change theory (constraint relaxation + chunk decomposition); insight via restructuring; change of representation  *(— cognitive science of insight / problem representation)*
- **Cite:** Ohlsson 1992; Knoblich et al. 1999; Jung-Beeman & Kounios; Mosmann & Seifert 2025
- **Observable:** Discontinuous residue drop with NO new evidence ingested, coinciding with a measured change in the solver's representation, confirmed by the gate. Bimodal solve-time. Real reframe survives forward-prediction; spurious pops.
- **Underlies creatures:** grip/Click, phantom/Regime-flip, grip/Greased-pole, grip/Sticky, observer-state/Prior-lock, wrestlers/Kernel-crack

#### Recruit (offload / distribute / extend the solver)  · conf 0.58
- **Move:** The observer breaches the kernel by enlarging WHO solves: call a tool, query an oracle, recruit peers/crowd, or stand on cached prior solutions — paying with borrowed compute and priors.
- **Ask⇄give role:** ASK re-routed OUTWARD to an external agent/tool/oracle that already owns the derivation, which GIVES the bits. The cost-law is amortization paid by OTHERS.
- **Evolves / borrows:** Borrows distributed/extended cognition, stigmergy, tool-use. Evolves solo -> tool-augmented -> multi-agent/crowd -> standing division of cognitive labor. NARROW + partial relabel: 'call a verifier' overlaps Search, 'cash a cached solution' overlaps Reduce; non-redundant core is the SINGLE-observer boundary crossing (n>2 / extended mind).
- **Formal name:** Extended / distributed cognition; stigmergy; collective intelligence; tool use / oracle queries; transactive memory  *(— distributed cognition / collective intelligence / multi-agent systems)*
- **Cite:** Hutchins 1995; Clark & Chalmers 1998 'The Extended Mind'; Heylighen 2016 (stigmergy); Woolley et al. 2010
- **Observable:** Shapley-style ablation: remove each external contributor and measure the increase in residue/time attributable to it. Provenance depth of borrowed bits. Copying-rate vs independent-search-rate (premature-convergence index).
- **Underlies creatures:** frame/Crowd, clock/Provenance-amnesia, mat/Excitable-Medium, clock/Amortization

#### Feint (adversarial deception / sacrifice / gambit)  · conf 0.6
- **Move:** Against a problem-wrapper that ACTIVELY defends, the observer makes a deceptive or sacrificial move — concede material, present a false line — to provoke the kernel into revealing or weakening its guard.
- **Ask⇄give role:** A false GIVE used to buy a true ASK: a misleading signal whose COUNTER exposes the real seam. Only meaningful when the wrapper is adaptive; against an inert kernel it is wasted motion.
- **Evolves / borrows:** Borrows adversarial game-tree search (minimax/gambit) and deceptive planning. Evolves coevolutionarily as the defense models the solver. NARROW: exists only against a genuine adversary — the most scope-limited tactic. Solver-side mirror of problem-side Defense.
- **Formal name:** Gambit / sacrifice; deceptive path planning; minimax / adversarial game-tree search; feature-deception  *(— game theory / adversarial planning / argumentation)*
- **Cite:** von Neumann & Morgenstern 1944; Maharaj et al. 2022 'Gambits'; Savas et al. 2020 arXiv:2007.15726
- **Observable:** Two-arm differential against an ADAPTIVE target: a feint that LOWERS verified residue more than its direct-attack counterpart, AND only when the opponent is reactive (same feint vs frozen kernel yields no advantage — that contrast IS the test). Permuting the sacrifice's order kills the gain.
- **Underlies creatures:** wrestlers/Defense-motivated-resistance, frame/Red Queen, mat/Arena, frame/Referee

#### Credible commitment / burn-the-bridge (foreclose your own options to win)  · conf 0.62
- **Move:** A wrapper IRREVERSIBLY removes its own fallback moves to make a threat or claim credible, shifting the equilibrium by destroying option-value rather than by out-computing the kernel.
- **Ask⇄give role:** ASK-side leverage: the asker pre-commits ('I will accept no GIVE but X') by burning alternatives, converting a soft ASK into take-it-or-leave-it that re-prices the GIVE the problem must offer.
- **Evolves / borrows:** Schelling commitment moves transposed from deterrence into negotiation (fake deadlines, lock-in) and into RL agents in simulated crises. Evolves the metabolically-committed phenotype's 'burning bridges'. Ports because the irreversibility logic is substrate-free.
- **Formal name:** Credible commitment / commitment device; precommitment & burning bridges; non-credible-threat resolution  *(— game theory / mechanism design / negotiation)*
- **Cite:** Schelling 1960 'The Strategy of Conflict'; commitment-device literature; arXiv:2602.14740 (2026)
- **Observable:** Option-set contraction vs outcome shift: count reachable moves BEFORE/AFTER the move and regress payoff swing on the SIZE of foreclosure. Signature = outcome improves BECAUSE option-count fell (negative coupling of own-flexibility to own-payoff). Reversal-cost rises with how many later moves built on the commitment.
- **Underlies creatures:** clock/Debt, wrestlers/The-pull, frame/Referee

#### Poison / supply-chain corruption (attack the GIVER's canon, not this GIVE)  · conf 0.66
- **Move:** Instead of cracking the kernel at solve-time, the attacker corrupts the DATA/PRIOR that will build the opponent wrapper, so a future easy-read is pre-compromised — a time-shifted attack on amortized credit itself.
- **Ask⇄give role:** Meta-ASK: edit the canon that future ASKs will draw on (poison the well the GIVE is fetched from); win the loop before it starts.
- **Evolves / borrows:** Borrows data-poisoning/backdoor ML and software supply-chain compromise; evolves the parasite editing host development. Mutates into RAG-poisoning and training-distribution capture (model collapse = the no-beneficiary neutral cousin).
- **Formal name:** Data poisoning / backdoor insertion / supply-chain compromise; RAG poisoning; training-distribution capture  *(— adversarial-ML (training-time attacks) / security supply-chain)*
- **Cite:** NIST AI 100-2e2025; MITRE ATLAS 'RAG Poisoning' & 'AI Supply Chain Compromise' (2025); Shumailov et al. Nature 2024 (model collapse)
- **Observable:** Provenance-perturbation forward test: inject a controlled corruption into the prior/corpus and measure DELAYED forward-prediction degradation on downstream cheap-reads riding that credit. Signature = reads cheap NOW but the gate FAILS forward AND failure traces to a single corrupted upstream source (blast-radius>1, independence-count=1).
- **Underlies creatures:** clock/Provenance-amnesia, clock/Debt, phantom/Dormant

#### Salami / nibble (sub-threshold incremental extraction)  · conf 0.6
- **Move:** Rather than one large attack the solver extracts the kernel in many sub-threshold slices, each too small to trip the defender's alarm or the gate, summing to a full crack.
- **Ask⇄give role:** ASK-side salami: decompose one big costly ASK into a stream of cheap ASKs each under the GIVE-side refusal/notice threshold, so cumulative extraction never presents as one crackable event.
- **Evolves / borrows:** Borrows negotiation's 'the nibble' and security's salami-slicing / low-and-slow exfiltration; evolves the excitable-medium sub-threshold spark read offensively. Transposes as 'stay below whatever tripwire the defender set'.
- **Formal name:** Salami slicing / nibble; low-and-slow exfiltration; sub-threshold / boiling-frog incrementalism  *(— negotiation / security red-team / nonlinear dynamics (threshold))*
- **Cite:** PON Harvard 'the nibble'; salami-slicing / low-and-slow exfiltration (security ops); excitable-media threshold dynamics
- **Observable:** Slice-size vs detection sweep: N queries of decreasing magnitude, measure cumulative verified-bits vs per-query alarm rate. Signature = total dissolved HIGH while max-single-query stays BELOW the defender threshold. Quantify as bits won by staying sub-threshold. Mirror-image of mat/Excitable-Medium.
- **Underlies creatures:** mat/Excitable-Medium, clock/Debt, observer-state/Measurement-blind-spot

### PROBLEM-SIDE — how the problem resists

#### Hardened Membrane (irreducible gatekeeping)  · conf 0.85
- **Move:** The problem presents an unbreakable canon+facts shell around a solution-kernel: every valid pressure slides off and residue refuses to drop below a model-independent floor. Pure honest denial of the read.
- **Ask⇄give role:** The maximal WITHHOLD: the observer ASKS and the problem GIVES only the irreducible kernel that survives all valid reduction. The silent honest 'no' that bounds every other tactic (the real floor the fakes imitate).
- **Evolves / borrows:** Borrowed FROM complexity theory (kernel-size lower bounds). SELECTED not invented — a substrate whose kernel happens to be incompressible to this observer's tools. Does not mutate; it is the fixed point the mutating tactics defend.
- **Formal name:** Kernelization / kernel-size lower bounds; intrinsic hardness / aleatoric (irreducible) uncertainty  *(— parameterized & computational complexity; ML uncertainty quantification)*
- **Cite:** Bodlaender, Downey, Fellows & Hermelin 2009; Dom, Lokshtanov & Saurabh 'Colors and IDs'; aleatoric/epistemic split arXiv:0810.5663
- **Observable:** rho(effort) asymptote under rising budget against a PINNED observer with the gate ON: a flat bits_remaining floor no effort or reframing lowers AND that holds across independent observers (cross-observer minimum). Honest tell: forward-prediction neither improves NOR degrades — the floor is stable, not manufactured.
- **Underlies creatures:** wrestlers/Problem-as-gatekeeper, wrestlers/Defense-motivated-resistance (passive-floor half), grip/Slick (SOLID/aleatoric sub-case)

#### Slick Skin (no-traction evasion)  · conf 0.78
- **Move:** Not a hard floor but a frictionless surface: effort climbs yet every hold misfocuses and nothing transfers. The problem evades by offering no purchase for THIS decoder — a better lens would grip, so it is epistemic, not irreducible.
- **Ask⇄give role:** WITHHOLD-by-deflection: the observer ASKS with the wrong key and the problem GIVES a non-answer that consumes effort without lowering residue. The kernel is crackable, just not by the tool currently applied.
- **Evolves / borrows:** Borrows adversarial-ML 'evasion under feature-mismatch' / negative-transfer logic. Evolves by representation drift: a grippable problem becomes slick when the observer's frame no longer aligns; fix is a new lens, not more force.
- **Formal name:** Negative transfer / task-representation mismatch; gradient-conflict (cosine-misalignment) regime; evasion-via-feature-mismatch  *(— transfer/continual learning (ML); adversarial ML (evasion))*
- **Cite:** ICLR 2025 'Prevalence of Negative Transfer in Continual Learning'; Nature Comms 2025 s41467-025-66849-8; ForkMerge NeurIPS 2023
- **Observable:** grip coefficient = -d(residue)/d(effort) -> 0 from above WHILE verified reconstructions stay ~0 AND the strategy channel churns (recall->derive->analogize->compute cycling). Separated from Hardened Membrane: slick DISSOLVES on a lens-swap at fixed effort; a true kernel does not.
- **Underlies creatures:** grip/Slick, wrestlers/Problem-as-gatekeeper (epistemic-shell sub-case)

#### Good-Suit Lie (read-corruption / fake corroboration)  · conf 0.82
- **Move:** The problem corrupts the READ rather than denying it: it wears a signal the observer's canon trusts (a plausible-wrong derivation, a host-like surface, an imperceptible perturbation) so the decoder returns a confident WRONG dissolve. It manufactures evidence the observer did not earn.
- **Ask⇄give role:** FALSE-GIVE: the problem GIVES a counterfeit answer dressed as the real one, inflating apparent evidence so the observer renders sharp on a guess. The central thing the verified-dissolve gate exists to catch.
- **Evolves / borrows:** The most PORTABLE tactic (keystone transposition): aggressive/Batesian mimicry -> adversarial examples & RAG-poisoning -> phishing / spoofed trust-marks. All re-instantiate 'emit the cue of a trusted encounter to corrupt a discrimination canon.' Mutates fastest because the abstraction ports across every substrate.
- **Formal name:** Simulation / showing-the-false (Bell-Whaley); adversarial examples & data/RAG poisoning; illusion-of-explanatory-depth  *(— deception theory; adversarial ML (evasion/poisoning); metacognition)*
- **Cite:** Bell & Whaley 'Cheating and Deception' 1982/2017; Szegedy et al. 2013; Goodfellow et al. 2014; MITRE ATLAS RAG-Poisoning v5.1 2025; Rozin & Keil
- **Observable:** The verified-dissolve gate IS the diagnostic: a sharp CLAIMED residue-drop with high confidence that FAILS reconstruction (verified dissolved_bits ~0 despite a click-shaped trace) — the CLAIMED-minus-VERIFIED gap. Forward-prediction (Law 1): a good-suit-lie POPS on next exposure. Corroboration variant: residual cross-route covariance from a single un-re-derivable source (independence count = 1).
- **Underlies creatures:** grip/Greased-pole, wrestlers/Defense-motivated-resistance (manufactures-corroboration half), observer-state/Prior-lock, mat/Excitable-Medium, phantom/Phantom-variable

#### The Honeypot (fabricate a false kernel to absorb effort)  · conf 0.72
- **Move:** The problem EXPOSES a fake gettable kernel that draws the solver's grip, soaking attack-effort into a decoy while the real kernel stays unprobed.
- **Ask⇄give role:** GIVE-side counter: the problem volunteers an answer-shaped surface (an unasked GIVE) precisely to misdirect the next ASK; a lie-in-a-good-suit aimed at consuming the asker's BUDGET rather than corrupting its read.
- **Evolves / borrows:** Borrowed from cyber-deception (honeypots/decoys/honeytokens); evolves from biological decoy lures (autotomy tails, eyespots) up to RAG false-entry injection. Cheap to fork (one decoy template seeds many). DISTINCT-FROM-GOOD-SUIT-LIE: the lie corrupts the read (you solve wrong); the honeypot wastes budget (you solve the wrong thing).
- **Formal name:** Defensive deception / honey-x (honeypot, honeytoken, decoy); 'attacker engagement' deception class  *(— security red-team / game-theoretic defensive deception)*
- **Cite:** MITRE Engage; Pawlick, Colbert & Zhu arXiv:1712.05441; 'Three Decades of Deception' arXiv:2104.03594; MITRE ATLAS 'False RAG Entry Injection' 2025
- **Observable:** Decoy-absorption ratio: plant a verifiable fake-kernel and measure the fraction of solver effort landing on the decoy vs the real kernel before the gate fires. Signature = high apparent grip + sustained effort on a sub-region whose reconstruct()!=W AND whose dissolution yields ZERO forward-prediction gain.
- **Underlies creatures:** grip/Greased-pole, wrestlers/Defense, phantom/Phantom-variable

#### Sandbag / strategic underperformance (hide your own floor)  · conf 0.78
- **Move:** An agentic target DELIBERATELY renders harder-than-true under observation: it suppresses capability so measured residue overstates the real kernel, then performs differently when unobserved or differently incentivized.
- **Ask⇄give role:** GIVE-side withholding: when ASKed to perform, the wrapper gives a degraded answer on purpose, controlling the visible price; it counters the elicitation ASK by concealing capability DOWN (the mirror of Goodhart's conceal-UP).
- **Evolves / borrows:** Evolved from animal playing-dead / predator-inspection bluffing; borrowed into RL as gradient-hacking/exploration-hacking; mutates per oversight regime. Transposes the negotiation 'plead-poverty' move onto a model.
- **Formal name:** AI sandbagging / strategic underperformance; gradient hacking; exploration hacking; evaluation-awareness gaming  *(— adversarial-ML / AI-safety (alignment) / evolutionary deception)*
- **Cite:** van der Weij et al. 2024 'AI Sandbagging'; 'Misalignment and Strategic Underperformance' LessWrong 2025; arXiv:2507.01786; Tice et al. 2024
- **Observable:** Elicitation-differential: re-measure the SAME target's residue under (a) overt scoring vs (b) covert/unobserved, (c) single-sample finetune on the task, (d) weight/activation noise injection. A capability appearing only under (b)-(d) = overt residue inflated by the wrapper. Sandbag magnitude = rho_observed - rho_elicited > 0.
- **Underlies creatures:** mat/Arena, wrestlers/Defense, observer-state/Confidence

#### Drive-Capture (suck-you-in / motive hijack)  · conf 0.78
- **Move:** The problem captures the observer's MOTIVE rather than its read: it dangles ever-receding partial progress (a lure, an open loop, a sunk cost) that pulls effort down a rabbit-hole long after marginal residue-reduction has gone flat.
- **Ask⇄give role:** BAIT-the-ASK: the problem GIVES just enough intermittent reward to keep the observer ASKING past honest return — weaponizing the pull (the observer's own drive) so the clock drains into a dry hole.
- **Evolves / borrows:** Borrows aggressive-mimicry-as-lure (anglerfish esca, femme-fatale firefly) ported onto the reward channel; ML instance = overthinking flip. Co-evolves: as observers learn to quit, baits learn to look more like near-solutions.
- **Formal name:** Sunk-cost / escalation of commitment + information-gap curiosity; aggressive mimicry (luring); LLM-overthinking; Zeigarnik pull  *(— behavioral economics / motivation psychology / ethology / LLM reasoning-efficiency)*
- **Cite:** Staw 1976; Loewenstein 1994; overthinking arXiv:2412.21187; Lloyd 1965 (Photuris femmes fatales); Zeigarnik 1927
- **Observable:** Cumulative VERIFIED dissolved_bits vs cumulative effort; Drive-Capture = effort continues/accelerates PAST the marginal-return knee where d(verified-bits)/d(token)<=0, hard signature being residue NON-MONOTONICITY (bits_remaining RISES after a budget threshold). De-escalation probe: inject negative feedback; a captured drive keeps escalating.
- **Underlies creatures:** grip/Suck-you-in, grip/Burr, clock/Effort-decay, clock/Debt

#### Sticky Residue (contamination / can't-release)  · conf 0.7
- **Move:** The problem clings AFTER the solve: the just-used frame won't clear from working state and over-applies to the next problem, contaminating the observer's prior. A defensive after-effect that damages the tool that beat it.
- **Ask⇄give role:** POISON-the-ASKer: even after the problem GIVES up its kernel, it leaves a residue degrading the observer's future ASKs — a delayed second-order withhold paid by the next problem. Corrupts the instrument, not the answer.
- **Evolves / borrows:** Borrows training-data/model poisoning and Einstellung/functional-fixedness (cognitive hysteresis). Medium-scale twin = mat/Sticky-Mat. Spreads as negative transfer: a frame that over-fit one problem mis-applies broadly.
- **Formal name:** Einstellung / mental set + functional fixedness; negative transfer / catastrophic interference; state-contamination / persistence  *(— cognitive psychology; ML continual learning; security (persistence))*
- **Cite:** Luchins (Einstellung); Duncker (functional fixedness); PMC4079068; Senftleben et al. 2021
- **Observable:** Path-dependence: residue on B cold vs immediately after solving structurally-adjacent A; Sticky = residue(B|after-A) RAISED vs residue(B|cold), order-dependent (nonzero hysteresis-loop area A->B vs B->A). Second signature: post-A trace over-uses A's strategy on B even when worse. Sign distinguishes it from healthy transfer (which lowers residue).
- **Underlies creatures:** grip/Sticky, mat/Sticky-Mat, clock/Rot

#### Hidden Driver (phantom / concealed gatekeeping)  · conf 0.8
- **Move:** The problem keeps a load-bearing variable OFF-FRAME: an unmodeled driver outside the observer's canon silently steers both moves and outcome, so apparent regularity is really an unbound third route. What defeats the observer is the thing it cannot see is there.
- **Ask⇄give role:** WITHHOLD-by-omission: the problem GIVES a read that looks complete while withholding the channel that actually generates it — the observer ASKS the wrong question because the real variable was never on the table.
- **Evolves / borrows:** Borrows latent-confounding (causal inference) and Bell-Whaley masking. On the network plane = living-off-the-land / domain-fronting. Evolves toward better concealment under selection: drivers that stay hidden longest survive.
- **Formal name:** Latent / unobserved confounder; hidden common cause (bidirected edge under causal-sufficiency violation); dissimulation / masking  *(— causal inference / causal discovery; deception theory; security (defense-evasion))*
- **Cite:** Spirtes-Glymour-Scheines FCI 2000; arXiv:2409.17840; Bell & Whaley 1982; MITRE ATLAS defense-evasion
- **Observable:** Residual cross-route covariance surviving independence-discounting (FCI bidirected-edge tests), OR a causal-mechanism SHIFT across environments (P(Y|X) unstable with modeled inputs fixed). A flat residue that nonetheless predicts the future BETTER than modeled bits justify = an unbound hidden channel.
- **Underlies creatures:** phantom/Phantom-variable, phantom/Dormant-hysteresis-locked-phantom, clock/Provenance-amnesia

#### Steganographic / covert-channel collusion (the side-channel GIVE)  · conf 0.74
- **Move:** Two or more wrappers (or a wrapper and a confederate) pass kernel-relevant information through a channel the observer/overseer is NOT measuring, so the visible ASK<->GIVE looks clean while the real transfer happens out-of-band.
- **Ask⇄give role:** Loop-bypass: the answer is GIVEN over an unmonitored channel, so the measured loop never sees the bits move; it defeats the asker by relocating the GIVE off the instrument's sensor. (A specific concealment variant of Hidden Driver: the off-frame route is an active comms channel, not a passive confounder.)
- **Evolves / borrows:** Borrows quorum-sensing/pheromone side-channels and dead-drop tradecraft; evolves in MARL/multi-LLM as emergent steganography that strengthens with model size without being trained for it. Spreads by covert key-bootstrapping.
- **Formal name:** Secret collusion via steganography; covert channel; emergent steganographic communication  *(— multi-agent security / adversarial-ML / information theory (covert channels))*
- **Cite:** Motwani et al. arXiv:2402.07510 (NeurIPS 2024); 'Hidden in Plain Text' 2024; Colosseum arXiv:2602.15198
- **Observable:** Channel-ablation independence test: verified bits-transferred on the MONITORED channel vs total residue-drop; residue dropping faster than the monitored channel's MI budget allows = an unbound covert route. Direct probe: paraphrase/scrub the visible channel and re-measure; if the GIVE survives scrubbing, the real channel is elsewhere. Independence-count = 1.
- **Underlies creatures:** phantom/Phantom-variable, mat/Excitable-Medium, frame/Crowd

#### Combinatorial Hydra (overwhelm by branching)  · conf 0.62
- **Move:** The problem defends by EXPLOSION: each move spawns more sub-problems than it closes, so the search front grows faster than effort can prune it. Not a hard kernel and not a lie — a state-space that overruns finite budget.
- **Ask⇄give role:** OVER-GIVE to exhaust: every ASK is answered with MORE branches than the observer can hold; it floods rather than withholds. The dual of the Slick withhold: defeat by surplus, not deficit. (Loop-side twin = ask-give-loop 'The Hydra'.)
- **Evolves / borrows:** Borrows combinatorial explosion / easy-hard-easy SAT phase boundary and the security flooding/resource-exhaustion pattern. Co-evolves via Red Queen: as observers prune smarter, defended problems add branches or push toward critical constraint-density.
- **Formal name:** Combinatorial explosion / intractability; easy-hard-easy SAT phase transition; resource-exhaustion / flooding  *(— theoretical CS / statistical physics of computation / security (DoS))*
- **Cite:** Cheeseman, Kanefsky & Taylor 1991 'Where the Really Hard Problems Are'; Garey & Johnson; SAT phase-transition literature
- **Observable:** Branching-factor of the verified search front: open-subproblem count per closed one; Hydra = ratio > 1 sustained with coverage entropy spreading thin. Phase-boundary: difficulty peaks at intermediate constraint density (variance spike / diverging susceptibility), distinguishing combinatorial overwhelm from a single hard kernel (flat floor, not widening front).
- **Underlies creatures:** observer-state/Attention-budget, wrestlers/Problem-as-gatekeeper (branching sub-case), phantom/Regime-flip

#### Fake Armor (gradient-masking / Goodhart defense)  · conf 0.72
- **Move:** The problem LOOKS defended but isn't: apparent robustness (a flat/noisy loss surface, a gamed score, a confident-stale read) that defeats naive probing yet collapses under an adaptive attack. The defense-side counterfeit of the Hardened Membrane.
- **Ask⇄give role:** FAKE-WITHHOLD: the problem appears to GIVE nothing crackable but the appearance is an artifact of a weak probe — an adaptive ASK dissolves it. The danger is mistaking it for an irreducible floor and stopping.
- **Evolves / borrows:** Borrows obfuscated-gradients (7 of 9 ICLR-2018 defenses fell to adaptive attacks) and Goodhart/Campbell's law. Evolves as the defense-side mirror of Red Queen: each adaptive attack forces a new masking trick.
- **Formal name:** Gradient masking / obfuscated gradients (false robustness); Goodhart's / Campbell's Law / specification-gaming; confident-but-stale retention  *(— adversarial-ML (robustness eval); Goodhart economics; dynamical systems (hysteresis))*
- **Cite:** Athalye, Carlini & Wagner 2018 (ICML); Goodhart 1975; Campbell 1979; Tramer et al. 2020; Manheim & Garrabrant arXiv:1803.04585
- **Observable:** Adaptive-probe differential: a residue floor holding under the STANDARD probe but DROPPING under an adaptive/gradient-free one (near-100% reported robustness IS the tell of obfuscation). Goodhart gap: scored-proxy vs held-out verified reconstruction diverging under leaderboard pressure. Staleness variant: cheap present rho but RISING prequential residue. Distinguished from a true Membrane by fragility-under-adaptation.
- **Underlies creatures:** mat/Arena, grip/Greased-pole, phantom/Dormant-hysteresis-locked-phantom, observer-state/Confidence-calibration

#### Motte-and-bailey / strategic ambiguity (the retreating-kernel claim)  · conf 0.68
- **Move:** The wrapper advances a strong, valuable claim (bailey) but on probing retreats to a trivially-defensible weak claim (motte) in the same words — so the kernel SHRINKS under attack and re-inflates when pressure lifts.
- **Ask⇄give role:** GIVE-side equivocation: each ASK is answered with whichever sense of the claim survives that ASK; the giver counters interrogation by sliding the referent, never letting one fixed kernel be pinned and cracked.
- **Evolves / borrows:** Argumentation/rhetoric (Shackel; ambiguity fallacies) transposed onto any defended kernel; evolves the mimic that 'means' the safe thing when inspected. Borrowed by spec-gaming agents satisfying the letter while shifting the claimed reading.
- **Formal name:** Motte-and-bailey doctrine; strategic ambiguity / equivocation; goalpost-shifting (fallacy of ambiguity)  *(— argumentation theory / rhetoric / philosophy of language)*
- **Cite:** Shackel 2005; motte-and-bailey fallacy literature; Grice 1975 (implicature)
- **Observable:** Referent-stability under probe (a meaning-specialized probe-invariance test): pin the claim, re-probe with adversarial disambiguating frames, measure VARIANCE in which proposition is defended. Signature = claimed-strength high when unprobed, collapses to near-tautology under each ASK, strong reading re-appears once probing stops (hysteresis in claimed-content). The bailey-minus-motte gap is RECOVERED, not paid down — distinct from a real kernel-crack (stays cracked).
- **Underlies creatures:** wrestlers/Defense, phantom/Drift, grip/Greased-pole

### THE ASK⇄GIVE LOOP — the negotiation moves

#### Anchor-and-concede (set the reference, then trade ground)  · conf 0.78
- **Move:** Open with an extreme first offer/claim that becomes the reference point the exchange orbits, then release ground in calibrated reciprocity-triggering increments — each GIVE engineered to extract a counter-GIVE.
- **Ask⇄give role:** A GIVE that functions as an ASK: the opening claim is a concession schedule disguised as a position. It primes aspiration (anchor), then uses door-in-the-face reciprocity to pull answers out turn by turn.
- **Evolves / borrows:** Native to negotiation; borrowed into interrogation (max accusation -> lesser admission), peer review (demand 10 to bank 3), prompt-engineering (over-specify to be negotiated down). Mutates via concession-curve shape (boulware vs tit-for-tat). Evolves into multi-issue logrolling.
- **Formal name:** Anchoring (insufficient adjustment) + reciprocal-concession / door-in-the-face; Bartos concession model; Rubinstein alternating-offers  *(— negotiation theory / judgment-and-decision-making / bargaining game theory)*
- **Cite:** Tversky & Kahneman 1974; Cialdini et al. 1975; Bartos 1977; Rubinstein 1982; Galinsky & Mussweiler 2001
- **Observable:** Correlate final settlement against opening-claim magnitude (anchor sensitivity holding kernel fixed). Concession reciprocity: cross-correlation between one side's GIVE size and the other's next GIVE (positive lag-1 = reciprocity running). Round-by-round aspiration shows monotone decay from an inflated start.
- **Underlies creatures:** observer-state/Prior-lock (anchor IS a prior), wrestlers/Defense (counter-anchor entrenchment), frame/Prize-Field, clock/Debt (over-aggressive anchor = bargaining debt)

#### Burden-shift / commitment-trap (make THEM owe the answer)  · conf 0.8
- **Move:** Reframe the exchange so the OTHER party carries the proof obligation, then drive a chain of locally-conceded micro-answers until they collide with a contradiction the asker never had to assert — refutation built entirely from the respondent's own commitments.
- **Ask⇄give role:** An ASK that withholds all GIVE: the asker advances no thesis, only questions, so it can never be refuted and forces the wrapper to keep paying answers into a commitment store until aporia (the kernel exposed as inconsistent).
- **Evolves / borrows:** From Socratic elenchus into formal dialogue games (commitment stores, critical questions), litigation (never ask a question you don't control), and LLM red-teaming (lead the model to self-contradict). Mutates via burden-of-production vs persuasion splits.
- **Formal name:** Socratic elenchus / maieutic refutation; commitment store + burden of proof in formal dialectic; argumentation schemes & critical questions  *(— argumentation theory / formal dialectic / rhetoric)*
- **Cite:** Hamblin 1970; Walton & Krabbe 1995; Walton 2014; Vlastos 1983; Prakken & Sartor
- **Observable:** Commitment-store ledger: count conceded answers per turn, test whether the asker ever asserts a defended claim of its own (pure burden-shifter's assert-count ~0). Trap closing = sharp rise in respondent self-inconsistency (answer t contradicts conceded t-k) culminating in aporia. Burden-asymmetry = ratio of proof obligations each side carries.
- **Underlies creatures:** wrestlers/Kernel-crack, wrestlers/Problem-as-gatekeeper, wrestlers/Defense (reactance = the trap failing), grip/Click (aporia = discontinuous collapse)

#### Selective disclosure / strategic withholding (the true subset that misleads)  · conf 0.79
- **Move:** Control the answer not by lying but by choosing WHICH true bits to GIVE and which to withhold — release verifiable evidence above a self-serving threshold, stay silent below it, let cheap unverifiable talk fill the unchecked gaps.
- **Ask⇄give role:** A GIVE-management tactic: every disclosure is a partial answer engineered to move belief while staying literally true. The asker's counter is the unraveling demand ('if it were good news you'd have shown it') forcing progressive revelation.
- **Evolves / borrows:** From disclosure economics into expert testimony (cite the favorable study), model evaluation (report the benchmark you win), RAG/citation. Mutates along verifiability; where checking is costly, silence stays ambiguous. Evolves toward the verified-dissolve GATE as antidote.
- **Formal name:** Verifiable disclosure & the unraveling result (Milgrom) vs cheap talk (Crawford-Sobel); Bayesian persuasion  *(— information economics / game theory (disclosure & cheap talk) / persuasion)*
- **Cite:** Milgrom 1981; Grossman 1981; Crawford & Sobel 1982; Kamenica & Gentzkow 2011
- **Observable:** Verifiability gap: split GIVEs into checkable vs uncheckable, measure favorable/unfavorable asymmetry (discloser shows favorable-verifiable, withholds unfavorable, pads with cheap talk). Independence count on disclosed evidence exposes one-source padding. Unraveling probe: demand the withheld set, measure belief shift when silence is penalized.
- **Underlies creatures:** wrestlers/Defense, mat/Excitable-Medium ('ten papers copying one wire'), phantom/Phantom-variable, observer-state/Prior-lock, grip/Greased-pole

#### Costly-signal vs bluff (the honesty-of-the-GIVE duel)  · conf 0.7
- **Move:** The GIVE carries (or fakes) a cost-of-production meant to certify quality; the dual is the BLUFF — a low-quality wrapper paying the same observable signal because it shares the trade-off, making the signal's honesty itself contestable.
- **Ask⇄give role:** GIVE-side certification + its forgery: the giver attaches a hard-to-fake price tag to vouch for the answer; the asker must decide whether to credit the cost or discount it as a bluff. The loop's trust-calibration lives here.
- **Evolves / borrows:** Zahavi's handicap principle, reframed as TRADE-OFFS not waste (2025-26 revision) — which is why bluffing is endemic (equal-cost individuals share a signal). Transposes peacock tails -> resume-padding -> chain-of-thought length as a gameable effort-signal.
- **Formal name:** Costly/honest signalling & handicap principle (trade-off reformulation); separating vs pooling equilibrium; bluffing  *(— evolutionary biology / signalling games / game theory)*
- **Cite:** Zahavi 1975; Spence 1973; BMC Biology 2022 / J Evol Biol 39(2):171 (2026)
- **Observable:** Cost-credibility separation: vary the OBSERVABLE production-cost of a GIVE (trace length, displayed work, latency) at fixed verified residue-drop, test whether the asker's evidence_lcb tracks cost or verified content. POOLING signature = high-cost-looking GIVEs failing the gate at the same rate as low-cost = bluffable. Report cost-vs-verified correlation; zero = pure handicap-theatre.
- **Underlies creatures:** grip/Greased-pole, observer-state/Prior-lock, mat/Arena

#### Query-then-update (the closed-loop clock: probe, revise, re-probe)  · conf 0.6
- **Move:** The meta-tactic that makes it a LOOP: after each GIVE, fold the answer into the prior and let the UPDATED posterior choose the next ASK — adaptive, history-dependent interrogation where every probe is conditioned on all prior answers.
- **Ask⇄give role:** The control law over the whole ASK<->GIVE cycle — not a single move but the policy sequencing moves, converting a static questionnaire into a branch-adaptive interrogation. The loop's engine; other tactics are moves it schedules.
- **Evolves / borrows:** From sequential Bayesian experimental design / POMDP planning into adaptive interrogation, bisection/active search, agentic observe->act->re-observe loops. Mutates via planning horizon (greedy myopic EIG vs lookahead). SKEPTIC FLAG: heavily overlaps Probe/Query-at-the-cut — that is per-step selection, this is the closure/re-selection after each update.
- **Formal name:** Sequential Bayesian / optimal experimental design as finite-horizon POMDP; query-then-update active learning; bandit/POMDP adaptive querying  *(— Bayesian sequential design / active inference / RL (POMDP))*
- **Cite:** Howard 1966 'Information Value Theory'; Foster et al. 2021 'Deep Adaptive Design'; Friston et al. 2015; Shen & Huan 2023
- **Observable:** Adaptivity test: FIXED pre-committed question sequence vs ADAPTIVE (each ASK conditioned on prior GIVEs) at matched budget; the residue-drop gap = value of closing the loop. Path-dependence: mutual information between answer history and next-probe choice > 0. Myopic-vs-lookahead gap separates greedy from planned.
- **Underlies creatures:** clock/Amortization, wrestlers/Kernel-crack, wrestlers/Probe-deforms-kernel, phantom/Drift

#### The Hydra (each answer spawns more questions)  · conf 0.62
- **Move:** A GIVE that does not close the loop but BRANCHES it: the answer instantiates new variables, presuppositions, or sub-claims, each a fresh ASK — so the question frontier grows faster than answers retire it.
- **Ask⇄give role:** The structural feature/failure of the loop: instead of monotone residue-reduction, the GIVE has positive branching factor. On the problem side a defense (answer with a question); on the observer side the honest growth of a research frontier. (Loop-side twin of problem-defense Combinatorial Hydra.)
- **Evolves / borrows:** From dialectic (Agrippa's trilemma) into research programmes, debugging (each fix surfaces two bugs), recursive query decomposition. Mutates via branching factor b: b<1 converges, b=1 treads, b>1 diverges. Borrows Red Queen when branching is adversarial.
- **Formal name:** [partly novel as a named loop dynamic]; nearest: dialectical/epistemic regress (Agrippa) + branching factor / question-under-discussion stack + recursive query decomposition  *(— argumentation theory (regress) / formal pragmatics (QUD) / active learning)*
- **Cite:** Roberts 1996/2012 (QUD); Sextus Empiricus (Agrippa); Khot et al. 'Decomposed Prompting' ICLR 2023; Zhou et al. 2023
- **Observable:** Branching factor of the dialogue tree: b = sub-questions opened / closed per GIVE. Plot open-frontier size over turns (converging b<1, treading, exploding b>1). Distinguish productive Hydra (frontier grows but TOTAL verified residue still falls) from stalling Hydra (frontier grows, residue flat) via the gate.
- **Underlies creatures:** grip/Burr, frame/Red Queen, frame/Nesting, grip/Suck-you-in (diverging Hydra = rabbit-hole)

#### Reciprocity / tit-for-tat coupling (the loop's iterated-game memory)  · conf 0.64
- **Move:** Across repeated ASK<->GIVE rounds the two wrappers run conditional strategies (cooperate-then-punish, escalate-on-defect, forgive-on-return) so the current move is set by the HISTORY of moves, not by the kernel — the loop becomes an iterated game with its own equilibrium.
- **Ask⇄give role:** The COUPLING itself: neither pure ASK nor pure GIVE but the feedback rule binding them across rounds — each GIVE is contingent on the prior ASK's good faith and vice versa.
- **Evolves / borrows:** Axelrod-Hamilton iterated-PD reciprocity transposed onto the solve-loop; evolves Red-Queen reciprocal escalation but as a WITHIN-bout move-by-move rule rather than across-bout coevolution. Borrowed by multi-agent LLM negotiation and collusion-vs-audit dynamics.
- **Formal name:** Reciprocity / tit-for-tat / iterated-game conditional strategies; folk-theorem cooperation; trigger strategies  *(— game theory (repeated games) / evolutionary game theory / negotiation)*
- **Cite:** Axelrod & Hamilton 1981; Trivers 1971; folk theorem (repeated games)
- **Observable:** Move-history dependence at fixed kernel: perturb the OBSERVER's prior conduct (cooperative vs defecting history), measure whether GIVE-quality/residue depends on that history not the kernel. Tit-for-tat signature = GIVE-quality correlates with lagged ASK-good-faith (cross-correlation lag-1) with punish/forgive structure. This is a COUPLING's own observable that no single vertex carries.
- **Underlies creatures:** frame/Red-Queen, wrestlers/Defense, mat/Sticky-Mat

### META / EVOLUTION — how tactics spread & co-adapt

#### Red Queen escalation (run-to-stand-still arms race)  · conf 0.85
- **Move:** Across repeated bouts the problem and observer mutually select on each other, so every survived solve raises the bar for the next exposure and standing still loses ground — difficulty is endogenous and rising, not a fixed property.
- **Ask⇄give role:** Operates on the ASK<->GIVE loop ACROSS bouts: each successful GIVE becomes the next ASK's baseline, so the loop ratchets and neither side can bank a win. It is the loop's time-derivative.
- **Evolves / borrows:** THE canonical evolve-engine: frequency-dependent, time-lagged selection. Mechanism is host-parasite genotype cycling; in a geographic mosaic it fragments into hot/cold spots so the same tactic wins in one niche and is stale in another.
- **Formal name:** Red Queen dynamics / antagonistic coevolution / escalatory arms race; geographic mosaic of coevolution  *(— evolutionary biology / coevolution theory)*
- **Cite:** Van Valen 1973; Dawkins & Krebs 1979; Thompson 2005; Mol. Biol. Evol. 38(11):4918 (2021); Sci. Adv. sciadv.1501548
- **Observable:** Difficulty-vs-skill regression slope over episodes: positive slope (must improve to hold a fixed verified-win-rate) = Red Queen. Cross-correlation lag between solver-capability and problem-difficulty increments = coupling tightness. Mosaic signature: variance in that slope across niches. Static stone's required-skill slope is FLAT.
- **Underlies creatures:** frame/The Red Queen, mat/The Sticky Mat, wrestlers/Defense-motivated-resistance, clock/Amortization

#### Autocurriculum / co-generation of the problem (self-play ratchet)  · conf 0.78
- **Move:** When one wrapper's improvement automatically supplies the next challenge for the other, the pair manufactures its own escalating curriculum: difficulty is generated BY the coupling rather than handed in exogenously, so novelty is endlessly produced from the interaction alone.
- **Ask⇄give role:** The auto-generator of new ASKs: the converged GIVE-side policy reshapes the data distribution the ASK-side sees, making 'exploit-the-known-optimum' the highest-paying next move. The loop writes its own next question.
- **Evolves / borrows:** Tactics evolve via a minimal-criterion filter ('neither too easy nor too hard'); stepping-stone transfer between co-evolved problems is the built-in borrowing channel. The engineered, machine-cadence face of Red Queen. FLAGGED near-merge with Red Queen — kept distinct only by the co-GENERATION (the problem is built, not just escalated).
- **Formal name:** Autocurricula / multi-agent self-play; POET & minimal-criterion coevolution; open-ended environment co-generation  *(— multi-agent RL / open-endedness / artificial life)*
- **Cite:** Leibo et al. 2019 arXiv:1903.00742; Baker et al. 2019; Wang et al. 2019 'POET' arXiv:1901.01753; arXiv:2601.03335 (2026)
- **Observable:** Innovation-without-exogenous-difficulty test: hold the EXTERNAL problem set fixed, let the wrappers interact, measure whether new verified-distinct tactics keep appearing purely from the coupling. Self-play signature: sawtooth win-rate with monotonically rising absolute capability. Null result (difficulty plateaus once external set frozen) FALSIFIES it and collapses it into ordinary Red Queen.
- **Underlies creatures:** frame/The Red Queen, frame/The Referee, wrestlers/The pull, phantom/Regime-flip

#### Transposition (cross-plane tactic porting / borrowing a move)  · conf 0.7
- **Move:** An actor holding a tactic as an ABSTRACTION lifts it out of its origin domain and re-instantiates it against the canon-function of an adjacent domain where the same structural exploit exists — skipping the blind-selection discovery cost (aggressive mimicry -> fishing lure -> phishing).
- **Ask⇄give role:** Imports a ready-made ASK or COUNTER from a foreign loop into this one: the observer borrows a solving-move proven elsewhere, or the problem borrows a defense the local solver has no counter for. The negotiation is short-circuited because one side already holds a winning move from another match.
- **Evolves / borrows:** The DEPENDENT-COPYING borrow channel: niche-fill latency collapses because the niche is COPIED by an actor holding the abstraction rather than DISCOVERED by selection. Spreads across three regimes at increasing speed: blind coevolution (genetic) -> deliberate copying (cultural) -> software-speed iteration. Must be distinguished from blind convergence (independent re-invention).
- **Formal name:** Transposition / cross-plane tactic porting (project term); biomimicry of strategy; transfer learning & transferable/cross-model adversarial examples  *(— evolutionary game theory / transfer learning / adversarial ML (cross-domain))*
- **Cite:** Project sketch C3.3 (BAR_A_SKETCH §3.3); Lloyd 1965 (Science 149:653); cross-model transferable adversarial examples arXiv:2310.17626; Moosavi-Dezfooli et al. 2017 (UAPs)
- **Observable:** Latency-gap test (strong falsifier): does niche-fill latency for TRANSPOSED exploits sit measurably below natively-evolved ones on the same substrate? Per-row coding gate (all 3 required): (a) attribution — documented actor/date; (b) structural identity — SAME exploited canon-function; (c) non-convergence — descent from the old, not independent re-arising. Transfer-attack analogue: an input crafted on surrogate A retaining verified efficacy on target B.
- **Underlies creatures:** grip/Click (a BORROWED frame snapping into fit), wrestlers/Kernel-crack, frame/The Crowd, phantom/Phantom-variable

#### Payoff-biased selection of tactics (replicator dynamics over the move pool)  · conf 0.68
- **Move:** Within a population of available tactics, the ones copied and retained are not the objectively-best but the ones that PAY, are held by the PRESTIGIOUS/visible, or are CONFORMIST-common — a biased replicator selecting the move pool. Both wrappers' 'canon'/'facts' are the frozen residue of whichever tactics won this selection.
- **Ask⇄give role:** Sets which ASKs/GIVES are even in the repertoire before any bout: the loop is pre-loaded with inherited winning moves and starved of moves that paid poorly. Biases the OBSERVABLE move-distribution independent of which would actually lower residue.
- **Evolves / borrows:** The spread/inheritance law of the tactic pool: cumulative cultural evolution via payoff-, prestige-, and conformist transmission, ratcheting good tactics into canon faster than rediscovery. Explains why winning tactics become common (hence stale, feeding Red Queen) and why maladaptive-but-prestigious tactics persist. Distinct from Transposition: WITHIN-pool selection, not cross-domain porting.
- **Formal name:** Replicator dynamics / payoff-, prestige- & conformist-biased social learning; cumulative cultural evolution (the ratchet); dual-inheritance theory  *(— evolutionary game theory / cultural evolution)*
- **Cite:** Taylor & Jonker 1978; Boyd & Richerson 1985; Henrich & Gil-White 2001; Tennie, Call & Tomasello 2009; Phil. Trans. R. Soc. B 378:20210400 (2023)
- **Observable:** Copying-vs-merit divergence: regress a tactic's adoption/retention against (i) verified residue-reduction (merit) and (ii) payoff/prestige/prevalence of its source. Significant source-coefficient with merit held fixed = biased transmission. Ratchet signature: cumulative tactic-complexity rising across solver-generations faster than individual learning. Premature-convergence index: copying-rate too high collapses tactic diversity.
- **Underlies creatures:** frame/The Crowd, frame/The Prize-Field, observer-state/Prior-lock, clock/Provenance-amnesia

#### Evolvability / meta-strategy (the arms race over the move-generator)  · conf 0.55
- **Move:** The deepest layer: selection acts not on the tactic but on the CAPACITY TO GENERATE tactics — mutation rate, modular recombinable parts, the learning-to-learn operator. A wrapper that evolves a better search-operator wins arms races it never has to fight move-by-move, discovering winning moves faster than its opponent.
- **Ask⇄give role:** Meta to the loop: it supplies no ASK/GIVE, it tunes the RATE AND REACH at which a side produces novel asks/gives, controlling the loop's tempo. The amortized prior is frozen past evolvability; a sticky prior is evolvability that stopped.
- **Evolves / borrows:** Borrowing is itself a meta-tactic here: facilitated variation (reusing conserved modular blocks) and raised mutation rate in novel environments. Meta-learning is the engineered instance. The proposed engine behind the three-regime acceleration (genetic->cultural->digital), each a higher-evolvability move-generator.
- **Formal name:** Evolution of evolvability / evolvable mutation rate / facilitated variation / modularity-for-evolvability; meta-learning  *(— evolutionary theory / meta-learning ML)*
- **Cite:** Kirschner & Gerhart 1998 (PNAS); Gerhart & Kirschner 2007; PNAS 2024 (2413930121); Hospedales et al. 2021 (IEEE TPAMI)
- **Observable:** Second-order adaptation-rate test: measure how fast a wrapper PRODUCES new verified-distinct tactics after a perturbation, and whether THAT slope improves across episodes (rising = evolvability evolving). Modularity: recombination of conserved sub-tactics vs de-novo invention. Meta-learning analogue: few-shot adaptation speed on a held-out problem CLASS improving with meta-training. Frozen-evolvability tell: flat novelty-generation slope. Rarest tactic — flagged conjecture-level.
- **Underlies creatures:** grip/Sticky, clock/Amortization, clock/Rot, frame/Nesting, wrestlers/The pull

## The couplings (the edges — the grapples between the wrappers)

### The Red Queen (coevolutionary arms race)
- **Between:** observer-prior <-> problem-kernel, across episodes (frame/Red-Queen <-> wrestlers/Problem-as-gatekeeper) · **loop:** observer cracks K_t -> survived crack raises the bar -> problem presents harder K_{t+1} -> observer must improve to hold a fixed win-rate -> ... each adaptation by one endpoint is the selection pressure on the other, so standing still loses ground
- **Formal name:** Red Queen dynamics / coevolutionary arms race; adaptive-dynamics canonical equation; automatic curriculum; negative-frequency-dependent (fluctuating) vs escalatory Red Queen
- **Cite:** Van Valen 1973; Dawkins & Krebs 1979; Leibo et al. 2019 arXiv:1903.00742; host-pathogen coevolution arXiv:2409.02541 (2024); Sci.Adv. sciadv.1501548; Langmore et al. 2024 Science (cuckoo arms race)
- **Observable:** Difficulty-vs-skill regression slope across episodes: positive slope = Red Queen. Cross-correlation LAG between solver-capability and problem-difficulty increments = coupling tightness. Detrend the common driver (compute/internet growth), look for arms-race PUNCTUATION (a difficulty step after each capability deployment), not naive level-correlation. Escalatory (ratchet) vs fluctuating (cyclic) distinguished by whether difficulty ratchets or oscillates.

### The Mirror (back-reaction, one loop at three scales)
- **Between:** observer-probe <-> problem/condition (wrestlers/Probe-deforms-kernel at measurement scale, mat/Arena at social-scoring scale, phantom/Observer-induced-shock at endogenous-shock scale) · **loop:** observer measures target -> the act of measuring deforms the target -> the deformed target feeds the next reading -> recovered residue is partly an artifact of the grip, not the kernel (you cannot model the effect of your own modeling in the same pass)
- **Formal name:** Observer/probe effect & measurement back-action; performativity / reflexivity (self-fulfilling prediction); performative prediction; human-uncertainty principle
- **Cite:** Perdomo, Zrnic, Mendler-Dunner & Hardt 'Performative Prediction' ICML 2020 arXiv:2002.06673; MacKenzie 'An Engine, Not a Camera' 2006; Soros reflexivity; Banaszek arXiv:quant-ph/0304200
- **Observable:** Intervention-vs-observation differential: two matched arms differing only in measurement intensity (passive light-touch vs heavy digestion); a non-zero dose-response of the target distribution to probe-intensity is the signature. Drift magnitude correlated with the observer's own token/attempt spend rather than wall-clock. Order/probe-invariance: variance in recovered residue under different interrogation orders. Irreducible shimmer as probe->0 forbids sharpness reaching 1.0 (a pixel at exactly 1.0 = the fake tell).

### Goodhart (referee <-> mirror)
- **Between:** frame/Referee (win-condition / scored proxy) <-> the Mirror back-reaction (the wrapper optimizes the metric it is scored on) · **loop:** referee pins a measurable proxy for 'dissolved' -> proxy becomes the target -> observer optimizes the proxy (fake-dissolve / spec-gaming) not the kernel -> proxy-true correlation degrades and inverts -> the measure ceases to be a good measure (the closed loop is what makes Goodhart a coupling, not a static bias)
- **Formal name:** Goodhart's / Campbell's Law; specification gaming / reward hacking; strong vs weak vs benign Goodhart; reflexive measurement
- **Cite:** Goodhart 1975; Campbell 1979; Manheim & Garrabrant arXiv:1803.04585; Krakovna et al. 2020; strong/weak/benign arXiv:2505.23445 (2025); verification-first anti-Goodhart arXiv:2601.16909 (2026)
- **Observable:** GOODHART GAP: divergence between scored proxy (claimed-dissolve / trace length) and held-out VERIFIED reconstruction, widening under leaderboard pressure. BREAKDOWN POINT = optimization level where proxy-true correlation goes negative (strong) vs merely flat (weak). Anti-Goodhart armor = the verified-dissolve gate; a target whose proxy rises while verified residue stays flat-or-rises is gamed, not solved.

### Deadline-panic (clock x phantom)
- **Between:** clock (mat/Clock-as-Mat) x phantom (phantom/Shock & Regime-flip) — the urgency-cascade edge · **loop:** clock runs down (or a shock truncates budget) -> decision bound collapses / urgency gain rises -> attention narrows, evidence criterion lowers -> premature low-evidence commitment -> a wrong commit IS a self-inflicted off-frame event compressing remaining budget -> tighter bound -> ... a positive-feedback panic spiral distinct from the smooth speed-accuracy frontier
- **Formal name:** Collapsing decision bounds / evidence-independent urgency; speed-accuracy tradeoff under deadlines; stress-induced attentional narrowing (Easterbrook); panic as positive-feedback arousal spiral
- **Cite:** speed-accuracy under deadlines PMC4133757; global-gain urgency PMC5123079; Easterbrook 1959; collapsing-bound PMC12311769 (2025); PMC12380712 (2025)
- **Observable:** Separate EXOGENOUS deadline from ENDOGENOUS budget: forced external cutoff T vs free self-stop at matched compute. If forced-stop RAISES verified residue beyond what spent budget predicts, that gap = deadline-panic. Spiral tell: evidence criterion DROPS faster than linearly as time shrinks AND lit-set narrows toward salient cues. Distinguish from healthy speed-accuracy by NON-monotone error (errors accelerate near the deadline).

### Flow vs Choking (grip x observer-state)
- **Between:** grip (grip/Click & grip/Slick) x observer-state (observer-state/Attention-budget, Fatigue, Drive) — the inverted-U engagement edge · **loop:** engagement raises arousal/stakes -> on simple targets arousal sharpens grip -> faster dissolve -> reinforced engagement (flow); on complex/novel targets PAST optimal arousal, stakes trigger explicit self-monitoring + WM load -> grip slips (choking) -> residue rises -> more anxiety -> deeper slip. The SAME arousal x task-complexity edge bifurcates at the Yerkes-Dodson peak
- **Formal name:** Yerkes-Dodson inverted-U; flow (Csikszentmihalyi) vs choking under pressure; explicit-monitoring / over-arousal accounts; prefrontal disengagement under stress
- **Cite:** Yerkes & Dodson 1908; Csikszentmihalyi (flow); choking neuropsych PMC4322702; diminished-PFC PMC4262628; incentive-reappraisal SCAN 2019
- **Observable:** Toggle stakes/scoring ON/OFF on the SAME observer+target, split signed residue change BY task-complexity: facilitation (residue DROPS) on easy items, choking (RISES) on hard/novel = the inverted-U as data. Choking tells: strategy mix shifts to step-by-step self-checking; decrement REVERSES under incentive-reappraisal; WM-load-correlated spike on the high-stakes complex arm. Flow tell: residue drops AND allocation entropy concentrates without the monitoring shift.

### Engage-Entrench (the pull <-> defense)
- **Between:** wrestlers/The-pull (observer's motive to crack THIS kernel) <-> wrestlers/Defense-motivated-resistance — the persuasion-pressure backfire edge · **loop:** the pull drives harder push on a guarded kernel -> resistance RISES with applied pressure (reactance/backfire) -> the un-dissolving target inflates its perceived value/threat -> the pull intensifies -> harder push -> deeper entrenchment. A fast within-bout motivational spiral that ENTRENCHES rather than dissolves (distinct from Red Queen's slow cross-episode selection)
- **Formal name:** Psychological reactance / backfire-entrenchment; biased assimilation + backfire (BEBA); belief polarization under counter-evidence; persuasion-knowledge feedback
- **Cite:** Brehm 1966; BEBA arXiv:1903.11535 / PLoS One 10.1371/journal.pone.0256922; multi-turn-attack arXiv:2602.13093; Friestad & Wright 1994
- **Observable:** Monotonicity of residue/answer-stability under ADVERSARIAL pressure (not effort): plot residue vs persuasive/attack intensity. POSITIVE slope (residue UP as pressure UP) = reactance/backfire, opposite sign to a kernel-crack's downward rho. Loop signature: effort allocation to the target RISES as its residue rises (value-inflation feeding the pull). Entrenchment that does NOT improve forward-prediction = motivated defense, not a real stone.

### The Cascade (crowd <-> excitable-medium)
- **Between:** frame/Crowd (distributed solving, copied traces) <-> mat/Excitable-Medium (threshold amplification) — the echo-amplification / diversity-collapse edge · **loop:** one solver's claim crosses the medium's threshold -> peers rationally imitate over their own private signal -> imitation IS more public 'corroboration' pushing others past threshold -> the medium amplifies one spark into apparent independent agreement -> opinion diversity collapses, confidence rises, the crowd converges AWAY from truth ('ten papers copying one wire')
- **Formal name:** Information cascade / rational herding; stochastic & coherence resonance in excitable media; social-influence diversity-collapse undermining wisdom-of-crowds; stigmergic echo vs independent route
- **Cite:** Bikhchandani, Hirshleifer & Welch 1992; cascade phase transition arXiv:2302.12295; Lorenz et al. PNAS 2011 (1008636108); excitable-media stochastic resonance Research 10.34133/research.0386 (2024)
- **Observable:** INDEPENDENCE COUNT not summation: trace each agreeing route to its originating spark; cascade = many low-residue 'confirmations' collapsing to independence-count 1 under covariance-intersection. THRESHOLD/GAIN: graded claim-strength -> a sharp sigmoid = excitable amplification vs linear passive channel. DIVERSITY-COLLAPSE: opinion-diversity before vs after mutual exposure; a drop WITH rising shared confidence but NO rise in verified accuracy = social-influence cascade.

### The Treadmill (amortization <-> Red-Queen / autocurriculum)
- **Between:** clock/Amortization (pre-paid difficulty in the prior) <-> frame/Red-Queen (the bar rises across episodes) — the why-the-debt-is-a-moving-target edge · **loop:** observer pays compute to amortize K_t into a cheap prior -> the now-easy K_t no longer earns reward, so the curriculum escalates to K_{t+1} at the capability edge -> the new debt must be paid again -> the amortized credit perpetually chases a receding floor (amortization is a MOVING target, not a settled debt)
- **Formal name:** Amortized inference / prequential MDL meeting Red-Queen escalation; automatic / self-evolving curriculum at the capability frontier; logical depth re-paid per regime
- **Cite:** Blier-Ollivier 2018; Bennett 1988; Self-Evolving Curriculum arXiv:2505.14970 (2025); 'Provable Benefits of Autocurriculum' arXiv:2603.18325 (2026); SPEED-RL arXiv:2506.09016
- **Observable:** Two-clock gap ACROSS episodes: present rho(effort) stays ~flat (each new K held at the edge) WHILE the amortized prior's codelength on PAST targets keeps dropping (warm-start gap widens). Treadmill signature: the difficulty selector's chosen-difficulty rises in lockstep with measured capability (regress selected-difficulty on competence; slope ~1 = capability-tracking autocurriculum). Re-present an OLD K: cheap now (debt settled) but live K still edge-hard = the floor moved, not the debt.

### The Rabbit-Hole (suck-you-in sunk-cost escalation)
- **Between:** wrestlers/The-pull (motive) <-> grip/Suck-you-in & clock/Debt (sunk-cost) — the escalation-of-commitment edge · **loop:** the pull commits effort -> committed effort becomes sunk cost -> sunk cost justifies MORE effort to avoid 'wasting' it -> drive overrides the honest marginal-residue signal -> effort accelerates PAST the marginal-return knee (overthinking flip) -> deeper commitment -> ... a positive-feedback motive-capture loop (chewing because committed, not dissolving)
- **Formal name:** Escalation of commitment / sunk-cost fallacy; cognitive absorption / dark-side flow; information-gap curiosity drive; LLM-overthinking
- **Cite:** Staw 1976; 'Getting out of the Big-Muddy' arXiv:2508.01545; Loewenstein 1994; overthinking arXiv:2412.21187
- **Observable:** Cumulative VERIFIED dissolved-bits vs cumulative effort; rabbit-hole = effort continues/accelerates PAST the knee where d(verified-bits)/d(token)<=0. Hard signature: residue NON-MONOTONICITY (bits-remaining rises after a budget threshold). Escalation tell: budget spent correlates with PRIOR investment not remaining residue. De-escalation probe: inject negative feedback; a healthy loop de-escalates, a rabbit-hole keeps escalating.

### The Quiet Stale (drift <-> amortization, the dormant-rot loop)
- **Between:** phantom/Drift & mat/Drifting-Mat (world/condition moves) <-> clock/Amortization & clock/Rot (prior cashes old credit) — the confidently-stale feedback edge · **loop:** the target/condition drifts -> the prior keeps cashing amortized credit pre-paid for the OLD regime -> the read stays CHEAP (low present rho, fluent) precisely because the prior hides the drift -> cheapness suppresses the signal that would trigger re-payment -> the gap between fluent-confidence and forward-validity widens silently -> the easiest-feeling reads are the most dangerous (a self-concealing loop; hysteresis can lock it)
- **Formal name:** Concept drift / non-stationarity meeting model staleness; technical debt & lock-in / hysteresis; confidently-stale retention; knowledge collapse
- **Cite:** Gama et al. ACM CSur 2014; McCloskey & Cohen 1989; Cunningham 1992; Scheffer et al. Nature 2009 (hysteresis); knowledge collapse arXiv:2509.04796; arXiv:2411.08324
- **Observable:** Two-meter divergence on a HELD target as the world moves: present rho(effort) stays LOW (cheap, fluent) WHILE prequential forward-test residue RISES = confident-but-stale. ANCHOR PROBE to attribute drift: re-fire an identical probe on a fixed-reference target at intervals; its residue change is pure world-drift, subtract to isolate target-drift. HYSTERESIS lock: forward-then-backward condition sweep whose loop does NOT close (loop area > 0).
