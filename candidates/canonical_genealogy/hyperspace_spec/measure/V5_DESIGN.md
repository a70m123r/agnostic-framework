## Dead children
- WIDE-vs-DEEP with distractor-PADDING to equalize total line count -- creates corr(span,#distractors)=+0.901; a model that emits fewer tokens when more lines are flagged ignorable yields a spurious DEEP>WIDE with zero depth cost. The padding mechanism is the dead child; matched attended-line construction replaces it.
- Pooling W=14 and W=29 into one primary analysis -- makes corr(span,work_ops)=+0.486 (W leaks into span); span/work/structure become collinear and the partial cannot attribute signal to span. Use ONE W level for the primary span ladder.
- Sign-flip-of-the-MEAN permutation as the primary paired test -- fragile to v4's 3.3% right-tail token spikes (>2x median); replaced by median + Wilcoxon signed-rank.
- Treating prompt_words as a 'near-no-op covariate like v4' -- it is a span-collinear ACTIVE confound (rho up to 1.0 within W); must be an accept/reject invariant, not a regression afterthought.
- A 2-level (binary) within-W span axis -- too thin for the dose-response that outcomes [3]/[4] lean on; the span ladder needs >=4 distinct levels per work level.

## Open questions
- Does the V4 work->tokens slope survive a SMALLER budget (low/medium tier), i.e. is +0.894 need-driven or a fixed-high-budget allocation artifact? (TIER-SWEEP answers this directly.)
- Can a matched WIDE-vs-DEEP pair be built where span is genuinely decoupled (corr<0.1) from prompt_words, attended-real-lines, AND live-var count simultaneously -- or does the reduction step inherently re-introduce one of them? (The offline rebuild gate is the test.)
- Is 'serial-carry length along the answer path' separable from Brent critical-path SPAN in these items, given they numerically coincide? A WIDE variant whose longest block equals DEEP's chain but with independent siblings would dissociate parallelism from carried-value-chain length.
- If the model SERIALIZES wide problems internally, does depth still cost EXTRA beyond work -- or is a null under serialization the correct (not spurious) demotion? The matched contrast keeps this clean only if the confounds are removed.
- Speculation / register-shift: is 'reasoning_tokens' fundamentally a TRANSCRIPTION meter (scratchpad lines emitted) rather than a DEPTH meter, such that NO fixed-work manipulation can make it rise with span -- making the honest 'latent camera' reading that it measures lines-written, with depth invisible to this instrument by construction? Tier-sweep outcome [III] (constant tokens/op across budgets) would be the first hard evidence for that ceiling.
- What questions should we be asking: instead of 'does effort track depth?', perhaps 'what is the smallest manipulation that moves reasoning_tokens at FIXED transcription length?' -- if span at fixed lines does not move it but answer-uncertainty or backtracking-inducing traps do, the camera measures search/retry volume, not depth. Worth a cheap trap-vs-clean matched probe alongside the span ladder.
