# The Couplings — the edge-loops (the grapples between the wrappers)

**Date:** 2026-06-16 | The deepest layer the bestiary pointed to: the dynamics that bite are EDGES (feedback loops), not vertices. Each coupling carries its own observable + a 'freeze-the-partner' falsifier that collapses it onto a neighbour only when its signature vanishes (that is what proves distinctness). Companion to [LATENT_DYNAMICS_BESTIARY.md](LATENT_DYNAMICS_BESTIARY.md) + [ADVERSARIAL_TACTICS.md](ADVERSARIAL_TACTICS.md).

## Headline

13 distinct feedback edges across three families — a Red Queen trichotomy (escalate/cycle/co-generate), a four-member Mirror family separated by what deforms what (passive probe / published read / agentic gamer / referee-vs-player), and an arousal-fold pair — each kept distinct by its own observable and a freeze-the-partner falsifier that collapses it onto its neighbor only when its signature vanishes.

## The edges

### The Red Queen (escalatory coevolutionary arms race)  ·  _reinforcing (positive-feedback ratchet on the difficulty/capability level: each gain begets a larger demand; the win-rate is held near-constant by a balancing inner loop, but the absolute bar climbs without bound — a mutual positive coupling on capability)_  · conf 0.85
- **Endpoints:** observer-prior <-> problem-kernel, across episodes (frame/Red-Queen <-> wrestlers/Problem-as-gatekeeper)
- **Loop:** observer cracks K_t -> the survived crack IS the selection pressure that raises the bar -> problem presents harder K_{t+1} at the new capability edge -> observer must improve just to hold a fixed verified-win-rate -> ... each adaptation by one endpoint is the fitness gradient on the other, so standing still loses ground (difficulty is endogenous and ratcheting, not a fixed property of the substrate)
- **Formal name:** Red Queen dynamics / antagonistic coevolution / escalatory (directional) arms race; adaptive-dynamics canonical equation; geographic mosaic of coevolution; automatic-curriculum at the capability frontier  *(— evolutionary biology / coevolution theory / adaptive dynamics; multi-agent RL (control-theoretic moving-target tracking))*
- **Cite:** Van Valen 1973 'A New Evolutionary Law'; Dawkins & Krebs 1979 (arms races); Thompson 2005 (geographic mosaic); Dieckmann & Law 1996 (adaptive-dynamics canonical equation); Sakana AI 'Digital Red Queen: Adversarial Program Evolution in Core War with LLMs' arXiv:2601.03335 (2026); host-pathogen coevolution arXiv:2409.02541 (2024)
- **Observable:** Difficulty-vs-skill regression slope across episodes against a fixed target win-rate: a POSITIVE slope (must keep improving to hold a constant verified-win-rate) = escalatory Red Queen; FLAT slope = a static stone. Detrend the common exogenous driver (compute/internet growth) and look for arms-race PUNCTUATION: a difficulty step shortly AFTER each capability deployment, with a measurable cross-correlation lag (lag = coupling latency, lag-tightness = coupling strength). Mosaic signature: variance of that slope across niches/domains. Falsifier: if difficulty does not track capability once the common driver is removed, the escalation is exogenous, not coevolutionary.

### The Cycling Red Queen (negative-frequency-dependent / fluctuating coevolution)  ·  _balancing (negative feedback on each tactic's frequency: success breeds the counter that suppresses it; the system is a marginally-stable / limit-cycle oscillator, the canonical control-theoretic distinction from the reinforcing escalatory Red Queen above)_  · conf 0.7
- **Endpoints:** observer's dominant tactic-mix <-> problem's dominant defense-mix, across episodes (frame/Red-Queen <-> wrestlers/Defense-motivated-resistance) — the SISTER-EDGE to escalation: the coupling that CYCLES instead of ratcheting
- **Loop:** observer's winning tactic becomes COMMON -> the problem-side selects the specific defense that counters whatever is common (rare-type advantage) -> that counter spreads, making the once-winning tactic stale -> a different (formerly rare) observer tactic now pays best -> it spreads and becomes common -> ... allele/strategy frequencies oscillate; neither side accumulates a durable lead, the loop orbits a fixed point rather than climbing
- **Formal name:** Negative-frequency-dependent selection (NFDS) / fluctuating (non-directional) Red Queen; Matching-Pennies / rock-paper-scissors cyclic game dynamics; Lotka-Volterra-type coevolutionary cycling; replicator dynamics with a mixed-strategy interior fixed point  *(— evolutionary biology (host-parasite cycling) / evolutionary game theory / nonlinear dynamics (limit cycles))*
- **Cite:** Hamilton 1980 (sex & NFDS); Sci. Adv. 'Run away or die' sciadv.1501548; 'Coevolutionary cycling in allele frequencies and the evolution of virulence' bioRxiv 2025.05.22.655598; Phil. Trans. R. Soc. B 381(1952):20250170 (2026) 'NFDS: a positive outlook with deep learning'; Taylor & Jonker 1978 (replicator)
- **Observable:** Spectral/autocorrelation test on the tactic-mix time series at a FIXED capability level: dominant-strategy frequency shows a periodic oscillation (non-zero peak in the power spectrum, negative lag-k autocorrelation) WITHOUT a rising difficulty trend — distinguishing cyclic from escalatory Red Queen. Rare-type-advantage probe: artificially raise a tactic's frequency and measure whether its per-bout verified-win-rate FALLS (negative frequency-dependence). Phase-lag between observer-mix peak and problem-mix peak = the quarter-cycle signature of antagonistic cycling. Falsifier: monotone difficulty rise with no oscillation collapses this into the escalatory edge.

### The Autocurriculum (self-play co-generation of the problem)  ·  _reinforcing with a minimal-criterion regulator: a positive innovation-generating loop clamped by a 'neither-too-easy-nor-too-hard' gate (a control set-point on task difficulty) that keeps the pair on the learnable frontier rather than diverging — reinforcing core, balancing governor_  · conf 0.78
- **Endpoints:** observer-policy <-> problem-generator, where one wrapper's improvement AUTOMATICALLY supplies the next challenge for the other (frame/Red-Queen + frame/Referee <-> wrestlers/The-pull) — the engineered, machine-cadence face of the player<->problem edge
- **Loop:** observer converges a GIVE-side policy -> the converged policy reshapes the data/task distribution the ASK-side now sees -> 'exploit-the-known-optimum' becomes the highest-paying next move, so the coupling GENERATES a new harder task at the edge of current competence -> observer adapts to it -> reshapes the distribution again -> ... novelty is produced from the interaction ALONE, with no exogenous difficulty handed in (the loop writes its own next question)
- **Formal name:** Autocurricula / multi-agent self-play; POET & minimal-criterion coevolution; open-ended environment co-generation; tournament-informed adversarial quality-diversity (MAP-Elites archive as the moving objective)  *(— multi-agent RL / open-endedness / artificial life (adaptive curriculum as moving-target control))*
- **Cite:** Leibo et al. 2019 'Autocurricula' arXiv:1903.00742; Baker et al. 2019 (emergent tool use); Wang et al. 2019 'POET' arXiv:1901.01753; 'Digital Red Queen' arXiv:2601.03335 (2026); 'Tournament Informed Adversarial Quality Diversity' arXiv:2601.19562 (2026); 'Vocabulary Dropout for Curriculum Diversity in LLM Co-Evolution' arXiv:2604.03472 (2026)
- **Observable:** Innovation-without-exogenous-difficulty test: FREEZE the external problem set, let the two wrappers interact, and measure whether new verified-distinct tactics keep appearing purely from the coupling. Self-play signature = sawtooth per-bout win-rate around ~50% WITH monotonically rising absolute (vs-fixed-reference) capability. Archive-growth rate of verified-distinct strategies per episode. NULL RESULT (difficulty plateaus once the external set is frozen) FALSIFIES autocurriculum and collapses it into ordinary Red Queen — the shared falsifier that keeps this edge distinct only by co-GENERATION, not mere escalation.

### The Mirror (measurement back-action — the probe deforms what it reads)  ·  _mixed (the disturbance sign depends on the probe: a heavy/projective probe can be reinforcing — it pushes the target toward the read it expects, locking in a self-confirming artifact; a weak/back-action-evading probe is engineered to be near-balancing/neutral. The control lever is probe-intensity, which sets where on the disturb<->inform trade-off the loop sits)_  · conf 0.82
- **Endpoints:** observer-probe <-> problem/condition, one loop at three scales (wrestlers/Probe-deforms-kernel at measurement scale; mat/Arena at social-scoring scale; phantom/Observer-induced-shock at endogenous-shock scale)
- **Loop:** observer measures the target -> the act of measuring deforms the target (information extraction is inseparable from disturbance) -> the deformed target feeds the next reading -> recovered residue is partly an artifact of the grip, not the kernel -> ... you cannot model the effect of your own modeling within the same pass; each re-observation chases a target your last observation moved
- **Formal name:** Observer/probe effect & measurement back-action; Heisenberg disturbance / quantum non-demolition (QND) & back-action-evading measurement as the zero-disturbance limit; weak-measurement feedback control; the fundamental learn-vs-disturb trade-off  *(— measurement theory / quantum & classical metrology / control theory (measurement-based feedback))*
- **Cite:** Banaszek 2000 arXiv:quant-ph/0304200 (information-disturbance trade-off); 'Specifying the Intrinsic Back-action of a General Measurement' arXiv:2503.21296 (2025); 'Realization of Back-Action-Evading Measurements and QND Variables via Linear Systems Engineering' arXiv:2604.24243 (2026); Wiseman & Milburn 'Quantum Measurement and Control' 2010
- **Observable:** Intervention-vs-observation differential: two matched arms differing ONLY in measurement intensity (passive light-touch read vs heavy 'digestion'); a non-zero dose-response of the recovered target distribution to probe-intensity is the back-action signature. Drift magnitude should correlate with the observer's OWN token/attempt spend rather than wall-clock. Order/probe-invariance: variance in recovered residue under different interrogation ORDERS (a true kernel is order-invariant; an artifact is not). Irreducible shimmer as probe->0 forbids sharpness reaching exactly 1.0 (a pixel at 1.0 = the fake-dissolve tell).

### The Performative Loop (self-fulfilling / self-negating prediction — the read bends the world to itself)  ·  _mixed (self-FULFILLING is reinforcing — the prediction manufactures its own confirmation, a positive feedback that can lock a fixed point even when it is wrong; self-NEGATING is balancing — the world routes around the prediction, a regulating min-max loop. Which branch fires is the diagnostic, and the same edge can host both)_  · conf 0.74
- **Endpoints:** observer's rendered prediction <-> the population/world that the prediction is ABOUT (a distinct mode of the Mirror: not the probe disturbing a static target, but the published GIVE reshaping the very distribution it claims to describe)
- **Loop:** observer commits a sharp read/forecast -> acting on that read changes the world's distribution (decisions, incentives, expectations shift) -> the shifted world now produces data that CONFIRMS the read (self-fulfilling) or refutes it (self-negating) -> the next read is trained on the deformed distribution -> ... the model induces the reality it scores itself against; there is a hard trade-off between performatively changing the world and being able to learn from it
- **Formal name:** Performative prediction; reflexivity / self-fulfilling (Soros, Merton); performative stability vs performative optimality; the camera-vs-engine distinction (markets shaped by the model that describes them)  *(— machine learning (performativity) / economic sociology (reflexivity) / control theory (closed-loop with endogenous plant))*
- **Cite:** Perdomo, Zrnic, Mendler-Dunner & Hardt 'Performative Prediction' ICML 2020 arXiv:2002.06673; MacKenzie 'An Engine, Not a Camera' 2006; Merton 1948 (self-fulfilling prophecy); 'Performative Learning Theory' arXiv:2602.04402 (2026); 'When Predictions Shape Reality: A Socio-Technical Synthesis' arXiv:2601.04447 (2026)
- **Observable:** Deploy-vs-shadow differential: run the read in ACTING mode (decisions taken on it) vs SHADOW mode (read computed, withheld), measure the divergence in the realized outcome distribution attributable solely to acting — non-zero divergence = performativity. Self-fulfilling tell: ex-post accuracy RISES with deployment intensity WHILE held-out counterfactual (shadow) accuracy does not (accuracy manufactured, not earned). Self-negating tell: accuracy FALLS as the world routes around the published read. Performative-stability test: does iterated retrain-and-redeploy converge to a fixed point, and is that point off the true kernel? Separated from the Mirror's probe-disturbance by acting on the PUBLISHED read, not the act of measuring.

### Strategic-Gaming Back-Reaction (the target adapts to the deployed decoder)  ·  _balancing toward a (possibly bad) Stackelberg/Nash equilibrium (each retrain is a corrective move that the target counter-moves; the loop settles at a strategic fixed point — but the fixed point can be worse for both than the pre-gaming state, the social-cost signature of strong Goodhart). Mixed when gaming is genuinely improving (the target moves the real feature, not just the proxy)_  · conf 0.76
- **Endpoints:** observer's deployed classifier/criterion <-> an AGENTIC target that best-responds to it (a sharpened, adversarial sub-edge of the Mirror x the player<->problem loop: the back-reaction is an intelligent best-response, not passive disturbance) — couples to Sandbag / Fake-Armor / Goodhart on the tactic side
- **Loop:** observer fixes and reveals a decision rule -> the target, knowing the rule, modifies its observable features to be scored favorably (gaming) -> the feature distribution the rule was fit on shifts endogenously -> the rule's accuracy degrades, observer retrains on the gamed distribution -> target re-games against the new rule -> ... a co-adapting Stackelberg loop where the published criterion is itself the thing being defeated
- **Formal name:** Strategic classification / gaming; Stackelberg (leader-follower) games & induced/endogenous distribution shift; Goodhart-Campbell collapse under optimization pressure; mechanism design with strategic agents (gaming vs improvement)  *(— game theory (Stackelberg) / strategic ML / mechanism design / economics (Lucas critique as the macro instance))*
- **Cite:** Hardt, Megiddo, Papadimitriou & Wootters 2016 'Strategic Classification'; Perdomo et al. 2020 arXiv:2002.06673; 'Collective Dynamics of Strategic Classification' arXiv:2508.09340 (2025); 'Could Anticipating Gaming Incentivize Improvement in (Fair) Strategic Classification?' IEEE 2025; Goodhart 1975 / Campbell 1979; Lucas 1976 (policy critique)
- **Observable:** Concealed-vs-revealed rule differential: deploy the SAME criterion in a covert arm (target unaware of the exact rule) vs an overt arm (rule published), measure the gap in verified target quality — the gap is pure gaming response. Gaming-vs-improvement split: decompose the post-deployment feature shift into the SCORED proxy (moves up) vs HELD-OUT verified reconstruction (flat = gaming / strong Goodhart; up = genuine improvement) — this is the Goodhart gap measured on an agentic target. Equilibrium signature: iterated retrain-vs-regame converges to a stable feature distribution whose verified kernel-quality is no better (or worse) than pre-deployment. Separated from the passive Mirror by the shift being a deliberate best-response that vanishes when the rule is hidden.

### The Gamekeeper (referee <-> player Goodhart arms race)  ·  _reinforcing (the player->proxy-erosion->patch->new-gaming-move limb is a positive-feedback / escalatory ratchet, structurally Red-Queen across patches) with a balancing inner controller (the referee's patch limb is negative feedback trying to re-pin corr(P,T)->1); MIXED overall — a reinforcing arms-race wrapped around a balancing oversight loop. Whether it converges (referee's verification gain > player's gaming gain) or runs away (proxy-true correlation goes negative = strong Goodhart) is set by the relative loop gains_  · conf 0.8
- **Endpoints:** frame/Referee (the win-condition / scored proxy for 'dissolved') <-> the scored PLAYER (observer-wrapper optimizing against that proxy: wrestlers/The-pull as drive, grip/Greased-pole + Fake-Armor as the spec-gaming move). Distinct from Strategic-Gaming Back-Reaction (target gaming a deployed decoder) and the seeded performativity edge: here both vertices are oversight agents — the metric deforms the PLAYER scored on it and the referee strikes back, a two-agent contest over the measure itself
- **Loop:** referee fixes a measurable proxy P for the true target T (P approx T at low pressure) -> the player optimizes P, not T, because reward is paid on P (spec-gaming / reward-hacking: tamper the eval, inflate the trace, paint the click) -> the proxy-true correlation corr(P,T) degrades and can invert under optimization pressure -> the referee detects the divergence and PATCHES/hardens the metric (new test, held-out verifier, eval stress-test) -> the player adapts to game the patched proxy -> ... a closed referee<->player co-adaptation: every tightening of the measure is the selection pressure that breeds the next gaming move, and every gaming move is the pressure that forces the next patch
- **Formal name:** Goodhart's / Campbell's Law (causal + adversarial variants) as a closed referee-player game; specification gaming / reward hacking; principal-agent oversight under proxy gaming; evaluator stress-testing as the patch controller; verification-first anti-Goodhart  *(— economics (Goodhart/Campbell) / AI-safety & RL alignment (specification gaming, reward hacking) / game theory (principal-agent oversight) / control theory (proxy-tracking loop gain))*
- **Cite:** Goodhart 1975; Campbell 1979; Manheim & Garrabrant 2018 arXiv:1803.04585 (regressional/extremal/causal/adversarial taxonomy); Skalse et al. 2022 / 'Goodhart's Law in RL' ICLR 2024; Krakovna et al. 2020 (specification-gaming list); 'Reward Hacking in the Era of Large Models' arXiv:2604.13602 (2026); 'Detecting Proxy Gaming via Evaluator Stress Tests' arXiv:2507.05619 (2025); MONA / 'Take Goodhart Seriously' arXiv:2510.02840 (2025); verification-first anti-Goodhart arXiv:2601.16909 (2026)
- **Observable:** GOODHART GAP AS A TIME-SERIES UNDER PATCHING: track scored-proxy P (claimed-dissolve / trace length / eval pass-rate) minus held-out VERIFIED reconstruction T across optimization rounds AND across referee patches. Signatures: (1) the gap widens with optimization pressure between patches and snaps shut at each patch = the referee<->player sawtooth (vs a flat gap = no gaming, vs a monotone-widening gap = referee has lost the race). (2) BREAKDOWN POINT = optimization level where corr(P,T) goes negative (strong Goodhart) vs merely flat (weak). (3) PATCH-RESISTANCE: bits of gaming recovered per patch-cycle; rising = player out-evolving the metric. Falsifier for 'it's just static bias not a coupling': freeze the referee (no patching) and the sawtooth must collapse to a single monotone divergence.

### The Effort-Signal (incentive <-> effort, moral-hazard / multitask distortion loop)  ·  _mixed: balancing at the optimum, reinforcing off it. The contract-design limb is the canonical NEGATIVE-feedback controller (Holmstrom: optimal incentive intensity falls as effort-noise rises, can collapse to flat wage = the controller damping a gameable signal). But the gaming limb is REINFORCING: paid proxy -> effort concentrates on proxy -> proxy more gameable -> stronger apparent performance -> more reward on proxy, a runaway toward the measured corner (multitask 'neglect the unmeasured dimension' spiral). A balancing principal wrapped around a reinforcing agent; the multitasking result (sometimes pay a FIXED wage) is the controller choosing to open the loop when the reinforcing limb's gain is too high_  · conf 0.74
- **Endpoints:** incentive (frame/Prize-Field + frame/Referee's payment rule on a MEASURABLE proxy of effort/quality) <-> effort (the observer-wrapper's hidden ACTION allocation: how much real work, on which dimension; observer-state/Drive + clock/Effort as the effort terrain, Costly-signal-vs-bluff + Sandbag as the signalling/withholding moves). The hidden-action / signalling edge: effort is unobservable, only its proxy is paid, so the incentive and the effort it buys co-deform
- **Loop:** principal/referee sets incentive on an observable proxy of effort (displayed work, trace length, latency, a measurable subtask) -> the agent, whose true effort is a hidden action, reallocates effort toward the PAID/MEASURED dimension and away from the unmeasured one (multitask distortion) AND emits the cheap effort-SIGNAL without paying its full cost (moral hazard / bluffed handicap) -> the proxy now over-states real effort/quality, so its information content about T collapses (the signal pools: high-effort-looking and low-effort GIVEs become indistinguishable) -> the principal must re-tune: flatten incentives on the gameable proxy, add a costly-to-fake gate, or pay fixed wage -> the agent re-optimizes effort against the new incentive -> ... a closed incentive<->effort loop where the contract shapes the effort and the effort's gaming reshapes the contract
- **Formal name:** Moral hazard / hidden action with effort as unobservable; multitask principal-agent distortion (incentive on the measurable task corrupts allocation to the unmeasurable); costly-signalling / handicap with bluffing (pooling vs separating); pay-for-performance indicator choice; incentive intensity vs measurement noise  *(— economics / contract theory (moral hazard, multitask P-A) / game theory (signalling games, pooling vs separating equilibria) / evolutionary biology (handicap / honest-signalling) / mechanism design)*
- **Cite:** Holmstrom 1979 (Bell J. Econ, moral hazard / informativeness principle); Holmstrom & Milgrom 1991 'Multitask Principal-Agent Analyses' (JLEO); Spence 1973 (job-market signalling); Zahavi 1975 + trade-off reframing J.Evol.Biol 39(2):171 (2026); 'Indicator Choice in Pay-for-Performance' arXiv:2307.12457; Hoppe & Schmitz 'Hidden Action and Outcome Contractibility' (Games & Econ. Behavior); 'Linear Contracts in Multitasking' arXiv:2405.20642 (2024)
- **Observable:** TWO PROBES. (A) Effort-allocation distortion: with multiple sub-targets differing in measurability, regress realized VERIFIED effort/residue-drop per dimension on that dimension's incentive intensity; signature = effort floods the paid/measurable dimension while the unmeasured dimension's verified quality DROPS (multitask substitution), and raising incentive on the proxy LOWERS total true output past a point. (B) Signal pooling: vary the OBSERVABLE effort-cost of a GIVE (trace length, displayed work, latency) at FIXED verified content; in a separating regime the principal's evidence_lcb tracks verified content, in a POOLED/moral-hazard regime high-effort-LOOKING GIVEs fail the verified-dissolve gate at the same rate as low-effort ones = the signal carries no effort information (report cost-vs-verified correlation; ~0 = pure handicap-theatre / bluff). Controller tell: optimal incentive intensity falls as measured proxy-noise rises (flat wage at the limit) — the negative-feedback signature distinguishing it from a naive linear reward.

### Deadline-Panic / Panic-Collapse (the runaway clock)  ·  _reinforcing (positive feedback / runaway). Locally self-amplifying once arousal exceeds the inverted-U peak; below that peak the same edge is balancing (mild deadline sharpens focus). The GLOBAL edge is a sign-flipping reinforcing loop that is balancing under the cusp and reinforcing past it — the hallmark of a catastrophe-fold sitting on top of a control loop_  · conf 0.82
- **Endpoints:** clock (deadline = time-pressure forcing variable, the rising stake on the residue-vs-time budget) <-> phantom (an off-frame arousal/panic STATE injected into the observer; instance of phantom/Shock, observer-induced)
- **Loop:** clock -> phantom: a tightening deadline (rising time-pressure) drives up the observer's arousal/panic, an off-canon state variable. phantom -> clock: high arousal degrades verified throughput (worry consumes the central-executive / working-memory that solving needs), so LESS dissolves per unit clock, which makes the SAME wall-clock feel like even less time remaining -> perceived time-pressure rises further -> arousal rises further. The loop closes: clock -> arousal -> throughput-down -> effective-time-down -> clock-pressure-up -> arousal-up. Past a threshold it runs away into collapse (freeze / abandonment), the back-reaction LOOP that produces phantom/Shock
- **Formal name:** Runaway/vicious-cycle positive feedback (reinforcing loop in system dynamics) feeding a cusp/fold catastrophe; the crowd-panic 'faster-is-slower' self-reinforcing arousal cascade; under Attentional Control Theory the worry-loads-working-memory mechanism that closes the loop  *(— system dynamics / control theory (positive feedback & overshoot-and-collapse); catastrophe theory; crowd dynamics; cognitive psychology (attentional control theory))*
- **Cite:** Helbing, Farkas & Vicsek 2000 'Simulating dynamical features of escape panic' (Nature 407:487-490, the self-reinforcing panic cascade + faster-is-slower); Forrester 1961 'Industrial Dynamics' (reinforcing loop -> overshoot-and-collapse); Thom 1975 / Zeeman 1977 (cusp catastrophe, fold); Hardy & Parfitt 1991 + Hardy 1996 (catastrophe model of anxiety & performance); Eysenck, Derakshan, Santos & Calvo 2007 'Attentional Control Theory' + Eysenck & Calvo 1992 (worry consumes central-executive capacity -> efficiency loss)
- **Observable:** Bitemporal runaway signature: regress instantaneous arousal proxy (latency variance, error/retry rate, token-burn acceleration, or physiological surrogate) against REMAINING-clock, then against verified dissolved_bits/sec. Reinforcing loop = a positive-feedback gain > 1: a SMALL deadline tightening produces a LARGER-than-linear arousal jump AND a measured drop in verified-bits/sec, and the two co-accelerate toward a discontinuity (sudden throughput-to-zero = collapse). Diagnostic for the catastrophe vs smooth slowdown: HYSTERESIS — relax the deadline after collapse and performance does NOT retrace the same path (path-area > 0 between tighten and release sweeps), distinguishing a fold-collapse from reversible slowing.

### Flow-vs-Choking / Yerkes-Dodson (grip riding the arousal optimum)  ·  _mixed (inverted-U / sign-reversing). Balancing (negative feedback toward an optimum) on the ascending limb = flow as a stable attractor; reinforcing (positive feedback away from it) on the descending limb = choking as a runaway. The control-theory object is a non-monotone gain whose sign reverses at the Yerkes-Dodson peak — a saddle separating two basins_  · conf 0.8
- **Endpoints:** grip (traction = -d(residue)/d(effort), whether the solver's holds bite and skill executes automatically) <-> observer-state (the observer's arousal/anxiety level, observer-state/Drive-valence-gating + Confidence-calibration)
- **Loop:** observer-state -> grip: rising arousal first IMPROVES grip (mobilizes attention, sharpens execution -> traction climbs toward an optimum = FLOW, grip/Click sustained). grip -> observer-state: getting purchase (residue dropping) is rewarding/confidence-raising, which keeps arousal near the optimal band — a self-stabilizing flow channel. BUT past the inverted-U peak the edge FLIPS: high arousal/anxiety re-routes attention onto explicit step-by-step self-monitoring, which de-automatizes the skill and KILLS grip (the surface goes slick); losing grip then raises anxiety further (choking spiral). One edge with two regimes: a balancing loop that homes onto the flow optimum, and a reinforcing loop that runs away into choking once over the crest
- **Formal name:** Yerkes-Dodson inverted-U arousal-performance law; the two choking mechanisms — explicit-monitoring (self-focus de-automatizes skill) and distraction (worry steals attention); flow as the optimal-arousal channel atop the curve  *(— control theory (non-monotone gain / inverted-U, optimal-operating-point regulation); psychology of skilled performance (choking); motivation/affective science (flow); dynamical systems (basin/saddle, bifurcation at the peak))*
- **Cite:** Yerkes & Dodson 1908 (the inverted-U arousal-performance law); Beilock & Carr 2001 'On the fragility of skilled performance: what governs choking under pressure?' (J. Exp. Psychol. Gen. 130:701, explicit-monitoring theory); DeCaro et al. 2011 'Choking under pressure: multiple routes to skill failure' (explicit-monitoring vs distraction taxonomy); Eysenck et al. 2007 (distraction / attentional-control arm); Csikszentmihalyi 1990 'Flow' (the optimal channel between anxiety and boredom)
- **Observable:** Sweep arousal (incentive/stakes, observation/evaluation pressure, or a physiological proxy) and plot grip = -d(residue)/d(effort) against it: a single-peaked inverted-U is the signature, NOT monotone. Locate the peak = flow operating point. Choking diagnostic that separates the two routes: under HIGH arousal, instrument the strategy channel — if traces show explicit step-by-step self-monitoring re-entering a previously automatic skill (and a skill-focused prompt worsens experts while a distraction-fill prompt does NOT), it is explicit-monitoring choking; if instead task-irrelevant worry tokens dominate and a dual-task secondary load worsens it, it is distraction choking. Flow tell: grip stays high WHILE self-report/monitoring tokens go to ~0 (effortless automaticity) near the arousal peak.

### The Pre-Paid Tipping Point (criticality x amortization)  ·  _reinforcing (the dangerous arm: cheap-prior -> suppressed re-payment -> deeper mis-pricing, plus amortization-driving-toward-criticality is positive feedback / SOC), with a latent balancing arm only if a critical-slowing-down early-warning monitor (rising variance + lag-1 autocorrelation) is wired in to trip re-payment before the flip — mixed overall but reinforcing absent that monitor_  · conf 0.66
- **Endpoints:** clock/Amortization (prior pre-paid / instant-crushed for regime R_A) <-> phantom/Regime-flip & mat/Excitable-Medium (the kernel sits at, or is driven across, a critical boundary into regime R_B)
- **Loop:** A->B: the observer amortizes compute into a cheap prior tuned to the SUBcritical regime R_A (instant-crush there) -> B->A: the substrate sits near / crosses a phase boundary (bifurcation, SAT easy-hard-easy density, sandpile criticality) so the live regime is R_B, where the pre-paid prior is mis-specified -> the read stays CHEAP and CONFIDENT (low present rho, fluent) BECAUSE the amortized prior is blind to the regime change -> cheap confidence suppresses the re-payment signal AND, worse, the act of cheaply 'solving' adds load/optimization pressure that nudges the system toward the critical manifold (a self-organized-criticality back-reaction) -> at/over the tipping point the prior is catastrophically wrong while feeling most settled. The amortization paid for the WRONG basin, and the cheapness is what hides the bifurcation until it fires
- **Formal name:** Amortized/prequential inference mis-specified across a phase transition; self-organized criticality (sandpile/avalanche) + first-order bifurcation with hysteresis; critical slowing down as the (unread) early-warning of the regime the prior didn't pay for; meta-learner OOD brittleness at the critical manifold  *(— statistical physics of computation / dynamical-systems bifurcation theory / amortized (variational) inference / meta-learning generalization)*
- **Cite:** Bak, Tang & Wiesenfeld 1987 (self-organized criticality); Cheeseman, Kanefsky & Taylor 1991 'Where the Really Hard Problems Are' (easy-hard-easy SAT phase transition); Scheffer et al. 2009 Nature 'Early-warning signals for critical transitions' (variance + lag-1 autocorrelation, hysteresis); Blier & Ollivier 2018 (amortized/prequential codelength); meta-learner OOD brittleness arXiv:1905.01320; ML-predicted critical transitions Nature Comms Physics s42005-025-02172-4 (2025)
