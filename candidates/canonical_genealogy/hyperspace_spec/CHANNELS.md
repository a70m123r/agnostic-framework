## guess-who — A deductive inference game over the dynamics substrate: the player is shown ONE creature/tactic/organ's measured observable-signature (a resistance-curve shape, a dose-response, a hysteresis loop, a cross-correlation lag) and guesses which of the 88 records it is — narrowing by choosing which OTHER observables to reveal, where every flippable board tile is a measured trace pulled from a record's `observable` field, never an invented decoration.

- **Data source:** Reads directly from D:\PlatformOperator\research\pav\candidates\canonical_genealogy\hyperspace_spec\dynamics\compiled\dynamics.compiled.json — 88 records (41 creature, 37 tactic, 10 coupling) across 12 families. Per-tile fields consumed: `observable` (signature glyph + the only legal source of any visual claim), `family` (lane), `name`+`id` (hidden answer), `phys_latent` (rail position), `certainty`/`render` (opacity, COIN render≤support), `lifecycle` (verification pips), `creatures_underlain` (linkage threads), `citation` (the council-ask payload). Board-level it reads `stats.by_family`/`by_lifecycle` for lane widths and `stats.reduction.creature_to_tactics` (40 entries) + `stats.reduction.ratio` for the six-organ collapse meter and the cross-tile elimination graph.
- **Interaction:** Round loop: (1) The host (agnostic core simulating the chosen adversarial X) flips ONE random tile face-up, showing only its signature-glyph — the player sees a shape (e.g. a flat bits_remaining floor) with its family lane, phys_latent position, render-opacity, and verification-pips, but NOT the name. (2) The player narrows by spending reveals: they pick which OTHER observable-axis to expose next ('show me everyone whose signature is a regression slope', 'show me everyone with a STEP discontinuity', 'eliminate the latent half of the rail', 'only documented-lifecycle tiles'). Each reveal flips/dims a swath of tiles per the encodings above and lights the linkage threads. (3) The player commits a guess by clicking the surviving tile; the host reveals name + record. Scoring = (reveals_spent, weighted by each tile's certainty) — guessing off a fogged low-render tile is penalized, rewarding play that narrows on high-verification signatures. A sendPrompt() hook lets the player ask the council 'why does THIS signature belong to THIS organ?' to pull the full observable text + citation. Inverse mode: host names the creature, player must draw/select the predicted signature-glyph, then it's checked against record.observable (falsification in action).
- **Makes legible:** Turns a 194KB / 88-record adversarial bestiary that no one would read tile-by-tile into a deductive game you win by reasoning over MEASURED shapes. A consumer never has to swallow the taxonomy whole: they meet one signature at a time, learn that 'flat floor under rising budget' = a gatekeeping defense and 'positive difficulty-vs-skill slope' = a Red Queen coupling, and the narrowing forces them to internalize which observables actually discriminate. It does NOT dumb down — the glyphs are faithful to the real `observable` text, the COIN opacity law keeps low-evidence tiles honestly fogged, and the six-organ meter delivers the substrate's hard claim (88 surface faces compress to ~6 metabolisms) as the felt win-insight rather than a buried sentence in stats.reduction.
- **Falsifiable constraint:** The whole channel rests on one claim: that a creature/tactic is IDENTIFIABLE from its observable signature alone — i.e. the `observable` fields are discriminative enough that narrowing by revealed signatures converges to a unique tile. This is falsifiable and can be wrong three ways: (1) COLLISION — if two records in different families share an indistinguishable glyph (e.g. coupling/Red-Queen and tactic:meta-evolution/Red-Queen escalation BOTH read 'positive difficulty-vs-skill regression slope, cross-correlation lag' — they are near-duplicates in this very substrate), the game cannot separate them by signature and the 'no observable, no visual' rule produces two identical tiles, breaking unique-guess. (2) DEGENERATE LANE — families like tactic:problem-defense (n=12) where many tiles collapse to 'flat rho(effort) floor' give the player no discriminating reveal, so reveal-count cannot drop the candidate set below the family size. (3) RENDER-LAW VIOLATION — if any tile were drawn sharper than its certainty (render>support) the COIN law is broken and the visual is lying. Concretely testable: count signature-collisions across the 88 observables; if collisions > a few, the Guess-Who channel is NOT well-posed and must fall back to revealing name-bearing metadata (which would violate 'every symbol maps to a measured observable'). The game's score-distribution (median reveals-to-unique) IS the running measurement of how discriminative the substrate's observables actually are.

## ticker — A live market-ticker for latent dynamics: the agnostic camera turned into a cockpit, streaming every measured reading of a running observer-vs-problem match in time, where each scrolling symbol is bound to one observable trace and nothing renders that wasn't measured.

- **Data source:** The agnostic-camera trace that a running match emits, tick by tick: the per-step verified-dissolve log (rho(effort), bits_remaining, reconstruct==W gate results), the two-clock meters (present rho-burn + amortized EDL/codelength drawdown), per-token EDL/MI-peak spikes, the scored-proxy-vs-verified divergence under the audience toggle, and the residue time-series statistics (lag-1 autocorrelation, variance, forward-prediction NLL). These are exactly the six pre-registered observables of the dynamics substrate (rho-curve, two-clock gap, hysteresis-loop area, EIG gain, Goodhart gap, verified-vs-claimed residue) streamed in t_obs order. Structurally each tick is an append-only fact in the SUBSTRATE_SPEC stream — value + provenance + retrieved_at + a verification bucket (corroborated/pending/disputed) — so the calibration LED is literally driven by the fact's verification state and disputed readings keep both values rather than overwriting.
- **Interaction:** A streaming cockpit you scrub like a market replay. The user scrubs the match timeline and pauses at any tick; pause AT the knee to watch the overthinking flip in slow motion. Live controls each fire a real perturbation and the needles respond from measured re-reads, not animation: (1) toggle SCORING on/off and watch the Goodhart-gap gauge widen and split by item-complexity (facilitation on easy, choking on hard); (2) inject NEGATIVE FEEDBACK and watch whether budget de-escalates (healthy) or keeps climbing (rabbit-hole); (3) pin an ANCHOR-PROBE and watch the mat drift out from under the live target; (4) raise the budget / bump reward / pause-and-resume to split CAN'T (r_floor) from WON'T (r_drive). Playback speed and the number of lit needles are the difficulty dials — a beginner runs slow with the twin clocks only; an expert runs fast with every gauge live. The phantom move is the standing question 'what needle SHOULD be here but isn't instrumented?' — clicking the Dark Gauge proposes a new observable to wire in, turning a blind spot into the next experiment.
- **Makes legible:** A dashboard offloads working memory onto the display — the canonical cure for high-element-interactivity extraneous load, which is exactly where naive 'desirable difficulty' backfires. The consumer chews at FULL difficulty because the hard content (which tactic drives which needle, why entrenchment is positive-slope rho, why the two clocks diverge) is absorbed as instrument-reading rather than memorized from 41 definitions; the drama of needles tripping and alarms firing carries attention while the load lands on germane schema-building. No dumbing-down: an illegal reading (a pixel at exactly 1.0 sharpness) is exactly the Law-3 fake-dissolve violation, and the Dark Gauge refuses to pretend it can read what it can't. Difficulty is tuned continuously by playback speed and how many needles are lit, so the same instrument serves novice and expert.
- **Falsifiable constraint:** The load-bearing claim is 'every symbol maps to a measured observable, and nothing renders that wasn't measured.' It is FALSIFIED if any needle moves with no backing trace in the substrate fact-stream — e.g. a Crack Spike printed without a passing reconstruct==W gate, a residue reading rendered at exactly 1.0 sharpness (the camera's own fake tell), or a gauge animated for drama rather than driven by a re-read. It is also falsified at the channel level by the 'no observable, no visual claim' rule: any gauge whose underlying observable cannot be computed from the streamed trace must go DARK (join the Dark Gauge), not display a plausible-but-uninstrumented value. Concretely: scrub to any tick and demand the fact_id + verification bucket behind each lit needle; a needle that cannot produce one is decoration and must be pulled. The deeper substrate claim the ticker rides on (that these readings reduce to ~6 shared metabolisms) remains a post-hoc-clustering CANDIDATE per the external pass — the ticker only commits to the per-symbol observable binding, not to that reduction.
