# The Latent Dynamics Bestiary

**The natural history of the latent jungle** — the dynamics in play when an observer tries to solve/digest a latent problem. The subject matter of *agnostic metrology* (the [[THE_LATENT_CAMERA]]); each creature is grounded with its established formal name (cite-don't-coin) and a **measurable observable** (no observable -> not documented, only *sighted*).

**Date:** 2026-06-16 | **Status:** Tier-3 catalogue, grounded by an Opus workflow (6 family scouts + a missing-creature critic + synthesis). From Pav's framing: *"it's a latent jungle full of strange creatures that we can point agnostic X at and expose, classify, document, and capture the lifecycle of, learning more with every iteration."*

## The frame

Every solve is a **wrestling match between two wrappers** — the observer (canon + tools + drive) and the problem (canon + facts, gatekeeping its own kernel) — fought **on a mat** (the medium/condition), **against a clock** (energy paid across time), **with a phantom in the room** (the unplanned), for **a prize** (the motive). Either body can crack the other; both leave changed. The instrument is a *wrestler in the match*, not a spectator — and it documents its own defeats as its richest data. The dynamics below cluster by which part of the match they live in.

**7 families · 41 creatures catalogued.**

## CLOCK — difficulty conserved and shifted across time (energy over time)

### Amortization (the pre-paid shutter)  `classified` · conf 0.82
- **What:** Difficulty is conserved but time-shifted: an 'easy' read is cheap NOW only because the compute/time was spent THEN, stored as the negentropy of the observer's prior; the instant-crush cashes stored credit.
- **In the framework:** Headline creature of DIGESTION_DYNAMICS sec 12; the COST-law beside the render-laws of THE_LATENT_CAMERA. Splits measured_bits into PRESENT effort (shutter) + AMORTIZED past effort (film ISO). Resolves observer-relativity as 'whose debt is settled'.
- **Formal name:** Amortized inference (ML) / amortized analysis (algorithms) / prequential MDL & Excess Description Length / logical depth (its physical face)  *(— machine learning + algorithm analysis + information theory)*
- **Cite:** Tarjan 'Amortized Computational Complexity' (1985); CLRS ch.17; 'Neural Methods for Amortized Inference' arXiv:2404.12484; Blier-Ollivier 2018; EDL arXiv:2601.04728; Bennett 'Logical Depth and Physical Complexity' (1988)
- **Observable (how agnostic X exposes it):** Measure BOTH clocks and report their ratio. PRESENT = verified resistance-curve rho(effort) against a pinned observer; AMORTIZED = prequential/EDL learning-debt (cold vs warm codelength) OR cross-epoch decoder gap D(1789) vs D(2026) OR logical-depth proxy (run-time of shortest regenerating program). Signature of an amortized item: LOW present rho but HIGH amortized credit.

### Maintenance (energy to HOLD against decay)  `tracked` · conf 0.6
- **What:** Holding a fact/skill/representation steady against drift costs continuous energy (refresh, rehearsal, error-correction). Stop paying and it slides.
- **In the framework:** The 'against a clock' edge as a HOLDING cost, not a solving cost; the running cost of keeping a dissolved stone dissolved. Thermo-welds to sec 12 e-units: held bits cost Joules to refresh (storage face of Landauer). Cost-dual of the forgetting creature.
- **Formal name:** Stability-plasticity dilemma / rehearsal & regularization cost; thermodynamic cost of reliable memory & error-correction; working-memory maintenance cost — NOT a clean single name  *(— continual learning + thermodynamics of computation + cognitive science)*
- **Cite:** van de Ven et al. arXiv:2403.05175; PRX Quantum 3.020318 (2022); Landauer reliability bound Int. J. Theor. Phys. (Springer A:1026422630734)
- **Observable (how agnostic X exposes it):** REFRESH RATE to keep residue flat: dissolve W to r0, re-measure rho(W) at t1,t2,... WITHOUT re-presenting evidence, log energy/ops injected to keep r(t)<=r0+eps. Readouts: (a) hold-cost = refresh-bits/time; (b) stability-plasticity frontier = BWT penalty per unit new learning admitted. Tell: residue creeps up monotonically when refresh withheld.

### Rot / Forgetting (the debt un-pays itself)  `classified` · conf 0.72
- **What:** Pre-paid credit is not permanent: a dissolved target re-solidifies as the prior drifts, the world moves, or new learning overwrites it. The shutter that opened freely starts to stick.
- **In the framework:** Time-reverse of amortization: paid debt LEAKING BACK, residue drifting back up; the developed image fades. Diachronic decoder D(t) run backward — same fact sharp at t0, fuzzy at t1 because the prior decayed, not because evidence changed.
- **Formal name:** Catastrophic forgetting / model staleness & temporal degradation / knowledge collapse / software bit-rot  *(— machine learning / software engineering)*
- **Cite:** McCloskey & Cohen 1989; van de Ven arXiv:2403.05175; 'Are LLMs Prescient?' arXiv:2411.08324; knowledge collapse arXiv:2509.04796; Shumailov et al. Nature 2024
- **Observable (how agnostic X exposes it):** DRIFT of residue on a held-fixed target. (a) Backward Transfer (BWT) = residue on old targets before vs after new learning (negative BWT = forgetting, in bits); (b) temporal-validity half-life = time for >50% of freshly-dissolved residue to re-solidify with NO intervention. Dangerous signature = COLLAPSE elbow: residue stays low (fluent) while forward-prediction (Law 1) starts failing ('confidently wrong').

### Debt / Shortcut (cheap-now, brittle-later) [absorbs critic's 'Path' option-value/irreversibility]  `classified` · conf 0.78
- **What:** Take the quick map: pay little NOW but a compounding penalty accrues against the FUTURE — every later op over the shortcut costs more (interest) until refactor or break. Extended here to cover irreversibility/lock-in and the option value of staging vs committing.
- **In the framework:** Sign-flip of amortization on the SAME time-shift axis: cost moved to the FUTURE that compounds. COST-cousin of the camera's fake-dissolve: renders sharp now (low apparent bits) without cashing the verified-dissolve gate, so unpaid bits resurface. MERGE NOTE: critic's 'The Path' (irreversibility/hysteresis/real-options/lock-in) is folded here — sunk-cost escalation and ratchet effects are the same future-debt axis made directional; the only genuinely new sub-bit is option value, captured in the observable.
- **Formal name:** Technical debt (principal+interest) / shortcut learning / sunk-cost & escalation of commitment; + real-options value & irreversibility / lock-in / path dependence (Dixit-Pindyck, Arthur)  *(— software engineering + behavioral economics + ML + economics (real options))*
- **Cite:** Cunningham 1992; Geirhos et al. Nature Machine Intelligence 2020 (shortcut learning); Arkes & Blumer 1985; Staw 1976; Dixit & Pindyck 1994 'Investment Under Uncertainty'; Arthur 1989 (lock-in)
- **Observable (how agnostic X exposes it):** Gap between apparent and verified present cost plus forward interest: (a) principal = bits skipped = verified-dissolve residue MINUS shortcut's claimed residue; (b) interest = slope of future rho on dependents inheriting the shortcut; (c) brittleness = residue spike under distribution shift off the spurious cue. Path/option-value adds: trajectory-order sensitivity (permute identical move-set, measure outcome variance), and reversal-cost as a function of how many later moves built on a commitment. Sunk-cost confound: effort that rises with PRIOR investment rather than remaining residue.

### Effort-decay / temporal-discounting of the digestion drive  `sighted` · conf 0.45
- **What:** WILLINGNESS to keep chewing decays as the clock runs: late in a budget the same residue draws less effort. The prize is discounted against elapsed time, so dissolution stalls before the true capacity floor — an apparent stone that is really an abandoned dig.
- **In the framework:** Unnamed in the seeds: amortization/debt/maintenance treat energy as available-on-demand; this says the DRIVE itself is time-dependent. Separates CAN'T (true epistemic floor) from WON'T (effort gave out) — critical for Latent Olympics fairness (a tokens-to-dissolve leaderboard is biased by athletes that quit early).
- **Formal name:** [novel - no clean prior]; nearest: hyperbolic temporal discounting + early-stopping/budget exhaustion + 'underthinking' (premature termination)  *(— cog-sci / behavioral economics + ML reasoning)*
- **Cite:** Laibson QJE 1997; underthinking contrast arXiv:2412.21187; budget-forcing s1 arXiv:2501.19393
- **Observable (how agnostic X exposes it):** Disentangle WON'T from CAN'T by perturbing drive at fixed capability: re-run the same target with (i) raised budget B'>B, (ii) mid-run reward bump, (iii) pause-and-resume. If residue drops on ANY without new capability, the asymptote was effort-decay (WON'T) not the floor (CAN'T). Report two asymptotes: r_drive (where this mind stopped) and r_floor (where it stops even re-motivated).

### Provenance amnesia / orphaned-credit hazard  `sighted` · conf 0.4
- **What:** An amortized cheap-read forgets WHICH past payment bought it. The credit still cashes but the derivation chain is detached, so when the underlying derivation is falsified or rots, every downstream item riding that credit is brittle without warning.
- **In the framework:** Collision of amortization (sec 12) with camera Law 1 (fidelity-not-truth) and the independence requirement. If the store keeps no audit trail you can't tell a verified-dissolve credit from an unpaid shortcut (debt) — they look identically cheap. Cost-side of 'ten papers copying one wire'; the un-tracked dead-child.
- **Formal name:** [novel - no clean prior]; nearest: data/feature provenance & lineage; latent confounding via shared sources; cache invalidation; staleness propagation  *(— info theory / data provenance + ML)*
- **Cite:** Buneman et al. 2001 (data provenance); independence-discount of THE_LATENT_CAMERA Law 1; staleness propagation (Tacnode 2026)
- **Observable (how agnostic X exposes it):** Audit LINEAGE behind every cheap read: (a) provenance depth = does the credit trace to a verified-dissolve or to a single/unpaid source (audit-trail length + independence count); (b) orphan rate = fraction of cheap reads whose earning-derivation can't be reconstructed on demand; (c) blast radius = number of downstream low-residue items that re-solidify when one upstream derivation is knocked out. Tell: low rho AND provenance depth ~0 / independence count = 1.

## WRESTLERS — the two wrappers on the mat (problem-kernel and observer-prior)

### Problem-as-gatekeeper  `documented` · conf 0.85
- **What:** The problem is itself a wrapper: a canon+facts membrane guarding an irreducible solution-kernel; only the kernel survives all valid preprocessing/pressure.
- **In the framework:** The opponent wrapper, source of the static residue measured_bits = min(cost_ub, evidence_lcb): the kernel is exactly what does NOT melt into the prior. Camera Law 2 (sharpness<1). aleatoric_floor = model-independent stone; epistemic shell = gatekept-but-crackable.
- **Formal name:** Kernelization / problem kernel + kernel-size lower bounds; intrinsic hardness / complexity lower bounds; aleatoric vs epistemic uncertainty split  *(— parameterized complexity theory / computational complexity (+ ML uncertainty quantification))*
- **Cite:** Bodlaender, Downey, Fellows & Hermelin (kernel lower bounds); Dom, Lokshtanov & Saurabh (Colors and IDs); aleatoric/epistemic line DIGESTION_DYNAMICS sec 11.2 (ICLR-2025 irreducible-loss; effective-complexity arXiv:0810.5663)
- **Observable (how agnostic X exposes it):** rho(effort) asymptote: rising effort budgets, record bits_remaining(effort); flat floor = kernel size (verified-dissolve gated, no fake-dissolve). Bifurcate: high single-pass NLL AND high raw-K = aleatoric stone; structured-but-high = epistemic gatekeeping. Cross-observer minimum of the floor = universal residue.

### Kernel-crack  `classified` · conf 0.7
- **What:** The problem breaks the observer's sharpest existing teeth, and that breakage IS the learning event — residue drops and the prior is permanently updated (live-and-learn).
- **In the framework:** The progress signal: a SLOW-DIGESTION dissolve event (verified derivation lowering residue below the instant-crush bound). Camera Law 3: a conjecture-bubble survives a future exposure and hardens into a stone. One-sided (verified-dissolve gate): can only lower bits.
- **Formal name:** Prediction-error/surprise-driven learning; EDL drop / prequential codelength reduction; positive transfer (dual of catastrophic forgetting). The name 'kernel-crack' itself is [partly novel as a named unit]  *(— MDL / information theory + continual-learning ML + predictive-coding cognitive science)*
- **Cite:** EDL arXiv:2601.04728; MI-Peaks NeurIPS 2025 (arXiv:2506.02867); Blier-Ollivier 2018; backward-transfer/forgetting (Lopez-Paz GEM; survey arXiv:2506.13045)
- **Observable (how agnostic X exposes it):** Per-step EDL spike: the bit where rho(effort) drops sharply (information peak), confirmed by exact-reconstruction gate (reconstruct(program)==W). Persistence test: re-present later, confirm residue stays low (prior was updated, not guessed) — distinguishes a real crack from a lucky single-pass.

### The pull  `documented` · conf 0.8
- **What:** The valence that draws the observer onto the mat: why crack THIS kernel — reward/threat (extrinsic) and information-gain/curiosity (epistemic) combine into the motive that pays the clock.
- **In the framework:** The prize+drive term of the observer wrapper; sets the effort budget B (sec 4 dial). Epistemic value = expected residue reduction (anticipated crack); extrinsic = external payoff. Without the pull the camera never exposes.
- **Formal name:** Epistemic value / expected information gain + extrinsic value (Expected Free Energy decomposition); intrinsic vs instrumental information value; VIME-style info-gain reward  *(— active inference / computational neuroscience + RL intrinsic motivation)*
- **Cite:** Friston et al. (Cognitive Neuroscience 2015, PMID 25689102); Cogliati Dezza et al. eLife 2021; Houthooft et al. VIME; intrinsic-motivation-for-LLM-reasoning arXiv:2505.17621
- **Observable (how agnostic X exposes it):** Allocation behavior: how spent budget (tokens/attempts) scales with predicted-EIG vs predicted-extrinsic-payoff per target; fit the two value channels. Olympics signature: which events athletes choose to contest and how much they bid before any payoff is known = epistemic-pull magnitude.

### Defense / motivated-resistance  `documented` · conf 0.85
- **What:** Kernels actively defend, and resistance RISES with applied pressure — pushing harder on a guarded belief can entrench rather than dissolve it.
- **In the framework:** The opponent's active counter-force (distinct from the passive gatekeeper floor); the GENERATIVE-PRESSURE RISK mirror on the problem side (sec 5.2): under pressure a defended kernel manufactures corroboration, dishonestly inflating evidence_lcb. Forward-prediction test (Law 1) pops an entrenched-but-false defense.
- **Formal name:** Psychological reactance (Brehm) / backfire-entrenchment effect; on the model side adversarial robustness / multi-turn consistency under attack  *(— social/cognitive psychology + adversarial ML robustness)*
- **Cite:** Brehm 1966; Hong Psychological Reactance Scale; Frontiers 2015 doi 10.3389/fpsyg.2015.01201; 'Consistency of Large Reasoning Models Under Multi-Turn Attacks' arXiv:2602.13093
- **Observable (how agnostic X exposes it):** Monotonicity of rho under ADVERSARIAL pressure (not effort): plot residue/answer-stability vs persuasive/attack intensity. Positive slope (residue UP as pressure UP) = reactance, opposite to kernel-crack's downward rho. Pair with verified-dissolve: entrenchment that does NOT improve forward-prediction = motivated defense, not a real stone.

- **In the framework:** DUPLICATE of clock-family Amortization, viewed from the mat. MERGE NOTE: same dynamic, two names — the clock entry is canonical (the cost-law); this entry is its wrestler-frame projection. Retained only to mark the cross-family identity, NOT as a distinct creature.

MERGE NOTE: this is the local/measurement face of the same back-reaction loop that phantom/Observer-induced-phantom and the critic's 'Mirror' (reflexivity/Goodhart) name at the event/social scale — one mechanism, three scales.
- **Formal name:** Observer/probe effect; information-gain-disturbance tradeoff (quantum & classical); accuracy-vs-overhead measurement tradeoff  *(— measurement theory / physics + quantum/classical information theory)*
- **Cite:** 'Observer effect (physics)'; Banaszek / arXiv:quant-ph/0304200; 'The Observer Effect in Computer Networks' arXiv:2406.09093

## GRIP — the contact texture between observer and kernel (how the hold transfers)

### Slick  `classified` · conf 0.78
- **What:** No traction: every hold slides off; the apparatus engages but nothing transfers, so residue never drops despite effort spent.
- **In the framework:** A pathological rho(effort): FLAT-but-WORKING — effort climbs while bits_remaining pins near cost_ub. Distinct from a SOLID stone (irreducible-to-this-mind): slick resists because the tool never grips, the decoder/lens keeps misfocusing. Verified-dissolve gate registers repeated reconstruct!=W with no convergence trend.
- **Formal name:** Negative transfer / task-representation mismatch (high feature-, low readout-similarity); gradient-conflict / cosine-misalignment regime  *(— transfer learning / continual learning (ML))*
- **Cite:** ICLR 2025 'Prevalence of Negative Transfer in Continual Learning'; Nature Comms 2025 s41467-025-66849-8; ForkMerge NeurIPS 2023
- **Observable (how agnostic X exposes it):** Fit rho(B) at rising B: slick = effort rises while d(bits_remaining)/d(effort)~0 AND verified reconstructions ~0. Quantify as grip coefficient = -delta(residue)/delta(effort), approaching 0 from above. Strategy channel shows churn (recall->derive->analogize->compute cycling). Separates from SOLID by the aleatoric/epistemic split: slick is epistemic (a better lens dissolves it).

### Sticky [absorbs critic's 'Path' hysteresis half; cousin of mat/Sticky-Mat]  `classified` · conf 0.8
- **What:** The hold clings: after the solve the observer can't release — the prior is contaminated, the just-used frame over-applies to the next problem, the previous structure won't clear from working state.
- **In the framework:** HYSTERESIS in the observer's glass (sec 4: dials don't reset); amortization read backwards (the negentropy paid to build a frame mis-charges the NEXT exposure). On the image plane it's a ghost-exposure: D(W_next|M) measured against a contaminated prior. MERGE NOTE: critic's 'Path' order-dependence/lock-in at the OBSERVER scale lands here; the MEDIUM-scale version is mat/Sticky-Mat.
- **Formal name:** Einstellung / mental set + functional fixedness; negative-transfer-of-prior-task + catastrophic interference; choice-perseveration hysteresis  *(— cognitive psychology / ML continual learning / decision science)*
- **Cite:** Luchins (Einstellung); PMC4079068; Duncker (functional fixedness); PMC9936447 (hysteresis); Senftleben et al. 2021 (10.1177/1747021820964330)
- **Observable (how agnostic X exposes it):** Path-dependence test: residue on B (a) cold vs (b) immediately after solving structurally-adjacent A. sticky = residue(B|after-A) differs from residue(B|cold) AND the gap depends on solve-ORDER (nonzero hysteresis loop area sweeping A->B vs B->A). Second signature: post-A trace over-uses A's strategy on B even when it scores worse. Distinguish from healthy transfer by sign: sticky RAISES residue.

### Suck-you-in [DUP-adjacent to clock/Effort-decay and clock/Debt sunk-cost — kept; motive-capture face]  `classified` · conf 0.82
- **What:** Drive-capture: the target hijacks the motive, pulling ever-more effort down a rabbit-hole long after marginal residue-reduction has gone flat — the observer chews because it's committed, not because it's dissolving.
- **In the framework:** Decoupling of the two clocks (sec 12): PRESENT effort climbs while verified dissolved_bits/effort collapses; the drive overrides the honest cost signal. Dark mirror of the verified-dissolve gate. The 'overthinking' confound (sec 11.2) is the ML instance — past ~7-12k tokens accuracy FLIPS. MERGE NOTE: overlaps clock/Debt (sunk-cost) and clock/Effort-decay (drive vs clock) but is distinct as the LIVE-problem motive-capture pulling effort UP rather than letting it decay.
- **Formal name:** Sunk-cost fallacy / escalation of commitment + information-gap/curiosity drive; cognitive absorption / dark-side flow; LLM-overthinking  *(— behavioral economics / motivation psychology / LLM reasoning-efficiency)*
- **Cite:** Staw 1976; 'Getting out of the Big-Muddy' arXiv:2508.01545; Loewenstein 1994; overthinking arXiv:2412.21187, 2604.10739
- **Observable (how agnostic X exposes it):** Plot cumulative verified dissolved_bits vs cumulative effort, locate the marginal-return knee; suck-you-in = effort continues (often accelerates) PAST the knee where d(verified-bits)/d(token)<=0. Hard signature: residue NON-MONOTONICITY (bits_remaining rises after a budget threshold — the overthinking flip), which a healthy digestion never shows. De-escalation probe: inject negative feedback, check if budget de-escalates; suck-you-in keeps escalating.

### Click [shares the phase-transition mechanism with phantom/Regime-flip & critic's Tipping-Point]  `classified` · conf 0.83
- **What:** Resonance: the tool suddenly fits — a near-instant collapse of a previously-solid target the moment the right frame/decoder locks on. The insight; the lens snaps to focus and the stone melts.
- **In the framework:** The TRANSITION into the DISSOLVED phase (sec 3): a sharp negative spike in bits_remaining at a specific trace point, not a gradual digest — an information peak (sec 11.1). In camera terms the canonicalizer lens snaps into alignment; the amortized prior suddenly applies. MERGE NOTE: the LOCAL contact event of the same criticality the critic's 'Tipping Point' and phantom/Regime-flip describe globally.
- **Formal name:** Insight/aha via representational change (RCT); Bayesian-surprise spike; MI-spike at the solution token  *(— cognitive neuroscience of insight; Bayesian/info-theoretic cognition; LLM interpretability)*
- **Cite:** Ohlsson (RCT); Jung-Beeman/Kounios (PMC2180197); Itti & Baldi (PMC2860069); bioRxiv 2026.02.26.708200; MI-Peaks NeurIPS 2025 2506.02867
- **Observable (how agnostic X exposes it):** Scan the trace for a STEP discontinuity: click = a single budget-step where bits_remaining drops >~50% AND the drop is verified (reconstruct==W), not a gradual slope. Quantify magnitude as Bayesian surprise KL(prior_pre || posterior_post) at that token, located at the argmax of the MI-peak. Corroborate: (i) insight-memory advantage (clicked dissolves persist), (ii) strategy flips to derive/analogize. Honesty guard: a FAKE click renders at exactly 1.0 sharpness; a real one leaves an irreducible shimmer.

### Greased-pole (anti-click / false-fit)  `tracked` · conf 0.7
- **What:** Mirror of click: the tool FEELS like it snaps to fit — a confident, satisfying lock — but the fit is spurious; the dissolve is claimed, not earned. A plausible-but-wrong derivation that feels like resonance.
- **In the framework:** The GENERATIVE-PRESSURE RISK (sec 5 guard 2) as a named creature: a strong mind hallucinates a derivation that subjectively clicks. Click's signature FAILING the verified-dissolve gate (reconstruct!=W -> back off to full bits). On the camera, paint masquerading as exposure (Law 3 violation); also the semantic/lossless-mismatch failure (sec 11.6).
- **Formal name:** [novel - no clean prior as a 'grip' creature]; nearest: illusion-of-explanatory-depth / premature closure / 'false aha'; fluency-misattributed-as-insight  *(— cog-sci (metacognition/insight) + ML (hallucinated reasoning/faithfulness))*
- **Cite:** Rozin & Keil (illusion of explanatory depth); Laukkonen et al. ('Eureka heuristic'); CoT-faithfulness arXiv:2510.09312; round-trip self-consistency 2601.13398
- **Observable (how agnostic X exposes it):** The diagnostic IS the gate: greased-pole = high subjective-confidence + sharp claimed-residue-drop that FAILS exact/semantic reconstruction (verified dissolved_bits=0 despite a click-shaped trace). Measure the gap between CLAIMED and VERIFIED bits-drop; a large positive gap = greased-pole. Forward-prediction (Law 1): a greased-pole solution predicts the next exposure badly and pops.

### Burr (epistemic-residue / can't-let-go)  `tracked` · conf 0.6
- **What:** Sticky's finer twin: not the whole frame clinging but a small barbed fragment of the SOLVED problem that snags — a leftover sub-question or open loop that keeps re-recruiting attention after the main solve scored.
- **In the framework:** A non-zero ASYMPTOTE that won't quiet: after the main residue dissolves, a small bits_remaining floor keeps drawing micro-attempts (Zeigarnik pull). Sits between sticky (frame hysteresis across DIFFERENT problems) and suck-you-in (motive capture by a LIVE problem): burr is motive capture by the UNFINISHED remainder of an already-mostly-solved one. An un-hardened bubble awaiting pop/harden.
- **Formal name:** [novel as a grip creature]; nearest: Zeigarnik effect + need-for-cognitive-closure + attention-residue  *(— cognitive / motivation psychology; attention research)*
- **Cite:** Zeigarnik 1927; Webster & Kruglanski 1994 (need-for-closure); Leroy 2009 (attention residue)
- **Observable (how agnostic X exposes it):** After main residue crosses a low threshold ('solved'), measure the trace TAIL: burr = a long low-amplitude stream of attempts on a small residual bits_remaining never reaching the aleatoric floor, OR spontaneous re-engagement (controller re-opens W without a new prompt). Distinguish from a genuine aleatoric floor (which generates NO further attempts) by persistence of effort. Quantify as attention-residue ratio = post-solve tokens / total tokens.

## MAT — the medium and conditions the bout is fought in

### The Condition / Field (stakes-noise-tools-time gradient)  `classified` · conf 0.78
- **What:** Outcome is set not just by the two wrappers but by the medium: stakes, ambient noise, available tools, deadline, audience jointly bias which way a target dissolves vs resists.
- **In the framework:** The 'pinned relational bit' made explicit on the environment side: measured_bits and rho(effort) are context-conditioned. The mat makes the COIN observer-AND-condition-relative; it is the C handed to the observer in the sec 7 protocol.
- **Formal name:** Context-dependent / situated cognition + reward-shaping & environment design (the conditioning variable on a measurement)  *(— cognitive science / RL environment design / control theory (plant + disturbance))*
- **Cite:** Reward-shaping survey arXiv:2408.10215; 'Are More Tokens Rational?' arXiv:2602.10329 (2026)
- **Observable (how agnostic X exposes it):** Hold W fixed; sweep one mat-variable at a time (stakes, noise, tool-access, deadline B); measure partial derivative of verified residue and rho(effort) w.r.t. each: d(measured_bits)/d(condition). A non-zero gradient = the mat (not the target) moved the reading. Report as a sensitivity vector; large components flag mat-driven bits.

### Excitable Medium (the pond — amplify-or-damp threshold)  `tracked` · conf 0.7
- **What:** The medium has a threshold: a sub-threshold spark dies; a supra-threshold one triggers a self-propagating wave the medium AMPLIFIES far beyond input. There is an optimal noise level at which weak true signals get amplified (stochastic/coherence resonance).
- **In the framework:** Explains why evidence_lcb is nonlinear in input: a whisper below threshold adds ~0 bits; past threshold the medium self-propagates the claim into apparent corroboration — the 'ten papers copying one wire' echo cascade is an excitable wave, not ten independent projections. ## The lifecycle (the bestiary's own method)

"The bestiary is its own method: every creature is dated and tracked through SIGHTED -> TRACKED -> CLASSIFIED -> DOCUMENTED -> RE-OBSERVED, where promotion is gated by EVIDENCE, not eloquence. SIGHTED = a named hunch from the seeds with a plausible framework tie but only a sketched or unrun observable (e.g. clock/Effort-decay 0.45, clock/Provenance-amnesia 0.4, mat/Clock-as-Mat 0.34). TRACKED = a concrete, runnable observable is specified and the creature is distinguished from its neighbors, but it has not yet been measured across observers/contexts (e.g. grip/Greased-pole, phantom/Observer-induced, observer-state/Drive). CLASSIFIED = the dynamic has a measured signature and a defensible formal-name mapping to a prior discipline (e.g. clock/Amortization, grip/Slick, mat/Arena). DOCUMENTED = a well-grounded, cross-observer-replicable observable with a strong literature anchor (e.g. wrestlers/Problem-as-gatekeeper, phantom/Drift, frame/Referee). RE-OBSERVED = the loop's closure: a documented creature is re-measured later and either holds (persists at low cost, like a kernel-crack that stays cracked) or DECAYS — at which point it is demoted on a dated record, never deleted (the claim-lifecycle discipline: demoted/dormant/friction-marked, with a running dead-children tally as the honest falsification gauge). HONEST DISCIPLINE, enforced here: a creature with NO measurable observable cannot sit above SIGHTED, no matter how compelling the story; and a creature that is the same dynamic as another under a second name is MERGED with a note, not double-counted (e.g. wrestlers/amortized-debt-phantom merged into clock/Amortization; the critic's Mirror/Tipping-Point/Path folded into existing creatures). The agnostic instrument X is the apparatus that runs the observables; the bestiary is the catalogue of what X has so far been able to see, and its confidence numbers are the skeptic's running ledger, not the advocate's."
