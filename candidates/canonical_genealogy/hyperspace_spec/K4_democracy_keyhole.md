# The Democracy Keyhole

*A worked example for the hyperdimensional canonical-space viewer (SPEC-ONLY). Reconstructing the shape of an authorless, no-nucleus, multi-yolk construct from many keyhole-bursts across time, under the COIN law (rendered_sharpness <= measured_bits; blur is the honesty badge).*

**Frame note (standing).** This is an exploratory INSTRUMENT, not a confirmatory test. Every cell is badged `[MEASURED]` (an index/inscription/excavation reports it), `[ESTIMATE]` (analyst synthesis across sources), or `[CONJECTURE]` (a falsifiable stub the block carries at low bits). Claims are demoted, not killed. The viewer renders zones as Monte-Carlo stipple, never isolines — an isoline would assert a measured bit the limited angles do not possess. A fresh web scan (June 2026) was run before writing; it confirmed the prior-art digest rather than overturning it.

**One-line reading of the model.** Democracy is not a fuzzy blob to be sharpened into a point. It is a CONSTELLATION of ~6 partially-overlapping principle-regions whose pairwise intersection is near-empty. Each historical spark is a low-rank Radon projection lighting a different subset of axes from a different definition-angle, capped by its evidence modality. The block compiles by maximum-entropy tomography (MENT): it deviates from a flat prior ONLY as far as a spark's measured bits force it, and refuses to invent a signal in a region no angle touched. The famous "Greek nucleus" is a back-projection streak — an artifact of where the angles cluster, not a feature of the object.

## 1. The reconstructed object: the constellation, not the blob

The latent block is a density `rho(v, r, t)` over a principle-vector `v`, a spatial cradle `r` (six disconnected regions), and a definition-epoch `t`. The principle-vector axes — forced by the prior-art, not chosen for tidiness — are:

| Axis | What it is | Lit strongly by |
|---|---|---|
| VOTE | majority decision by ballot/show-of-hands | poleis, Rome, Italian communes |
| LOT | selection by sortition/lottery | Athens (almost alone) |
| CONSENSUS | "one mind" agreement, no count | Haudenosaunee, kgotla, palaver, Althing law-council |
| ROTATION + TERM-LIMITS | fixed terms, mandatory turnover | Gadaa, Italian consuls, Athenian offices |
| COMMONER-ACCESS | office open to non-elites | Tlaxcallan, late-poleis |
| RECALL + WOMEN'S-POWER | removal of leaders; gendered authority | Haudenosaunee clan mothers |

`[ESTIMATE — axis-basis is itself a definition-light]` The choice of these six axes is a `P = D(t_def)` decision, badged ESTIMATE not MEASURED. Change the basis and the same sparks reconstruct a different shape. This is the deepest honesty caveat in the whole reconstruction and it is named again in the STRAINS section.

The block-universe / many-keyhole reading is the live scholarly consensus, not a fringe reframing.

## 3. Definition-drift D(t) and the subtype spikes

The diachronic spine. Each arrow moves a DIFFERENT wrapper axis, which is why the subtypes are a branching set of origin spikes, not a single ladder.

```
  direct (Athens, citizen=ruler)
     |  + representation
  representative (Rome / 1789, citizen=elector)
     |  + rights & checks layered on
  liberal
     |  + distributive/welfare substance (Bernstein ~1899)
  social
     |  - liberal layer subtracted, electoral shell kept (Zakaria 1997; Orban 2010; "sovereign democracy")
  illiberal / managed
```

| Subtype | Origin | Authorship | Badge |
|---|---|---|---|
| direct | Athens ~5th c. BCE | authorless | `[ESTIMATE]` permanently-fuzzy WHAT |
| representative | Rome -> 1789 | many-yolk | `[MEASURED]` event-frontier |

| illiberal | **Zakaria, Foreign Affairs, 1997** | near-single-caller, datable | `[MEASURED]` sharp origin spike, high bits |

The meta-name for the whole premise already exists and is current: democracy is an ESSENTIALLY CONTESTED CONCEPT (Gallie 1956), studied as "DEMOCRACY WITH ADJECTIVES" (Collier & Levitsky 1997, 550+ subtypes). V-Dem's own 2025 working paper (WP 140) concedes "no one operationalization is likely to reign supreme." This both supplies the parent->child budding mechanism (attach an adjective to one axis) and validates the standing frame.

**Diachronic re-illumination, concretely.** `D(2026)` and `D(1789)` light almost disjoint regions. Probing 1789 with the modern probe retroactively re-illuminates old nodes — e.g. the 2025-26 reading of "latent liberal potential" (negative-liberty immunities) in Athenian direct democracy, a concept that did not exist when the spark was lit.

**The dense modern time-series (the WHO / HOW-MUCH axes).** V-Dem Democracy Report 2026, *"Unraveling the Democratic Era?"* (10th ed., 17 Mar 2026): 1789-2025, 202 countries, >32M data points, >4,200 coders, 600+ attributes. `[MEASURED]` This is the highest-bits stream available. A textbook per-axis measured_bits collapse: V-Dem reclassified the **US from liberal to electoral democracy** (Liberal Democracy Index -24% in one year, rank 20th -> 51st of 179) — the liberal/WHAT layer going fuzzy while the electoral/WHO layer stays sharp. Subtype membership is a definition-relative classification, not a substrate fact: the block re-bins without the underlying voxel moving.

## 4. The present zone and the VALENCE minus-lobes (opposition)

VALENCE is the ratified third coin: signed, observer-relative. A single unsigned "influence" magnitude is FORBIDDEN — the same event carries opposite valence for different observers.

**The plus-lobe is contracting** `[MEASURED, high agreement on two of three streams]`:
- V-Dem 2026: autocracies outnumber democracies 92 to 87; ~72-75% of humanity under autocracy; global democracy back at ~1978 levels; 44 autocratizing vs 18 democratizing (record).
- Freedom House 2026: 20th consecutive year of decline; only 21% live in "Free" countries (down from 46% two decades ago).

**Minus-lobe, sub-lobe (a) — EXTERNAL: the CRINK axis** (China-Russia-Iran-North Korea). `[ESTIMATE]` A loose, transactional anti-US-hegemony coalition, NOT a NATO-style alliance (China spying on Russia; unable to save Syria/Venezuela/Iran clients in the last 12 months). Render as a weak-coupling edge, not a bloc.

**Minus-lobe, sub-lobe (b) — INTERNAL: incumbent-led backsliding via legal/legislative pathways** `[MEASURED/ESTIMATE]`. El Salvador abolished presidential term limits (Bukele, Aug 2025; term extended 5->6 yrs; emergency-framework civil-society capture); Hungary banned Pride; Tanzania jailed Tundu Lissu and banned Chadema; plus a coup cluster (Guinea-Bissau, Burkina Faso, Madagascar). The dominant 2025-26 mechanism is the elected executive hollowing institutions from within — not the tank.

**The biggest 2026 signal — a CORE-NODE DEFECTION** `[MEASURED]`: the former chief *exporter* of the construct, the US, flipped into the autocratizer set across all indices simultaneously (V-Dem "unprecedented" for a Western democracy; FH sharpest decline among Free since 2002; EIU 28th -> 34th). The fuzzy region sharpening fastest is the one imprinting a *backsliding* signature.

**Observer-relative valence flip** `[MEASURED]`: the US ~75% cut to democracy/human-rights/governance funding (FY24->FY25), USAID ~83% of programs cancelled/folded into State, NED defunding proposed — a PLUS for the autocratic lobe (vacuum-filling) and a MINUS for the democratic lobe. *Same event, opposite sign.* Congress partially restored ($50B foreign assistance, -16%) in Feb 2026 — a counter-lobe.

**Counter-lobe (resilience)** `[MEASURED/ESTIMATE]`: Poland (June 2025 + 2023 Tusk coalition, rare reversal); Brazil 2026 election; new democratizers Botswana, Guatemala, Mauritius; strengthened pro-democracy protest waves (Momentum HU, USR RO). Backsliding triggers its own opposing valence-lobe.

## 5. Conjecture-stubs at the fuzzy WHAT / HOW / WHY (falsifiable holes)

These are the missing-wedge zones the COIN forbids us to fill. Each is a low-bit overlay the block carries openly.

- **STUB-1 (the highest-value hostile probe — the COIN-violation detector).** `[CONJECTURE]` Expert-coded indices (V-Dem, FH) may render sharpness *above* their measured bits. Falsifiable: objective indicators (incumbent vote share, turnover rate, peaceful-transfer count) should track expert-coded decline; persistent divergence = inflated measured-bits in the expert channel — a literal "fake measured bit." Little & Meng-style work finds little objective decline over the past decade; Pew (Apr 2026) counters with convergent US indicators. The probe that could later collapse the blur honestly.

- **STUB-2 (definition-keyed blur).** `[CONJECTURE]` Three indices, three answers, same year: V-Dem/FH say sharp 2025 decline; EIU 2025 says *stabilization* ("end of the recession," global avg 5.17 -> 5.19). The block-value for "democracy 2025" must render with sigma = inter-index variance, never a single number. Prediction: index disagreement is largest precisely where definitions diverge most (liberal vs. minimal/Przeworski axis) — testable by decomposing each index's sub-components.

- **STUB-3 (the fiscal-foundations eigen-direction).** `[CONJECTURE]` Feinman external-revenue->autocracy vs internal-taxation->shared-power = Stasavage state-strength = possibly the *same eigenvector seen from two epochs*. If one fiscal axis predicts spark presence/absence across all six disconnected cradles, the constellation has a low-rank backbone — a structural claim, not a catalogue. Directly testable: PCA on Zenodo 17856819 + the 11-spark principle-vectors.

- **STUB-4 (causes are a cycle, not a typed edge).** `[CONJECTURE]` Decline-causes (inequality, affective polarization, status-threat, elite incentives) are non-nested competing hypotheses. Polarization <-> backsliding is a two-way loop (Gessler 2025) that breaks the ratified typed-edge DAG — render it as a first-class diamond/cycle, with NO single signed influence magnitude (VALENCE rule). Each mechanism = its own low-bit overlay with its own predicted spread/decline topology.

- **STUB-5 (sortition's single-angle unidentifiability).** `[CONJECTURE]` Sortition is lit by essentially one ancient spark (Athens). A latent axis seen from one angle cannot be triangulated; its regional extent is formally unidentifiable. Render as a sharp LOCAL spike with an explicit "no-extent" stub. NOTE (fresh scan): the 2010-2026 OECD "deliberative wave" / citizens'-assembly revival is a *modern re-illumination of the same Athenian spike*, not a second independent ancient angle — it sharpens the kernel but does not give sortition regional extent in deep time.

- **STUB-6 (founding-WHEN as irreducible fuzz).** `[CONJECTURE]` Peacemaker/Deganawida, Gadaa originators, Tlaxcallan (no named founder), communes (many-small) are tradition-figures / multi-instances. Render WHEN as wide spikes (Great Law ~1142-1450) not points. Open: do these narrow with more angles, or are they *irreducibly* fuzzy because the object genuinely has no nucleus — no number of angles resolves a thing that was never a point?

## 6. What the model REVEALS vs. where it STRAINS

**REVEALS:**
1. **The single-blob render is a measurement artifact.** The geometry — angles clustered on the inscribed Greco-Roman wedge — forces the constellation reading. Naive back-projection streaks Athens's sortition across all regions and manufactures a Greek nucleus. The reframe is forced by tomography, not chosen for ideology.
2. **The keystone is literally instantiated.** The COIN inequality `rendered_sharpness <= measured_bits` and the MENT cap on each Lagrange function `h_k` are the SAME inequality. The missing wedge = the authorless/oral evidence gap; max-entropy = never render a fake measured bit; stipple = honest blur as badge.
3. **The block compiles non-uniformly and in epoch-order.** t0 textual antiquity lights a false-nucleus shell; t1 oral/archaeological + Feinman 2026 enters the empty wedge and the nucleus *dissolves*; t2 modern indices sharpen the diachronic spine but inject inter-instrument disagreement that must render as blur-width. Institutional WHAT/WHERE converges; procedural HOW and founding WHEN stay limited-angle.
4. **Live claim-lifecycle events match the standing frame.** Rome demoted (Mouritsen: populus "passive source of legitimacy"); Haudenosaunee->US arrow contested. Both dimmed, not deleted.

**STRAINS:**
1. **MENT prior-dependence / axis-basis is a definition-light.** The reconstruction is only as honest as `rho*` and the chosen six axes. This is badged ESTIMATE, but it is load-bearing: a different basis reconstructs a different shape from identical sparks. The model cannot bootstrap its own axes from the data — a genuine limit, not a tuning knob.
2. **Demotion-without-deletion is delicate on a projector.** Rome's down-weighted `h_k` must dim, not vanish: drive the Lagrange function to zero and you drop an angle entirely. Over-demotion is as dishonest as over-claiming. The friction-mark must *reduce* measured_bits, not censor.
3. **An authorless no-nucleus object may be irreducibly unreconstructable in WHEN.** If democracy genuinely never was a point, no accumulation of angles will narrow the founding-WHEN spikes to ranges. The tomography assumes a latent object exists to reconstruct; for a multi-yolk authorless construct, "the shape" may be the spike-cloud itself, with no sharper truth hiding underneath. This is the model's honest horizon.
4. **The cycle breaks the DAG.** Polarization<->backsliding is a first-class diamond the typed-edge machinery handles only by special-casing. The architecture admits diamonds, but the *causal* sub-block is where the construct most resists the substrate's preferred shape.

## SPECULATION (disclosed; register-shift; low-bits) [SPEC]

- `[SPEC]` The frame-reversal of solid<->fuzzy is itself an *observable* here. Under a minimal/Przeworski frame ("parties can lose elections") democracy renders SHARPER and more stable; under a thick/substantive frame it renders FUZZIER and more in-decline. The 2026 "decline signal" may be partly a frame-dependent artifact — render the block under both definition-wavelengths and compare the contraction magnitude. The most interesting falsifiable hole of all.
- `[SPEC]` If STUB-3 holds, the entire constellation collapses to roughly one structural axis (fiscal foundations / state-strength) plus residual decoration. That would mean the six-axis basis is over-parameterized and the "shape" of democracy is closer to a 1-D ridge than a 6-D cloud — a dramatic simplification that would make the single-blob render *wrong for a different reason* than currently believed (wrong dimensionality, not wrong localization).
- `[SPEC]` The US core-node defection might be the single most information-rich burst the block ever receives — because watching the chief *exporter* run the construct in reverse, in high-bits real time, projects the WHAT axis (which liberal components are load-bearing) from an angle no historical spark could provide. Backsliding is a stress-test that reveals structure invisible at equilibrium.
- `[SPEC]` "Authorless" may be the *generic* case for latent social constructs, and democracy merely the cleanest specimen. If so, the keyhole/block/tomography apparatus is really a general instrument for no-nucleus constructs (markets, language, "the rule of law"), and democracy is the calibration target.

## QUESTIONS WE SHOULD BE ASKING (register-shift)

- Is democracy best modeled as ONE fuzzy region or as a CONSTELLATION of partially-overlapping principle-regions whose intersection is near-empty? The data leans constellation — which would make the single-blob render an artifact to be *corrected*, not refined.
- Are we measuring the object, or measuring our instruments measuring it? When three indices disagree on the *sign* of the 2025 trend, the honest block-value is the disagreement itself. Are we comfortable that "democracy 2025" has no scalar truth-value?
- What does it mean to reconstruct the shape of a thing that may have no nucleus? If founding-WHEN is irreducibly fuzzy, is "the shape" the spike-cloud — full stop — or are we assuming a hidden point that does not exist?
- Should the apparatus ever be allowed to *collapse* a blur? STUB-1 (objective vs. expert indicators) is the one probe that could honestly sharpen a fuzzy cell. What is the bit-threshold at which a CONJECTURE-stub is promoted to MEASURED — and who sets it?
- Is the choice of axis-basis a measurement we can make, or only a definition we can declare? If the latter, the block has a permanent ESTIMATE floor it can never drop below.
