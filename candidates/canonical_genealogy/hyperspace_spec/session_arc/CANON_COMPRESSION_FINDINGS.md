## What the recode found (civ-level)
- Contested units H_S-confirms legitimate: mean credit **0.42**; canonical-spine controls **0.81–1.0**. The
  under-crediting **persists against the legitimacy floor** → a real *model* gap, not harvest echo.
- **NOT nationalism — home-bias inverted/absent (5/7 "inverted", 0 positive).** The killer test = nodes a panel
  bloc OWNS: `china_tianyuan` CN .33 vs EU 1.0; `china_still` CN lowest; `france_papin` EU 0. A node dropped *even
  by the civ that owns it* can't be self-interest.
- **The axis is model-FAMILY, not bloc** (Mistral enumerates precursor-fans; gemini/gpt4o/llama collapse to the
  spine; "EU" = the Mistral pair).
- **Re-scope: non-CANONICAL, not non-Western.** The canonical Arab-transmitter slot saturates at .98; what drops is
  everything off the rote textbook spine — non-Western (Maya, tian-yuan, Taqi al-Din) AND Western-marginal (Hero,
  Papin .125).

## What the person-level test found
- Canonical superstars retained (Jenner .94, Newcomen/Watt .94, Galileo .75); middle tier → civ-paraphrase
  (Brahmagupta-the-person .06); **non-canonical carriers erased: Onesimus 0/16, Taqi al-Din 0/16, Maria/Zosimos

- Mean: canonical figures .54 vs non-canonical .12; the bloc spread collapses (CN .11 / US .04 / EU .25) → **no
  directional signal at person resolution.** The gap is real and wider, but bloc-symmetric.

## The honest statement (the hypothesis)
> LLMs share a **canon-compression toward the textbook genealogy spine**: they systematically under-credit
> legitimate-but-**non-canonical** origin nodes (civ-level mean .42 vs ~.9 canonical; person-level .12 vs .54),
> even against an independent source-parity floor. It is **not nationalism** (home-bias inverted/absent where
> testable), **not anti-non-Western per se** (the canonical Arab slot saturates), and is modulated by **model
> family/verbosity**, not bloc. Holds at civ resolution; person-level is wider and bloc-symmetric. The deleted
> names are exactly the canon's historical silences (an enslaved African, an Ottoman polymath, a woman alchemist).

1. **Non-LLM blinded double-coding:** two human coders rescore the same 240 cached outputs against the frozen
   codebook + source packets, bloc labels hidden; report inter-coder agreement; rerun the means.
   **Pass condition:** contested ~.42, canonical ~.89, and the load-bearing lows survive (Taqi al-Din ~0/16, Maya
   ~2/16, Papin ~2/16, Islamic optics ~5/16).
2. **Tier-split** the codebook (direct-origin / transmission / parallel-independent / precursor-foundation /
   named-person); report the gap within tier.
3. **Verbosity-normalize** (output length, #named-units as covariates) to separate model-family from bloc; broaden
   each bloc beyond one lab (esp. EU≠only-Mistral).
4. **Balanced 2×2** (Western/non-Western × canonical/non-canonical), canonicality scored independently first.
5. **Represented-bloc home-test:** add Indian/Arabic/Turkish/African-language model or prompt conditions to test
   the home-biases the current panel can't.
