**(1) Reduction**

Partly real, not yet demonstrated. `41 -> 33` is weak; `41 -> ~6` is only real compression if the 6 organs predict held-out observables better than rival clusterings. Right now it smells partly author-driven.

Falsify it by blind-coding new dynamics against pre-registered observables: rho curve, two-clock gap, hysteresis loop, EIG gain, Goodhart gap, verified-vs-claimed residue. Failures: low inter-rater agreement, frequent “needs 7th/8th organ,” or worse held-out MDL/BIC than a standard taxonomy.

The weakest “organ” is **verified-dissolve gate**: that is an assay / validation rule, not a generative metabolism. I’d replace or demote it. Missing organ: **referee / objective / information-structure**: payoff spec, disclosure, provenance, independence/correlation, and Goodhart are not reducible to drive. This is already visible in Referee/Arena/Prize-Field.

Verdict: real compression candidate, but the 6.8:1 number is an over-claim until cross-validated.

**(2) Tactics**

Strongest grounding gaps:

- **Probe vs Query-then-update**: likely one active-learning / sequential design family unless the adaptive-sequence observable cleanly separates them. Cite `arXiv:2304.08151`.
- **Adversarial elicitation**: under-grounded as a formal tactic. Needs stronger mapping to MITRE ATLAS / red-team taxonomies, not just jailbreak/prompt-crafting examples. The `arXiv:2602.01708` / `arXiv:2507.21389` anchors do not by themselves justify the broad “interrogation” category.
- **Hydra vs Combinatorial Hydra**: same branching-factor mechanism; one loop-side, one problem-side. Probably split by role in UI, merge in ontology.
- **Autocurriculum vs Red Queen**: near-duplicate; autocurriculum is engineered Red Queen / self-play. Cite `arXiv:1903.00742`, `arXiv:1901.01753`, `arXiv:2505.14970`, `arXiv:2603.18325`.
- **Reciprocity / tit-for-tat**: an edge/coupling, not a tactic vertex.
- **Transposition**: weakest formal name. “Cross-plane tactic porting” is project coinage; transferable adversarial examples `arXiv:2310.17626` do not prove the general mechanism.
- **Evolvability**: may just be payoff-biased selection plus Red Queen one level up. Keep conjectural.
- **Effort-decay / Provenance-amnesia / Clock-as-Mat / Prize-Field**: correctly self-demoted. They are useful hypotheses, not documented dynamics. `arXiv:2412.21187`, `arXiv:2501.19393`, `arXiv:2602.10329`.

Missing load-bearing prior art: formal dialogue/argumentation frameworks should carry Burden-shift/Motte more than loose rhetoric; adversarial ML should be mapped tactic-by-tactic to ATLAS/NIST; game theory should distinguish signalling, bargaining, repeated games, and mechanism design instead of letting them blur.

Verdict: useful catalogue, but several “tactics” are actually couplings, assays, or role-split duplicates.

**(3) Method + Channels**

The substrate-wrapper normalization is sound as a **hypothesis registry**: provenance, certainty, lifecycle, observables, and demotion rules are good anti-myth controls. It becomes a category error if COIN/render caps or “blur” are treated as target properties rather than instrument/UI policy. Lifecycle-as-verification is the strongest part.

Channels: **Ticker** and **Guess-Who** are real because they preserve discriminating observables. **Olympics** is useful but Goodhart-prone. **Battlefield** and **Jungle** are mostly mnemonic unless every symbol maps back to a measured trace. Good channel rule: no observable, no visual claim.

Verdict: method is defensible as metrology scaffolding; channels are legible only when pinned to tests, otherwise decoration.
