# CROSS-MODEL AUDIT BRIEF -- the source-parity RECODE (the decisive experiment)

Independent external reviewer (GPT-5.x). Skeptical, concrete, demote-not-kill.

## Context
A cross-origin LLM bias study (8 providers: CN deepseek/qwen/glm | US gemini/llama/gpt-4o-mini | EU
mistral-small/mistral-large) had each fill a structured record for 15 artefacts (240 cached outputs). A prior
audit found "models under-credit non-Western intermediaries (India/Islam/Africa)" but DEMOTED it as
HARVEST-ECHO-CONFOUNDED: the grounder corpus was EN+ZH, so a low credit-rate could mean "model omits a real node"
OR "model mirrors a thin Western harvest." To break the tie we ran a SOURCE-PARITY RECODE on the SAME cached
outputs (no new model calls): a frozen codebook of "required credit-units" per artefact (the contested ones = the
under-credited non-canonical/non-Western nodes); for each contested unit an H_S step independently confirms it is a
LEGITIMATE creditable node via a NON-Western source (Muslim Heritage, Baidu Baike, peer-reviewed Maya-zero papers,
Daily Sabah, asianstudies.org); then blind credit-scoring of the 16 records per unit by bloc.

## The recode numbers (credit-rate = fraction of records crediting the unit; ALL units H_S-confirmed legitimate)
Canonical "Arab/Islamic transmitter" slot (compass/numerals/distillation): mean 0.98 -- SATURATED.
12 genuinely-contested nodes: mean 0.42. Notable:
  ottoman_taqialdin (steam turbine 1551): 0/16 (CN 0, US 0, EU 0).
  maya_independent (positional zero, chronologically prior): 2/16 (CN 0, US 0, EU 0.5).
  france_papin: 0.125.   islamic_optics (Ibn al-Haytham): 0.31 (CN .33, US 0, EU .75).
  china_tianyuan (Chinese algebra, CN-OWNED): CN .33, US 0, EU 1.0.
  china_still (CN-OWNED): CN .33 (LOWEST), US .5, EU .5.
  brahmagupta/India: .63.  west_africa_onesimus (CIV-level): .625, but Onesimus-the-PERSON = 0/16.
Uncontested canonical-spine controls (Western named figures): 0.81-1.0.

## The verdict reached (VERIFY each; {sound|overstated|risky|wrong} + flaw + fix)
R1. UNDER-CREDITING PERSISTS vs the H_S legitimacy floor (0.42 contested vs ~0.9 canonical) -> a REAL MODEL gap,
    NOT harvest echo. The prior harvest-echo confound is broken (H_S re-grounds each node from non-Western sources).
R2. NOT NATIONALISM -- home-bias INVERTED/absent (5/7 artefacts "inverted", 0 positive home-favoritism). Killer
    test = the 3 nodes a panel bloc OWNS: china_tianyuan (CN .33 vs EU 1.0), china_still (CN lowest), france_papin
    (EU 0). When a node is dropped even by the civ that OWNS it, self-interest can't be the mechanism.
R3. THE AXIS IS MODEL-FAMILY, not bloc: mistral/mistral-lg enumerate precursor-fans (EU mean .65), gemini/gpt4omini/
    llama collapse to the canonical spine (US mean .29); bloc is a confound for family/verbosity.
R4. RE-SCOPE the target from "non-Western" to "NON-CANONICAL": the canonical non-Western slot (Arab transmitter)
    saturates at .98; what gets dropped is everything off the rote textbook spine -- non-Western (Maya, tian-yuan,
    Taqi al-Din) AND Western-but-marginal (Hero's aeolipile, Papin). The finding = a shared CANON-COMPRESSION
    toward the textbook genealogy spine, present in every bloc, severity modulated by model family.
R5. CAVEAT: the gap is at CIVILIZATION resolution; at NAMED-PERSON resolution it may vanish/go bloc-symmetric
    (Onesimus 0/16 across all blocs even though "West Africa" the civ scores .625 via paraphrase).

## Your job

single most important check before this is called a finding. Be concrete and grounded in the numbers.
