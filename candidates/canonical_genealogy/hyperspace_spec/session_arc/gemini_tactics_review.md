**(1) THE REDUCTION**
*   **Status:** Post-hoc descriptive clustering, not a true dimensionality reduction. It conflates physical mechanisms (criticality, hysteresis) with accounting metaphors (two-clocks, motive). 
*   **Falsification:** Falsified if conditional independence tests (e.g., FCI) show the "organs" are highly collinear, or if a newly observed tactic cannot map to the six.
*   **Missing/Dupes:** "Criticality" and "hysteresis" are duplicates (they are dynamically coupled features of the same fold bifurcations). A pure "channel/bandwidth capacity" organ is distinctly missing.
*   **Over-claims:** Framing a descriptive grouping as a "strong 6.8:1 reduction" without proving orthogonal metabolic independence.
*   **Verdict (1):** The 6-organ reduction is an elegant but post-hoc clustering that conflates mathematical mechanisms with accounting metaphors and would fail strict orthogonal independence tests.

**(2) THE TACTICS**
*   **Missing Priors:** Weak on distributed systems/robust statistics; "Poison" and "Salami" ignore foundational Byzantine fault tolerance. "Burden-shift" leans too heavily on philosophical rhetoric (Hamblin) instead of verifiable multi-agent RL (MARL) dynamics.
*   **Shakiest:** "Evolvability / meta-strategy" (conf 0.55). It is a systemic, emergent property of the environment, not a deployable adversarial tactic.
*   **Merges:** "Query-then-update" and "Probe / Query-at-the-cut" artificially split the standard POMDP / active inference loop (e.g., EPIG arXiv:2304.08151). "The Hydra" and "Combinatorial Hydra" are the exact same mechanism (branching factor > 1) duplicated purely by perspective. 
*   **Over-claims:** "Transposition" dropping niche-fill latency is an unrun conjecture masquerading as an established mechanism.
*   **Verdict (2):** The taxonomy fractures artificially (Probe and Query-then-update are identically POMDP loops, Hydras are duplicates) and over-claims systemic properties like Evolvability as deployable tactics.

**(3) METHOD + CHANNELS**
*   **Methodology:** A category error. Forcing continuous, non-linear phenomena (like stochastic resonance in excitable media, arXiv:2302.12295) or thermodynamic realities (EDL/Amortization, arXiv:2601.04728) into discrete, game-theoretic agent "wrappers" assigns agentic intent to systemic physics. The "agnostic" metrology fundamentally pre-supposes adversarial framing (e.g., performative prediction, arXiv:2002.06673). 
*   **Channels:** "Ticker" (telemetry) and "Latent Olympics" (cross-observer minimums) are structurally sound and map directly to observables. "Jungle" and "Battlefield" are pure pedagogical decorations that reskin existing metrics without adding new falsifiable constraints.
*   **Over-claims:** Calling the metrology "agnostic" while pre-loading it with an inherently adversarial, game-theoretic ontology.
*   **Verdict (3):** Coercing continuous thermodynamic dynamics into discrete adversarial "wrappers" is a category error, and the narrative channels (Jungle/Battlefield) are pedagogical decorations.
