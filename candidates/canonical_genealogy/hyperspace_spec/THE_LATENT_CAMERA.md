## The instrument is TWO halves + the channels

The earlier "optical column" sketch was only the **lower half** (collect). The full pipe, read bottom -> top:

- **LOWER HALF = COLLECT (the camera).** probe (concept-light D(t)) -> aperture (keyhole) -> lens (decoder / glasses / **canonicalizer** focus) -> shutter (digestion: instant-crush <-> slow-digestion = exposure time / compute) -> film (the agnostic substrate, light on a sensor). Honest exposure: `render <= measured`.
- **THE HINGE = THE FILM / IMAGE PLANE.** The measured pixels are **testimony** (claims + their evidence-structure), **NOT truths** (law 1).
- **UPPER HALF = PREDICT (the projector / generative pole).** On top of the measured pixels we **plant conjectures**, inflate them into **bubbles**, and **test them against future exposures**. A bubble that survives -> **hardens** into a stone; one the future contradicts -> **pops** (a dead child). This is the falsification engine, and it is the COIN's *generate* pole — it lives ABOVE the plane and is never written onto the film below it (the fact / anti-fact mirror separation).
- **THE GLASSES ON TOP = CHANNELS.** The same honest image read through swappable views: **entertainment** (the Latent Olympics), **research**, a **ticker** (live readout). A channel is how you *watch* the instrument; it never edits the film.

What a generative model does is paint the upper half straight onto the plane. The latent camera keeps the halves ontologically separate — that separation is the in-viewer watermark.

## The three honest laws (Pav, 2026-06-16) — the strict form of the COIN

### Law 1 — FIDELITY, not truth (honest collect != true collect)
"Never lie on the collect" never meant *what we collect is true.* The camera is honest about the **exposure** — what was asserted, by whom, with what independent support — not about the world. Every measured pixel is testimony, and much testimony is a lie in a good suit (narrative, fake news, spin). The unit already refuses to render it sharp: a fluent fabrication is **high `cost_ub`, ~0 `evidence_lcb`**, so `measured_bits = min(cost, evidence)` floors it to the fuzzy, single-route blob it is. **`measured_bits` are FIDELITY bits, not TRUTH bits.** Truth was never an input — it is the **residue that survives** (a) the upper half (forward prediction tests) and (b) *genuinely* independent routes. Since coordinated fake news manufactures corroboration ("ten papers copying one wire = one projection"), **independence itself must be measured** (discount shared sources, covariance-intersection), and the **forward-prediction test is the final arbiter: a lie predicts the future badly, and pops.** A pixel can be perfectly recorded and perfectly false; the camera shows it fuzzy *because it is unsupported* — which is the honest thing.

### Law 2 — SHARPNESS < 1, always (the strict inequality)
`render <= measured` was the inequality; **`measured` itself never reaches 1.** Look harder at the hardest fact and its structure dissolves — like a particle. Three independent reasons sharpness 1 is unreachable: (i) resolving to 1 needs infinite resolution = **unbounded compute** (the shortest program is *uncomputable*; every residue is a budget-relative upper bound that more compute lowers but never floors); (ii) the probe **disturbs** the target (participatory back-reaction from the keyhole/observer layer); (iii) there is an **aleatoric floor** (sharpness 1 = zero entropy = impossible). So sharpness is **scale-relative** — crisp at the scale you paid for, fuzzy below it — and the floor is asymptotic. Even the hardest stone keeps an irreducible shimmer. **COROLLARY: a pixel that renders at exactly 1.0 is the tell of a FAKE** — real measurement never reaches the floor; perfect sharpness is the signature of paint, not exposure. (This is the COIN meeting the uncomputability of K, the aleatoric/epistemic split, and the LOD law `rendered_bits = min(measured, lod_budget(zoom))` in one statement.)

### Law 3 — the two halves share one law
The generative upper half is COIN-true only because (a) its bubbles are **ontologically separate** from the measured film (fiction, badged — the anti-fact mirror), and (b) a conjecture earns sharpness **only by surviving future exposure** (verified-dissolve, run in *time* instead of by reconstruction). **Generate freely above the plane; never write a generated bit onto the film below it; let the future do the hardening.**
