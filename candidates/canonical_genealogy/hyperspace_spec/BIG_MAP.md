## What the skeptic KILLED (we were overclaiming)
- ❌ **"The honesty law (render ≤ measured-bits, blur-is-the-badge) has zero prior art."** It is structurally
  the **selective-prediction / reject-option / abstention / calibrated-uncertainty** family (Chow 1970
  reject option; modern calibration). It's an *instrument-side* under-claim governor — a useful reframing —
  but **not** unprecedented. **Cite it, don't claim it.**
- ❌ **"Rediscovery-IS-validation is a novel epistemic stance."** It is textbook **construct validity via
  the nomological network** (Cronbach & Meehl, 1955): validate an instrument by showing it reproduces the
  law-like relations of the construct. We're doing classical psychometric validation — name it.
- ✅ **Threat cleared:** *Decomposing Reasoning Efficiency* ([2602.09805](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.09805)),
  the paper that might have been a contemporaneous work-vs-span rerun, was fetched: it splits cost into
  completion-rate / conditional-correctness / length (a **verbosity-vs-recovery** split) — **not** our
  work-vs-span. It does **not** collapse us.

## What SURVIVES — the genuinely defensible flag
A narrower-than-we-hoped but real, under-occupied cell — an **assembly of known parts**:
1. **Cost-functional as the *object* of measurement** — reading cost as **signal of latent structure**,
   not as efficiency to minimize (OckBench, ThreadWeaver), audit (faithfulness), or bound (TTC scaling).
   *The whole field reads cost to minimize/audit/bound; we read it as signal.* Nearest but-not-there:
   State-over-Tokens ([2512.12777](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.12777), tokens-as-state, no cost-functional)
   and Derivation Entropy ([2511.19156](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.19156), cost-reveals-structure-as-physics,
   no instrument).
2. **Black-box / substrate-agnostic** (token counts only) — differentiates from the white-box twins:
   Deep-Thinking-Tokens ([2602.13517](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.13517)), CRV, Huginn-style internal depth.
3. **The chronometric inversion** (tokens → hidden operation-count via a **Sternberg additive-factors**
   signature) — **direction-open and un-run anywhere.** This is the un-executed bridge.

The flag is the **JOIN** of (1)+(2)+(3) on a cheap API-only camera, **validated by reproducing named laws.**

## The biggest threat to the camera's pixel
**Faithfulness decay / the reasoning horizon k\*** ([2602.11201](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11201)):
reasoning tokens **stop causally mattering at ~70–85%** of the chain; verbosity ≠ faithfulness
([2510.27378](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.27378)); CoT often doesn't reflect the computation
([2605.24286](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24286), [2510.09312](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.09312)). **The
pixel may be partly fake past a horizon** — the camera could be photographing floundering, not work.
This must be confronted, not assumed away.

## The 2026 frontier (where the center of mass is moving)
- **From token-counting → structural measurement of the trace** ([2606.03883](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.03883):
  reasoning as a measurable object with analyzable topology). *Our token-count camera is one frontier behind
  this — we should move toward structure too.*
- **Parallel-vs-serial as an efficiency axis to optimize** (ThreadWeaver 1.53×; ParaThinker; Global Forking).
- **Physics-of-tokens formalized** (Photons=Tokens [2603.06630](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.06630) Landauer+Shannon;

- **METR time-horizon 1.1** (~16h @ 50% for the frontier, Jan 2026) = the macro serial-depth trend; **our
  per-instance reasoning_tokens surcharge is the microscope under that telescope** — a clean place to stand.

## Recommended next (the un-run experiments that plant the flag)
1. **Run the Sternberg ADDITIVE-FACTORS test** — two *orthogonal* structural factors; are their token-costs
   **additive** (separate serial stages) or **interactive**? This is the chronometric signature that turns
   "rediscovery-as-validation" from rhetoric into a load-bearing result, and it's un-run for LLM tokens.
2. **Confront the reasoning horizon** — step-corruption (NLDD) on DEEP vs WIDE: does the +38.5 surcharge land
   *before* or *after* k\* (70–85%)? If after, the pixel is suspect.
3. **Correctness-conditioning discipline** (Deep-Thinking-Tokens, r=−0.544): report the work-slope on
   *correct traces only*, or the camera photographs floundering.
4. **Cross-instrument calibration** — run our outside black-box camera against an inside white-box depth
   meter on an open model (Huginn [2502.05171](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.05171)).
5. **Cite-don't-claim the result laws up front**; declare the calibrated regime is **pre-collapse** (map the
   Illusion-of-Thinking collapse as the boundary).
