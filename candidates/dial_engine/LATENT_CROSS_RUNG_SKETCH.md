## 1. The clean ANALOGUE (latent pond that obeys the physics)

**Internet attention as the pond.** Ripples = minute/hour reply-chains and trending bursts; waves = day/week news cycles and memes; tides = month/year topic regimes and semantic drift; climate = decadal language change. The physical asymmetry transfers intact: a news-cycle observer can always aggregate up to the drift (given window); a yearly-corpus observer cannot reconstruct which burst carried a meme (aggregation destroyed the waveform — DPI holds); the sweet spot for meme-law inference is Δt ≈ the attention correlation time (days), not minutes (redundant) or years (dynamics-blind).

**Fetchable, auth-free, instrument-ready:** Wikipedia pageviews (REST API, hourly/daily, years of history, per-article). Run the EXACT committed Q6 rung instrument on a pageview series: persistence law, rung ladder, the −½·log₂(2(1−ρ₁)) identity, B-row. Bonus: pageviews carry KNOWN imported physical tides (circadian + weekly human rhythms = literal spectral lines) — a calibration target the flare never had.

## 2. The BREAKERS (where latent violates the physical assumptions)

**B1 — The archive breaks irreversibility (DPI-in-practice).** Physics: nature keeps no tape; coarse-graining is forever. Latent: the fine scale is ARCHIVED — Wayback Machine, edit histories, reply chains, git logs. The coarse observer can go back and refetch the ripples: **the latent past is re-queryable at full resolution**, so the backward fuzz-horizon is a budget/index artifact, not physics. The theorem isn't violated (information was stored, not destroyed) but the ASSUMPTION "coarse observer = fine-blind" breaks. Honest leak: archives rot (link rot, deletions, retroactive robots.txt) — *the latent past is sharp but moth-eaten*. For the substrate-as-light reading: the latent universe ships with its own photographic plates.

**B2 — Observation back-action: the render is an action (Goodhart/reflexivity).** Mars does not care that JPL watches. The internet does: publishing a trend amplifies it; a ranking measured becomes a ranking gamed (Goodhart's law, Lucas critique, Soros reflexivity). The viewer's output feeds back into the framed phenomenon — **the perception–action loop CLOSES for real**, which is exactly the thing Gemini said our physical probe lacked ("the data remains passive"). The latent domain is the native home of experiment **A4** (the formalism dial must pay): here active inference stops being decorative *by construction*, because the internet observer entity is INSIDE the system it reads — its renders are forces. Frame/engine/render stop being cleanly separable: a published render is an engine-action on the next frame.

**B3 — Nyquist breaks: the fine scale is GENERATED on query.** Physics: between samples, nature ran and you missed it. Latent: much fine structure does not exist until asked for — LLM responses, recommendation feeds, dynamically rendered pages. The ripples are created by observation (lazy evaluation): **the latent pond only ripples where you look.** "Sampling rate" half-loses meaning; the fine scale is observer-conditional. Consequence for the coin: in latent substrates, REPLAY vs GENERATE blurs at the source — some "measured bits" were themselves generated, so never-render-fake-measured-bits needs a provenance tag for *born-generated* data (the C1 route machinery already fits: a new route value, `generated-on-query`).

**B4 — Scale-free is the DEFAULT, with institutional spectral lines.** Physical ponds have characteristic scales; the internet is power-law everywhere (Zipf, preferential attachment, cascade sizes, attention) — so the latent domain sits near the MAXIMUM-inference end by default: one rung of a cascade distribution buys the whole ladder. The exceptions are man-made: platform constants (280 chars, 24h stories, weekly releases, election cycles) cut **artificial delta-spikes and gaps** into an otherwise scale-free spectrum. Latent spectrum = power-law background + institutional lines. Both halves are testable on pageviews.

**B5 — One ripple dates the tide (holographic redundancy) + the membrane fights back.** Physics: one ripple says nothing about the moon (rare exceptions: a single zircon dates an eon — geology does this; in latent it is PERVASIVE, not exceptional). Latent: a single paragraph carries its era — vocabulary, references, style; an LLM dates one sentence to a decade. **The slow structure is imprinted in each fine sample**, not only in their aggregate: the tide is readable from one ripple, no window needed. And the Mori–Zwanzig noise gains agency: the eliminated fine scale is made of AGENTS who model the coarse law and optimize against it (SEO vs ranking, spam vs filter) — the membrane is adversarial, so latent laws DECAY under publication and law-worldlines bend under observation.
