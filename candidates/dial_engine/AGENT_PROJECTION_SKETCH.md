## 1. The three bands ARE the coin's generative face, valenced

The coin had two faces: sharp/REPLAY (the lawful, low-surprise present trajectory) and fuzzy/GENERATE (sampled counterfactuals). For an AGENT the generative face is not value-neutral — it splits into two valenced tails around the replay band:
- **OK / tolerance / continuation** = the REPLAY band: the known, lawful, low-surprise default — "if nothing changes, this." The kernel.
- **BAD** = the avoid-tail of the generated manifold: projected-bad **for them, in their action space, relative to their kernel canon.** Not objective — bad *to this observer's model* (frame-relative valence; the same future is a different band in a different canon).
- **AMAZING** = the approach-tail: pure fantasy, the aspirational attractor.

So an agent does not merely generate the fuzzy appearance — it generates it **with a value gradient**, and acts to drag the realized future from BAD, through OK, toward AMAZING. The coin extension: *replay is the present; the two generated tails are the feared and the wished future, and valence is the agent's render-coloring of its own generated manifold.* (Established: prospect-theory reference point / gains-vs-losses; self-discrepancy theory's actual/ideal/feared selves = ok/amazing/bad; approach-avoidance BIS/BAS.)

## 3. The two oceans ARE the dial-family attribution rule (the load-bearing new fold)

To expand the pond into an ocean = to close the BAD↔AMAZING gap = to minimize free energy. There are exactly two levers, and they are the framework's own dial families:
- **PASSIVE / "become the ocean in oneself, a calm one" = the FRAME + RENDER dials (change the MAP).** Meditation, existential awareness, "tune out the noise" — re-tune the kernel canon and the render so the BAD/AMAZING tails collapse toward OK: the gap shrinks because you changed how you SEE, not what IS. "Tune out the noise" is literally a **low-pass render dial on perception**. Turned to its limit it dissolves the pond/ocean boundary itself (the straw, B9) — no separation, so the self IS the ocean: **zero gradient → no waves → no surprise → free energy ~ 0 → equanimity.** The calm ocean is the *frame-limit fixed point*: a perfect predictive model of a still world has no residual. (Established: ego-dissolution / default-mode-network quieting; Stoic dichotomy-of-control; acceptance.)
- **FULL-SPECTRUM / "create it, build your vision" = the ENGINE dials (change the TERRITORY).** Act on the world, expand the *actual* pond toward the AMAZING projection. The created ocean is the *engine-limit fixed point*: you didn't re-perceive the boundary, you moved it. (Established: agency, world-building, the change half of acceptance-vs-change.)

Same attribution rule we already run: *is the variance from turning the map-dials or the world-dials?* The serenity-prayer carving (accept vs change) turns out to BE the frame-vs-engine carving. And there is a full spectrum between the two fixed points — most lives are a mixed dial-vector.
