## 1. The picture

A wrapper is a **certain core wrapped in a fuzzy membrane on every side** — top, bottom, left, right — not a crisp boundary, and not merely a fuzzy outer frontier. The core is where confidence ≈ 1; the membrane is the graded halo where it fades toward the frontier. **Reframing tightens the whole skin at once** — each added frame is a question answered, i.e. *conditioning*, which can only lower the uncertainty (`H(X | more frames) ≤ H(X)`) — but the membrane never fully closes: there is always *some* fuzz, by design (the fuzzy-LOD principle, made boundary-complete).

## 2. Agnostic units = frame-relative **fuzzy ratios**

Each frame has a **min** — its finest distinguishable unit, the resolution quantum, *itself fuzzy* (below some grain you cannot tell structure from noise; there is no sharp floor) — and a **max** — everything currently mapped in the frame plus the fuzzy padding. The **agnostic unit is a RATIO within [min, max]**: dimensionless, so it travels across substrates (a molecule's bits and an idea's bits are not comparable; *fraction-of-frame-capacity* is). Because both ends are fuzzy, the unit is a **fuzzy ratio** — an interval / smear — not a crisp percentage; an agnostic measurement arrives with its own fog attached. The denominator is alive: **max** grows as more is mapped and **compresses** when a unifier (a real W_C) folds many things into one (the "compressor" leg of the utility function), so a thing's agnostic value *breathes* as its frame breathes.

**Grounding (established):** absolute (differential) entropy is frame- and even coordinate-dependent; the frame-*invariant*, portable quantity is the **relative** one (KL / normalized entropy). The framework's **§IT spine** (`adversarial_substrate_dynamics_BAR_A_SKETCH.md §IT`, `parents_produce_WC_FORMALIZATION_DRAFT.md`) already runs on KL/MDL; the agnostic-unit is its **normalized reading**. *(Exact alignment to the §IT leverage-ratio is a ratification check, not asserted here.)*

## 3. Interaction is **forced** at the cowlicks

The membrane is hairy. Cont 25 §5 promoted the **hairy-sphere** to "the standard topology of emergent wrappers in the active phase," and Steer 5b is Pav's own: parents persist as **stubs / procedural roots / "receptors or hooks that adjacent wrappers connect onto."** The **hairy-ball theorem** sharpens this *from description to necessity*: you cannot comb a closed membrane perfectly flat — there must be at least one singularity (a cowlick). So a wrapper with a smooth, sealed, **interaction-free boundary is topologically impossible**; the interaction ports are forced, not optional. Total isolation is forbidden by the shape.

The **cell-membrane reading** (already half-canon in cont 25: lens → fusion pore; the Nov-2024 finding that myoblast fusion **localizes to topological defects of compressive membrane stress**): the forced singularities are the **IN/OUT ports** —
- **pointy flicks = OUT** — where the wrapper projects, champions, sends;
- **bald spots = IN** — where the outside reaches in and acts on it.

**Budget (established):** **Poincaré–Hopf** — the singularities' indices sum to a fixed number (the Euler characteristic; 2 for a sphere). A wrapper's interaction ports are therefore a **topologically constrained ledger**: movable, mergeable, splittable, but the total conserved by the membrane's shape.

## 4. The membrane unifies the framework's interaction machinery

All of it lives on this one skin:
- **Welds** form where two membranes touch flick-to-flick — the **shared sub-object S is the contact patch** (cont 25: the lens / fusion pore; cell fusion localizes at the membrane's topological defects). The **synergy** is what is exchanged through those ports that neither parent had alone.

- The **D2 actors** plug **into** it — carriers reaching out through the flicks, inhabitants nested behind the pores.
- The **agnostic ratio (§2)** is the unit in which all of this is read, frame-relative.
