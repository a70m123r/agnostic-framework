## §1 — Framing, provenance, and relationship to the rule of three

**The concept (Pav's "agnostic internet hook").** Latent Olympics is public-facing infrastructure that ranks conjectures / hypotheses / theories into **"events"** — each event like a separate sport or field event with its own scoring discipline — **top-N per category**, with **lineage / heritage as the base**, a **dream→fact race-to-the-top** dynamic, **community-populated** (the public submits and develops their own conjectures). It is the *scaled public-engagement application* of the "rule of three" method-principle; the rule-of-three is the underlying selection mechanism, Latent Olympics is one way to scale it.

**Pav's verbatim steers (load-bearing context, preserved).**
- *Sharpening:* "the function counts, 3 ... derived from the n list, rated by how wide spead they are, utility, legacy alignment and scientific riggor. its a dream to fact horse race dynamic."
- *Scope:* "3 is one way to organise it could be top 10 top 100 or top via category (which would be interesting to populate as it would force a search under some rocks ... engaging thing for community to get involved and populate their own plenty of theory's conjectures) the rating needs to be sussed out quantified tried and defined ... each one like a separate sport in a category or field event. latent Olympics - a race to the top with linage and heritage as a base ... the agnostic internet hook, one of them at least."
- *Handoff:* "yes lets pass it on to claude cause with my providence, while we finish up the other bits in line and mark it as incoming."

**Relationship to the rule of three (per [audit v09 §5.5](../audits/v09_DRAFT.md) — settled findings this design rests on, not re-litigated):**
1. The rule-of-three **folds** into cont 27 §2-3 + cont 18 + audit-cadence. So the Olympics rating function is a **sharpening** of those (cont 27 tier-criteria + cont 18 lineage + the one genuinely-new *spread* criterion), **not a new primitive**. This design is a consolidation that rests on settled canon — consistent with the brief's "no promotion" guardrail.
2. "Three is structurally privileged" was judged a **confirmation-bias artifact** (the canon spans N=2…9; the most load-bearing decomposition is a *two*). So Latent Olympics uses **top-N-per-category**, N event-dependent — the number 3 is never privileged. This is Pav's own scope steer.
3. The rule-of-three's reflexive self-validation was judged an **artifact** (likelihood ratio 1). The Olympics rating function **run on real conjectures with outcomes not pre-determined** is the *single* genuine, non-reflexive Bar-A vehicle §5.5 names — which is the strongest honest reason to build even a narrow v0.

**Discipline framing.** Stage-1 design-sketch only. Latent Olympics stays **Tier-3**; nothing is compiled, no continuation is written, no canon or candidate file is modified. The adversarial-substrate taxonomy is applied to the Olympics *honestly* (§4) — where an attack has no clean defense, "don't build without solving X" is reported as the correct finding, not hidden.

## §2 — The four rating dimensions, operationalized

Pav's sharpening names four scoring dimensions. Per [audit v09 §5.5.1](../audits/v09_DRAFT.md), **three fold onto existing canon and only *spread* is genuinely new** — so each dimension below cites the parent it sharpens rather than inventing scoring from scratch.

- **Legacy alignment** (folds onto [cont 18](../continuations/18.md) procedural lineage + [cont 22 §7](../continuations/22.md) canon-stack). *Scores:* how richly a conjecture composes with what is already established — its procedural-lineage depth ("rings on a tree," cont 18 §2). *Computes:* a canon-stack-composition score — count the named procedural-transition layers the conjecture can be traced through (the cont 22 §7 drill-down template), weighted by how *measurable-and-observable* each connection is (cont 18 §1), **not** by citation count. *Fails by:* citation-stuffing; orthodoxy capture (rewarding safe restatements over novelty); penalizing frontier conjectures with legitimately opaque hidden-protocol lineage (cont 18 §4).
- **Scientific rigor** (folds onto [cont 27 §2](../continuations/27.md) tier-criteria). *Scores:* falsifiability, empirical grounding, named-promotion-bar progress. *Computes:* tier position (an ordinal floor) + within-tier bar progress — directly readable from the tier-assignment header cont 27 §2 already mandates. This dimension carries the **dream→fact directionality** (dream = Tier 3, fact = Tier 1). *Fails by:* falsifiability theatre; premature graduation; rigor-without-utility (sterile correctness).
- **Utility** (folds onto the framework's standing **load-bearing-vs-decoration** audit standard; [v06 §10.4](../audits/v06.md)). *Scores:* whether the conjecture generates predictions/insight the existing vocabulary could *not* produce. *Computes:* enumerate what the conjecture licenses, subtract what the prior vocabulary already licensed; utility is the remainder. *Fails by:* re-description-as-novelty; substrate-mismatch (utility is observer-relative, cont 22 §6). Functions less as a coordinate than as a **zero-or-positive gate** — decoration scores zero and is filtered.
- **Spread** — *the one genuinely-new criterion, developed most.* *Scores:* the candidate pool's **coverage of conceptual space** — whether the top-N cover different regions or are near-duplicates dressed as alternatives. Critically, **spread is relational** (a conjecture's *marginal contribution to pool diversity*) where the other three are *per-item* — which is exactly why it is structurally irreducible to them. *Computes:* embedding-dispersion — embed each conjecture (statement + lineage + predictions), measure pool coverage (a determinantal-point-process volume / max-min diversity subset-selection), and score a conjecture by its distance to the nearest already-selected member. This ties to canon without folding: the embedding manifold *is* the latent-kernel substrate (cont 22 §2 Charm-Kanon semantic manifold). Spread operationalizes Pav's **"force a search under some rocks"** — empty embedding regions are exactly where the pool is thin and population is incentivized.

**The actual axis structure (the honest answer to "if three fold, what is left?").** The four are **not** four independent axes. Beneath the four community-facing knobs is the framework's own **dual-kernel pair** (cont 22 §2): a **physical-kernel heritage-depth axis** (legacy alignment) × a **latent-kernel diversity axis** (spread), **graded by** the rigor/tier coordinate (the dream→fact track), **gated by** utility (the decoration filter). Legacy and spread are deliberately **anti-correlated** — they are the framework's attraction/repulsion pair operating on the conjecture pool.

**Selection is a Pareto front, never a scalar.** The top-N per event are the **non-dominated set** (a conjecture survives if no other beats it on *all* dimensions), truncated to event-dependent N. This preserves "**no winner crowned by declaration, only by surviving development**." A weighted-sum scalar is forbidden — it would crown a winner by fiat. N is event-dependent (top-3 / top-10 / top-100), never privileging 3.

**Computability split (a constraint, not a flaw to engineer away).** Spread is mostly-computable *but only as trustworthy as its embedding* (an attack surface). Rigor is a computable ordinal + a *judged* bar-progress increment. Legacy and utility are **irreducibly human-judgment**. So Latent Olympics is necessarily a **human-judgment system with computational assists — not an automated leaderboard.** This is honest and load-bearing: it pushes weight onto governance/moderation (§4, §6) and means the cheap-to-build version (an automated scalar leaderboard) is exactly the trivially-gameable one.

**The most serious dimension-level gate (reported honestly).** Spread's coverage objective is **trivially gamed by Sybil/adversarial padding** — inject distant junk to occupy empty regions and inflate the pool's spread (or crowd a rival out of the top-N). Coverage is *maximized by spreading out*, which is what a flooder does. Therefore **spread must run only on a pool already filtered by a rigor/utility quality floor; spread as a standalone selector is unbuildable.** (This is the dimension-level face of the §4 friction-vs-flooding conflict.)

## §3 — Candidate events / categories

An "event" is a scoring discipline: a bounded question-space with its own N, its own per-dimension weights (summing to 1.0; illustrative starting points to be "sussed out, quantified, tried"), and — the load-bearing constraint — its own **Tier-1 anchor floor**. An event with no Tier-1 anchors is a pure speculation marketplace; an event that is *all* Tier-1 is a closed record with no race. A live event needs anchors to define the discipline *and* a dream→fact gradient to climb. The five below carve events along five axes (domain, structural-pattern, epistemic-register, substrate-scale, adversarial-load), expanding Cowork's two.

## §4 — The adversarial-substrate taxonomy applied to the Olympics (anti-gaming architecture)

Latent Olympics is a substrate with an L0 mediator (the rating function) and a beneficiary-bearing reward gradient (top-N placement, with reputational/attention payoff). [`adversarial_substrate_dynamics.md`](adversarial_substrate_dynamics.md) §11 predicts that *any* such substrate **will** grow an evolved adversarial population (Red Queen dynamics). So this is not an optional add-on — it is the honest pre-mortem, and (run on real conjectures) the strongest reason to build: a non-reflexive worked example of the framework's taxonomy applied to the framework's own infrastructure.

**Master finding ordering the analysis:** the **rating function IS the kernel-canon-protocol-function** ([cont 22 §0/§3](../continuations/22.md)) for the Olympics substrate — the protocol that decides which conjecture/event compiles at all. That makes **§2.1 canon hijacking the apex attack: whoever captures the rating function adjudicates all five others.** Every defense below is only as strong as the §2.1 defense — which is the one with no clean solution.

| Sub-primitive | Attack on the Olympics | Design defense | Residual risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **§2.1 Canon hijacking** | Capture of the rating function + category taxonomy: reweight spread/utility/legacy/rigor or gate category-creation so favored conjectures place while the board still *renders* as legitimate competition (vertical capture of the protocol that decides what matters at all). | Make the protocol the most-versioned, most-audited artifact: weight-vector + schema public, diffed, changed only via the cont 27 §2 three-tier procedure with named bars. Lean on cont 18 lineage so a silent reweight shows as a discontinuity. **Plural, forkable rating functions** (publish under 2–3 declared weightings, like cont 25 §10's mapping-toggle). | **EXISTENTIAL — no clean defense.** A protocol cannot adjudicate its own capture (the reflexive gap §5.5 found fatal to the rule-of-three). Transparency/forking raise cost and shorten detection but do not remove the niche — whoever owns the canonical fork sets the Schelling point. **Do not build a single canonical Olympics without first solving custody / change-process / fork-legitimacy.** |
| **§2.2 Memetic warfare** | Coordinated entry-flooding engineered to look high-confidence-low-cost-to-verify + coordinated up/down-rating, to exhaust the reviewers' finite A⁻ budget (cont 13) so low-rigor entries place by default. | Make rigor a **submitter-pre-paid A⁻ cost**: require a falsifier/method/checkable lineage *before* an entry can score (cont 27 §2 Tier-1 bar as the entry gate) — converts volume from attack into self-imposed cost. Rate-limit + lineage-weight ratings (a fresh account carries near-zero weight). | **Manageable, not eliminable.** Pre-paid rigor defeats *cheap* flooding but not *funded* Sybil farms or LLM-generated entries clearing a shallow gate (advsub §11.3 "invasive species," generation outpacing detection). Needs an ongoing detection-vs-generation **budget**, not a one-time gate. |
| **§2.3 Bad-faith rendering** | An entry dressed in a high-prestige event's discipline (e.g. rendered as a rigor-event physics conjecture) while running a low-cost discipline (ideology/marketing). Rendered-A⁻ ≠ actual-A⁻; Batesian mimicry. | Bind each event's score to that event's *actual checkable* discipline: a rigor score requires a rigor falsifier **on file and surviving a challenge round** — discipline demonstrated, not asserted. Admit only demonstrable-A⁻ events, or accept heavy human moderation. |

Capture routes attention to a patron, imposes a placement rent, or — most insidious — corrupts legacy-alignment so "aligns with established lineage" silently means "aligns with incumbents," rigging the *fact* pole of the race. | Split custody: ranking-compute, category-definition, legacy-scoring under separate change-logs. Make legacy-alignment a **traceable-lineage score** (cont 18: "what does this descend from, checkably") **not an authority/endorsement score**. **No paid placement, ever.** | **EXISTENTIAL where it overlaps §2.1.** Legacy is the load-bearing *base* (Pav's steer) so it cannot be dropped, yet "alignment with legacy" is one keystroke from "alignment with the powerful." The legacy weight is an **unsolved knob**: too high rigs the race for incumbents, too low makes the heritage base vestigial. Needs a mechanism for a low-legacy dream to *earn* legacy by performance — not yet specified. |
| **§2.5 Suppression dynamics** | Remove a rival's capacity-to-cycle by denying the pull phase (cont 30 §2.2): deprecate a category, shadow-remove an entry, bury a conjecture below visibility — dense A⁻ with no A⁺ space, forced dormancy. | Removal is **append-only and reversible** (cont 20 dormancy = preserved, wakeable; logged reason). Guarantee every entry a **minimum pull-phase** (a challenge-and-response window) before demotion — structurally denying squeeze-without-pull. | **Manageable — the cleanest defense of the six.** But it holds only if moderation custody is not captured (loops to §2.1), and the minimum-exposure floor is itself a flooding surface (trades off against §2.2 — co-tune them). |

Lineage-weighting rewards entries that spawn checkable descendants. | **Manageable but never "done" — Red Queen.** advsub §11.1/§11.5: defenses breed the next attack; any fixed contribution-metric is Goodhart-gamed once known. **The honest split.**
- **Existential (do-not-build-without-solving):** §2.1 canon hijacking + the §2.4/§2.1 overlap on **legacy-alignment custody**. Both reduce to one unsolved problem — **governance of the rating function** (custody, change-process, fork-legitimacy, and how a low-legacy dream earns legacy without incumbent permission). This is the reflexive gap §5.5 already identified: a protocol cannot certify its own legitimacy from inside.
- **Manageable (with named residual arms-race cost):** §2.2, §2.3 (legible events only), §2.5 (cleanest), §2.6.
- **Cross-cutting, unpatchable-by-design:** Red Queen — *every* manageable defense generates its successor attack, so the anti-gaming layer is a **maintained cadence, not a fixed wall.** This is simultaneously an argument *for* building (it exercises the framework's audit-cadence on live, non-reflexive data) and *against* building it naively (a fire-and-forget leaderboard rots into monoculture within one arms-race cycle).

Run honestly, this section *is* a candidate Bar-A worked example for the **adversarial-substrate** candidate itself (independent of the rule-of-three): it generates concrete, not-pre-determined predictions about which niche fills first (funded-flooding §2.2 and spread-farming §2.6) — exactly the advsub §11.5 next-niche-emergence form.

## §5 — Anchoring + lineage architecture (the heritage backbone)

Pav makes lineage the *base*: "a race to the top with linage and heritage as a base." The framework already supplies the primitive — **procedural lineage** ([cont 18 §1](../continuations/18.md): "canon is procedural ... each layer the measurable-and-observable compile of the layer beneath it"; "you *are* canon, with all previous layers active inside you"). The design move: make every entry carry its rings visibly.

**The provenance trail.** Each entry declares (a) the *tradition* it descends from, (b) the *prior conjectures* it composes with (parent entries by id), (c) the *canon/established theory* it builds on — the [cont 22 §7](../continuations/22.md) procedural-drill-down applied as a submission requirement. Entries need not reach Kanon; they name the layers they sit on and **stop honestly where the drill stops** ("drill not yet walked" is itself legible and rate-able). The data structure is the [cont 25](../continuations/25.md) hairy-sphere topology: declared parents persist as **stubs** — the literal join-points where descendants attach. So the trail is not metadata beside the conjecture; it *is* the connective tissue of the event graph. **The Olympics is structurally the cont-18 procedural-lineage graph made public and editable** — which is what makes it legible: a visitor traces any top conjecture back through its rings to tradition+canon and forward to all its descendants. That browsable heritage-tree is the intended public-engagement payload (but see §6 — it is also readable as a *read-only internal artifact*).

**Lineage-richness feeds legacy-alignment (§2).** Legacy-alignment is scored off the trail — on **depth** (drill layers, cont 22 §7), **load-bearing connection** (does the entry *compile on* its parents, the cont 18 §1 measurable-and-observable test — not name-dropping), and **unity-cost paid** (cont 18 §3: genuine descent inherits the tradition's open tensions; a laundered citation does not). This keeps legacy-alignment from collapsing into a citation count.

**Two entry classes, kept in fair competition.** The hard problem: a deep-provenance descendant scores high on legacy by construction; a sui-generis newcomer (the genuine "dream" end) scores low *no matter how good*. A naive sum would suppress exactly the novelty the Olympics exists to surface. Two coupled mechanisms:
- **(a) Spread carries the newcomer.** Spread (the one genuinely-new dimension) and legacy are **anti-correlated by design** — deep descendants cluster near their lineage (high legacy, low spread); rootless newcomers sit in open space (low legacy, high spread). The Pareto front holds both without either dominating. An entry with *no* parent stub is not disqualified; it is a fresh sphere rewarded for reaching territory the graph has not.
- **(b) Lineage is earnable — the dream→fact track.** A newcomer is not frozen at its low legacy score: when later entries compose onto its stub, or it is shown to drill into canon nobody had connected, its trail *grows* and its legacy *rises*. Heritage is an **accruable emergent property**, not only an inherited endowment. This is the honest mechanism behind dream→fact: the dream is rewarded for spread now and earns legacy as it climbs.

**Two failure modes, named and structurally guarded:** *ossification into orthodoxy* (prevented by legacy being 1-of-4 weighted dims with spread its counterweight + top-N event-tuned weights; a monotone-by-heritage board is an observable signal the legacy weight is too high) and *rootless novelty marketplace* (prevented by the trail being a *requirement* with a load-bearing-connection test + the cont 27 §3 pruning of dream-end entries that never accrue lineage or development). Per cont 18 §1, this is **balance held by ongoing pressure**, not a solved equilibrium — the design makes the tension *visible and tunable per event*, it does not claim to have removed it.

**Honest anti-gaming finding (provenance is itself attackable).** Legacy-alignment auto-scored from *self-declared* trails is gameable: **provenance laundering** (declare prestigious parents an entry doesn't compile on — the cuckoo-egg / canon-hijacking move) and **citation-ring / mutual-stub farming** (colluding entries declaring each other as parents). The cont 18 load-bearing-connection test + the C0.2 beneficiary-boundary test are the principled defenses, but they require **community-verifiable/challengeable parent-edges or curator-adjudicated descent** for high-rank entries. **Finding: do not ship legacy-alignment on raw self-declaration.** A legacy score anyone can trace, challenge, and falsify against the public graph is also what makes the run *non-reflexive* (an outcome not pre-determined by the scorer) — the genuine reason-to-build the heritage backbone.

## §6 — Honest design-questions report

### §6.1 — Strongest reasons to build (a v0)
1. **The genuine non-reflexive Bar-A vehicle.** [Audit v09 §5.5.5](../audits/v09_DRAFT.md) names exactly one escape from the rule-of-three's likelihood-ratio-1 self-validation: the rating function *run on real conjectures with the answer not pre-determined*. Events 1 and 2 are concrete instances (convergence-count and time-adjudicated predictions resolve exogenously). This is the one reason-to-build the framework cannot manufacture internally.
2. **A worked example of the adversarial-substrate taxonomy on the framework's own infrastructure** — §4 is non-trivially generative (it predicts which niche fills first), so the Olympics-as-substrate is itself a candidate Bar-A example *for the adversarial candidate*. Building it exercises two stuck Tier-3 candidates against reality at once.
3. **Spread does real, irreducible work** — it is relational where the other three are per-item, and it only has a pool to be relational over at top-N scale (it is vestigial in cont 27's sequential single-candidate procedure). If spread is worth anything, an N-per-category surface is where it earns it.
4. **The lineage base maps cleanly onto cont 18** (a consolidation, not a new claim) and yields a browsable public-legibility artifact.
5. **A forcing function for under-explored conjecture-space** (Pav's "search under rocks") — a populated category map surfaces conjectures the closed council would not generate.

### §6.2 — Strongest reasons NOT to build (or not naively)
1. **A half-formed Olympics demonstrates the adversarial taxonomy AT THE FRAMEWORK'S OWN EXPENSE** (the decisive reason, per the brief). If a single canonical Olympics ships before §2.1 custody is solved, the framework will have publicly built the exact captured-canon-stack its own theory predicts — discrediting the taxonomy by confirming it *against the builder*. Worse than not building.
2. **The internet-hook and the anti-gaming requirement are structurally opposed.** Low-friction public submission is the §2.2 flooding surface; every defense raises friction; the engagement goal demands lowering it. Unresolved by any component.
3. **It is necessarily human-judgment-heavy** (legacy + utility irreducible; rigor's increment too) — a human-adjudication system, not the scalable automated leaderboard the public framing implies. The cheap build is the gameable one; the gaming-resistant build does not scale to the volume Pav wants.
4. **Red Queen makes it a perpetual operating cost** (a maintained cadence, not a fixed artifact) — and the arms-race budget is unscoped. A fire-and-forget version is *known* to rot within one cycle.
5. **The canonical delta is thin** — three of four dimensions fold; only spread + N-simultaneity is new (below the v06 §10.4 bar; cont 30 §2 precedent). Large public-infrastructure surface for a small new-canon delta.
6. **Soft/illegible events have no anti-gaming defense and Pav's scope wants them** — admitting them imports the unsolvable §2.3 niche; excluding them narrows the stated concept. Either way a core part of the concept is unbuildable as specified.

### §6.3 — The defensible v0 (narrower than the stated concept)
An **internal, curated, closed-submission "exhibition Olympics"** that **drops the public-submission layer entirely** and runs only on framework-native conjectures the council already holds:
- **One event** — Event 1 (Convergence) or Event 2 (Predictions): both clear the anchor floor *and* carry the non-reflexive Bar-A property.
- **No public intake** — so §2.1 canon hijacking, §2.2 flooding, and §2.3 bad-faith rendering are out of scope *by construction* (no exogenous submitter to capture or flood the protocol).
- **Curator-adjudicated rating**, explicitly labelled a human-judgment system with compute assists — **no automated scalar leaderboard**.
- **Pareto non-dominated top-N** (event-N, not 3), **no cross-event meta-score** (honor "each one like a separate sport"; defer the all-around).
- **Spread runs only on the rigor/utility-floored pool**; **lineage shipped as a curator-verified heritage-tree**, provenance a *requirement*, edges adjudicated not auto-scored.

**Purpose:** RUN the rating function once on real conjectures with outcomes not pre-determined — discharging the §5.5.5 non-reflexive Bar-A test for the rule-of-three and generating one adversarial-taxonomy worked example — **without exposing the kernel-canon-protocol-function to public capture.** **It explicitly does NOT yet satisfy Pav's headline goal (the public hook); that gap is stated plainly, because a defensible v0 and Pav's stated want are not the same object.**

### §6.4 — The "agnostic internet hook" assessment: **vestigial as currently argued**
The public-engagement function has not been shown doing irreplaceable work. (1) The two honest Bar-A-bearing events run on framework-native conjectures and **don't need public submission** to deliver their value — the internal v0 captures it with zero public hook (the tell: if the internal version captures the load-bearing value, the public layer is addable decoration). (2) Everywhere the public layer *would* do distinctive work (Event 4's "search under rocks"), it is exactly the §2.2/§2.3 capture surface — **the hook's distinctive contribution and its existential vulnerability are the same surface.** (3) The browsable heritage-tree is equally a read-only internal artifact; nothing requires the public to *write* for it to be legible. **What would make the hook load-bearing:** a demonstration that public submission produces conjectures/edges/resolutions the closed council provably could not generate *and* that survive the quality floor — i.e. that the "under the rocks" yield is **net-positive after the flooding is filtered out.** Pav's intuition is the right candidate; the design *asserts* the yield rather than *showing* it exceeds the flooding cost. Until then, the honest read: an internal tool with a public costume, where the costume carries the existential risk.

### §6.5 — The unsolved / existential blockers (carry-forward)
- **§2.1 canon hijacking** — no clean defense; gates the whole canonical build.
- **§2.4 legacy-alignment custody** — coupled to §2.1; the legacy weight is an unsolved scalar; the "earn legacy by performance" mechanism is unspecified.
- **§2.2 funded/Sybil/LLM flooding** — no clean defense at scale; the reason v0 drops public submission.
- **§2.3 soft-event bad-faith** — no defense but exclusion.

### §6.6 — Design questions needing a Pav/Cowork steer (nothing builds until these have answers)
1. **Governance/custody/fork-legitimacy of the rating function** — the gating question. Single canonical instance with a custody body, or plural forkable weightings with no canonical Schelling point? (§2.1 has no internal design defense — it needs a decision, not a sketch.)
2. **Is the v0 the internal exhibition** (no public submission), and is Pav willing to accept that this **defers the actual agnostic-internet-hook indefinitely**? The defensible v0 and the headline goal are different objects.
3. **The legacy-alignment weight** — how set, and by what mechanism does a low-legacy "dream" earn legacy by *performance* rather than incumbent endorsement? (Decides whether the race is real or rigged for incumbents.)
4. **Siloed events vs a cross-event "all-around"** — "each one like a separate sport" (siloed) vs "race to the top" (implies cross-event standing). Incompatible at the design level (cross-event entries aren't commensurable); pick one.
5. **Scope of admissible events** — legible-falsifier-only (defensible, narrower) vs admit soft conjectures (Pav's stated breadth, imports the unsolvable §2.3 niche). A direct trade.
6. **Appetite for a perpetual criterion-maintenance (Red Queen) operating cost** — at what cadence/resourcing? A fire-and-forget version is known to fail.
