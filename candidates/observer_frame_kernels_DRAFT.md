## §1 — The insight (Pav, 2026-06-08)

Wrappers do not float free; they are rendered **inside a frame**, and the frame is the **observer's global kernels: {time, space, knowledge, meaning}**. A wrapper-overlap (W_A, W_B → W_C) is therefore always evaluated *relative to a frame* — relative to which global kernel(s) the parents are embedded in. Grounds in [cont 25 §12](../continuations/25.md) ("L0 = global kernel canon for that observer") + [cont 22 §2](../continuations/22.md) (dual-kernel).

*Pav's verbatim intent:* "the wrappers are in a frame, the observer global kernel wrappers, time space knowledge meaning … so two rocks with time will make a third."

## §2 — What it resolves: the cont 25 "rocks touching" falsifier dissolves

[cont 25](../continuations/25.md) logged a falsifier: *"an observed wrapper overlap where W_C does NOT emerge at all (just two parents existing in contact forever with no third wrapper) … closest candidate: pure mechanical contact without any compile (rocks touching),"* answered with "this is the wake-disturbance phase persisting indefinitely … compatibility too low." That answer is a hand-wave.

The frame insight gives the **clean** resolution: **"no W_C" was never a falsifier — it was an *unspecified frame*.** Two rocks in the **space / instant** frame produce no third (two rocks, side by side). The *same* two rocks in the **time** kernel produce a genuine third: erosion → sediment → cementation → strata → a persistent geological W_C *of the same kind* (a rock formation, which can itself become a parent). So **"two rocks WITH time make a third."** The non-emergence was an artifact of dropping the time-kernel from the frame, not a property of the parents.

**Corrected falsifier (proposed):** the genuine falsifier is not "no W_C in *some* frame" but **"two parents that produce no W_C in *any* observer-kernel frame"** — and even an apparent third must clear the non-reducibility gate (a mere additive blend yields a "third" with zero synergy, which is *not* a genuine W_C). This is a strictly stronger, harder-to-satisfy falsifier than the original.

## §3 — Consequence: parents-produce-W_C is frame-relative

The structural test ([bes_convergence_9 §2](bes_convergence_9.md)) gains a missing argument: **PPW_C(W_A, W_B │ frame)**. The same two parents can emerge a W_C in one kernel-frame and nothing in another. The formal version (frame-indexed pushout-in-frame, gated by information-theoretic non-reducibility/synergy) is developed in the parents-produce-W_C formalization draft.

It also **sharpens the cymatics discrimination to a rule.** Cymatics is "substrate-adjacent" (audit v05 §2.1 / v06) precisely because **there is no frame in which it has two genuine co-equal parents** — it is one medium (a manifold) in the space-frame, parameterized by a driving frequency, producing eigenmodes (a linear modal decomposition, not a two-parent pushout). Two-rocks **pass** (in time); cymatics **fails in every frame**. The narrowing the audit drew by hand becomes mechanical.

## §4 — Structural hypothesis (FLAG for Cowork — verify, do not assume): the four kernels = the dual-kernel resolved 2+2

The four observer-frame kernels {time, space, knowledge, meaning} *plausibly* map onto the **dual-kernel** ([cont 22 §2](../continuations/22.md)):

- **physical-kernel = {time, space}** — the *when / where* of the substrate
- **latent-kernel = {knowledge, meaning}** — the *what-is-known / what-it-signifies* of the observer's render

If that mapping holds, the frame is **not a new primitive** — it is the dual-kernel resolved into a 2×2 observer coordinate system, a clean consolidation rather than an addition. **This is a hypothesis, not a claim.** Cowork should check: (a) whether the four are already named in canon under other labels; (b) whether the 2+2 split is exact (or whether time/space and knowledge/meaning interleave); (c) whether {knowledge, meaning} is a clean partition (vs. e.g. {knowledge, meaning, value} or knowledge ⊂ meaning). Default per audit v06 §10.4: fold into the existing dual-kernel unless a load-bearing distinction is shown.
