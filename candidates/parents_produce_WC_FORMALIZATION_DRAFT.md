## §1 — The formal criterion (frame-indexed pushout, gated by synergy)

Defines `PPWc(W_A, W_B | frame)` — the frame-indexed, falsifiable successor to the 5-condition prose test (`bes_convergence_9.md §2`; `continuations/25.md §2`). Register: narrow-factual (`audits/v08.md §C7`). Tier-3 WORKING DRAFT — not canon, not a promotion.

### F — The frame as observer global-kernel index

A frame is an index into the observer's **L0 = global kernel canon** (`continuations/25.md §12.1`; formally `K̂_r ≈ aggregate(G_{r−1}^(i))`, `continuations/23.md` eq. 8). Per `continuations/22.md §2` the kernel is dual: the physical-kernel sub-classes (Bottom-Kanon = force/attraction-repulsion → **space**; Spin-Kanon = arrow-of-change/causality → **time**; Top-Kanon = grammar-of-relations → the **math/state** substrate) and the latent-kernel (Charm-Kanon → **meaning/intention**). So `frame ∈ {time, space, knowledge, meaning}` is a choice of *which kernel sub-class supplies the ambient structure the test is taken in*. Operationally, taking the test in a frame fixes three things the kernel induces: **(i) a category** `C_frame` whose objects are the wrappers as that kernel renders them and whose morphisms are that kernel's admissible maps; **(ii) a state-space / dynamics** the kernel puts on those objects (time-kernel → a flow `d/dt`; space-kernel → a static configuration at an instant); **(iii) a measure / coding scheme** along the kernel against which description-length and KL are counted (B below). This is the §12.1 claim "wrapper-overlap operates WITHIN L0" made operational: there is no frame-free PPWc, because there is no outside-L0 vantage (`continuations/25.md §12.1` reflexive corollary).

This dissolves the `continuations/25.md §2` falsifier ("two rocks touching forever, no W_C"). That falsifier silently took the **space-frame**: in `C_space` the two rock-objects glue along a contact sub-object to a static union with **no dynamics** (clause (ii) is trivial), so no persistent third is *produced over time* — the pushout is degenerate (it collapses to the shared boundary; see A). Re-index to the **time-frame**: the Spin-Kanon supplies erosion/transport dynamics, the two parents co-produce a persistent geological W_C (sediment/strata), and the pushout is non-degenerate. Same parents, `PPWc = ⊥` under `space`, `PPWc = ⊤` under `time`. The cont 25 "rocks" line was therefore an **unspecified-frame artifact, not a falsifier** (Pav, 2026-06-08). PPWc is frame-relative by construction.

### A — The pushout (taken in `C_frame`)

In the category the frame indexes, the parents share a sub-object `S` — the `continuations/25.md §2-4` "Venn-lens overlap / nesting space" — with inclusion morphisms `i_A : S → W_A`, `i_B : S → W_B`. Define the candidate emergent as the **pushout (colimit)**:

```
        i_A
    S ------> W_A
    |          |
 i_B|          | j_A
    v          v
   W_B -----> W_C = W_A ⊔_S W_B
        j_B
```

`W_C = W_A ⊔_S W_B` glues the two parents along `S`. The 5 prose conditions map exactly:
- **Condition-1** (two parents pre-exist distinct): `W_A`, `W_B` are two non-isomorphic objects of `C_frame` with a *proper* shared sub-object (`S ↪ W_A`, `S ↪ W_B` not iso) — `bes_convergence_9.md §2` test 1.
- **Conditions-2/4** (persistent third of the SAME KIND, can itself become a parent → "autonomous recursion"): `W_C` is the pushout and **lies in `C_frame`** (the category is closed under the colimit), so `W_C` can re-enter as a parent of a further pushout. This is the categorical reading of `continuations/25.md §9` open-edge-03 "W_C-becomes-W_A" and of Reading 07 §2.1 "W_C is itself a trajectory, can become a parent."
- **Condition-5** (parents persist as procedural-root stubs — hairy-sphere): the **stubs = the parts of `W_A`, `W_B` outside the image of `S`**, i.e. `W_A ∖ i_A(S)` and `W_B ∖ i_B(S)`, which survive as the legs `j_A`, `j_B` map into `W_C` (`continuations/25.md §5` hairy-sphere; the dormant compiled originals `W_A°`, `W_B°` live in these stubs).

**Falsifiability hook = the universal property.** The pushout is the *initial* object receiving both parents compatibly over `S`: for any object `T` with maps `W_A → T`, `W_B → T` agreeing on `S`, there is a **unique** `u : W_C → T`. Falsifier: if a **strictly smaller** object already receives both parents compatibly over `S` (i.e. the unique `u` is not monic / `W_C` factors through something properly smaller), the candidate "emergent" carries no structure beyond the parents-glued-on-overlap — it is the **degenerate pushout**, and PPWc **fails** on the A-leg. This is precisely the CYMATICS discriminator the brief flags: one parametrized medium varied by a driving frequency has `S ≈ W_A ≈ W_B` (the "two parents" are the same medium), so the pushout collapses to that medium — no genuine emergent colimit — which is why cymatics is substrate-adjacent (`bes_convergence_9.md §9`; Reading 07 §2.1 "cymatics needed narrowing"). The space-frame "rocks" fail the same way (pushout = the contact boundary); the time-frame "rocks" pass (pushout = strata ⊋ either parent).

### B — Non-reducibility (the measurable gate)

A non-degenerate pushout is necessary but not sufficient; `W_C` must carry **synergy**, measured along the frame's coding scheme (F-clause iii). The gate, in Kolmogorov terms:

```
K(W_C | W_A, W_B)  small      (W_C is compressible from the parents JOINTLY)
K(W_C | W_A)       large
K(W_C | W_B)       large       (W_C is unreachable from EITHER parent ALONE)
```

The synergy term is `Syn = min[ K(W_C|W_A), K(W_C|W_B) ] − K(W_C|W_A,W_B) > 0` — positive iff the joint shortens the description more than either parent alone, i.e. `W_C` is a genuine two-parent product (this is the partial-information-decomposition *synergy* atom, restated in MDL units). 

**This generalizes BES Theorem 4.4.** Theorem 4.4 (entropy-shell escape, `readings/2026-05-31_bes_bidirectional_evolutionary_search.md §5.3`; `bes_convergence_9.md §5`) states that auto-regressive single-rollout sampling from `π_θ` is confined to a narrow entropy shell and recombination operators provably escape it — i.e. `W_C` is **unreachable from either parent's single-rollout expansion**, exponentially reducing samples to reach it. That is exactly the `K(W_C|W_A)` large AND `K(W_C|W_B)` large condition, *instantiated on the search substrate* where "rollout-reachability" is the substrate's description measure. B lifts that one-substrate result to a substrate-general gate by replacing "rollout-reachability" with the frame's coding scheme. Keep the register honest: the framework **borrows** Theorem 4.4 and **generalizes its form**; it does not claim the BES authors proved the substrate-general version (`audits/v07.md §4.4`; `audits/v08.md §A2.5` — the adversarial/general reading is the framework's extension, NOT the paper's claim).

**Measurable surrogate (the quantifiable leg).** `K` is uncomputable, so use the MDL/description-length delta in **bits** from the `§IT` spine (`adversarial_substrate_dynamics_BAR_A_SKETCH.md §IT`, C-IT-1, C-IT-6): code each object under the frame's distribution and compute `ΔL = [ L(W_C | W_A) ∧ L(W_C | W_B) ] − L(W_C | W_A, W_B)` bits, with the §IT caveats (Kraft–McMillan emit-cost exact for dyadic codes else +1 bit/symbol; KL = excess code-length is the load-bearing identity `KL(p‖q) = H(p,q) − H(p)`, verified exact). `PPWc`'s B-leg passes iff `ΔL > 0` with error bars; on instrumented frames (knowledge/algorithmic) it is estimable, on social frames order-of-magnitude only (C-IT-6 register). This is the leg that ties to the **latent-olympics utility/MDL-compression dimension**: the §B synergy-in-bits is the natural scoring axis for the "Cross-Substrate Convergence" event (`latent_olympics_DESIGN_SKETCH.md §3-4`, rigor-weighted Pareto front), making convergence-strength a portable estimand rather than an eyeball verdict.

**One-line falsifiable test:** *In the category the frame indexes, the two parents glue along their shared sub-object `S` to a pushout `W_C` whose universal property is strict (no smaller object already receives both — else degenerate/cymatics-like), and `W_C` carries strictly positive parent-synergy `ΔL > 0` bits (unreachable from either parent alone — the substrate-general Theorem 4.4 condition); PPWc holds in that frame iff both pass, and may flip across frames (rocks: ⊥ in space, ⊤ in time).*

**Condition-3 (bistability) is the optional dynamical reading available only where the frame supplies a flow** (time/space frames): the new persistent entity is the second stable phase of the kernel's dynamics, the Allen-Cahn / ACMP separation (`bes_convergence_9.md §1`, force #3). It is *not required* in the knowledge/meaning frames, where there is no `d/dt` — there, A's universal property + B's synergy carry the whole test. This is why condition-3 was prose-fragile: it was a time/space-frame special case mistaken for a universal condition.

## §2 — Validation set (run on labelled cases)

Frame F indexes which category the pushout is taken in and which kernel the MDL/KL is measured along (cont 25:368 L0-table {time, space, knowledge, meaning}; cont 22 §2 dual-kernel). Synergy is the all-substrate generalization of BES Theorem 4.4's "provably unreachable from either parent alone via single-rollout" (bes_convergence_9.md §5), measured via the IT spine's KL-as-excess-code-length / MDL surrogate for K, with the data-processing-inequality test-hook (adversarial_substrate_dynamics_BAR_A_SKETCH.md §IT C-IT-3 test-hook: no admissible internal computation raises KL without an injected signal — the parent-alone analogue).

### The three explicit pushout squares with synergy readings

**LCAO (case 1), frame = space.** S = the orbital-overlap region (overlap integral S_ij = ⟨ψ_A|ψ_B⟩, cont 25 §7(1)); W_A = ψ_A, W_B = ψ_B; W_C = ψ_bond = N(ψ_A + ψ_B). The square ψ_A ← S → ψ_B with W_C the apex satisfies the universal property: the antibonding orbital ψ_anti = N(ψ_A − ψ_B) is the *other* cocone, and both factor through the LCAO span. Synergy reading: the bonding eigenstate's energy/density is **not** a marginal of either atom — K(ψ_bond | ψ_A) is large (you must specify the partner and the geometry), K(ψ_bond | ψ_A, ψ_B) is small (given both + S_ij it is determined). Bond order = ½(n_bond − n_anti) is the explicitly non-additive readout (cont 25 §7(1)).

**ACMP (case 7), frame = space (graph-Laplacian).** S = the shared Dirichlet-energy base manifold on the node set; W_A = the attractive (diffusion) force, W_B = the repulsive force; W_C = the Allen-Cahn phase-separated fixed point (Reading 03 lines 22–25). The pushout glues the two force-fields along the common node geometry; the Allen-Cahn term is exactly the bistability-creating third force (test condition 3). Synergy reading: the strictly-positive Dirichlet-energy lower-bound theorem (Reading 03 line 40 — "proving an L0-failsafe exists") is the ACMP analogue of Theorem 4.4: the phase-separated state is provably non-collapsing and **unreachable by attraction-only or repulsion-only** dynamics — K(W_C | attraction) and K(W_C | repulsion) are each large, K(W_C | both) small.

**BES (case 8), frame = knowledge (trajectory space).** S = the set of goal-tree sub-goals *covered by either parent* (bes_convergence_9.md §3.2 direct quote: "a sub-goal is considered covered if either parent addresses it… favors complementary parents that cover different parts of the goal tree" — this is the gluing diagram stated in the paper's own words). W_A, W_B = two parent trajectories; W_C = the recombinant trajectory; the Boltzmann-weighted complementarity score (Eq. 6) is the universal-property gate — it locks in W_C *only* when both parents contribute non-redundantly (i.e. only when the cocone is genuinely over S, not over one parent alone). Synergy reading is the cleanest in the set: **Theorem 4.4 (entropy-shell escape)** states W_C is "provably unreachable from either parent alone via single-rollout expansion" (bes_convergence_9.md §5) — this is literally K(W_C | W_A) large, K(W_C | W_B) large, K(W_C | {W_A,W_B}) small, with a proof rather than an estimate. BES is the substrate where the synergy clause is a theorem, which is why it is the load-bearing instantiation.

### The two sharpest discriminations

**Cymatics fails in every frame (the key negative — reproduces audit v05/v06 "substrate-adjacent" narrowing mechanically).** Reading 06 §2.1 caught this in prose; the criterion now derives it. Run the frame sweep: in **frame = space**, a Chladni/Faraday pattern is the nodal set of an eigenfunction of one operator (ψ_tt = c²∇²ψ + boundary) selected by a *driving frequency* f. There are not two pre-existing co-equal parent wrappers — there is one medium (L0 itself, per Reading 06 §2.1 line 62: "the two frequencies f_A and f_B… are *not* analogs of W_A and W_B as wrappers — they are *driving frequencies*, and the medium is L0"). Condition (i) fails: no two distinct objects to glue. Condition (ii) consequently fails: an eigenmode is a *fixed point of a self-map*, categorically an equalizer/limit, **not a pushout of a span** — there is no S = W_A ∩ W_B because there is no W_B. Condition (iii) fails quantitatively and this is the mechanical core: the pattern is a deterministic function of (medium, boundary, f), so **K(pattern | medium, f) ≈ 0 — synergy ≈ 0**. Critically, switching the frame does not rescue it: in **time** you get the temporal ring-down of the *same* single medium; in **knowledge/meaning** there is no learned-competence or semantic parent pair either — the driving frequency is exogenous in every kernel. The correct two-parent cymatic analog (Reading 06 §2.1 line 64) is **coupled oscillators / normal-mode splitting** (two plates radiating into a shared cavity), which *would* pass — but that is a different system, confirming the narrowing rather than rescuing standard cymatics. This is the formalization reproducing the audit's verdict by mechanism: cymatics is substrate-adjacent because it has the surface 3-pattern (constructive/destructive/phase-locked) without ever instantiating a pushout of two co-equal parents.

**Two-rocks-in-time passes; two-rocks-in-space is ∅ (the dissolved falsifier).** Pav's 2026-06-08 sharpening dissolves the cont 25 §2 / §10 falsifier ("two parents touching forever with no W_C — rocks touching"). The criterion makes the dissolution exact via the frame argument. In **frame = space (instantaneous)** (case 11): two mineral bodies are co-present and in contact, but contact is not gluing — no process maps them onto a shared interface that generates new structure, so no pushout forms. Verdict **∅** (not FALSE — *undefined*, the honest reading: the frame was underspecified, per Pav). In **frame = time-kernel** (case 10): the same two distinct mineral parents W_A, W_B, glued along their contact-interface S, under the time-kernel admit the geological pushout W_C = erosion/sediment/strata — a persistent third of the **same kind** (rock), which can itself become a parent (metamorphic protolith), with parents persisting as procedural-root stubs (the unweathered cores — exactly the hairy-sphere topology, cont 25 §5). Synergy > 0: the stratigraphic/sedimentary record is **not** reconstructible from either parent rock alone — K(strata | rock_A) and K(strata | rock_B) are each large (you cannot derive a depositional sequence from one source mineralogy), K(strata | rock_A, rock_B, time) is small. So PPWc(rock_A, rock_B | space) = ∅ but PPWc(rock_A, rock_B | time) = PASS. The "falsifier" was an unspecified frame, exactly as the frame-relativization predicts: the same parents emerge a W_C in one kernel-frame and nothing in another.

### The two honest negatives (criterion handles non-emergence and emergence-then-collapse)

**Additive blend (case 12).** An ideal mixture / linear superposition (e.g. a non-reacting solution, an alpha-composite of two images) has the surface look of "two things make a third," but it is the **coproduct W_A ⊕ W_B, not a pushout** — there is no non-trivial shared sub-object being glued and no emergent structure. Synergy is exactly 0: K(mixture | W_A, W_B) = 0 (fully specified by listing the parts and proportions), and the mixture is reducible to W_A + W_B by construction. The criterion FAILs it on clause (iii), which is the discriminator the 5-prose-conditions lacked — they could not cleanly separate "emergent third" from "additive third," which is precisely the gap GPT/Gemini flagged. This is the synergy clause earning its place: it is what stops the criterion from over-admitting mixtures.

**1⁻ break-apart (case 13).** The 1⁻ lifecycle branch (cont 25 §2, §6: "1⁻ — (1+1) + wake (recurrent)" — W_C disintegrates, parents return to pre-W_C state) is the emergence-then-collapse case. The empirical anchor is the 1f_l0_failsafe_signature.md substrate: a candidate W_C that formed and then was falsified/collapsed (the cycling-amplitude operationalization, demoted 2026-06-05 after its pre-registered falsifier fired — A_cyc *rose* during transition rather than collapsing, per the §10.3 fourth amendment). The criterion handles this correctly by being **time-indexed through the frame's time-kernel**: PPWc(W_A, W_B | frame) evaluated at t_during = PASS (the pushout exists, synergy > 0 transiently), at t_after = FAIL/∅ (W_C has disintegrated; the cocone collapses back to the parents). The verdict is not a single Boolean but a trajectory; the 1⁻ branch is the case where the trajectory passes through PASS and returns to ∅. This is the failsafe-signature's break-apart read as a criterion-level event, and it shows the criterion does not mistake a transient W_C for a persistent one — persistence (condition 2 of the original 5) becomes "the PASS verdict is stable under forward time-evolution in the frame," not a static check.

### Where the criterion is ambiguous or mis-fires (the falsification test of the formalization itself)

1. **Case 2/4/5 — S is not always a canonical object.** Conceptual blending's colimit (Goguen) and creole's feature-pool are well-defined only after a category is fixed; if the frame under-specifies the morphisms (what counts as a feature-mapping), the pushout is not unique and the universal-property test is vacuous. This is cont 25 §9 open-edge 02 (compatibility/S grounding) resurfacing as a criterion-level ambiguity: the criterion is only as sharp as the frame's category is explicit. Flag: **the frame must name its morphisms, not just its kernel**, or clause (ii) is under-determined.

2. **Synergy is frame-and-coarse-graining dependent.** K(W_C | W_A) depends on the description language (MDL surrogate; Kolmogorov K is uncomputable — adversarial_substrate_dynamics_BAR_A_SKETCH.md §IT notes "code-relative (MDL surrogate; Kolmogorov DL uncomputable)"). A blend that looks additive at one resolution can carry synergy at another (e.g. an alloy: macroscopically additive, microscopically a phase with emergent properties). The criterion can flip between case 12 (FAIL) and case 1 (PASS) under re-graining. Flag: **synergy > 0 must be stated relative to the frame's kernel resolution**; absent that, cases 12 and 1 are not cleanly separable — this is the single most exploitable hole.

3. **Frame-shopping risk (the cymatics-rescue temptation).** Because PPWc is frame-relative, any negative can in principle be "rescued" by inventing a frame with two parents (as coupled-oscillators rescues cymatics). This is a feature for rocks-in-time but a **bug if unconstrained** — it threatens falsifiability (the GPT/Gemini concern in a new dress). The discipline that keeps it honest: the frame must be the *observer's actual global kernel* (cont 25:368), not a frame reverse-engineered to produce a pass. Flag: **without a constraint that the frame pre-exists the test, the criterion is unfalsifiable** — the frame argument must be fixed before evaluation, exactly as Pilot 2's falsifier was locked before data (the audit v08 §C7 discipline applied to frame-selection).

4. **ACMP/cymatics boundary is thin.** ACMP passes and cymatics fails, yet both are PDE eigenstructure with a stabilizing term. The discriminator is solely "two pre-existing co-equal force-fields (attraction, repulsion) vs one medium + exogenous drive." If a reader models ACMP's two forces as "one operator with a parameter" (as cymatics is "one medium with a frequency"), ACMP would collapse to FAIL. Flag: **the criterion's verdict on case 7 depends on a modeling choice** about whether attraction and repulsion are two parents or one parametrized operator — honest exposure of the same one-medium-vs-two-parents ambiguity that sinks cymatics. This is the case most likely to mis-fire and the sharpest internal stress-test of the formalization.
