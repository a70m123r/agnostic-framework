## §2 Why audit v07 ran 12 days early

Audit v06 §9 set the cadence at 15 days (target 2026-06-16). Running at day 3 of the cadence requires justification per cont 30 §7 lesson 1 (the framework's own discipline against running ad-hoc audits).

**Three structural reasons supported early-run:**

1. **Ship density.** Within 72 hours of audit v06 closing, the framework shipped 6 substantive artifacts: cont 30 (~4,500 words, canon integration), pilot #150 result-commit (~14k words discussion + locked numbers), feedback log (~2,300 words), adversarial-substrate-dynamics candidate (~3,200 words), /feedback/ mode-page (substantial publishing surface), cont 31 + candidate §11 (~5,700 + 1,800 words). Total Cowork-side conceptual output: ~18,000 words across a window that audit v06's discipline framework was not designed to cover. Without early audit, ~18,000 words of conceptual work would accumulate unaudited for 12 more days.

2. **Closure-mode prediction.** Cont 31 §12 explicitly predicted "cont 31 should be the LAST major candidate-surface addition before audit v07 lands, regardless of what additional structural insights surface — discipline closure-mode." This is testable commitment: either the framework actually stopped (evidence for honest self-discipline) or it kept going (evidence for post-hoc rationalization). Running early operationalizes the prediction as binding — between cont 31's ship and audit v07's run, the framework's task list shows #178 completed (cont 31 shipped) and #179 in_progress (audit fan-out). No cont 32 exists. The early-audit launch IS the structural mechanism that locks the closure-mode commitment in place.

3. **Self-flag in cont 31 §10.4.** Cont 31 §10.4 explicitly named the audit duty: "audit v07 has the duty to check whether this volume is structural-discipline-running or candidate-displacement-of-empirical-work." The framework explicitly delegated the verification responsibility to v07. Waiting 12 days to discharge that responsibility while more candidate surface might accumulate is the audit-cadence-lag failure mode in miniature.

**One structural concern:** running at 3-day cadence sets a precedent the framework needs to be careful with. The 15-day cadence was designed to prevent audit-multiplication (running audits ad-hoc when the framework feels uncertain). Three-day audits could become a new failure mode (audit-as-anxiety-response). The discipline going forward: 3-day cadence is appropriate when (a) ship density warrants AND (b) a prior continuation explicitly delegated audit duty AND (c) the audit can be run with parallel subagent fan-out (which itself has cost). Otherwise the 15-day cadence holds. Audit v07 is the worked example; v08 should default back to 15-day cadence unless similar conditions trigger.
