## §1. One-paragraph summary

V17 added a harmonic-preset dropdown, two frequency sliders (f_A and the ratio f_B/f_A), a pulse-amplitude toggle, and three new readout banners on top of the v16 wrapper-overlap diagram. The mechanism: a `harmonicCompatibility(ratio)` function classifies the user-selected frequency ratio as consonant / dissonant / inharmonic against a 9-element anchor list (1:1, 6:5, 5:4, 4:3, 3:2, 8:5, 5:3, 2:1, 3:1) and returns a multiplier that gates the existing emergence computation. The W_C wrapper is then visualized as pulsing at the beat frequency |f_A − f_B|. Two opus subagents ran in parallel — one on code/integration soundness, one on claim validity and cross-substrate accuracy — and converged on the same three classes of problem above. At octave default (1:2 ratio) the harmonic multiplier is 1.0 and v17 behavior is v16-equivalent, so the regression risk from v17 to existing primitives is zero. The artifact's central pedagogical move (consonant ratio → stable W_C; inharmonic → broken W_C) works as intuition transfer even though one of the four code mechanisms behind it is wrong and the LCAO label on the perfect-fifth preset is an overclaim. The artifact does not smuggle Tier 3 cymatics-fringe claims — it overclaims the *Tier 2 convergence-classification* itself rather than reaching for adjacent woo. This is the right kind of discipline failure to have: framework-internal-procedural rather than framework-external-credibility. It is also the right kind to catch in audit before the cymatic framing propagates externally via outreach.

## §3. Subagent B — claim validity / cross-substrate audit findings

Cymatics is **substrate-adjacent but not structurally isomorphic** to the existing seven convergences. The seven (LCAO, cell fusion, symbiogenesis, creole genesis, conceptual blending, model merging, ACMP) share a specific pattern: *two parent wrappers W_A and W_B that pre-exist as distinct entities interact and produce a third persistent W_C that is itself a wrapper of the same kind.* Cymatics in its standard form (Chladni plate, water-on-speaker, Faraday waves) has **one medium and a forcing function**, not two pre-existing wrappers producing a third. The substrate-level pattern (eigenmode formation under three-force balance with boundary-selected discrete spectrum) is genuinely shared, but the *parents-produce-third-wrapper* structural element is missing in the Chladni-style imagery v17 evokes.

The correct cymatic analog of the existing seven would be: two pre-existing standing-wave patterns (coupled plates, beating sources radiating into shared cavity) producing a new persistent third pattern that itself qualifies as a stable mode of the joint system. That is the **coupled-oscillator / normal-mode-splitting / avoided-crossing** literature, not the popular cymatics imagery. The framework owes itself a worked example in that literature before convergence #8 can stand at full strength.

The LCAO-bonding analogy is *physically thin*. LCAO bonding is constructive interference of two atomic wavefunctions in the spatial region between nuclei — sign-counting at fixed energy, not interval-counting at fixed spatial mode. There is no "2:3 ratio" anywhere in molecular orbital theory. H₂ bonds because two 1s orbitals overlap with same-sign symmetry; bond order = ½(bonding−antibonding) = ½(2−0) = 1. A physical chemist would not say "the perfect fifth is the bonding case and the tritone is the antibonding case." The preset label "2:3 perfect fifth (LCAO bonding analog)" asserts a structural correspondence that does not exist. Both phenomena involve "wave superposition" in the loose pedagogical sense, but that is true of almost all of physics and does not earn the specific LCAO mapping.

The `harmonicCompatibility` function is a **folk consonance proxy, not a consonance model**. The 30+ years of formal consonance research (Plomp-Levelt 1965 critical-band model, Sethares 1993 dissonance curves, Tenney harmonic distance, Stolzenburg relative periodicity) all compute consonance from the *spectrum* of the tones, not from the bare fundamental ratio. v17 has no spectrum at all. The 2.5% snap window is ~50 cents — well beyond human pitch-discrimination thresholds (~5-10 cents for trained listeners). The tritone preset value 1.414 is the equal-tempered approximation while other ratios are just-intonation values — that is a category mix a music theorist would catch immediately.

The "W_C pulses at beat frequency |f_A − f_B|" claim is **internally inconsistent**. Dissonant ratios produce both *faster* W_C pulse (more visible "interference signature") AND *lower* W_C compatibility (less existence). Pulse rate and existence amplitude end up encoding opposite aspects of the same parameter. For the octave case (ratio 2.0), beat frequency = f_A, so the "beat" pulses at the carrier rate — which isn't a beat in the technical sense, just a coincidence at every period of the lower tone. Real beat phenomena (audible AM envelope) only occur for ratios close to 1:1, which is the "1:1.05 beat frequency" preset and nowhere else.

The Tier 2 → Tier 1 banner slide: Reading 06 §2 carefully marked cymatics-as-convergence-#8 as Tier 2 candidate *conditional on the convergence-list framework-coherence*. The v17 widget title reads `cymatics = cross-substrate convergence #8` with an `=` sign. **That is a Tier-1-style equality claim. Cont 27 §2 explicitly names this as failure mode (i): "treating Tier 2 claims as already canon."** v17 the artifact silently promoted Reading 06's conditional Tier 2 to assertive Tier 1 by deleting the conditional. The CHANGELOG entry is more careful ("makes Reading 06's cymatics-as-convergence-#8 *visible*") but the banner is the public surface.
