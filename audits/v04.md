## §4. The diagram-iteration methodology

Continuation 16 §13 named the carrier-before-canon pattern as methodology. The diagram-iteration loop on diagram 06 (v1–v6) was the worked example. v04's structural assessment:

Whether it generalizes to other dyads is open. v04 marks this as the framework's strongest *operational methodology* but with explicit "tested on N=1 dyad" caveat.

**Recommendation:** keep the methodology as a named tool; track its use across future rounds; pay attention to whether other dyads (Pav + a different model, or Pav + a researcher, etc.) reproduce comparable productivity.

## §6. Discipline gap caught and corrected

The diagram-overwrite problem (v1–v5 of diagram 06 destroyed) was a real discipline gap. v04 audits the response:

**What was lost:** original full-detail v1–v5 of diagram 06. Roughly five SVG files, each 60-200 lines. The structural signatures are preserved in the case-study schematics; the original visual fidelity is not.

**What was caught:** the gap itself, named by Pav during the case-study build round. The framework's own provenance discipline (preserve what was tried, including failures) wasn't being applied to its own diagram artifacts.

**What was instituted in response:**
- `CHANGELOG.md` at repo root tracking all session-level changes
- `diagrams/CHANGELOG.md` with explicit version provenance per diagram, including honest acknowledgment of the discipline gap
- A "Discipline (going forward)" section at the top of `diagrams/CHANGELOG.md` stating the rules: structural changes get versioned filenames; cosmetic edits can overwrite; archive folder for superseded versions

**v04's assessment:** the gap was caught at roughly v4 (when v1-v3 were already lost), which is late but not catastrophic. The infrastructure response is appropriately weighty (two changelog files + explicit rule statement) without being over-engineered. The going-forward discipline applies the framework's own preservation principle to its artifacts.

**v04's structural read:** this episode is *itself a worked example* of the framework's claim about A⁻ as primary discipline. The diagram production (A⁺) was abundant and fluent. The check that caught the discipline gap (A⁻) was slower and required Pav's contextual awareness ("I can see the older diagrams here in the sessions chat, artefacts like this should be iterative on disc"). What survives the check (the changelog infrastructure + the discipline statement) compiles to canon-level practice. This is the framework operating on its own meta-practices, which v03 §13 had predicted should happen and v04 confirms is now happening.
