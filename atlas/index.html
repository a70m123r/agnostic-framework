00What changed from v1

Atlas v3 · sight, sound, music, military v2 established three layers (primitives → configurations → emergent states) and four sensory registers (touch, oral, olfactory, excretory). v3 follows Pav's seed: "lets add music into the mix and military and introduce sight and sound and see how they map into the classification, full effort". Three additions: (1) sight and sound as new sensory registers with full 6-ring substrate coverage; (2) music and military as new domain renderings alongside dance/cooking/farming; (3) two new polar primitives surfaced by the sight and sound subagents — exposed/hidden (sight's state-pair with reveal/conceal as transitions) and resonate/damp (sound's deeper primitive than contact/separation, because sound is intrinsically repeated contact at frequency). Bubble 02's reduction claim (vision = olfactory at EM scale; hearing = touch at pressure-wave scale) is tested explicitly — verdict: ~70% clean. Both registers carry the structural homology but specialize enough at the action layer to earn their own treatment. Military's neutralize confirmed as the most doctrinally-developed instance of the cont-21 emergent verb anywhere — military is the observer-type with the richest neutralize-vocabulary on the planet.

The structural insight that drove the refactor: complexity emerged from primitive polar oppositions. The four-mode frame from continuation 21 (push, pull, squeeze, pop) and the touch-verbs from atlas v1 (tap, stroke, poke, stomp, etc.) are not primitives. They are configurations — compounds built from oscillation between simpler poles. Hold and neutralize are not primitives either; they are the emergent states a substrate reaches when oscillation between polar opposites settles into a stable rhythm. The atlas's job is now to make the layered stack visible.

This also lets the framework absorb a structural lesson from the three sensory-register subagent scans: each register has its own deepest polar-primitive pair, slightly different from the others. Touch's primitive is contact / separation. Oral's is gated transformative intake / regurgitation. Olfactory's is volatilize / resolve (or more sharply: diffuse-at-distance / extract-from-mixture). Excretion's is accumulate / spill (not the more container-bound absorb/expel, which is too symmetric and too anatomy-presuming). The four registers share family resemblance but differ at the polar root — which means the atlas's vocabulary actually has four bases, not one.

01The primitive polar pairs

The deepest layer. Each pair names a fundamental opposition the substrate can do or undergo. Configurations and emergent states above are built from oscillation between these.

Five polar pairs that recur across all sensory registers

expand   ⇄   compress

Volume change. Substrate occupies more space / less space. Mechanical at every ring: cosmic dark energy expanding spacetime; lungs inflating; markets growing; institutions consolidating.

absorb   ⇄   expel

Boundary-crossing flux inward / outward. Cellular endo- and exocytosis; mouth and rear-end; black hole accretion and jets; AI ingestion and generation.

accumulate   ⇄   spill

Threshold-gated retention then release (the excretion subagent's correction to absorb/expel — more general because it doesn't require a bounded container, just a load-bearing threshold). Ca²⁺ vesicle fusion; CME magnetic reconnection; LLM token emission; layoffs at margin threshold; sewer overflow.

contact   ⇄   separation

Surfaces touching / not touching. Touch's primitive. Cell adhesion; haptic feedback; symbiosis; collision; severing.

emit   ⇄   resolve

Information released into a shared medium / extracted at distance. Olfactory's primitive AND sight's primitive (at EM medium). Pheromone release and detection; vision and reflection; spectroscopy; AI embedding broadcast and ANN search; packet sniffing.

resonate   ⇄   damp  NEW v3

Sustained coupling between oscillator and receiver / energy loss to surroundings. Sound's deepest primitive (the sound subagent's analysis — contact/separation is a single-event polarity; sound is intrinsically repeated contact at frequency, so resonate/damp is the right layer). Cochlear amplification ↔ presbycusis; vibrational strong coupling ↔ dephasing; QNM excitation ↔ ringdown; sonar return ↔ anechoic absorption.

exposed   ⇄   hidden  NEW v3

Sight's local state primitive (the sight subagent's analysis — emit/resolve is the physics layer, but the action layer needs reveal/conceal as transition operators between exposed/hidden states). JWST unveiling distant epochs; facial-recognition revealing identity; military camouflage concealing position; surveillance exposing dissent; anti-drone optics blinding adversary sensors.

Three pairs about directed force and pressure

pump-in   ⇄   pump-out

Active transport. Energy-consuming directed flux against gradient. Ion pumps; ventricular cycle; capital flows; data ingestion pipelines.

pressure   ⇄   no-pressure

Zero / not-zero binary at the energetic level. The threshold itself. Phase boundaries; engagement / disengagement; activation / refractory state. The substrate is either being touched or not.

vector change   ⇄   vector hold

Force direction shifting / staying. Turning vs holding course. Maneuver in physics, steering in cognition, pivot in commerce, refactor in canon.

These eight pairs are candidate-grade as the framework's primitive-layer. The discipline going forward: any verb in the atlas should be decomposable into oscillation between one or more of these pairs. If a verb resists decomposition, that's evidence the primitive set is incomplete and a new pair needs naming.

Worth noting: the eight pairs aren't fully independent. Pump-in/pump-out is absorb/expel with directed energy. Vector change/hold is the meta-level pair operating on any of the others. Pressure/no-pressure is the on/off switch that gates whether any other pair is engaged. The five core pairs (expand/compress, absorb/expel, accumulate/spill, contact/separation, emit/resolve) cover most of the verb-space; the three meta pairs (pump, pressure, vector) describe how the core pairs are run.

The notation from PAVPAV's whiteboard (see notation walkthrough) — A⁺ / A⁻, the dual-merge ±/∓ signs — operationalizes this layer. The ± marker on a recursive-bind ⋈ operator is the framework's algebraic shorthand for oscillation between polar opposites. The atlas v2 is the natural-language vocabulary of what the whiteboard's operators are doing.

Hold and neutralize as emergent

This is the structural insight that drove the v2 refactor. Hold (cont 21 §2) and neutralize (added in v2, drawn from Tai Chi's hua jin — absorb without redirect) are not primitive force-modes. They are what a substrate looks like when oscillation between polar opposites reaches equilibrium. A guitar string at rest is in hold; that hold is an emergent state from many cycles of push/pull oscillation damping out. A canon in hold (cont 21) is an emergent state from many cycles of refactor-and-test damping out. A neutralized force in Tai Chi push-hands is an emergent state from continuous absorb-and-yield oscillation that prevents the partner's force from finding traction.

Why this matters: emergent states are *not* directly addressable. You cannot reach for hold the way you can reach for push. Hold has to arise. The vocabulary distinction matters because it changes what an observer is doing when it reaches for these verbs. Push is a direct action. Hold is a description of a state that arises when the right oscillation pattern is sustained. Trying to "do hold" directly produces freeze, not hold. Hold is what happens when push-and-pull have settled into a productive rhythm.

Manipulation and choreography are the larger emergent compounds. A manipulation is a chain of configurations directed toward an end-state. A choreography is a multi-actor protocol where each actor's configurations are timed against the others' so that the whole produces a coherent emergent pattern. Ballet, surgery, conjugation rituals, RAG inference pipelines, election cycles — all are choreographies at different substrate rings. The framework's mutual rendering primitive (cont 16) is the basic two-actor choreography; institutional life is the multi-actor case.

Light intermittent contact. Discrete signal. Polar-decomposition: contact/separation oscillation at high frequency, low pressure.

touch · gentle, sustained

stroke

Sustained gentle directional shaping. Polar-decomposition: contact maintained, vector held, low pressure.

touch · pointed, brief

poke

Pointed brief contact. Probing. High pressure, small area, brief duration.

touch · glancing

brush

Glancing surface contact. Vector held, contact-and-separation rapid.

touch · gentle directional

nudge

Gentle directional push for behavioral steering. Low-amplitude push with explicit attention to receiver state.

touch · forceful

stomp

Heavy crushing contact. Sustained pressure, vector down, often obliterating.

touch · v2 · NEW

pinch

Compressive small-area contact between two surfaces. Polar-decomposition: paired contact, compress, hold. Cooking (pinch of salt); pruning (pinch back); economics (pinching pennies).

touch · v2 · NEW

rip

Sudden split across surface. Polar-decomposition: sustained pull followed by abrupt separation at threshold. Tectonic faults; tendon rupture; data leak; institutional schism.

emergent · v2 · NEW

neutralize

Absorb without redirect. The Tai Chi hua jin. Polar-decomposition: emergent from continuous absorb-and-yield oscillation that prevents incoming force from finding traction. Not a direct verb — a state the receiver enters when the oscillation pattern is sustained.

5D. Sound — resonate/damp at the substrate, not contact/separation

The sound subagent's verdict on Bubble 02's reduction claim: hearing does reduce to touch in the biology and military rings (cochlear stereocilia are mechanoreceptors; sonar is mechanical contact at distance through water; LRAD literally pushes the eardrum past pain threshold), but strains in cosmology and AI. The deeper insight: contact/separation is a single-event polarity, but sound is intrinsically repeated contact at frequency. The right primitive is resonate/damp: sustained coupling between oscillator and receiver vs energy loss to the surroundings. Contact/separation is a special case at zero frequency.

Bubble 02 · rank 2 · v3 verdict — partial confirmation

The sensory-register reduction claim (v2 → v3 update)

v2 hypothesized that vision = olfactory at EM scale and hearing = touch at pressure-wave scale, with both derived from the four primary registers. v3 tested this with parallel subagents on sight and sound. Verdict: ~70% confirmation, with two structural refinements.

Sight emit/resolve works as the substrate primitive (sight = spectroscopic resolve in photon medium, structurally homologous to olfactory's chemical-volatile spectroscopy). But sight specializes resolve into a geometric 2D mode olfactory never developed; and the emit pole is asymmetric (most animals see but don't bioluminesce, breaking the emit/resolve symmetry). v3 keeps emit/resolve as substrate primitive and adds exposed/hidden as sight's local state primitive with reveal/conceal as the transition operators. Three-layer structure: emit/resolve (physics) → reveal/conceal (transition) → exposed/hidden (state).

Sound contact/separation works in biology + military + chemistry + society but strains in cosmology (GW ringing is metric oscillation in vacuum, no medium) and AI (audio models touch already-discretized samples, not pressure waves). The deeper insight from the sound subagent: contact/separation is a single-event polarity, but sound is intrinsically repeated contact at frequency. v3 promotes resonate/damp as sound's primitive — sustained coupling between oscillator and receiver vs energy loss to surroundings. Contact/separation becomes a special case at zero frequency.

Implication The four-register-completeness claim becomes a six-register-with-shared-primitives claim. Sight and sound earn their own treatment because their action layers (exposed/hidden; resonate/damp) are categorically different from the registers they reduce to at the physics layer. The full register count: touch, oral, olfactory, sight, sound, excretory.

Breaks if Further work finds a primary register that doesn't fit either the substrate-primitive or the local-state-primitive treatment. Candidate counter: time sense (circadian / interval timing) — still doesn't obviously fit; flag as open for v4.

Bubble 03 · rank 3 · medium confidence, large implication

Observer types with rich release-vocabulary (democratic states, organisms with sophisticated apoptosis, AI systems with thoughtful refusal/sampling modes) are structurally healthier than those with impoverished release-vocabulary (authoritarian regimes, cancer cells with apoptosis dysregulation, AI systems without refusal capacity).
