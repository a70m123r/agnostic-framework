A boundary's verb-vocabulary

v3 grew to 13,500 words and stopped being absorbable. v4 is the synthesis pass. One unifying insight: every blind sense is a boundary's strategy for handling a flux across itself, parameterized by two coordinates — the carrier medium and the temporal pattern of coupling. Three diagrams render the whole atlas. Three insights buried in v3 surfaced explicitly: thresholds (not oscillations), failure modes (as diagnostic entries), participatory taxonomy (actors identified by which fluxes they can act on).

01The unifying insight

v4 · the single sentence

Every blind sense is a boundary that something crosses. The eight primitive pairs are the eight degrees of freedom a boundary has — how much it opens, how much it loads, whether it couples to a medium, whether it sustains the coupling, whether it can be seen across. Configurations are recognizable signatures of those fluxes. Emergent states (hold, neutralize) are flux-patterns that have reached equilibrium.

This is what holds together six sensory registers, eight primitive polar pairs, dozens of touch-verb configurations, and five domain-renderings from v3. It is one parameterized object — a permeable boundary — with two universal coordinates that fully describe any blind-sense event:

carrier medium  ×  temporal coupling pattern

Six sensory registers are six standing combinations of (medium, pattern): touch has no medium and single-event coupling; sound has a pressure-wave medium and periodic coupling; sight has an EM medium and sustained coupling; oral has matter-as-medium and gated transformative pulse; olfactory has chemical-volatile medium and gradient-sampling; excretory is the inverse — accumulation in a load-bearing boundary released across threshold.

Every primitive polar pair from v3 (expand/compress, absorb/expel, accumulate/spill, contact/separation, emit/resolve, resonate/damp, exposed/hidden, pump-in/pump-out) names one degree of freedom the boundary has. Every configuration (push, pull, squeeze, pop, tap, stroke, poke, stomp, brush, press, nudge, grab, pinch, rip) names a recognizable shape of flux. Every emergent state (hold, neutralize, manipulation, choreography) names a stable phase-portrait the boundary can settle into.

Once you have this frame, the rest of the atlas is a catalog — and a catalog is much easier to read when you know what it's a catalog of.

04The six registers in one paragraph each — entered through their failure modes

Insight (ii) from the unification subagent: each register's diagnostic access is through what goes wrong. Failure modes reveal the boundary because that's when its hidden parameters become visible.

Touch

force medium · sustained contact · primitive contact/separation

Direct surface-to-surface coupling with no carrier between. The simplest register and the only one that needs co-location. Every touch-verb (tap, stroke, poke, stomp, pinch, grab) is a different shape-of-contact along the boundary. Configurations distinguish duration, pressure, surface-area, vector.

FAIL: numbness — the boundary's mechanoreceptors stop signaling crossing. The boundary still exists; the reporting fails. Used clinically as a diagnostic for nerve damage; used metaphorically (political numbness, market numbness) for systems that have stopped noticing what's pressing on them.

Oral

matter medium · single pulse + transformative · primitive gated intake

The boundary doesn't just admit matter — it transforms what crosses. Chewing, salivating, dissolving, Maillard browning, AI corpus-tokenization, accretion-disk heating. Every intake event runs ingestion + transformation in coupled stages.

FAIL: anorexia / refusal / regurgitation — the gate is intact but selection is broken. The boundary refuses to admit what it physiologically needs. Mapped at organism scale to anorexia, at AI scale to context-rejection, at cosmic scale to accretion-disk wind back-flow.

Olfactory

chemical medium · gradient sampling · primitive emit/resolve in diffusive carrier

Receivers extract a signal from a noisy, mixed, distance-attenuated chemical gradient. The medium is uncontrolled (air, water, plasma, packet-substrate); the resolve operation is what gives the register its identity.

FAIL: anosmia — the receiver still exists; the resolve operation fails. Post-COVID olfactory-bulb shrinkage is the canonical recent case. Olfactory failure is the cleanest case of the boundary's detection threshold being broken without the carrier or the source going away.

Sight

EM medium · sustained spectral · primitive emit/resolve + state pair exposed/hidden

Reduces structurally to olfactory at the EM-medium scale (both are spectroscopic — wavelength = identity), but specializes resolve into a geometric 2D mode olfactory never developed. Adds exposed/hidden as the register-local state pair, with reveal/conceal as transition operators.

FAIL: blindness — the resolve channel breaks. Adversarial counter-instances are also failures-by-design — military dazzlers (HELIOS, NUBURU Lyocon) deliberately drive resolve into failure on the adversary's optics. The framework's most weaponized failure mode.

Sound

pressure-wave medium · periodic · primitive resonate/damp

Reduces to touch at the pressure-wave scale (cochlear stereocilia are mechanoreceptors), but sound is intrinsically repeated contact at frequency, so the deeper primitive is resonate/damp — sustained coupling vs energy loss to surroundings. The right diagnostic question: is this sustaining a coupling, or breaking one?

FAIL: deafness / presbycusis — outer hair cells lose amplification, the boundary's resonate channel damps. Or its weaponized counterpart: LRAD/sonic-crowd-control overdriving the boundary past pain-threshold (Belgrade March 2025; Maldives Oct 2025). Same boundary, opposite failure direction.

Excretory

matter medium (outbound) · threshold-triggered · primitive accumulate/spill

The inverse of oral. Accumulation in a load-bearing boundary released across threshold. Defecation, sweating, exocytosis, CME ejecta, LLM token-emission, layoff-at-margin-threshold, sewer overflow, weeping. All instances of: load builds, threshold crosses, release happens.

FAIL: obstruction / over-accumulation / under-release — the threshold is not reached, or the spill channel is blocked. Constipation, hoarding, hallucination-suppression in over-aligned AI, organizational layoff-paralysis. Or the opposite failure: incontinence / leakage — the threshold collapsed, spill is uncontrolled.

Reading the registers through their failure modes makes them faster to absorb than reading them through their verb-vocabularies. The verbs are catalogue; the failures are diagnosis. The same insight applies to any future register a construct study reaches for: name the failure first; the verbs follow.

05Three insights v3 buried — pulled out

The unification subagent identified three insights the atlas was almost saying but not explicitly. v4 promotes them.

Insight i · the framework is secretly about thresholds, not oscillations

Threshold crossings are the structural primitive; oscillation is what happens when a system repeatedly approaches and crosses a threshold

Re-read v3's excretory section carefully. Accumulate/spill is threshold-gated retention then release. Pop is sustained accumulate followed by sudden spill at threshold. Rip is sustained pull followed by abrupt separation at threshold. Neutralize is the state where incoming flux fails to cross a coupling threshold. v3 kept saying "polar oscillation" but what's actually doing the structural work is threshold crossing.

This sharpens half the v3 forced-fits. Resonate/damp is a Q-factor threshold. Exposed/hidden is a visibility threshold. Contact/separation is the presence threshold. Pressure/no-pressure (0/≠0) is the engagement threshold. The framework's deepest formal layer might not be polar pairs at all — it might be a single class of threshold operators with different parameters per register.

v5 may want to refactor §01 around this. For v4, the takeaway is: when you reach for a polar-pair verb in a construct study, ask what is the threshold and when does it cross. The threshold is usually more informative than the pair.

Insight ii · failure modes are the diagnostic entry per register

Each register's parameters become visible only when it fails — so reading registers through failure modes is faster than reading through verb-catalogs

v4 §04 above demonstrates this directly: each register's one-paragraph summary ends with the failure mode in red. The verb-vocabularies are the catalog; the failures are the diagnosis. The boundary's hidden parameters (selectivity, threshold, refresh-rate, reversibility) become visible only when one fails — post-COVID anosmia, presbycusis, model collapse, sewer overflow, hallucination, tendon rupture, organizational paralysis.

This is also where v3 Bubble 03 lives — the under-developed-release-vocabulary hypothesis. Release is normally invisible; only its failures (CSO overflow, hallucination, layoff, weeping) force naming. Same shape across registers.

For a reader: if you want to know what a register really is, ask what its failure looks like. The failure is the parameter chart with the values filled in.

Insight iii · participatory taxonomy — every actor is identifiable by its verb-vocabulary

The atlas isn't a passive classification of nature. It's a map of which boundary-flux moves are available to which kind of system. Identity is a function of available verbs.

v3 §07 already had the observer-type vocabularies (government, market, organism, AI, religion, military) without quite naming what the table was for. Each one's signature vocabulary is a fingerprint of what that observer can do. AI gets tap, route, embed. State gets audit, sanction, pardon. Markets get price, squeeze, IPO. The vocabularies are not descriptive — they're constitutive. An actor IS the set of verbs it can perform across the six registers.

This is the framework's closest cousin to Cronin and Walker's assembly theory: both ask not what is, but what can be assembled by which assembler. The framework's claim: every actor in the universe is identifiable by its verb-vocabulary. Figure C below makes the fingerprint visible.

07What this lets you do

The v4 synthesis is operational, not just descriptive. Three things become available once the framework is read this way.

One. Diagnose a stuck system by asking which boundary-parameter has shifted. A system that has stopped learning, partnering, governing, or growing is a boundary whose parameters have moved out of operational range. Did the threshold rise? Did the refresh-rate stall? Did the reversibility break? Did the selectivity collapse? The 8-DOF dial (Figure A, bottom) is the diagnostic checklist. Apply it to a marriage, a startup, a religion, an AI deployment, a state agency.

Two. Predict candidate registers by reading the empty cells in Figure B. The periodic table has eight candidate cells (dashed) where speculative-register-space lives. Each empty cell is a prediction: what would a sense look like at that (medium, temporal) combination? Some are easy (impact = pulse-force; ambient hum = sustained-wave). Some are open (sustained-matter at the metabolic scale — is metabolism a sensory register?). The framework invites checking.

Three. Identify a new observer-type by drawing its fingerprint. Pick any actor not in Figure C — a corporation, a household, a search engine, a country, a microbiome, a forest. Score its verb-vocabulary density across the six registers. The shape that emerges is its identity. If two actors share a shape, they share an identity-class — and the framework predicts they will be susceptible to the same boundary-parameter failures.

The atlas is no longer a catalogue. It is a parameterized object you can apply.
