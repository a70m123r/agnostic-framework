Diagram 07 v14 — fluid-sim pairwise interactions (intra-wrapper repulsion + inter-wrapper membrane friction · particles still anchored by spring but now push and drag on each other)

"the points i the cloud field should interact with one another bound to their centre, so that theres friction as they slide past each others membranes, think fluid sim"— PAVPAV

Pairwise particle interactions added on top of v13's spring-anchored point cloud. Each particle still feels its anchor spring (v13 behaviour preserved), but now two additional forces act on close neighbours: short-range repulsion (push apart along the separation vector, soft falloff inside a 14px interaction radius) + viscosity (couple velocities so nearby particles drag each other — the friction term). Three interaction passes per frame: intra-A (W_A particles push/drag each other — prevents clustering, lets the cloud bulge under squeeze, gives a fluid-fill quality), intra-B (same for W_B), and inter-A×B (only when centre distance is small enough to matter — early-out for perf). The inter-wrapper pass is the headline: when W_A and W_B clouds overlap during wake/active phases, the two point fields slide past each other with actual friction rather than passing through transparently. updateCloud refactored to a three-phase loop: (1) spring force accumulates velocity, (2) pairwise interactions modify velocities, (3) damping + integration + render. Friction slider (0–1, default 0.50) scales both repulsion and viscosity strength. At 0 each particle only feels its anchor spring (v13 mode); at 1 the system behaves strongly fluid. Performance stays comfortable at worst-case density=140 (~40k pair-checks/frame) and is trivial at default density=60 (~5.5k checks/frame). Hero dek + title bar updated. Queued for v15: W_C particle spawning (when A and B particles reach sustained overlap in the contact zone, seed a third cloud — currently W_C is still arc-only), and a velocity-field/streamline overlay over the contact zone (the fluid sim provides this data for free).
