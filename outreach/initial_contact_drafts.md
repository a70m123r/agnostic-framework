## 6. Notes on sending

- **Send one at a time, not in a batch.** Each recipient deserves a personalized first sentence (e.g., "Saw your talk at NeurIPS 2025" / "Read your paper on X last week"). Generic openings get filed.
- **Plain text is fine.** No HTML, no images, no marketing wrapping. The framework's content does the work.
- **Don't expect replies fast.** Researchers at this tier get a lot of cold email. Realistic expectation: 30% read, 10% engage. The point is to plant the URL, not to require a reply.
- **Track who you contacted.** Add a row to `outreach/contact_log.md` with date, recipient, subject, channel (email/LinkedIn/Twitter/etc.) and outcome. Useful for future reading-scoring rounds.
- **Sign with whatever name signals correctly for the audience.** "Pav" is fine for casual; for academic outreach consider also signing with full name + a short epistemic identity ("independent researcher; previously [whatever role frames you]"). The framework's discipline applies to outreach too — what's the receiver's wrapper, and what carrier shape will pass their A⁻ check?
