This Reading surveys roughly forty open problems across six domains and identifies the ~eight where time-step-with-gaps offers a non-trivial reframing, the ~fifteen where it can be made consistent but adds nothing, and the rest where it actively shouldn't be invoked. The honest yield is small. That is the correct shape of a serious framework's engagement with open problems.

## 1. The framework lens, restated

The hypothesis being applied is the **time-step-with-gaps** family, developed earlier in this session:

- **Tier 1 (epistemological canon, cont 26 §4):** Perceptual rate is a sense the observer brings to the world. Different observers sampling the same wrapper at different rates get different fidelity reads.
- **Tier 2 (ontological-candidate, Reading 04):** Compilation-rate-as-time — the observer's experience of temporal flow IS the rate at which their wrapper compiles. Conditional on contested quantum-collapse physics (Diósi-Penrose / CSL).
- **Tier 3 (speculative, candidates/aux_channel_l0_occlusion.md):** L0 itself is occludable; the resulting gaps are real shared gaps across observers, not per-observer perceptual gaps; the gaps could serve as an auxiliary channel.

For this Reading, the relevant lens is the **substrate-level** claim: time is not a smooth external parameter but a process with intrinsic granularity and (per Tier 3) potential shared occlusion gaps. The candidate reframings asked of each open problem are: *if* time has substrate-level gap structure, does that change how this problem looks? Does it suggest non-obvious predictions? Does it dissolve a paradox or merely add another?

Two failure modes the framework must avoid:
- **Kitchen-sink syndrome.** A speculation that "explains everything" explains nothing. If time-step-with-gaps can be retro-fitted to any anomaly, that's a sign the speculation is too elastic to be useful. The discipline is to identify where it adds *non-obvious* explanatory traction.
- **Cross-tier laundering.** Even where the lens bites, candidate reframings stay at Tier 2 or Tier 3. They do not get to inherit confidence from the well-evidenced parts of the framework.

## 2. Quantum foundations & measurement

### 2.1 Strong fits

**Problem of time / arrival-time / tunneling-time (substrate fit: strong).** Pauli's theorem forbids a self-adjoint time operator for bounded-below Hamiltonians. This breaks symmetry with position and produces an entire family of unresolved sub-problems: how long does a particle take to tunnel, when does it arrive at a detector, what is the quantum probability distribution over arrival times. The Wheeler-DeWitt program inherits the deeper version (no time variable at all). **The time-step-with-gaps lens (Tier 2) sits naturally here**: the very fact that QM cannot give time the status of an observable suggests time has structure (operator-valued, granular, gappy) that the standard formalism cannot accommodate. The framework's contribution would be to articulate gappiness specifically — not just discreteness — as the missing structure. Recent papers in arrival-time / tunneling-time (Galapon 2024-25; quantum reference frame work by Giacomini, Brukner, Castro-Ruiz) are converging on operator-valued or POVM-based time treatments; framework-coherent extensions look possible. **Not an endorsement** — the literature already has serious mathematical treatments without invoking substrate gaps; the framework's claim is that gap structure may be the unifying feature.

**Diósi-Penrose / CSL collapse models — clock precision limits (substrate fit: very strong, by construction).** Bortolotti et al. 2025 already explicitly derive an intrinsic time uncertainty as a consequence of CSL/DP. This is the framework's Tier 2 anchor. The framework doesn't add new physics here; it offers a unifying *vocabulary* for what the existing physics is doing — "L0's evolved failsafe environment imposes a clock-precision floor." The honest claim is small: the framework reads Bortolotti et al. one way; physicists who reject collapse models entirely will reject the framework's reading along with the underlying physics.

### 2.2 Moderate fits

**Frauchiger-Renner / observer-dependent reality (substrate fit: moderate).** F-R constructs a scenario where agents using QM to reason about other agents reach mutually inconsistent conclusions. The standard responses (RQM, QBism, many-worlds, collapse) each pay a price. **A time-step-with-gaps lens reframes the inconsistency as a synchronization failure:** if each agent has their own compilation-rate-driven clock with intrinsic gaps, they do not share a common time-foliation, and "single measurement outcome at time t" becomes a category error. The framework's reading is structurally adjacent to RQM but adds the gap structure as the *mechanism* for why facts are observer-relative. The honest accounting: this is suggestive, not derived. RQM already handles F-R without invoking substrate gaps; the framework's reading needs to produce a non-obvious prediction RQM cannot to earn promotion above intuition pump.

**Quantum-to-classical transition (substrate fit: moderate).** Decoherence explains how classical-looking pointer states emerge but does not pick a single outcome. The transition is not a *size* boundary so much as a temporal-resolution boundary — observers whose compilation rate is too slow to resolve quantum dynamics see classical-looking outcomes. This is framework-coherent but not obviously needed: standard decoherence + Everett or decoherence + collapse already handle the transition. The framework's contribution would be to re-frame "macroscopic" as "operating at a compilation rate slow enough that quantum dynamics fall below resolution" — an interesting reformulation but not yet adding novel predictions.

### 2.3 Where the lens shouldn't be invoked

- **Bell-test loopholes (residual):** After the 2015 loophole-free tests and 2018 cosmic-Bell test, residual debate is mostly philosophical superdeterminism. Time-gaps don't help here; superdeterminism is its own thing.
- **Delayed-choice / quantum eraser:** Consensus is firming that no retrocausality is required (Qureshi 2025). The framework should not invoke gap-as-retrocausality.
- **Contextuality and Kochen-Specker:** Sequential KS tests assume the system is unchanged between measurements. The framework could question this assumption, but the existing literature (Spekkens contextuality, sheaf-theoretic, Bohmian) handles the interpretive load without needing substrate gaps. **No clear added value.**

## 3. Cosmology

### 3.1 Strong fits

**JWST "impossibly early" massive galaxies (substrate fit: strong, suggestive).** JWST has discovered massive galaxies at z ≈ 7–14 requiring star-formation efficiencies far above ΛCDM expectations. **If proper time available for collapse and star formation differs from inferred FRW time at high z** — for instance, if substrate-level time-steps stretch effective duration during dense epochs — early structures get "more time" to assemble than the ΛCDM accounting allows. This is genuinely framework-coherent and produces a non-obvious prediction: the apparent over-efficiency should be more severe in the densest regions and at the highest redshifts (where gap density would be highest under the framework's reading), and should track with substrate-density proxies rather than purely with halo mass. **Honest caveat:** Early Dark Energy and other ΛCDM modifications also explain this with fewer assumptions; the framework's reading is one candidate among several. The structural difference: EDE shifts the background expansion; the gap-time reframing shifts the *relationship between coordinate and proper time* during structure formation.

**The cosmological constant problem (substrate fit: strong, technical).** QFT estimates of zero-point vacuum energy exceed the observed Λ by ~10¹²⁰. **A time substrate with intrinsic granularity natively regulates high-frequency vacuum modes** — exactly the function "minimal length" / "minimal time" cutoff proposals already serve in the literature (CosmoVerse review, 2025 unimodular gravity work). The framework's reading would specifically prefer a *gappy* time substrate over a smoothly-quantized one, because gaps provide a mechanism for the cutoff to be observer-relative rather than universal — matching the cosmological constant's observer-dependence in different cosmologies. This is the most technically interesting fit in the cosmology section and the closest to producing a derivation rather than just a reframe.

### 3.2 Moderate fits

**Hubble tension (substrate fit: moderate).** The early-universe (Planck, DESI BAO) vs late-universe (SH0ES Cepheid+SNIa) H₀ values disagree at ~5σ. **A time-step substrate where the rate of effective time-passage varies between recombination and z=0** would shift distance-ladder calibrations across the gap. The framework's reading: the tension is not a *value* disagreement but a *clock-rate* disagreement between the two epochs. **Honest accounting:** many proposals already do this (Early Dark Energy, evolving dark energy, new relativistic dof). The framework's reading is one candidate explanation among many, and offers no precise quantitative prediction without more structure.

**DESI 2024-2025 evolving dark energy hints (substrate fit: moderate).** DESI DR2 prefers w(z) over a pure cosmological constant at 2.8–4.2σ. **A drifting w(z) could be a re-parameterization** of an underlying time-rate gap between background expansion and local clock processes, rather than a true field evolving in a single shared time. Same caveat as Hubble: the framework's reading doesn't currently produce predictions sharper than what dynamical-dark-energy models give.

**CMB large-scale anomalies (substrate fit: moderate).** Low quadrupole, axis-of-evil, hemispherical asymmetry, cold spot. **A preferred direction or low large-scale power could mark a substrate-level coherence gap during the surface-of-last-scattering era** — anomalies as imprints of where the time substrate itself was non-uniform during recombination. Interesting but the mainstream response (look-elsewhere inflation + local foregrounds) already accounts for most of the data; the framework's reading is most useful if a future CMB-S4 / LiteBIRD measurement *confirms* the anomalies are physical despite multiplicity correction.

### 3.3 Where the lens shouldn't be invoked

- **S8 / σ8 tension:** Plausibly framework-coherent (gap insertion damps integrated growth) but baryonic feedback or massive neutrinos handle it with less speculation. No added value.
- **Dark matter composition:** The framework can gesture ("time-gap mismatch indexing rather than a particle") but this is reaching. The dark matter problem has serious particle-physics candidates; the framework shouldn't compete here.
- **Baryon asymmetry:** Sakharov conditions + CP violation handle the question structurally; substrate gaps don't help.

## 4. Quantum gravity & black holes

### 4.1 Strong fits

**Christodoulou-Rovelli / BMV experiment proper-time interference (substrate fit: very strong, by construction).** Christodoulou-Rovelli explicitly argue that the BMV gravity-induced-entanglement signal IS a proper-time-interference effect, and that Planck-scale time discreteness would show up as quantized entanglement-entropy levels. **This is the cleanest near-term experimental window into time-gap hypotheses.** The framework's contribution is interpretive — the discreteness Christodoulou-Rovelli pursue is the substrate-gap structure cont 26 §3 / Reading 04 / aux-channel doc point at. If BMV is ever achieved experimentally (years away) and shows quantization of entanglement entropy, the framework's Tier 2 candidate gets a major boost. **This is the framework's most concrete experimental promotion path.**

**Black hole singularity problem (substrate fit: strong).** Classical GR predicts curvature singularities; quantum gravity is expected to resolve them. **A substrate where time is a sequence of discrete steps with possible gaps natively offers a "last well-defined step before the gap" replacement for the divergence** — the singularity becomes a substrate-level boundary where time-flow becomes ill-defined rather than where geometry becomes infinite. This is structurally adjacent to loop-quantum-gravity bounce models and the 2024-25 unimodular-gravity unitarity-enforced resolution (Alonso-Serrano et al. PRL 2025), and to Penrose's CCC. The framework's reading: the resolution mechanism is *temporal* not *geometric*. Non-trivial because it predicts that "singularity-resolution" signatures should appear in time-correlated observables (Hawking spectrum modulations, gravitational-wave ringdown anomalies near the photon sphere) rather than in static geometric features.

### 4.2 Moderate fits

**Problem of time in canonical quantum gravity / Wheeler-DeWitt (substrate fit: moderate-strong).** Ĥ|Ψ⟩ = 0 has no time parameter. Standard responses: Page-Wootters conditional probability, Rovelli relational / partial observables, internal-time approaches. **The framework's reading reframes "no fundamental time" as expected rather than paradoxical:** time becomes a coarse statistical quantity over an underlying timeless or intermittent substrate. This is structurally compatible with Rovelli's thermal-time hypothesis and with Gielen-Menendez-Pidal's 2025 critique of emergent time. The framework adds vocabulary (gap-structure) but doesn't yet derive new constraints; honest accounting says it's a candidate framing, not a derivation.

**Black hole information paradox / Page curve (substrate fit: moderate).** Page-curve derivation hinges on what counts as a moment of evaporation and how proper time near the horizon is sliced. **Gaps in the time substrate could reframe where unitarity is enforced** — per-step vs per-window vs averaged-over-gappy-substrate. Not obviously needed: the island/QES program handles the qualitative paradox without substrate gaps. The framework's reading would matter if (a) the realistic 4D evaporation calculation reveals an information-recovery mechanism that *requires* gap structure to work, or (b) Hawking-radiation observations (some far future) reveal time-correlated structure that smooth-time predictions don't capture.

**Firewall paradox / AMPS (substrate fit: moderate).** AMPS shows that unitarity + EFT outside the horizon + smooth infall cannot all hold past Page time. **If smooth infall and outside EFT sample non-overlapping time windows in a gappy substrate, the contradiction dissolves without invoking a firewall.** This is intriguing but adjacent to existing complementarity / Python's-lunch arguments. The 2025 paper "The firewall paradox is Wigner's friend paradox" (arXiv:2504.03835) is moving the field toward observer-relative readings that the framework is already structurally aligned with.

### 4.3 Where the lens shouldn't be invoked

- **ER=EPR and entanglement-as-spacetime:** Genuinely interesting, but the framework's reading ("traversable across occluded steps") is more poetic than productive. The Van Raamsdonk lineage handles spatial emergence; the framework's contribution to *temporal* emergence is separate.
- **Soft hair / gravitational memory:** Specific to BMS-symmetry encoding; not a time-gap question.

## 5. Time, thermodynamics, arrow of time

### 5.1 Strong fits

**Past hypothesis / low-entropy boundary condition (substrate fit: strong).** Why was the early universe in such an extraordinarily improbable low-entropy state? **If the substrate of time itself has occlusion/gaps, the very notion of a sharply-defined "initial macrostate" at t=0 is ill-posed.** The low-entropy boundary condition might be a coarse-graining artifact of an underlying intermittent time substrate — what looks like "extreme tuning at the singular t=0" might be a structural feature of how observers reading the universe's history reconstruct an initial condition from gap-structured time data. This connects naturally to Carroll-Chen multiverse arguments and Barbour-Mercati Janus-point models. Non-trivial because it suggests measurable signatures: the "initial low entropy" should be artifact-shaped (depend on observer's compilation rate) rather than absolute, and different observer-classes should reconstruct subtly different initial conditions.

**Boltzmann brain problem (substrate fit: strong).** The BB argument assumes a continuous, uniformly-flowing time substrate over cosmologically infinite intervals to generate the fluctuation count. **If time-substrate occlusions accumulate, the effective long-run fluctuation count may not be well-defined** — the integrand over which BBs are counted has gap structure the standard argument doesn't account for. This is a potentially important resolution: the framework would predict BB dominance is a finite-time artifact rather than an asymptotic problem. **Honest accounting:** specifying this rigorously would require a measure theory on gap-structured time that doesn't exist yet. The framework's reading is genuinely useful direction-setting; the math is open work.

### 5.2 Moderate fits

**Spontaneous collapse rate parameter (substrate fit: very strong, by construction).** Already covered — this is the Reading 04 anchor. Reading 05 just notes that the empirical bounds tightening on (λ, r_C) are direct constraints on the substrate-gap structure under the framework's reading.

**CP violation and matter-antimatter asymmetry (substrate fit: moderate).** T-violation is the experimental face of time-reversal asymmetry; **substrate-level time gaps could in principle source effective T-odd correlations not captured by smooth-time field theory.** The LHCb March 2025 first-observation of CP violation in baryon decays (Λ_b, 5.2σ) is the most recent specific data. The framework's reading: if CP violation has a substrate-level component beyond the Standard Model CKM phase, it should show up in time-correlated observables that depend on the compilation rate of the measuring apparatus. Speculative; not obviously needed.

### 5.3 Where the lens shouldn't be invoked

- **Time crystals:** Real physics, well-explained by Floquet theory + many-body localization. The framework's reading ("macroscopic probe of substrate temporal structure") is poetic but doesn't add explanatory traction.
- **Block universe vs presentism debates:** Philosophical, not a place the framework should plant flags.
- **Retrocausality (TSVF, Cramer transactional):** The framework's hypothesis is forward-time-with-gaps, not retrocausal. Different speculation.

## 6. Particle physics anomalies

### 6.1 Honest accounting — most of this section is "framework should not invoke"

The particle physics anomalies surveyed (muon g-2, B-meson, W boson, ATOMKI X17, hierarchy problem, sterile neutrinos) mostly have plausible mundane explanations (systematics, theoretical-method disagreement, model-building) or sit firmly in standard particle-physics extensions (leptoquarks, SUSY variants, dark sector). **The time-step-with-gaps lens has very limited traction here.** The framework should be especially careful — particle physics is a domain where speculative cross-domain frameworks routinely get applied promiscuously and dismissed for that reason. Most of the entries in the particle physics research bundle do *not* belong in this Reading.

### 6.2 The one substantive fit

**Hierarchy / naturalness problem in the Higgs sector (substrate fit: strong, structural).** The hierarchy problem requires fine-tuning to ~1 part in 10³⁴ across the Wilsonian RG flow from Planck to electroweak scale. **If scale (equivalently, time/energy resolution) has substrate-level granularity or occlusion, the very notion of arbitrarily fine cancellation across decades may be ill-posed.** Fine-tuning becomes a quantization-floor question rather than a model-building question. This is structurally adjacent to existing "naturalness is a pseudo-problem" arguments (Hossenfelder, others) but adds a specific mechanism: the substrate's gap structure sets the floor of meaningful precision in RG flow. The framework's reading: the fine-tuning problem is asking a question the substrate doesn't admit. **Honest accounting:** this is intriguing but requires the framework to specify what "gap structure in scale space" actually means, which is open work.

### 6.3 Notable null cases

- **Muon g-2:** Resolved against the anomaly in 2025 by Theory Initiative WP25 + lattice QCD. Framework should not retrofit.
- **W boson mass (CDF outlier):** Effectively resolved against CDF. Framework should not retrofit.
- **Sterile neutrino / gallium anomaly, B-meson anomalies, ATOMKI X17:** All have mundane candidate explanations (nuclear-physics systematics, hadronic form factors, detector specifics). Framework should not opportunistically claim relevance.

## 7. Consciousness science & cognitive open problems

### 7.1 Strong fits — at the Tier 1 epistemological level (cont 26 §4 already canon)

**Temporal binding window and individual variability (substrate fit: very strong).** Multisensory inputs ≤200ms perceived as single event; TBW varies dramatically across individuals (atypically wide in autism, schizophrenia, dyslexia, aging). **This is exactly what cont 26 §4 (perceptual rate as sense) predicts** — different observers have different sampling rates; individual TBW differences are sampling-rate differences. The Pav-built Michotte Launching extension tool (`/artifacts/michotte_launching_extension.html`) is literally the framework's instrument for testing this. **Strong fit, Tier 1.** The framework's contribution is naming TBW as the perceptual-rate canon at the human ring of observer-classes rather than as a peripheral cog-sci finding.

**Binding problem (substrate fit: strong).** How does the brain assemble distributed feature-processing into unified percepts? **Binding is fundamentally a question of what counts as simultaneous.** A substrate that grants only quantized "now" intervals would make binding a discretization phenomenon, not a synchrony phenomenon. Framework reading aligns with cont 26 §4 + cont 26 §3 L0 failsafes; gives a mechanism the gamma-synchrony hypothesis lacks.

**Time perception under psychedelics and meditation (substrate fit: strong, Tier 1).** Psilocybin / LSD / DMT reliably produce time dilation/compression/timelessness; deep meditation modulates time-perception similarly. **Both phenomena are consistent with 5-HT2A activation and deep meditation modulating the substrate's sampling rate or gap structure** — making "shared time" a function of two systems sampling on aligned cadences. Cont 26 §4 covers this directly at Tier 1.

### 7.2 Moderate fits — Tier 2 territory

**Anesthesia awareness and memory-without-recall paradox (substrate fit: moderate, Tier 2).** Patients show implicit memory of intraoperative events despite EEG signatures suggesting unconsciousness. **Anesthesia may not "turn off" consciousness uniformly but stretch the gaps between conscious frames** — encoding-without-binding happens *between* the sparser perception frames. Frame-rate dropout as a model of anesthesia is framework-coherent and produces predictions: depth of anesthesia should track with frame-rate not amplitude; recovery should show characteristic timing-stutter signatures. **Honest accounting:** Hameroff-aligned microtubule work is adjacent but contested; the framework should not anchor itself to Orch-OR specifically.

**Dying-brain gamma surge / RED (Borjigin 2023, Parnia AWARE) (substrate fit: moderate, Tier 2 for the gamma surge, Tier 3 for veridical-perception claims).** A 30-second gamma surge after cardiac arrest is well-documented (PNAS 2023). **If conscious time is substrate-quantized, a 30-second gamma surge could subjectively unfold over much longer felt durations** — addressing "life review" reports without invoking dualism. Tier 2 because the physiological gamma surge is mainstream; the veridical-perception claims (Parnia AWARE-II) are Tier 3 with explicit promotion bars (multi-site rigorous replication of corroborated cases).

### 7.3 Where the lens shouldn't be invoked

- **Hard problem of consciousness:** The framework can gesture (conscious moments quantized → search for spatial NCC may be misframed) but does not explain qualia or subjective experience. The framework's stance should remain agnostic on consciousness ontology per cont 17.
- **Inter-brain synchrony / hyperscanning (Gao et al. 2025):** Mainstream-but-early; the framework should not lean on it. If shared sampling-rate alignment is real, that's interesting; the replication base is too thin.
- **Presentiment / Bem-style retrocausal PSI:** **Should not be invoked** in this Reading. The empirical foundation is too weak (the Kekecs et al. 2025 PLOS One Transparent Psi Project is a clean negative). Cont 27 §3 pruning procedure should keep this at the speculative tier *or below* without any framework endorsement. Mentioning here only to mark explicitly that the framework rejects opportunistic alignment with paranormal research.

## 9. Honest risk accounting

The Reading must explicitly address the failure modes flagged in §1:

**Kitchen-sink syndrome.** The Reading lists ~13 candidate reframings. That's a lot. A skeptical reader will note that the framework finds the time-step-with-gaps hypothesis applicable to roughly a third of the open problems surveyed. **The defense is that the hypothesis is being applied selectively where the substrate structure of the open problem is already pointing at time** — not promiscuously across all anomalies. The hierarchy problem entry (§6.2) is a good test: the framework finds traction there only because the question itself is about scale-space resolution, which is structurally a time/scale-gap question. The framework does NOT claim relevance to muon g-2, B-meson ratios, dark matter composition, or most particle anomalies — and the Reading is explicit about this. The hit rate (~30% of surveyed problems) is high enough to be suspicious; the *selectivity* of the hits (clustering around time-substrate questions) is the defense.

**Cross-tier laundering.** Reading 05 must not let Tier 2 candidate reframings inherit confidence from Tier 1 perceptual-rate canon. The tables and tier columns in §8 are the defense. Each entry's promotion path is named so readers can see what would advance the candidate. **No Tier 3 claim** (L0 occlusion as aux channel) is invoked anywhere in this Reading except by direct cross-reference; the Reading operates at Tier 1 and Tier 2 throughout to keep the speculation-discipline tight.

**Selection bias in the research.** The subagents were given a brief that flagged time-uncertainty relevance for each problem. They were instructed not to apply the framework lens, but the brief itself biased what problems they surfaced. **A genuinely adversarial test would survey open problems WITHOUT the flag and see which ones the framework would *not* notice were relevant.** That follow-up is queued.

**The framework does not need this Reading to validate its core claims.** Cont 26 §4 perceptual-rate-as-sense is Tier 1 canon on its own well-evidenced cog-sci grounds. Cont 27 §1 energy-floor failsafe is canon on its own four-substrate evidence. Reading 04 compilation-rate-as-time is Tier 2 candidate conditional on Bortolotti et al. The aux-channel Tier 3 speculation has its own promotion bars. **This Reading is an exploration of where the framework's existing positions might find external traction; it is not load-bearing for the framework's existing positions.** If all 13 candidate reframings fail to gain external uptake, the framework's core canon and candidates are unaffected.

## §12 Addendum (2026-05-28, same day) — cognitive perception neurobiology + atomic tunneling deep dives

Pav flagged that §2.1 (problem of time / arrival-time / tunneling-time, lumped together) and §7 (consciousness science, fairly thin) under-covered the two areas where the framework should have most experimental traction. Two focused subagents researched each area in depth (~3,800 words of new research, ~30 additional cited sources). The §12 addendum incorporates the findings honestly per cont 27 §2 — strong fits get named as Tier 2 candidates with promotion paths; weak or contested findings get flagged; presentiment-adjacent claims continue to be rejected.

### §12.1 Cognitive perception neurobiology — what the deep dive found

The cog-sci research returned nine substantive topics with 2023–2026 empirical activity. Tier-tagged synthesis:

**Strong Tier 1 reinforcement** (cont 26 §4 perceptual rate as sense already canon; the deep dive provides additional empirical anchoring):

- **Attentional sampling at theta (~4-8Hz)** (Fiebelkorn, Kastner, Plöchl 2022; preprint 2024.02.25.581860). Single attended object samples at ~8Hz; two attended objects alternate at ~4Hz. Frontoparietal-pulvinar network with theta-modulated sampling/shifting. **This is the strongest empirical anchor the framework has for the perceptual-rate canon at the human ring.** Attention literally blinks; perception inherits the cadence. The Michotte Launching extension tool's Attention Sampling parameter is operationalizing exactly this body of work; the tool's findings would compose with the Fiebelkorn-line evidence.

- **Discrete vs continuous perception / "frame rate of consciousness"** (VanRullen-Koch lineage; 2024 alpha-phase-of-perception studies; 2025 bioRxiv challenge from Donoghue lab that aperiodic 1/f slope may matter more than alpha oscillations). Active debate, leaning quasi-discrete with ~100ms perceptual frames. **The Donoghue challenge is important honest-accounting** — the framework should not pin its claims on "alpha = frame rate" specifically, because the underlying mechanism may be aperiodic broadband structure rather than discrete oscillation. The cont 26 §4 canon is robust either way (rate matters), but the specific physiological mechanism is unsettled.

- **Psychophysics of temporal resolution + 30Hz between-individual range** (Manning, Mareschal & Jenkins 2024 PLOS ONE — "The speed of sight"). Critical flicker fusion thresholds vary by ~30Hz across healthy participants; ~80% of variance is between-individual, ~10% within-individual across sessions. Visual temporal resolution is a **stable individual trait**, not a noisy measurement. **This is the most accessible empirical demonstration of cont 26 §4 at the individual-observer level** — two people watching the same flicker have measurably different experiences because their substrate sampling differs by tens of Hz. Promotes from "framework prediction" to "documented psychophysical fact."

**Tier 2 candidate strengthening** (Reading 04 compilation-rate-as-time gets specific physiological mechanisms as candidate substrates):

- **Time cells (hippocampus → entorhinal → neocortex)** (Eichenbaum lineage; Umbach 2020 human evidence; 2025 *Cell Reports* mesoscale 2P imaging showing area-specific temporal coding). The brain has **dedicated cellular machinery for representing elapsed time** — distinct from clock-time, discretized into firing fields, log-spaced resolution in hippocampus, ramp-coded in PFC. **Framework reading:** if time cells *constitute* the substrate for subjective duration rather than just *representing* clock time, then experienced time is a coded readout. This is structurally compatible with Reading 04's compilation-rate-as-time and adds concrete neural mechanism candidate.

- **Thalamocortical gating of conscious access** (*Science* 2025, "Human high-order thalamic nuclei gate conscious perception through the thalamofrontal loop"). High-order thalamic nuclei fire before prefrontal ignition during perceptual breakthrough. The thalamic gate operates on ~100-200ms timescales — the "ignition" duration GNWT predicts. **If conscious access is literally gated by a discrete thalamic event, the substrate has a fundamental "tick" at that timescale.** This is a stronger version of the cont 26 §4 claim with a specific neural mechanism candidate.

- **Predictive coding / Active Inference temporal-prediction error** (Friston, Hohwy, Clark; honest accounting per Walsh et al. 2023 NBR review — empirical support is "modest"). The framework natively reads as "perception is constituted by time-gap discrepancies across hierarchical scales." Framework-coherent but the empirical base is the contested part; the framework should not lean too hard on a research program whose own foundations are unsettled.

**Tier 2 candidate, contested substrate** (the controversial mechanism that lives at exactly the framework's tier; honest accounting required):

- **Microtubule / Orch-OR / quantum-cognition hypotheses** (Hameroff-Penrose; status genuinely contested 2024-2025). 2024 Singh/Yagnik/Hameroff *eNeuro* paper: epothilone B (microtubule stabilizer) **delays anesthetic-induced unconsciousness in rats**, predicted by Orch-OR. Wiest 2025 *Neuroscience of Consciousness* review argues for the framework. Critique surveys (arxiv 2501.03241) argue the decoherence calculations remain contested. **Honest accounting:** Orch-OR is uniquely explicit that consciousness IS a sequence of discrete substrate-level events (OR collapses) at ~40Hz — structurally identical to what the framework's Tier 2 candidate predicts. **The framework should NOT endorse Orch-OR specifically** (per the cont 26 §4 forward-pointer's caution about pinning to a specific contested model). But the framework should note that Orch-OR is the cleanest articulation of the time-stepped-substrate position in current literature, that the 2024 epothilone B result is the kind of empirical evidence Tier 2 candidates need, and that the framework's reading is compatible with but not dependent on Orch-OR being correct.

**Mainstream cog-sci event the framework should engage with directly:**

- **Cogitate Consortium IIT vs GNWT adversarial collaboration** (Nature 642, 2025). Both theories wounded but neither killed. IIT challenged by absence of sustained posterior synchronization; GNWT challenged by lack of prefrontal "ignition" at stimulus offset. **The variable-stimulus-duration methodology speaks directly to whether consciousness is a continuous integrated state or a discrete ignition event.** The empirical impasse opens space for a third position — the framework's Tier 2 compilation-rate reading — but the framework must NOT claim the impasse vindicates it. The impasse is genuine; the framework's reading is one candidate among several.

**Topics where the lens shouldn't be invoked or should be invoked cautiously:**

- **Gamma synchrony binding hypothesis** — active 30-year debate, now favoring "weak binding by synchrony." Framework reading possible (non-sinusoidal entrainment-with-delay is closer to discrete-substrate position) but the literature is doing the framework's work without needing framework vocabulary.
- **CEMI electromagnetic field theory of consciousness** (McFadden 2025). Minority position; lacks decisive experiments. Framework should not align with specific minority physics-of-consciousness models opportunistically; mention only as adjacent.
- **Iconic memory / phenomenal-vs-access consciousness** (Block overflow). No major 2024-2026 empirical breakthrough; the question is mostly conceptual. Framework adds nothing here.

### §12.2 Atomic tunneling — what the deep dive found

The tunneling research returned nine substantive topics. The pattern is sharper than the cog-sci section: tunneling is *literally about what time means when a process operates inside a classically forbidden region*, so the framework's time-substrate lens has natively stronger traction. Tier-tagged synthesis:

**Tier 2 anchor — the framework's strongest experimental window:**

- **Attoclock measurements + Larmor clock unification (Spierings & Steinberg 2020-21; Schach & Giese 2024 Sci. Adv. unification framework).** The attoclock results (Sainadh et al. 2019 *Nature*: H tunneling time consistent with zero within ~1.8 as) and the Larmor/Ramsey clock results (Spierings 2021 PRL: ~0.59 ms dwell time in BEC tunneling) appear contradictory until Schach-Giese 2024 unified them: **operational tunneling time is clock-relative; different clocks give different finite times; no genuinely instantaneous or superluminal tunneling exists when the clock is properly operational.** This is *structurally* the time-as-fidelity-quantifier framing from cont 26 §4 made physics. The framework's contribution here is interpretive vocabulary: "tunneling time depends on which observer-clock you couple to the process" IS cont 26 §4 + Reading 04 applied to substrate-level processes. **This is the cleanest Tier 2 anchor the framework has after Bortolotti et al.** The Schach-Giese paper does the framework's work explicitly without using framework vocabulary.

**Strong Tier 2 candidate — biological substrates where time-gap structure would be functionally harnessed:**

- **Enzyme catalysis by hydrogen tunneling** (Klinman, Hammes-Schiffer; Klinman, Miller & Richards 2025 *JACS* "A Foundational Shift in Models for Enzyme Function"). Many enzymes accelerate H-transfer by tunneling, not thermal barrier crossing. Soybean lipoxygenase KIE ≈ 80 at 30°C, near-temperature-independent. The protein scaffold's role is to engineer the donor-acceptor distance distribution that makes tunneling probable. **Framework reading:** an enzyme is a biological device that *shapes the time-window* in which a particle can pass through a classically forbidden region — directly using substrate-level time-gap structure as a catalytic mechanism. This is the strongest Tier 2 candidate in the §12 addendum: the substrate-gap framing is structurally what the literature is already saying ("vibronically nonadiabatic PCET with promoting modes gating tunneling probability") with the framework's vocabulary being one possible synthesis.

- **DNA mutation by proton tunneling** (Löwdin 1963 → Slocombe, Sacchi, Al-Khalili 2022 *Commun. Phys.*). Proton-tunneling contribution to G-C tautomerization is **orders of magnitude larger than classical over-barrier hopping** at biological temperature. Canonical-to-tautomeric interconversion happens far faster than replication, providing a quantum-mechanical source of spontaneous point mutations. **Framework reading:** a single substrate-level tunneling event (proton transfer in a few-fs window) writes a permanent state change (mutation) that propagates through every downstream copy. This is the thermodynamic-ratchet structure where a transient quantum substrate-event becomes a permanent classical record — exactly the asymmetric write that a time-substrate framing predicts at the substrate-record boundary.

**Tier 2 candidate — historic puzzle the framework's reading reframes:**

- **Hartman effect / apparent superluminal tunneling** (Hartman 1962; Winful 2006 reinterpretation; reopened in attoclock/matter-wave contexts). Winful's resolution (group delay = cavity-lifetime/dwell-time of stored energy, not transit time) handles the photonics case. Matter-wave and strong-field contexts still see Hartman-like saturation. **Framework reading:** the "saturated phase delay" in thick-barrier tunneling is exactly what a substrate-level time-gap structure would produce — beyond a certain barrier width, the tunneling event passes through fewer substrate ticks than naïve classical accounting expects, producing the appearance of saturation. The framework's reading is structurally compatible with Winful's cavity-lifetime reinterpretation but adds the substrate-level grounding for *why* the cavity-lifetime accounting works.

**Tier 2 candidate — astrochemical and cold-temperature substrates:**

- **Cold-temperature reaction rates / astrochemical tunneling** (Senevirathne et al. 2017; Lamberts 2024; 2025 *A&A* CO→CH₃OH hydrogenation). In dense interstellar clouds (~10 K), classical Arrhenius rates are effectively zero, yet hydrogenated complex organics are abundant. Tunneling-mediated chemistry dominates at temperatures where the classical clock essentially stops. **Framework reading:** in regimes where temperature drives classical rates to zero, the substrate's "rate of happening" is entirely set by quantum tunneling events — the cleanest empirical regime where the substrate-level event-rate IS what's happening, with no classical-thermal alternative. Most direct Tier 2 evidence that time-flow is substrate-event-rate rather than parameter.

**Strong Tier 1 reinforcement — operational time depends on probe:**

- **Wigner / Eisenbud-Wigner-Smith delay vs Büttiker-Landauer dwell time** (Kheifets 2023 *J. Phys. B* review; multiple inequivalent operational definitions). The fact that **multiple operationally inequivalent "times" attach to the same physical process** is the substrate signature the framework already names at Tier 1 in cont 26 §4. Time is not a single scalar handed to you by the universe — it's an interaction-defined quantity that branches with the probe.

**Tier 2 candidate — engineered macroscopic test platform:**

- **Macroscopic quantum tunneling in superconducting circuits** (2025 Nobel Prize to Clarke/Devoret/Martinis; arXiv 2502.06246 on scalable cat states via quantum tunneling). Tunneling at macroscopic scales is now engineered routinely; superconducting qubits operate by exactly this mechanism. **Framework reading:** the cleanest engineered playground for asking what a tunneling event "takes" in time when the tunneling object is macroscopic. If substrate-level time-gap structure exists, macroscopic-tunneling qubits should reveal it through ringing/oscillation signatures that depend on the substrate's gap density rather than just the Josephson energy. Promotion path: testable in current-generation cQED.

**Topics where the lens shouldn't be invoked or should be qualified:**

- **Photosynthetic vibronic coherence** (Engel 2007; largely walked back by Duan et al. 2017, Cao 2020). The "wave-like quantum energy transfer" narrative is substantially retracted; vibronic coherence with modest functional role is current consensus. Framework should NOT invoke "quantum biology" at photosynthesis level; the field has self-corrected.
- **Olfaction quantum-tunneling (Turin vibrational theory)** — fringe-leaning minority. Block 2015 *PNAS* critique stands; the 2011 Drosophila isotope-discrimination result has not replicated in mammals. Framework should NOT align with this; the architecture (tunneling-rate spectrometer) is interesting but the specific application to olfaction is poorly supported.
- **Nuclear fusion tunneling** (Gamow) — settled physics, no live time-of-tunneling debate. Framework adds nothing.
