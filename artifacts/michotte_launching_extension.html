Michotte Launching, extended — billiard-chain · does perception scale with chain length?

v2 rebuild of Michotte's 1946/63 Launching paradigm. Replaces v1's single A→B with an N-ball billiard chain (2–6 configurable) — momentum transfers through the chain via per-link Mechanical Delay, like Newton's cradle with controlled hesitation at each collision. The observer must perceive each cause-effect link separately; partial-chain perception is now possible (saw links 0→1 and 1→2, missed 2→3). Lane 1 occlusion overlay makes the observer's blind moments visible on the stage itself — orange tint when between attention samples; purple tint when an L0 occlusion gap fires (per Tier 3 speculation). The temporal graph below tracks each link's contact and transfer events separately and computes link-by-link perception. The framework's prediction: as chain length grows, the probability of fully perceiving every link decays multiplicatively with attention-sampling rate — chain perception is a multiplicative test of the Tier 1 perceptual-rate canon.

Same programmatic/procedural/point-based format as wrapper_overlap_animated.html — single self-contained HTML, all state cascades from parameters, no fed-in data. §01What v2 adds beyond v1

v1 had a single A→B collision. v2 extends to a billiard chain of N balls (2–6 configurable) with real momentum-transfer collision physics. The chain is structurally important to the framework because perception of each cause-effect link is independent — the observer can perceive some links and miss others. With chain length K, there are K−1 causal links to perceive; the probability of perceiving ALL of them given attention sampling rate r and integration window w decays multiplicatively. This makes chain length a sharper experimental probe of the cont 26 §4 perceptual-rate canon than single-link Michotte alone.

The other v2 addition is Lane 1 occlusion overlay. v1 showed occlusion only in the Lane 3 temporal graph; you had to read the graph to know when the observer was blind. v2 makes the observer's blind moments visible on the stage itself — orange tint with "OBSERVER BLIND" label when between attention samples; purple tint with "L0 OCCLUSION — SHARED GAP IN TIME-FLOW" label when a substrate-level gap fires. The two overlays are visually distinct because they correspond to different framework tiers (Tier 1 per-observer effect vs Tier 3 shared-substrate speculation).

§02What this extension adds beyond classical Michotte

Classical Michotte varies the physical parameters: gap timing, spatial contact, direction. The observer is treated as a passive registrar. This extension treats the observer as a parameterizable observer-class per the agnostic framework. Three new parameters change what gets perceived even when physical parameters are held fixed:

ParameterWhat it doesFramework primitive it maps to
Chain Length
2–6 balls		 Number of balls in the billiard chain. With K balls there are K−1 causal links the observer must perceive. Each additional link is an independent perception test. Composition of Tier 1 canon — multiplicative test of perceptual-rate effects. Sharpens the empirical signal of cont 26 §4.
Mechanical Delay
0–500ms per link		 Per-collision delay between contact and momentum transfer to next ball. Classical Michotte parameter applied per-link. Substrate-level temporal parameter (the thing the framework's claims are about).
Attention Sampling
10–100%		 Observer sample frequency. With K-1 links to perceive, low attention means missed links throughout the chain. Cont 26 §4 perceptual rate as a sense — Tier 1 canon. Chain amplifies the test.
Sensory Bridging
checkbox		 Cross-modal integration. Widens temporal integration window from ~100ms (visual-only) to ~300ms. Each link gets the wider window. Cont 14 + cont 16 cross-modal membrane.
L0 Occlusion
0–50%		 Probability per second of a shared substrate-level gap. Visible as purple tint over Lane 1 + purple band in Lane 3. Affects all observers equally if the speculation is real. Tier 3 speculative — exposed as parameter per cont 27 §2 honest move.
Speed
0.25–2x		 Compilation rate. At slow speeds you see fine-grained chain dynamics; at fast speeds chain collapses to gross outcome. Same role as wrapper-overlap Speed slider. Per Reading 04, compilation-rate-as-time (Tier 2 candidate).

§03What to try — chain-aware presets

PRESET 1 · Newton's-cradle baseline (chain of 4, short delay)

Chain: 4 · Delay: 30ms · Attention: 80% · Bridging: OFF · L0: 0%

Expected: full chain perceived. Short delay keeps each link within visual-only window; high attention catches every event. Classical billiard cascade. Verdict: LAUNCHING.

PRESET 2 · Chain stretch test (longer chain, classical delay)

Chain: 6 · Delay: 80ms · Attention: 60% · Bridging: OFF · L0: 0%

Expected: partial chain. Five links to perceive at moderate attention; some links will fall in between observer samples. Verdict typically AMBIGUOUS with 3–4 of 5 links perceived. This is the multiplicative-perception effect making the chain length a sharper probe than single-link Michotte.

PRESET 3 · Cross-modal chain rescue (the headline test)

Chain: 5 · Delay: 200ms · Attention: 60% · Bridging: ON · L0: 0%

Expected (framework prediction): more links perceived with bridging on than off. Delays exceed visual-only window but stay under bridged window. The cont 14/16 mapping predicts cross-modal binding preserves chain perception. Try this with bridging on, then off — the delta is the framework-relevant empirical finding.

PRESET 4 · Attention starvation across chain

Chain: 5 · Delay: 30ms · Attention: 15% · Bridging: OFF · L0: 0%

Expected: broken or near-broken chain, even with short delays. Even when each individual link is physically within Michotte window, low attention means observer's samples skip most events. Watch the orange overlay flash repeatedly across Lane 1 as the chain progresses — the observer is blind through most of the cascade.

PRESET 5 · L0 occlusion across chain (Tier 3 speculation made visible)

Chain: 4 · Delay: 50ms · Attention: 80% · Bridging: OFF · L0: 30%

Expected: variable across runs. Even with high attention and short delays — when an L0 gap fires during the cascade, even multiple observers would simultaneously fail to perceive that link. Watch for purple overlay events on Lane 1 + purple bands in Lane 3 + the resulting partial-chain verdicts. Run multiple trials; the same parameters produce different outcomes because L0 occlusion is stochastic. This is what the Tier 3 speculation would predict if it were real; the framework does not claim it is.

§04What the framework predicts

Tier 1 — chain perception decays multiplicatively with chain length

If single-link perception has probability p (function of attention, bridging, delay), then K-link chain perception has probability p^(K−1) under independence. This is a sharp framework-relevant prediction: chain length should be a much more powerful diagnostic of attention-rate effects than single-link Michotte. A subject who perceives 95% of single-link trials may perceive only 60% of 4-link chains and 20% of 8-link chains.

Tier 1 — Lane 1 occlusion overlay validates the perception-vs-physics distinction

The orange-tint moments in Lane 1 are when the observer is between samples. The physics keeps running underneath — balls keep moving, momentum keeps transferring. But the observer doesn't get those frames. This is the cleanest visual demonstration of cont 26 §4: perception rate is a sense that selects which dynamics are legible. The physics is not affected; the observer is.

Tier 2 — Speed slider tests the compilation-rate hypothesis

If compilation-rate-as-time holds (Reading 04 Tier 2 candidate, contested physics), watching the chain at different Speed settings doesn't just rescale viewing — it changes which equilibria the chain settles into. At very slow speed you can perceive every micro-collision; at high speed the chain blurs together. This is observer-dependent at the substrate level, not just at the perceptual level.

Tier 3 — L0 occlusion would scale per-trial unpredictably across all observers

If L0 occlusion is real, the same physical chain run multiple times would produce different perception outcomes — and the unpredictability would be shared across observers, not per-observer. The L0 slider exposes this. Single-observer testing cannot validate Layer 3; the simulation makes the shape of the prediction legible.

§05How to interpret findings

The three-tier discipline from cont 27 §2 applies:

  • Layer 1 findings stay at Layer 1. Chain-perception decay with chain length confirms cont 26 §4 canon. It does NOT confirm Layer 2 (compilation-rate-as-time) nor Layer 3 (L0 occlusion).
  • Layer 2 requires external evidence. The Speed slider's chain-perception effects are intuition pumps, not evidence for compilation-rate-as-time. The contested physics (collapse models, Bortolotti et al. 2025) determines whether the Layer 2 reading is right.
  • Layer 3 findings are intuition pumps only. The L0 Occlusion slider lets you SEE what L0 occlusion would look like — purple overlays across Lane 1, shared gaps in Lane 3. That visualization is not evidence the speculation is correct. Promotion to Tier 2 requires the bars in /speculations/.

The simulation makes the layers visually distinct precisely because the temptation to laundering is high — three sliders that all change verdict outcomes look interchangeable but operate at three different epistemic tiers.
