## Status

This is an **active research framework**, not a published theory. The discipline is Lakatosian: a hard core of canon primitives, a surrounding belt of candidates being scouted, a speculative outer layer that may compile to canon or get pruned, and a record of what was tried and discarded. Every claim carries a status (canon / candidate / speculative / pruned) reflecting the framework's own compile discipline.

The framework predicts a shift from generative-prediction-with-amplification toward viability-by-constraint-satisfaction across intelligence-bearing systems. As of May 2026, four independent carriers (Aleph + EBMs, MAMMAL, ANDI, Lippmann plates) landed in a two-week window instantiating this shape across four substrate scales. See [continuation 15](continuations/15.md) for the convergence analysis.

## The framework in one paragraph

Intelligence is a fuzzy-to-canon compiler. Observers (with a harness + wrapper + inner sandbox + action-space) encounter the world as a fuzzy candidate field, generate plausible interpretations (A⁺), check those candidates against constraints from prior canon and the substrate (A⁻), and admit what survives as new canon. The compilation is asymmetric: A⁻ is the primary discipline — checking is what forms canon, generation is the easier upstream half. Canon then propagates as carrier-artifacts (words, tools, equations, rituals, institutions, AI models) which re-render at receiving observers. Different observers symbiose into richer combined observer-classes via slow-fast pushouts. The substrate is computationally irreducible — it must be run, not solved — and the discipline of cultivation is "build the rules and building blocks; nurture; observe what emerges; refine the shape over time" (the emergent garden).
